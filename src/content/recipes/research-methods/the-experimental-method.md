Use this procedure when you want to test a hypothesis by manipulating an independent variable and measuring the effect on a dependent variable.

1. Make an observation.
2. Formulate a hypothesis based upon the observation.
3. Design and run an experiment to test the hypothesis.
4. Analyze the results of the experiment.
5. Accept or reject the hypothesis.
6. If rejected, formulate and test a new hypothesis.
