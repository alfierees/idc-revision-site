Use this sequence when building a questionnaire. The lecture notes there are no hard rules, because designs vary with the researcher's needs.

1. Decide on the information required.
2. Define the population.
3. Select the appropriate method(s) of reaching your participants.
4. Decide on question content.
5. Formulate the question wording.
6. Place the questions in a meaningful order and format.
7. Run a pilot.
8. Develop the final survey.

## Common pitfalls

- Questions that are too long or ask several things at once.
- Ambiguous or unfamiliar words.
- Assumptions, generalizations and implicit alternatives.
- Biased questions that push the respondent toward an answer.
