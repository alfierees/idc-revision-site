# Principles of Micro Concepts

> One-stop glossary for Principles of Micro (Year 1, Semester 1). Each entry: a one-line definition + the note where it is taught. Tracker of which links exist: [[_Wiki-Link Registry]]. Subject hub: [[Principles of Micro]].

## Production

### Marginal Product of Labour
The extra output from one more worker, change in output divided by change in workers. Seen in: [[Macro-Economics (Intro)]]. → [[Lec_01-Production and Costs|Lec 01]]

### Diminishing Marginal Product
Each extra worker adds less output than the one before, so output rises at a falling rate. → [[Lec_01-Production and Costs|Lec 01]]

## Cost Concepts

### Fixed Costs
Costs that do not vary with the quantity produced. → [[Lec_01-Production and Costs|Lec 01]]

### Variable Costs
Costs that vary with the quantity produced. → [[Lec_01-Production and Costs|Lec 01]]

### Total Cost
Fixed cost plus variable cost. → [[Lec_01-Production and Costs|Lec 01]]

## Per-Unit and Marginal Costs

### Average Fixed Cost
Fixed cost divided by quantity; it falls as output rises. → [[Lec_01-Production and Costs|Lec 01]]

### Average Variable Cost
Variable cost divided by quantity; it rises in the worked bagel example. → [[Lec_01-Production and Costs|Lec 01]]

### Average Total Cost
Total cost divided by quantity, equal to AFC + AVC; U-shaped in the bagel example. → [[Lec_01-Production and Costs|Lec 01]]

### Marginal Cost
The change in total cost from producing one more unit; it crosses ATC at ATC's minimum. Seen in: [[Micro-Economics II]]. → [[Lec_01-Production and Costs|Lec 01]]
