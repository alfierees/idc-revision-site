      **From equations to RREF.** Write the system as an augmented matrix, reduce it to RREF, and find the solution.

      $$
      \begin{cases}3x_1+3x_2-4x_3+x_4=2\\ x_1+x_2-x_3-x_4=5\end{cases}
      $$
    solution: |
      > [!info] Working written for this site; the final answer is from the official sheet.

      $$
      \left[\begin{array}{cccc|c}3&3&-4&1&2\\1&1&-1&-1&5\end{array}\right]
      \xrightarrow{R_1\leftrightarrow R_2}
      \left[\begin{array}{cccc|c}1&1&-1&-1&5\\3&3&-4&1&2\end{array}\right]
      $$

      $R_2-3R_1$ gives $[0,0,-1,4\,|\,-13]$; scale by $-1$ to $[0,0,1,-4\,|\,13]$; then $R_1+R_2$:

      $$
      \left[\begin{array}{cccc|c}1&1&0&-5&18\\0&0&1&-4&13\end{array}\right]
      $$

      Free variables: $x_2=t$, $x_4=m$. Then $x_1=18-t+5m$ and $x_3=13+4m$.

      > [!success] Answer
      > $(18-t+5m,\;t,\;13+4m,\;m)$
