    text: |
      Given $x_1=3,\ x_2=5,\ x_3=-1,\ x_4=2,\ x_5=0,\ x_6=4$.

      **(1.1)** $\;\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{6}(x_i-1)$
    solution: |
      > [!note] Working reconstructed to reach the printed answer
      > $\sum x_i=3+5-1+2+0+4=13$, and $\sum_{i=1}^{6}1=6$. So $13-6=7$.

      > [!success] Answer (printed)
      > $7$.

      Given $x_1=3,\ x_2=5,\ x_3=-1,\ x_4=2,\ x_5=0,\ x_6=4$.

      **(1.3)** $\;\displaystyle\sum_{i=2}^{4}\left(\frac{1}{x_i}\right)-1$
    solution: |
      > [!note] Working reconstructed to reach the printed answer
      > The $-1$ is outside the sigma (only $1/x_i$ is inside the brackets):
      >
      > $$
      > \frac1{x_2}+\frac1{x_3}+\frac1{x_4}=\frac15-1+\frac12=-0.3,\qquad -0.3-1=-1.3.
      > $$

      > [!success] Answer (printed)
      > $-1.3$.

      > [!tip] Intuition
      > If the $-1$ were inside the sum, you would subtract $1$ three times and get $-3.3$. The printed answer $-1.3$ shows the $-1$ sits outside.
    related_terms: ["sigma-notation", "summation-rules"]
    source_doc_page: 1
  - id: "1.4"
    text: |
      Given $x_1=3,\ x_2=5,\ x_3=-1,\ x_4=2,\ x_5=0,\ x_6=4$.

      **(1.4)** $\;\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{4}x_1$
    solution: |
      > [!note] Working reconstructed to reach the printed answer
      > The term has no $i$ in it: it is the constant $x_1=3$ added four times. $4\cdot3=12$.

      > [!success] Answer (printed)
      > $12$.

      > [!tip] Intuition
      > Watch the subscript: it is $x_1$ (a fixed number), not $x_i$. If it were $x_i$ the sum would be $3+5-1+2=9$.

      Given $\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{6}x_j=-4$ and $\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{6}x_j^{2}=10$, use the summation properties to find:

      **(a)** $\;\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{6}(2x_j+3)$
    solution: |
      > [!note] Working reconstructed to reach the printed answer
      >
      > $$
      > 2\sum x_j+\sum_{j=1}^{6}3=2(-4)+6\cdot3=-8+18=10.
      > $$

      > [!success] Answer (printed)
      > $10$.
    related_terms: ["sigma-notation", "summation-rules"]

      Given $\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{6}x_j=-4$ and $\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{6}x_j^{2}=10$, use the summation properties to find:

      **(b)** $\;\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{6}x_j(x_j-1)$

      > Expand $x_j(x_j-1)=x_j^2-x_j$:
      >
      > $$
      > \sum x_j^2-\sum x_j=10-(-4)=14.
      > $$

      > [!success] Answer (printed)
      > $14$.

      **(c)** $\;\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{6}(x_j-5)^{2}$
