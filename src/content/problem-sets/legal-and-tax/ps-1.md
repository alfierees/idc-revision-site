      **Confidentiality Agreement (NDA).** Part of the midterm group assignment: an executive summary (up to five pages, Word, David or Times New Roman 12, groups of up to three, due 1 January 2026) of the key legal considerations of a venture your team invents.

      - Outline the **core principles** that a confidentiality agreement should encompass when dealing with **third parties**.
      - Present **three essential elements** that you believe should be incorporated into your venture's NDA.

      **Where the course covers this**

      - [[Lec_02-Legal Entrepreneurship — Fundamental Concepts]]: the [[NDA]], plus [[Non-Compete]] and [[Due Diligence]] (the usual moment third parties see your information).
      - [[Lec_05-Intellectual Property]]: [[Trade Secret]] protection, which is what an NDA is protecting, and how it compares with [[Patent]] protection.
      - [[Lec_04-Contract Law]]: enforceability of the NDA as a contract ([[Freedom of Contract]], [[Public Policy]], [[Definiteness (Mesuyamut)]]).
      - [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation]]: if the shared information includes personal data, the privacy rules apply on top of the NDA ([[GDPR]], [[Data Processor]]).

      **Legal Risk Assessment.** Part of the midterm group assignment: an executive summary (up to five pages, Word, David or Times New Roman 12, groups of up to three, due 1 January 2026) of the key legal considerations of a venture your team invents.

      - Identify **five significant legal risks** your venture may encounter.
      - Propose a **strategy** for effectively managing and mitigating each of these risks.

      **Where the course covers this**

      - [[Lec_02-Legal Entrepreneurship — Fundamental Concepts]]: the section "The Five Core Legal Risks" (no founders' agreement, improper corporate structure, no NDAs, neglecting privacy, improper open-source use). It is the course's own framework for this question; your five risks should be tied to your own venture.
      - [[Lec_05-Intellectual Property]]: ownership and protection of IP ([[Intellectual Property Rights]], [[Trade Secret]], [[Patent]], [[Trademark]], [[Copyright]]) and AI-era IP issues ([[AI and Copyright]]).
      - [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation]]: privacy and digital compliance ([[GDPR]], [[Personally Identifiable Information]], [[Privacy by Design]], [[EU AI Act]], [[Regulatory Compliance]]).
      - [[Lec_03-Company Law & Corporate Structures]]: liability and governance risks ([[Limited Liability]], [[Duty of Loyalty]], [[Duty of Care]], [[Agency Problem]]).
      - [[Lec_06-Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP)]]: tax and equity risk of employee grants ([[Section 102]], [[Section 3(i)]], [[Dilution]]).
      - [[Lec_04-Contract Law]]: contract risks such as vague or unenforceable terms ([[Definiteness (Mesuyamut)]], [[Writing Requirement]], [[Memorandum of Understanding]]).
