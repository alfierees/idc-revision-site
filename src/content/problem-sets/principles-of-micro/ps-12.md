      **Free market versus social optimum.** Suppose that demand for a product is

      $$
      P = 300 - 0.25\,Q
      $$

      and supply is

      $$
      P = 100 + \tfrac{1}{2}\,Q.
      $$

      Furthermore, suppose that the marginal external damage of this product is USD 8 per unit (this is the cost per unit of pollution).

      > [!tip] Intuition (the setup, not an answer)
      > The supply curve is the producers' own cost, the [[Marginal Private Cost]]. Pollution is a cost that lands on bystanders, so the [[Marginal Social Cost]] sits above the supply curve by the marginal external damage. The booklet's hint in part b says exactly this: add the cost per unit of pollution. The method is in [[Lec_12-Externalities]].
