The assumption that [[RTS]] falls as $l$ rises along an isoquant. It implies convex isoquants and a preference for input diversity.

## When to use

Cite it to justify that the tangency condition identifies a minimum-cost point and that a blend of inputs beats an extreme bundle (see [[Convex Isoquant]]).

See [[PS_02-Isoquants, RTS & Properties of the Profit Function|PS 02]].
