A hypothetical decision-maker maximising a social welfare function over feasible allocations (e.g. Cobb-Douglas $U_1^a U_2^{1-a}$). Its choice is compared with the market equilibrium and can be implemented by redistributing endowments.

## When to use

Solve it by maximising the welfare function subject to feasibility, then compare with the [[Walrasian Equilibrium]]. Appears in the exchange-equilibrium and core problem sets and past papers.

See [[PS_06-Exchange Equilibrium, Walrasian Prices & the Social Planner|PS 06]].
