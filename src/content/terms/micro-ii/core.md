Allocations that are both [[Pareto Efficiency|Pareto efficient]] and [[Individual Rationality|individually rational]]: the slice of the [[Contract Curve]] inside the lens of indifference curves through the endowment. Every [[Walrasian Equilibrium]] lies in it.

## When to use

Find it in exchange-economy problems by intersecting the contract curve with the lens of allocations each agent weakly prefers to their endowment. Shown in the core, corner-endowment and planner-comparison problem set.

See [[PS_07-Core, Corner Endowments & Equilibrium vs the Social Planner|PS 07]].
