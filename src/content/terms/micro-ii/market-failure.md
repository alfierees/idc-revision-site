A situation where the competitive equilibrium is not [[Pareto Efficiency|Pareto efficient]]. It arises when the [[First Welfare Theorem]]'s assumptions break: competitive markets, perfect information, no externalities.

## When to use

Name it when you show that an [[Externalities|externality]] or a [[Public Goods|public good]] makes the market outcome inefficient, then propose a remedy.

See [[Lec_05-Production Economy & Externalities|Part 2]].
