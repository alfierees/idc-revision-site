$\Pi^*(w,p)$: profit at the optimal $q^*$. It is [[Homogeneity of Degree 1|homogeneous of degree 1]], increasing in $p$ and decreasing in $w$.

## When to use

Prove these properties with revealed-preference arguments (the firm could still produce the old quantity but does better), and use the [[Envelope Theorem]] to get $q^*$.

See [[PS_01-Profit Maximisation & Properties of the Profit Function|PS 01]].
