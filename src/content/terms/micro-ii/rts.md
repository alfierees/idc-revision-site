Rate of Technical Substitution: $MP_l/MP_k$, the slope of the [[Isoquant]]; how much capital can be given up for one more unit of labour at the same output.

## When to use

Set $RTS = p_l/p_k$ for cost minimisation (see [[Tangency Condition]]) and $RTS_1 = RTS_2$ for production efficiency.

See [[Lec_04-Theory of the Firm, Partial & General Equilibrium|Part 1]].
