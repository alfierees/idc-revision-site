Allocation $A$ dominates $B$ if it is weakly better for all agents and strictly better for at least one.

## When to use

Use it to show an allocation is not [[Pareto Efficiency|Pareto efficient]]: exhibit a feasible allocation that dominates it.

See [[PS_05-Pareto Efficiency, Dominance & Social Welfare|PS 05]].
