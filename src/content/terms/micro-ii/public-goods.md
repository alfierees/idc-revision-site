A good whose aggregate quantity affects every agent's utility (national security, a pool, a joint seminar paper). It is an externality hitting many agents at once, which causes [[Under-Provision]] and [[Free Riding]].

## When to use

In the public-goods questions, compare the planner's condition ($V_1'+V_2'=1$) with the Nash equilibrium. Pairwise bargaining breaks down because each agent must guess everyone else's contribution.
