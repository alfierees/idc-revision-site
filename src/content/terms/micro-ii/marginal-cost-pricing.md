Competitive firms set price equal to marginal cost ($p = MC$). With constant $MC$ supply is a flat line and profit is zero.

## When to use

Gives the supply curve in partial-equilibrium problems and the price relation $p_x^* = C'(x^*)$ in the production economy.

See [[PS_04-Partial Equilibrium, Welfare & Price Ceilings|PS 04]].
