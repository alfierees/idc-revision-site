A price ratio and allocation in which each agent maximises utility on the budget line through their endowment and all markets clear.

## When to use

Find it by deriving demands, normalising a price (see [[Numeraire]]) and clearing one market. By the [[First Welfare Theorem]] the result is Pareto efficient and lies in the [[Core]].
