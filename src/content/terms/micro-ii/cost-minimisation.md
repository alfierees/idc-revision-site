The firm's problem of choosing $(l,k)$ to minimise $p_l l + p_k k$ subject to $f(l,k)=q_0$. It is the dual of the consumer's expenditure minimisation problem from Micro 1.

## When to use

The first step in any multi-input cost question: set $RTS = p_l/p_k$ for an interior solution (see [[Tangency Condition]]), check for a [[Corner Solution]], then compute the [[Cost Function]].

See [[PS_03-Cost Minimisation & Properties of the Cost Function|PS 03]].
