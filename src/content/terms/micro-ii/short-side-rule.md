Under a binding price control the quantity traded is the smaller of demand and supply: supply under a binding ceiling, demand under a binding floor.

## When to use

First step in every [[Price Ceiling]] or [[Price Floor]] question: decide which side is short.

See [[PS_04-Partial Equilibrium, Welfare & Price Ceilings|PS 04]].
