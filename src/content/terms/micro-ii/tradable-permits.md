A property right over units of the externality (e.g. noise) traded at price $r$. The permit price plays the role of the [[Pigouvian Tax]], and the initial split of permits changes who is paid, not the quantities.

## When to use

Use it as the market remedy in externality problems; solve for the price where both sides' marginal conditions match. Based on [[Coase Theorem]] logic.

See [[PS_10-Externalities in General Equilibrium & Public Goods|PS 10]].
