A legal minimum price (also a fixed price above equilibrium). It binds if above $p^*$; demand is then the short side and quantity traded is demand at the floor.

## When to use

Appears in partial-equilibrium and past-paper production-economy questions; use the [[Short-Side Rule]] to find the traded quantity.

See [[PS_04-Partial Equilibrium, Welfare & Price Ceilings|PS 04]].
