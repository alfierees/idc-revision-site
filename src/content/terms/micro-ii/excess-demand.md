$z$ = demand minus supply (endowment) of a good. By [[Walras' Law]], $p_x z_x + p_y z_y = 0$, so at non-equilibrium positive prices both goods have non-zero excess demand, and a price above equilibrium gives excess supply.

## When to use

Use it in exchange-economy true/false questions: it is how you reason that if one market clears, the other does too, and that a non-equilibrium price cannot have excess demand in only one good.
