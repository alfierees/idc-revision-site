The sum of all agents' marginal benefits from a public good. The planner provides the good until $V_1' + V_2'$ equals marginal cost (1).

## When to use

The efficiency condition for a [[Public Goods|public good]]. Compare it with the free-market condition, where only one agent's marginal benefit equals 1, to show [[Under-Provision]].

See [[Lec_06-Public Goods & Altruism|Lec 06]].
