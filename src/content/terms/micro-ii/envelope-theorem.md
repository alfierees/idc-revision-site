The derivative of a value function with respect to a parameter equals the partial derivative of the objective at the optimum. For profit, $\partial\Pi^*/\partial p = q^*$ (Hotelling's Lemma).

## When to use

Use it to read off optimal output from the [[Optimal Profit Function]] without re-solving the firm's problem, as in the profit-function problem set.

See [[PS_01-Profit Maximisation & Properties of the Profit Function|PS 01]].
