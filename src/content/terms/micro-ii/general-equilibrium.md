Simultaneous equilibrium across all markets. Needed because partial equilibrium ignores cross-market effects from substitutes, complements and income effects.

## When to use

Use it when a policy or shock in one market spills into others. In the course it is built up through the [[Exchange Economy]] and then the [[Production Economy]].
