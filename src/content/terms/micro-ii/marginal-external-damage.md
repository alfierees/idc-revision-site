The extra cost or harm that one unit of an agent's activity imposes on another at the margin. The [[Pigouvian Tax]] equals it at the efficient allocation.

## When to use

Compute it at the efficient allocation to set the tax. In the lecture's steel and fishery example it is $\partial C_F/\partial x$, giving $t^*=13$.
