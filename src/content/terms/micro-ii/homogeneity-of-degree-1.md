$\Pi^*(tw,tp) = t\Pi^*(w,p)$: doubling all prices doubles profit and leaves the optimal quantity unchanged, so only relative prices matter for production decisions.

## When to use

One of the three standard properties of the [[Optimal Profit Function]] to prove or state, alongside increasing in $p$ and decreasing in $w$.

See [[PS_01-Profit Maximisation & Properties of the Profit Function|PS 01]].
