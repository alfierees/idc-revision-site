The good whose price is fixed at 1 (e.g. $p_y = 1$) so that only relative prices matter. Under [[Quasilinear Utility]] the linear good plays this role.

## When to use

Normalise a price at the start of any equilibrium problem: prices are determined only up to a positive scalar, so set $p_y = 1$ and solve for $p_x$.

See [[PS_09-Corner Solutions, Quasilinear Utility & Production Economy Prices|PS 09]].
