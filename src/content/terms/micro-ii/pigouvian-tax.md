A per-unit tax on a negative externality set equal to the marginal external harm, $t^*=\partial C_F/\partial x$ at the optimum, so the polluter faces the full social cost.

## When to use

Use it as the government remedy in externality questions. Drawback: it requires the regulator to know both firms' cost functions. Alternative: the [[Coase Theorem]] market.
