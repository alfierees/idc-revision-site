A per-unit payment to buyers: consumers pay $p - \Delta$, shifting demand up by $\Delta$. It pushes quantity above the competitive level so $MC$ exceeds marginal value on the extra units and social welfare falls.

## When to use

Compute quantity under the shifted demand, then subtract government cost from $CS + FS$ to find the [[Deadweight Loss]] (lecture example: demand $100-p$, $C=x^2/2$, $\Delta=20$ gives a loss of 100).
