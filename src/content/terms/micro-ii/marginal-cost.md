$C'(x)$: the cost of one more unit of output. A competitive firm produces where $p = MC$, so MC is the supply curve (rising if $C$ is convex, constant under [[Constant Returns to Scale]]).

## When to use

Differentiate the [[Cost Function]] to get it, set it equal to price for supply, and use it as the supply curve when finding equilibrium and [[Firm Surplus]].

See [[Lec_04-Theory of the Firm, Partial & General Equilibrium|Lec 04]].
