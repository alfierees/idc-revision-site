In partial equilibrium $W(x)=v(x)-C(x)$, total value minus cost, maximised at the competitive quantity. In general equilibrium it is the planner's function $W(U_1,U_2)$, increasing in each agent's utility.

## When to use

Compute it as CS + FS in partial equilibrium; in exchange problems it is what the [[Social Planner]] maximises.

See [[PS_04-Partial Equilibrium, Welfare & Price Ceilings|PS 04]].
