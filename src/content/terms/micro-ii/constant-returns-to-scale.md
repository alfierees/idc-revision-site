Technology where scaling all inputs by $t$ scales output by $t$ (e.g. Cobb-Douglas with exponents summing to 1). Cost is then linear in output, $MC = AC$, competitive profit is zero, and scaling a production economy's resources by $t$ scales its [[PPF]] by $t$.

## When to use

Check it when a question asks about profit, supply or prices: with constant returns profit is zero and supply is flat at [[Marginal Cost]], which is what makes [[Price Indeterminacy]] possible in a production economy.
