An allocation is Pareto efficient if no feasible alternative makes everyone at least as well off and someone strictly better off. Equivalently, no one can be made better off without making someone else worse off.

## When to use

In the interior, check $MRS_1 = MRS_2$. Remember it is not fairness: efficient allocations can be very unequal.
