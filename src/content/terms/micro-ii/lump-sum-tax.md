A tax independent of the agent's choices, so it does not distort margins. It is the right tool for redistribution (see [[Second Welfare Theorem]]) but useless for correcting an externality.

## When to use

Contrast it with distortionary taxes and price controls in questions on equity versus efficiency; in practice real taxes always distort somewhat, giving an equity-efficiency tradeoff.
