$f = g(l) + k$ with $g$ concave: $MP_k = 1$ and $RTS = g'(l)$, so labour is fixed at the tangency level $l^*$ and output beyond $g(l^*)$ comes from capital (marginal cost then equals $p_k$).

## When to use

Set $g'(l^*) = p_l/p_k$ in [[Cost Minimisation]], then split by $q_0 \le g(l^*)$ (labour only) or above (labour $l^*$ plus capital). Appears in the production-economy past paper.
