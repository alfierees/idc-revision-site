An argument that bounds a value function by evaluating a new optimum at old parameters: the agent could still choose the old plan, so the new optimum is at least as good.

## When to use

Use it for properties of the [[Optimal Profit Function]], as in the lecture's proof that profit is increasing in $p$.

See [[PS_04-Partial Equilibrium, Welfare & Price Ceilings|PS 04]].
