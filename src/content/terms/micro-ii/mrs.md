Marginal Rate of Substitution: $-dy/dx$ along an indifference curve. It is equalised across agents at an interior [[Pareto Efficiency|Pareto efficient]] allocation.

## When to use

Set $MRS_1 = MRS_2$ to find the [[Contract Curve]]; in a production economy the optimum is $MRS = RPT$ (see [[RPT]]).

See [[PS_05-Pareto Efficiency, Dominance & Social Welfare|PS 05]].
