Production Possibility Frontier: all efficient $(x,y)$ output combinations. Points inside are feasible but inefficient, points outside infeasible. It is assumed concave because of diminishing marginal productivity.

## When to use

Derive it by mapping the [[Efficiency Locus]] into output space, then find the optimum where $MRS = RPT$.

See [[Lec_05-Production Economy & Externalities|Lec 05]].
