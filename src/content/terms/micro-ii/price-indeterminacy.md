With constant-returns technologies and a corner solution (or a zero-endowment agent), a whole range of price vectors clears all markets and gives zero profit, so prices are not pinned down although the real allocation is.

## When to use

Watch for it in production-economy questions with [[Constant Returns to Scale]] or zero-endowment agents: report the range of prices, not a single value.
