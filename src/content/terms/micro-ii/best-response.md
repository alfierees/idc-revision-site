An agent's optimal choice given what the others choose. For a public good, $BR_1(g_2) = \max(g_1^o - g_2,\,0)$, where $g_1^o$ solves $V_1' = 1$: she tops the total up to her ideal level, or contributes nothing if the other already provides enough.

## When to use

Used to find the [[Nash Equilibrium]] of the public-goods game: write both agents' best responses and find where they hold simultaneously. If $g_1^o \ne g_2^o$ the result is complete [[Free Riding]] by one agent.
