The market provides a public good at too low a level: in Nash equilibrium $V_1'+V_2'>1$ at the equilibrium total, so $g^e<g^*$. The gap grows with the number of agents.

## When to use

Show it by comparing the planner's $V_1'+V_2'=1$ with the [[Nash Equilibrium]] condition where only one agent's $V'$ equals 1.

See [[Lec_06-Public Goods & Altruism|Part 3]].
