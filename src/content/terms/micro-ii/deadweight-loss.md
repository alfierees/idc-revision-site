The welfare loss when a policy pushes quantity away from the competitive equilibrium. In the lecture's subsidy example, CS + FS rises from 2500 to 3600 but the government pays 1200, so net welfare is 2400 and the deadweight loss is 100.

## When to use

Compute it by comparing total welfare (CS + FS minus any government cost) with and without the policy, as in the [[Price Subsidy]], [[Price Ceiling]] and [[Price Floor]] questions.
