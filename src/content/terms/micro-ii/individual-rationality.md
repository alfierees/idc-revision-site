No agent ends up worse off than at their own endowment: $U_i \ge U_i(\omega_i)$. It holds at any equilibrium because the endowment is always affordable.

## When to use

Use it to restrict the [[Contract Curve]] to the [[Core]], and to check that a proposed allocation could arise from voluntary trade.

See [[PS_07-Core, Corner Endowments & Equilibrium vs the Social Planner|PS 07]].
