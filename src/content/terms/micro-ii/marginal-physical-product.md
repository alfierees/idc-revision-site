The extra output from one more unit of an input, other inputs held fixed: $MP_l = \partial f/\partial l$ and $MP_k = \partial f/\partial k$.

## When to use

The ratio $MP_l/MP_k$ is the [[RTS]]; compute both partial derivatives of the [[Production Function]] first in any cost-minimisation question.

See [[Lec_04-Theory of the Firm, Partial & General Equilibrium|Lec 04]].
