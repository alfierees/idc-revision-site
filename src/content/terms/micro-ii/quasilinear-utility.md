$U=v(x)+y$: no income effects on $x$, so surplus areas are money-valued and additive and the demand curve equals marginal utility.

## When to use

The assumption behind the partial-equilibrium welfare results: it is why $CS + FS = W$ and why the competitive quantity maximises welfare.
