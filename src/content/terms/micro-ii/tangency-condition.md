The interior cost-minimisation condition: $RTS = MP_l/MP_k = p_l/p_k$, where the isocost is tangent to the isoquant.

## When to use

If $RTS > p_l/p_k$ the firm should use more labour, if less, less labour. If it cannot hold, expect a [[Corner Solution]].

See [[PS_03-Cost Minimisation & Properties of the Cost Function|PS 03]].
