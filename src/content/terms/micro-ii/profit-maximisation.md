The firm chooses $q^*$ where $MR = MC$; the second-order condition requires $\Pi'' < 0$.

## When to use

Set $R'(q)=C'(q)$ and check concavity. For a price taker $R=pq$, so $p=MC$ (see [[Marginal Cost Pricing]]).

See [[PS_01-Profit Maximisation & Properties of the Profit Function|PS 01]].
