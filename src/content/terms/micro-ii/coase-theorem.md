If property rights over an externality are well defined and transaction costs are low, markets (bargaining) reach an efficient outcome regardless of who initially holds the rights. Making the harm tradeable turns the problem into an exchange economy, where the [[First Welfare Theorem]] applies.

## When to use

Use it as the market-based alternative to a [[Pigouvian Tax]] in externality questions: create tradeable rights, let the price clear, and note that the initial assignment changes who is paid, not the quantities. Limits: it needs clear rights, low transaction costs and no wealth effects.
