A legal maximum price. It binds if below $p^*$, creating a shortage in which quantity traded equals supply.

## When to use

Apply the [[Short-Side Rule]] for quantity, [[Efficient Rationing]] to compute consumer surplus, and compare welfare with the free market to get the [[Deadweight Loss]].

See [[PS_04-Partial Equilibrium, Welfare & Price Ceilings|PS 04]].
