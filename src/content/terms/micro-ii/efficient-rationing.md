The assumption that scarce units go to the consumers who value them most (the top of the demand curve), so $CS = \int (P^D - p)\,dx$. Random queuing would give lower welfare.

## When to use

State it explicitly when computing [[Consumer Surplus]] under a binding [[Price Ceiling]]; without it the allocation of the scarce units is undetermined.

See [[PS_04-Partial Equilibrium, Welfare & Price Ceilings|PS 04]].
