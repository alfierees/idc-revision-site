Altering incentives so that people take account of the external effects of their actions.

## When to use

The policy answer in Lec 12: tax a good with a negative [[Externality]] and subsidise one with a positive externality. A corrective tax equals the external cost (a [[Pigovian Tax]]) and a corrective subsidy equals the external benefit ([[Corrective Subsidy]]), moving the market quantity to the socially optimal one.

See [[Lec_12-Externalities|Lec 12]].
