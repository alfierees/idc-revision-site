A market surplus (excess supply) is when quantity supplied exceeds quantity demanded. At a price above equilibrium, sellers face unsold stock and cut the price until the market reaches equilibrium. It is not the same as [[Consumer Surplus]], [[Producer Surplus]] or [[Total Surplus]].

## When to use

In the coffee example at $P = 5$, $Q^D = 9$ and $Q^S = 25$, so the surplus is 16 cups. A binding price floor creates a lasting one (see [[Price Floor]]). Its opposite is [[Shortage]].
