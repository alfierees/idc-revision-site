Input costs that require a direct outlay of money, such as wages, raw materials, electricity and rent. Both economists and accountants count them.

## When to use

Economic total cost is explicit plus [[Implicit Costs]]; accounting total cost is explicit only. The Lec 05 hint is to ask what is actually paid with money: in Caroline's cookie factory the interest on a bank loan is paid in cash, so it is explicit.

See [[Lec_05-Supply and Costs of Production|Lec 05]].
