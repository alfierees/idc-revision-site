A group of buyers and sellers of a particular good or service.

## When to use

It is the unit of analysis in the first lectures, for example the market for eggs or for coffee, where [[Market Demand]] and [[Market Supply]] determine the price.

See [[Lec_01-Ten Principles of Economics|Lec 01]].
