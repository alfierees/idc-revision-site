The sum of all individual demands, built by adding the quantities buyers demand at each price (a horizontal sum of the [[Demand Curve]]s).

## When to use

Use it when a problem gives several buyers or groups and asks for the market curve: add quantities at each price, for example 32 + 45 = 77 tickets at a price of 65 NIS.

See [[Lec_03-Perfect Competition and Demand|Lec 03]].
