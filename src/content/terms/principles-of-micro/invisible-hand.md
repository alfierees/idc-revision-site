Adam Smith's idea (The Wealth of Nations, 1776) that households and firms in a [[Market]] act as if led by an invisible hand to promote general economic well-being. It works through prices, which reflect both the value of the good to society and the cost of producing it.

## When to use

Principle 6 in Lec 01. Lec 09 repeats it when the market equilibrium delivers the [[Efficient Quantity]]. It breaks down when there is [[Market Failure]], such as externalities or market power.

See [[Lec_01-Ten Principles of Economics|Lec 01]].
