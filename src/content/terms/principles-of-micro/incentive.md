Something that induces a person to act, such as the prospect of a reward or punishment. Rational people respond to incentives, so changing costs or benefits changes behaviour.

## When to use

Principle 4 in Lec 01. Slide examples are attendance counting 10% of the grade, higher petrol prices pushing buyers to hybrids, and cigarette taxes lowering teen smoking. In the Mustang problem the change in incentives from case A to case B (a smaller gain from repairing) changed the decision.

See [[Lec_01-Ten Principles of Economics|Lec 01]].
