Transaction costs are the costs of reaching an agreement, as negotiating can itself be costly (lawyers, time, information). When they are high, private bargaining can fail.

## When to use

Cite them as a reason the [[Coase Theorem]] often does not work: with costless bargaining private parties reach the efficient outcome, but transaction costs and many parties often block this, which is where the government steps in.
