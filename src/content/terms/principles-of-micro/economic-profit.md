Total revenue minus all costs, both explicit and implicit, where the implicit costs are the owner's opportunity cost.

## When to use

It is smaller than [[Accounting Profit]] by exactly the implicit costs. Zero economic profit means no incentive to enter or leave the market, even if accounting profit is positive (Lec 05, Lec 06, PS 05, PP 01, PP 07).

See [[Lec_05-Supply and Costs of Production|Lec 05]].
