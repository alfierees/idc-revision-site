Goods used together: a rise in the price of one lowers the demand for the other, so $P_A \uparrow \rightarrow D_B \downarrow$.

## When to use

It is the related-price determinant of demand, and the sign of the [[Cross-Price Elasticity of Demand]] is negative for complements (Lec 03, Lec 08, PS 03, PS 08, PP 02).

See [[Lec_03-Perfect Competition and Demand|Lec 03]].
