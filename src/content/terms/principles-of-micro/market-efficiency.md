A free-market equilibrium maximises [[Total Surplus]]: the lowest-cost sellers produce ([[Efficient Production]]), the highest-value buyers consume ([[Efficient Allocation]]) and the quantity is where marginal benefit equals marginal cost ([[Efficient Quantity]]).

## When to use

Use the three questions (who makes what, who gets what, how much) to show a market is efficient, or to show how a policy or distortion departs from it.

See [[Lec_09-Welfare Consumer and Producer Surplus|Lec 09]].
