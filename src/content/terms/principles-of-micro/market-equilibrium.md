The situation with one price at which quantity supplied equals quantity demanded, where the [[Supply Curve]] and [[Demand Curve]] cross, so there is no pressure on the price to change.

## When to use

Solve it by setting quantity demanded equal to quantity supplied (in the lecture example $P^E = 3$ and $Q^E = 15$); at any other price there is a [[Surplus]] or a [[Shortage]]. It is also the starting point for comparative statics and surplus questions.

See [[Lec_04-Supply and Market Equilibrium|Lec 04]].
