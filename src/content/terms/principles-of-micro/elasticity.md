A numerical measure of how responsive the quantity demanded or supplied is to a change in one of its determinants (price, income, or the price of other goods). Each elasticity is a ratio of percentage changes, so it has no units.

## When to use

Compute percentage changes with the [[Midpoint Method]] so the answer is the same in both directions, then compare the absolute value with 1 (elastic above, inelastic below). Lec 11 also uses it to decide who bears a tax (see [[Tax Incidence]]): the less elastic side bears more.

See [[Lec_08-Elasticity|Lec 08]].
