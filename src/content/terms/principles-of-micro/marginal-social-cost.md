The cost of one more unit to society: the [[Marginal Private Cost]] plus the external cost to bystanders. On the diagram the social cost curve lies above the private one by the external cost per unit.

## When to use

In a [[Negative Externality]] question, find the optimum where demand meets MSC, which gives a quantity below the market quantity (overproduction). A [[Pigovian Tax]] shifts supply up from private to social cost.

See [[Lec_12-Externalities|Lec 12]].
