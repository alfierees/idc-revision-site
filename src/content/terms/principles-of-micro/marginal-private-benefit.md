The benefit of one more unit to the buyer. It is the ordinary demand curve, and it leaves out any benefit to bystanders.

## When to use

$MSB = MPB + \text{external benefit}$ (Lec 12). The market settles where demand meets supply using private values, so with a [[Positive Externality]] the optimum exceeds the market quantity, $Q_{OPTIMUM} > Q_{MARKET}$, and the good is underproduced.

See [[Lec_12-Externalities|Lec 12]].
