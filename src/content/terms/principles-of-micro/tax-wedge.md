A tax drives a wedge between what leaves the buyer's pocket ($P_B$) and what reaches the seller's pocket ($P_S$). The wedge is the tax that goes to the government, equal to the [[Per-Unit Tax]].

## When to use

For a subsidy the gap is the same size in the other direction: $P_S - P_B$ equals the subsidy. Use it to check your answer: the difference between the two prices must equal the tax per unit. See [[Tax Incidence]].
