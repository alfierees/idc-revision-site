A regulation in which the government tells firms directly what to do, such as setting a maximum level of pollution a factory may emit or forcing firms to adopt a particular technology.

## When to use

Know its drawback: it requires the government to understand the industry, and a uniform rule ignores that some firms can cut pollution cheaply and others cannot. Contrast it with market-based tools such as the [[Pigovian Tax]] and [[Tradable Pollution Permits]] (Lec 12).
