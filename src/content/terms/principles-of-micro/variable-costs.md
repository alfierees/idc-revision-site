Variable costs are costs that vary with the quantity produced. In the lecture, workers at USD 10 each and the bagel variable cost (0.3 rising to 12) both grow as output grows.

## When to use

Identify them as the part of cost that rises with output, then add [[Fixed Costs]] to get [[Total Cost]]. Dividing by quantity gives [[Average Variable Cost]].
