Tax incidence is how the burden of a tax is shared between buyers and sellers, measured against the original equilibrium price. It does not matter whether the law taxes buyers or sellers, and the burden depends on elasticity.

## When to use

In the pizza example both versions give $Q = 450$, $P_B = 11.00$ and $P_S = 9.50$. The less elastic side bears more of the tax: see [[Price Elasticity of Demand]], [[Price Elasticity of Supply]] and [[Tax Wedge]].
