The uncompensated impact of one person's actions on the well-being of a bystander. Self-interested buyers and sellers neglect these external costs or benefits, so the market outcome is not efficient.

## When to use

Classify it as negative or positive, and as a production (supply side) or consumption (demand side) externality, then compare the market quantity with the socially optimal one (Lec 12). Pollution is the standard negative example and education the positive one. It is one source of [[Market Failure]].

See [[Lec_12-Externalities|Lec 12]].
