A country whose [[World Price]] is higher than its no-trade price, so it sells abroad. Its producers gain and its consumers lose, because the domestic price rises.

## When to use

Exports are quantity supplied minus quantity demanded at the world price. In the Lec 13 steel example consumer surplus falls by B, producer surplus rises by B + D, and [[Total Surplus]] rises by D, the [[Gains from Trade]].

See [[Lec_13-Public Goods and International Trade|Lec 13]].
