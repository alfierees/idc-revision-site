A good is excludable if people can be prevented from using it, typically because they have not paid, and not excludable if no one can stop them.

## When to use

The first of two questions for sorting goods in Lec 13: if I do not pay, can I be kept out? Crossed with [[Rivalry]] it gives [[Private Goods]], [[Club Goods]], [[Common Resources]] and [[Public Goods]]. Non-excludable goods bring the [[Free-Rider Problem]].

See [[Lec_13-Public Goods and International Trade|Lec 13]].
