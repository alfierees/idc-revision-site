The change in profit from producing one more unit: [[Marginal Revenue]] minus [[Marginal Cost]].

## When to use

Method 2 for choosing output in Lec 06. In the $P = 12$ example marginal profit is positive up to $Q = 8$ (0.1 at 8) and negative from $Q = 9$ (-1.1), so the firm stops at 8. A price taker produces where $P = MC$, on the rising part of the MC curve.

See [[Lec_06-Firms in Competitive Markets|Lec 06]].
