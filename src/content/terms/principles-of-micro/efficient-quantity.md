The quantity at which [[Marginal Benefit]] equals [[Marginal Cost]], which is exactly where demand crosses supply at the [[Market Equilibrium]]. It maximises [[Total Surplus]].

## When to use

The third efficiency question in Lec 09 (how much is bought and sold?). In the shoe example the equilibrium is $Q = 15$: at $Q = 20$ the marginal cost of 35 exceeds the marginal benefit of 20, so surplus rises if quantity falls; at $Q = 10$ the marginal benefit of 40 exceeds the marginal cost of 25, so surplus rises if quantity rises.

See [[Lec_09-Welfare Consumer and Producer Surplus|Lec 09]].
