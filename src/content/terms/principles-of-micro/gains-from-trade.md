Trade is beneficial if it lets countries consume more goods than they could produce on their own. Graphically, trade must let a country reach a consumption point above its own [[Production Possibility Frontier]].

## When to use

Lec 02: each country specialises where it has [[Comparative Advantage]] (Home in pears, Foreign in oranges) and trades at 1 orange for 1 pear, so the [[Consumption Possibility Frontier]] $Y = 20 - X$ lies outside both PPFs. The [[Terms of Trade]] must lie strictly between the two countries' opportunity costs. In Lec 13, free trade raises [[Total Surplus]] by area D.
