A movement along the demand curve, caused only by a change in the good's own price.

## When to use

A price change causes a change in quantity demanded; a change in income, related prices, tastes or expectations causes a [[Change in Demand]] (a shift) instead (Lec 03, PS 03, PP 01).

See [[Lec_03-Perfect Competition and Demand|Lec 03]].
