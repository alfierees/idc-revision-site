A situation in which a market left alone fails to allocate society's resources efficiently. The lecture names two causes: an [[Externality]] and [[Market Power]].

## When to use

Use it to justify government action (Principle 7), for example a tax or subsidy to correct an externality.

See [[Lec_01-Ten Principles of Economics|Lec 01]].
