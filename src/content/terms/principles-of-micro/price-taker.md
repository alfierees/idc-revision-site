A buyer or seller so small relative to the market that it cannot influence the price, so it takes the price as given.

## When to use

For a firm that is a price taker, $P = AR = MR$ and the demand curve it faces is horizontal, so it only chooses the quantity and produces where $P = MC$. A small country is treated the same way at the [[World Price]].
