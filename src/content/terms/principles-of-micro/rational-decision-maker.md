A rational decision maker systematically and purposefully does the best they can to reach their objectives, weighing small incremental adjustments to an existing plan. People differ in their answers because their preferences and incomes differ, not because they are irrational.

## When to use

It is the actor behind Principle 3 (rational people think at the margin) and the idea that a change in costs or benefits changes behaviour. Use it when a question asks how people respond to an [[Incentive]].
