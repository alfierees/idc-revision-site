The tragedy of the commons occurs when private incentives (for example, catching and selling fish) outweigh social incentives (keeping the fish population large enough to reproduce). People neglect the external cost of their own use, and everyone ends up worse off.

## When to use

It is the market problem for [[Common Resources]] (not excludable but rival): they are over-used, while [[Public Goods]] are under-provided through the [[Free-Rider Problem]]. Rivalry adds a negative consumption [[Externality]].
