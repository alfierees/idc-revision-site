The ability of a single buyer or seller to have substantial influence on the market price; a monopoly is the lecture's example.

## When to use

Mentioned as one of the two causes of [[Market Failure]], in contrast with the [[Price Taker]] behaviour assumed in [[Perfect Competition]].

See [[Lec_01-Ten Principles of Economics|Lec 01]].
