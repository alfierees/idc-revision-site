Supply is the amount of a good or service that sellers are willing and able to sell. The quantity supplied at each price is shown by the [[Supply Curve]].

## When to use

Distinguish supply (the whole relationship, shifted by [[Determinants of Supply]]) from quantity supplied (a point on the curve). Quantity supplied rises with price: the [[Law of Supply]].

See [[Lec_04-Supply and Market Equilibrium|Lec 04]].
