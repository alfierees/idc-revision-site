The marginal product of labour is the extra output from one more worker, $MPL = \Delta \text{Output} / \Delta \text{Workers}$.

## When to use

Compute it row by row from a workers and output table. For example, going from 1 to 2 workers raises output from 50 to 90, so $MPL = (90-50)/(2-1) = 40$. A falling MPL is [[Diminishing Marginal Product]].
