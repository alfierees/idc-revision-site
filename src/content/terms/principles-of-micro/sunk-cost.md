A sunk cost is a cost already committed that cannot be recovered. In the short run fixed costs are sunk, so they should not affect the shutdown decision.

## When to use

Ignore it when deciding whether to produce: the rule compares price with [[Average Variable Cost]] only. Restaurants and golf courses close at some hours because revenue would not cover variable costs, even though the rent cannot be avoided. See also [[Fixed Costs]].
