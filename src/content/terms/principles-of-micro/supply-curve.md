A supply curve is an upward-sloping line showing the quantity supplied at each price, other things equal. The producer's rising cost curve is the supply curve: at price $p$ they supply the quantity where the cost of the last unit equals $p$.

## When to use

A change in the good's own price moves you along the curve, while only non-price determinants shift it. See [[Law of Supply]], [[Change in Quantity Supplied]] and [[Change in Supply]].
