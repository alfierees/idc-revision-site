A movement along the supply curve, caused only by a change in the good's own price.

## When to use

Only the good's own price moves you along the curve; every other determinant causes a [[Change in Supply]] (a shift) instead (Lec 04, PS 04, PP 01).

See [[Lec_04-Supply and Market Equilibrium|Lec 04]].
