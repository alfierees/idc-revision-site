The percentage change in quantity demanded divided by the percentage change in price. Demand is elastic if its size is above one and inelastic if below one.

## When to use

Compute it with the [[Midpoint Method]], then compare its size with 1. It also tells you what happens to [[Total Revenue]] when price rises, and who bears a tax more heavily: those more able to adapt or leave the market pay less.
