A legal maximum on the price at which a good may be sold. It is binding only when it is set below the equilibrium price.

## When to use

Check first whether it is binding. A binding ceiling, such as [[Rent Control]], creates a [[Shortage]]; a ceiling above equilibrium has no effect.

See [[Lec_10-Price Controls|Lec 10]].
