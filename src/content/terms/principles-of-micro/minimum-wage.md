A [[Price Floor]] on labour. If it is set above the equilibrium wage it is binding and creates a surplus of labour, that is unemployment.

## When to use

In the lecture example a minimum wage of USD 15 above the equilibrium wage leaves firms wanting 400 workers while 550 want jobs, a labour surplus of 150; a minimum below equilibrium (USD 8) has no effect.
