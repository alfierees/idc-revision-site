Producing a given quantity of output at the lowest possible cost, which requires producing each good at the lowest [[Marginal Cost]]. At the market price, only sellers whose marginal cost is at most that price produce, so the lowest-cost sellers are the ones who supply.

## When to use

One of the three efficiency questions in Lec 09 (who makes what?), next to [[Efficient Allocation]] and [[Efficient Quantity]]. In the shoe example the price is 30, so every seller with marginal cost up to 30 produces and every seller above 30 does not.

See [[Lec_09-Welfare Consumer and Producer Surplus|Lec 09]].
