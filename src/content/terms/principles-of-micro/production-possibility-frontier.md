A graph of the combinations of two goods an economy can produce when it uses all its resources and given technology. Points on it are efficient, points inside it are not, and points outside it are unattainable.

## When to use

Use it to read off [[Opportunity Cost]] from the slope and to compare producers. In the lecture's one-input example the line is $Y = 10 - 0.5X$, with slope $-1/2$.

See [[Lec_02-PPF and Gains from Trade|Lec 02]].
