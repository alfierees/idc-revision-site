The fall in total surplus caused by a tax: units between the taxed and the free-market quantity are no longer traded, even though buyers value them more than they cost to produce. Nobody, not even the government, gets that surplus.

## When to use

It grows with the size of the tax and with the elasticities of supply and demand. If demand is fully inelastic, quantity does not fall and there is no deadweight loss (Lec 11, PS 11, PP 01, PP 02).
