When both the demand and supply curves shift in the same event, one effect (price or quantity) is certain and the other is ambiguous. The certain effect follows from analysing each shift separately and combining them.

## When to use

In the apples example, a drought shifts supply left and a health study shifts demand right: price rises for sure, but quantity depends on which shift is bigger. Do each shift alone first, then combine. See also [[Comparative Statics]].
