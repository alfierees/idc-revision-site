With free entry and exit, firms enter or leave until economic profit is zero. A single firm's long-run supply is its [[Marginal Cost]] curve from the point where marginal cost equals minimum [[Average Total Cost]] upwards.

## When to use

In the long run a firm produces only if $P \ge \min(ATC)$ and $P = MC$; it exits if $P < ATC$ and enters if $P > ATC$ (Lec 06). Market supply is then horizontal at $P = ATC$ as a benchmark, though it may slope up if resources are limited or firms have different costs. Compare the short-run rule, which uses minimum AVC ([[Short-Run Supply Curve]]).
