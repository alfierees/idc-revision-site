The non-price factors the lecture lists as shifting the supply curve: input prices, technology and expectations.

## When to use

Only the good's own price moves you along the curve; the determinants shift the whole curve. Shorthand from the lecture: price of inputs down means supply up (Lec 04, PS 04, PP 01).
