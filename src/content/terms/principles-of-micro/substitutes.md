Substitutes are goods used in place of each other, such as tea for coffee. When the price of one rises, the demand for the other rises.

## When to use

It is one of the determinants that shifts demand: $P_A \uparrow \rightarrow D_B \uparrow$. Contrast with [[Complements]], where demand falls. See also [[Cross-Price Elasticity of Demand]].
