The shutdown point is the minimum of [[Average Variable Cost]]. A firm shuts down in the short run when total revenue is below variable cost, equivalently when price is below AVC.

## When to use

Apply the rule: shut down if $TR < VC$, which is $P < AVC$. Shutdown is a short-run decision to produce zero while still paying fixed costs, unlike exit, which is long-run. Fixed costs are a [[Sunk Cost]], so they do not enter the rule.
