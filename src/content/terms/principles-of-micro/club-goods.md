Goods that are excludable but not rival, such as natural monopolies like cable TV, which can be excluded through subscription.

## When to use

They fill the excludable, not-rival cell of the goods classification. The lecture gives them one line and concentrates on public goods and common resources (Lec 13, PS 13, PP 01, PP 02).
