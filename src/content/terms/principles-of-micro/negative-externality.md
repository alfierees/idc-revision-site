A negative impact of one person's actions on the well-being of a bystander. The market ignores this cost, so it produces more than the social optimum (overproduction).

## When to use

Show it with [[Marginal Social Cost]] above the private cost curve, so the optimal quantity is below the market quantity; the lecture's remedy is a [[Pigovian Tax]], for example on pollution.

See [[Lec_12-Externalities|Lec 12]].
