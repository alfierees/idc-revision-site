The price received minus the seller's cost: the area above the supply curve and below the price.

## When to use

Use it with [[Consumer Surplus]] to measure the winners and losers from a price control, a tax or trade. Add the two for [[Total Surplus]] and compare with the free-market equilibrium to find the [[Deadweight Loss]].

See [[Lec_09-Welfare Consumer and Producer Surplus|Lec 09]].
