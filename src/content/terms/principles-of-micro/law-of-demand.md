Other things equal, when the price of a good rises the [[Quantity Demanded]] falls, and vice versa.

## When to use

A change in the good's own price moves you along the [[Demand Curve]] ([[Change in Quantity Demanded]]) and never shifts it; only non-price determinants shift it. Lec 03 explains the downward slope by the [[Substitution Effect]], the [[Income Effect]] and [[Diminishing Marginal Utility]]. [[Giffen Good]]s and Veblen goods are the exceptions (Lec 04).

See [[Lec_03-Perfect Competition and Demand|Lec 03]].
