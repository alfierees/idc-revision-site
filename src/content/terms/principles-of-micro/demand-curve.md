A graph of the relationship between price and quantity demanded, other things equal. It slopes downward.

## When to use

Plot P on the Y axis and Q on the X axis. It slopes down by the substitution effect, the income effect and diminishing marginal utility, which together make up the [[Law of Demand]] (Lec 03, PS 03).

See [[Lec_03-Perfect Competition and Demand|Lec 03]].
