The additional benefit from a change, such as one more unit of an activity. A rational decision maker acts while it exceeds [[Marginal Cost]]: $MB > MC$.

## When to use

In the Lec 01 Mustang problem, repair if the marginal benefit (the value gain from a working transmission) exceeds the USD 600 repair cost: 800 does, 500 does not. In Lec 09 a buyer's marginal benefit is their [[Willingness to Pay]], consumer surplus is $MB - P$, and the [[Efficient Quantity]] is where $MB = MC$.

See [[Lec_01-Ten Principles of Economics|Lec 01]].
