A producer has an absolute advantage in a good if it needs a smaller quantity of inputs to produce it, so it compares productivity. The test is how many hours one unit takes.

## When to use

Use it to compare productivity, then contrast it with [[Comparative Advantage]], which compares opportunity cost. The two can coincide but need not, and a country with an absolute advantage in everything still gains from trade (Lec 02, PS 02, PP 01, PP 02).

See [[Lec_02-PPF and Gains from Trade|Lec 02]].
