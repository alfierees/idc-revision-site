Other things equal, when the price of a good rises the quantity supplied rises.

## When to use

In Lec 04 the cost of each extra unit rises with output because of [[Diminishing Marginal Product]], so the [[Supply Curve]] slopes upward. A change in the good's own price moves you along the curve ([[Change in Quantity Supplied]]); input prices, technology and expectations shift it ([[Change in Supply]]).

See [[Lec_04-Supply and Market Equilibrium|Lec 04]].
