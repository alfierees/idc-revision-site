The short-run supply curve of a competitive firm is its [[Marginal Cost]] curve at all prices at or above the minimum of [[Average Variable Cost]]. Below that price the firm supplies zero.

## When to use

Draw MC, then keep only the part from its intersection with minimum AVC (the [[Shutdown Point]]) upwards. Compare with the [[Long-Run Supply Curve]], which starts where MC meets minimum average total cost.

See [[Lec_06-Firms in Competitive Markets|Lec 06]].
