If private parties can bargain without cost over the allocation of resources, they can solve the problem of externalities on their own, whatever the initial distribution of rights.

## When to use

Efficiency does not depend on who is given the right, only on who pays whom. It describes what bargaining could achieve if it were free, but [[Transaction Costs]] can make private solutions fail, and then the government can act (Lec 12).
