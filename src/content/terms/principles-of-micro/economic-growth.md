The ability to produce more. On the PPF diagram it is an outward movement of the frontier, for example from new technology or more resources.

## When to use

Use it when a question asks what shifts the frontier outward, as opposed to a movement along it (Lec 02, PS 02).

See [[Lec_02-PPF and Gains from Trade|Lec 02]].
