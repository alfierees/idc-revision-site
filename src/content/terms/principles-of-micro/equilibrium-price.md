The one price at which quantity demanded equals quantity supplied. Above it a [[Surplus]] pushes the price down; below it a [[Shortage]] pushes it up.

## When to use

Set the demand and supply curves equal and solve. The Lec 04 coffee example (curves read from the graphs) gives $P^E = 3$ and $Q^E = 15$. After a tax the equilibrium splits into two prices, $P_B$ for buyers and $P_S$ for sellers (Lec 11).

See [[Lec_04-Supply and Market Equilibrium|Lec 04]].
