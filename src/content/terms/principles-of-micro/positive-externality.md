A positive impact of one person's actions on the well-being of a bystander. The market ignores this benefit, so it produces less than the social optimum (underproduction).

## When to use

Show it with [[Marginal Social Benefit]] above the private demand curve, so the optimal quantity is above the market quantity; the remedy is a subsidy ([[Corrective Subsidy]]), with education and patents as lecture examples.

See [[Lec_12-Externalities|Lec 12]].
