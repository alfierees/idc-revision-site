A producer has a comparative advantage in a good if its opportunity cost of producing that good is smaller than the other producer's. The test is how much of the other good must be given up. It is the basis for gains from trade.

## When to use

Use it to decide who should specialise in what: in the Lec 02 example Foreign gives up 1/2 pear per orange (Home gives up 2), so Foreign has it in oranges. It can differ from [[Absolute Advantage]] (Lec 02, PS 02, PP 01, PP 02).
