The percentage change in quantity demanded divided by the percentage change in income, $\dfrac{\%\Delta Q^d}{\%\Delta \text{Income}}$. It is positive for a [[Normal Good]] and negative for an [[Inferior Good]].

## When to use

Use the sign to classify a good. Unlike price elasticity of demand, which is always negative, its sign carries the information (Lec 08). Compute the percentage changes with the midpoint method.

See [[Lec_08-Elasticity|Lec 08]].
