The cost of one more unit borne by the producer. It is the ordinary supply curve, and it includes the cost of inputs, taxes and finance but not the cost inflicted on bystanders.

## When to use

$MSC = MPC + \text{external cost}$ (Lec 12). With a [[Negative Externality]] such as pollution, the market quantity uses the private cost, so $Q_{OPTIMUM} < Q_{MARKET}$ and the good is overproduced. A tax that shifts supply from MPC up to MSC fixes it.

See [[Lec_12-Externalities|Lec 12]].
