The fall in total surplus that results from a market distortion such as a price control, tax or tariff. The surplus of the traders who left the market, on both sides, goes nowhere.

## When to use

In a binding price control the rectangle one side loses is transferred to the other side and does not change the total; only the lost triangle is deadweight loss (Lec 10, Lec 13, PP 02).
