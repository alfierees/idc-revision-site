Goods that are rival but not excludable, such as clean air or fish in the ocean. Non-excludability brings free-riding, and rivalry adds a negative consumption externality.

## When to use

They are one of the two awkward cells where government can help, and the setting for the [[Tragedy of the Commons]] (Lec 13, PS 13, PP 02).

See [[Lec_13-Public Goods and International Trade|Lec 13]].
