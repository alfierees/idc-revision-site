The inputs used to produce goods and services, such as labour, land, capital and entrepreneurship in the course glossary, and workers, machines and technology in Lec 02. A country has only a limited quantity of them.

## When to use

Lec 02 builds the [[Production Possibility Frontier]] from one factor, labour measured in hours (Home has 10 hours). Because the factors are limited, giving more of them to one good means producing less of the other, which is the source of [[Opportunity Cost]].

See [[Lec_02-PPF and Gains from Trade|Lec 02]].
