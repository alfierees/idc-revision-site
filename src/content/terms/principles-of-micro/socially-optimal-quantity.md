The socially optimal quantity is where the social marginal benefit equals the social marginal cost. The market, looking only at private values, settles somewhere else when there is an [[Externality]].

## When to use

Compare it with the market quantity to say whether a good is over- or under-produced. A tax or subsidy that moves the supply curve from [[Marginal Private Cost]] to [[Marginal Social Cost]] makes the equilibrium equal the socially optimal quantity.

See [[Lec_12-Externalities|Lec 12]].
