Goods that are neither excludable nor rival, such as national defence or a tsunami siren, so the market tends to under-provide them.

## When to use

Use it with the [[Free-Rider Problem]]: since no one can be excluded, the benefit spills over like a [[Positive Externality]]. The lecture's remedy is for the government to provide the good and fund it with a tax if the benefit exceeds the cost.

See [[Lec_13-Public Goods and International Trade|Lec 13]].
