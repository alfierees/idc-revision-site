A shift of the whole supply curve, caused by something other than the good's own price: input prices, technology or expectations.

## When to use

Use it for any non-price cause. When input prices fall, supply shifts right; when they rise, it shifts left. Contrast it with a [[Change in Quantity Supplied]], a movement along the curve (Lec 04, Lec 07, PS 04, PS 07, PS 11, PP 01, PP 02).

See [[Lec_04-Supply and Market Equilibrium|Lec 04]].
