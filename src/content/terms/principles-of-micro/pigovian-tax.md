A tax that equals the cost of the externality, so that the producer faces the [[Marginal Social Cost]] and the market reaches the socially optimal quantity.

## When to use

Use it as the market-based remedy for a [[Negative Externality]]: the tax shifts supply up from private to social cost. Unlike most taxes it moves the market towards efficiency, and it gives firms an incentive to develop cleaner technologies.

See [[Lec_12-Externalities|Lec 12]].
