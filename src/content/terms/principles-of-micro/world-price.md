The world price is the price of a good prevailing in the world market. A country that opens to trade is assumed to be a price taker: it can buy or sell as much as it wants at that price, drawn as a horizontal line.

## When to use

Compare it with the no-trade price: if the world price is above it the country becomes an [[Exporting Country]], if below an [[Importing Country]]. It is the base for the [[Tariff]] analysis.

See [[Lec_13-Public Goods and International Trade|Lec 13]].
