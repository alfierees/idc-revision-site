Total revenue minus explicit costs only. It is always at least as big as [[Economic Profit]], and the gap is exactly the [[Implicit Costs]].

## When to use

Use it when a question counts only [[Explicit Costs]]. When economic profit is zero, accounting profit may still be positive, because the owner earns what they could have earned elsewhere (Lec 05, Lec 06, PS 05, PP 01).

See [[Lec_05-Supply and Costs of Production|Lec 05]].
