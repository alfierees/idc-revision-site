The percentage change in quantity supplied divided by the percentage change in price. It is positive because supply slopes upward.

## When to use

In the lecture example a price rise of 8% with a rise of 16% in quantity supplied gives 2.0. The more easily sellers can change output the larger it is, and it is greater in the long run than in the short run.
