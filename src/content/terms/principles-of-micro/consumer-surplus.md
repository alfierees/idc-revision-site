Willingness to pay (the buyer's marginal benefit) minus the price paid. Total consumer surplus is the area under the demand curve and above the market price, out to the quantity bought.

## When to use

Compute it as a triangle: with demand starting at 120, a price of 50 and 200 units sold, it is $\tfrac{1}{2} \times 200 \times (120 - 50) = 7000$. The last unit bought earns zero consumer surplus (Lec 09, Lec 08, Lec 10, PS 09, PS 10, PP 01, PP 02).
