A subsidy set equal to the marginal external benefit, so a positive externality is internalised.

## When to use

It is the policy answer to a positive externality that leads to under-production, for example education or patents. Contrast it with the [[Pigovian Tax]] for a negative externality (Lec 12, PS 12, PP 01).

See [[Lec_12-Externalities|Lec 12]].
