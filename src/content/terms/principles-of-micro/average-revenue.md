Total revenue divided by quantity. For a price taker it equals the price.

## When to use

It appears in the revenue table for a [[Price Taker]], next to [[Total Revenue]] and [[Marginal Revenue]] (Lec 06).

See [[Lec_06-Firms in Competitive Markets|Lec 06]].
