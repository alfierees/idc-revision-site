A subsidy is a per-unit payment from the government, treated as a negative tax. Buyers pay less and sellers receive more, and the gap between the two prices equals the subsidy.

## When to use

Solve it like a [[Per-Unit Tax]] with the sign reversed. In the cooking-gas example (subsidy of 4 per tank) buyers pay 3, sellers receive 7 and quantity rises from 500 to 600. As with a tax, the split depends on relative elasticities, not on who receives it by law.

See [[Lec_11-Taxes|Lec 11]].
