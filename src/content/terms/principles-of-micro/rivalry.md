A good is rival if one person's use reduces what is left for others, and not rival if others can use it at the same time. It is the second question used to classify goods, after [[Excludability]].

## When to use

Ask: if I use it, is there less for the next person? Yes means rival. Not rival gives a [[Public Goods]] case (under-provided), while rival and not excludable gives a [[Common Resources]] case, which leads to the [[Tragedy of the Commons]].

See [[Lec_13-Public Goods and International Trade|Lec 13]].
