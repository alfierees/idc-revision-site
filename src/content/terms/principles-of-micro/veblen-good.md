A Veblen good is a good whose quantity demanded can rise as its price rises because the buyer wants status. The slides' examples are diamonds and yachts, named after Thorstein Veblen.

## When to use

It is an exception to the [[Law of Demand]], along with the [[Giffen Good]]. Giffen goods slope upward because the buyer is poor, Veblen goods because the buyer wants status.
