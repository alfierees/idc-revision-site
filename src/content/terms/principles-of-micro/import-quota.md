A limit on the quantity of a good that can be produced abroad and sold domestically. Like a [[Tariff]], it raises the domestic price above the [[World Price]] and cuts imports, but the surplus rectangle goes to licence holders rather than the government.

## When to use

In the Lec 13 steel example, a quota that limits imports to the same level as the tariff has the same [[Deadweight Loss]], D + F; producers gain C and consumers lose C + D + E' + E'' + F. If a question gives a quota and a tariff at different import levels, recompute the areas.

See [[Lec_13-Public Goods and International Trade|Lec 13]].
