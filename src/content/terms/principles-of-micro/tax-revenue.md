Tax revenue is the tax per unit times the quantity sold after the tax, $T \times Q_T$. It is counted as a gain because it funds services such as education, roads and police.

## When to use

Add it to [[Consumer Surplus]] and [[Producer Surplus]] when finding the welfare effect of a tax: on the diagram it is the areas B + D, and the area left over is the [[Deadweight Loss of a Tax]]. If demand is fully inelastic quantity does not fall and there is no deadweight loss.

See [[Lec_11-Taxes|Lec 11]].
