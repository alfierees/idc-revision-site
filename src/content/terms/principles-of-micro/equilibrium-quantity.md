The quantity bought and sold at the [[Equilibrium Price]], where the demand and supply curves cross.

## When to use

Substitute the equilibrium price back into either curve; in the Lec 04 coffee example both $24 - 3(3)$ and $5(3)$ give 15. In the Lec 11 pizza example a per-unit tax cuts it from 500 to 450.

See [[Lec_04-Supply and Market Equilibrium|Lec 04]].
