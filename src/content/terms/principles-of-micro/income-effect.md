When the price of a good goes down, the same income buys more units. The converse also holds: a higher price lowers buyers' purchasing power, so they can afford less of the good.

## When to use

One of three reasons the demand curve slopes down (Lec 03), alongside the [[Substitution Effect]] and [[Diminishing Marginal Utility]]. These explain the [[Law of Demand]].

See [[Lec_03-Perfect Competition and Demand|Lec 03]].
