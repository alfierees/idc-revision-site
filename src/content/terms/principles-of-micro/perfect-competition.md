A market with many buyers and sellers of an identical good, so no single buyer or seller can move the price; the lectures also call it a competitive market.

## When to use

It is the assumption behind the demand and supply analysis and the firm model: each participant is a [[Price Taker]], so a firm's demand curve is flat and $P = AR = MR$.

See [[Lec_03-Perfect Competition and Demand|Lec 03]].
