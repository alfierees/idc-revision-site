A country whose [[World Price]] is lower than its no-trade price, so it buys from abroad. Its consumers gain and its producers lose, because the domestic price falls.

## When to use

Imports are quantity demanded minus quantity supplied at the world price. In the Lec 13 steel example consumer surplus rises by B + D, producer surplus falls by B, and total surplus rises by D. This is also the starting point for the [[Tariff]] analysis.

See [[Lec_13-Public Goods and International Trade|Lec 13]].
