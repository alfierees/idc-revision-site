Rent control is the lecture's example of a [[Price Ceiling]], a legal maximum price on rents. When it is binding, tenants demand more flats than landlords supply, so there is a shortage.

## When to use

In the example, a ceiling of USD 1,000 below the equilibrium price leaves landlords supplying 250 flats against 400 demanded: a [[Shortage]] of 150. Check first whether the ceiling is below equilibrium (a [[Binding Constraint]]).
