A shortage (excess demand) is when quantity demanded exceeds quantity supplied. At a price below equilibrium, sellers see empty shelves and the price tends to rise until the market reaches equilibrium.

## When to use

In the coffee example it is read off the schedule at a price below equilibrium. A binding [[Price Ceiling]] such as [[Rent Control]] creates one that does not correct itself. Its opposite is [[Surplus]].

See [[Lec_04-Supply and Market Equilibrium|Lec 04]].
