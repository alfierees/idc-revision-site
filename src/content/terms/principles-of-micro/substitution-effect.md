The substitution effect is one reason demand slopes down: when the price of a good goes up, consumers switch to other goods that can substitute for it, so they buy less of it.

## When to use

Cite it, with the [[Income Effect]] and [[Diminishing Marginal Utility]], when asked why the [[Law of Demand]] holds or why the [[Demand Curve]] slopes downward.

See [[Lec_03-Perfect Competition and Demand|Lec 03]].
