Long-run average total cost falls as the quantity of output rises. Diseconomies of scale is the case where it rises.

## When to use

In the long run [[Average Total Cost]] can fall (economies of scale), stay flat, or rise (diseconomies of scale) (Lec 05).

See [[Lec_05-Supply and Costs of Production|Lec 05]].
