The relationship between the quantity of inputs used and the quantity of output produced.

## When to use

In the lecture each added worker adds 50, 40, 30, 20, 10 units, so output rises at a falling rate and the function flattens ([[Diminishing Marginal Product]]); this is the mirror image of the [[Total Cost]] curve.

See [[Lec_05-Supply and Costs of Production|Lec 05]].
