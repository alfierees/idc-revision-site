A legal minimum on the price at which a good may be sold. It is binding only when it is set above the equilibrium price.

## When to use

Check first whether it is binding. A binding floor, such as the [[Minimum Wage]], creates a [[Surplus]]; a floor below equilibrium has no effect.

See [[Lec_10-Price Controls|Lec 10]].
