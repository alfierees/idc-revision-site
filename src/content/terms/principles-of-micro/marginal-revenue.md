The change in [[Total Revenue]] from selling one more unit. For a [[Price Taker]] it equals the price, so $P = AR = MR$.

## When to use

Compare it with [[Marginal Cost]] to pick output (Lec 06): $MR > MC$ means increase output, $MR < MC$ means decrease it, and $MR = MC$ is profit-maximising. For a price taker this becomes $P = MC$, on the rising part of MC.

See [[Lec_06-Firms in Competitive Markets|Lec 06]].
