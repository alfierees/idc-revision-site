A fixed amount T charged on every unit sold. It creates two prices, the one the buyer pays and the one the seller keeps, which differ by T (the [[Tax Wedge]]).

## When to use

A tax on buyers shifts demand down by T and a tax on sellers shifts supply up by T; either way buyers pay more, sellers receive less and the quantity traded falls. Who bears the burden is a [[Tax Incidence]] question.
