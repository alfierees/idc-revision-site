A government rule that fixes the price by law, either as a maximum ([[Price Ceiling]]) or as a minimum ([[Price Floor]]).

## When to use

Start by asking whether the control is binding, then work out the resulting shortage or surplus and the effect on consumer and producer surplus.

See [[Lec_10-Price Controls|Lec 10]].
