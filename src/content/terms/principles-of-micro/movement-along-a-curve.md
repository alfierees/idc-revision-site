A change in quantity demanded or quantity supplied caused only by a change in the good's own price; the curve itself does not move.

## When to use

After a curve shifts, the price change that follows is a movement along the curve that did not shift. In exam questions, separate this from a shift of the whole curve ([[Change in Demand]] or [[Change in Supply]]).
