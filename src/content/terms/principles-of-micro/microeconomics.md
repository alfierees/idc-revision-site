The study of how households and firms make decisions and interact in specific markets, in contrast with [[Macroeconomics]], which studies economy-wide phenomena.

## When to use

A framing definition from the first lecture: use it to tell micro questions (a single [[Market]], household or firm) from macro ones.

See [[Lec_01-Ten Principles of Economics|Lec 01]].
