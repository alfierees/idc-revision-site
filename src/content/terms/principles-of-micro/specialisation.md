Specialisation is when each producer concentrates on the good in which it has a [[Comparative Advantage]]. In the Home and Foreign example, together they then produce more of both goods than either could alone.

## When to use

Use it with the [[Production Possibility Frontier]] to show that specialising and trading puts consumption on a frontier outside both PPFs. Countries specialise if they can trade at a ratio between their opportunity costs, the [[Terms of Trade]].

See [[Lec_02-PPF and Gains from Trade|Lec 02]].
