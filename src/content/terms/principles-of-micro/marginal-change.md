A small incremental adjustment to an existing plan of action. Rational people decide by comparing the [[Marginal Benefit]] and [[Marginal Cost]] of such changes.

## When to use

Principle 3 in Lec 01. Examples are the hotel upgrade (the real decision is the 47 difference, not the full price) and business class (compare the extra USD 1,000 with the extra benefit). In the Mustang problem the USD 1,000 already spent on repairs is irrelevant, because only the marginal repair matters (the [[Sunk Cost]] idea).

See [[Lec_01-Ten Principles of Economics|Lec 01]].
