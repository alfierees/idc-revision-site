A good whose demand rises when income rises, and falls when income falls.

## When to use

Use it to predict which way the demand curve shifts when income changes ([[Change in Demand]]); a positive [[Income Elasticity of Demand]] identifies a normal good, and a negative one an [[Inferior Good]].

See [[Lec_03-Perfect Competition and Demand|Lec 03]].
