The combinations of goods a country can consume once it specialises and trades at a given price ratio. It lies outside the country's PPF.

## When to use

In the Lec 02 example it runs from 20 oranges (all Foreign) to 20 pears (all Home), $Y = 20 - X$, outside both PPFs, so both countries can consume more of both goods (PS 02).

See [[Lec_02-PPF and Gains from Trade|Lec 02]].
