A production possibility frontier that bends outwards because opportunity cost rises as more of one good is produced. With workers of different abilities, the cheapest-in-opportunity-cost workers are shifted first, so the slope rises along the curve.

## When to use

Use it when producers differ in opportunity cost. In the Neverland example the successive slopes are 1/2, 2/3 and 4 (Lec 02, PS 02).
