Tradable pollution permits are rights to emit a fixed amount of pollution that firms may buy and sell. The government fixes the total quantity and lets the market set the price.

## When to use

Contrast with a corrective tax: with a tax the regulator sets the price, with permits it limits the quantity (a vertical supply curve) and the market sets the price. A market in permits is governed by supply and demand. See [[Pigovian Tax]].
