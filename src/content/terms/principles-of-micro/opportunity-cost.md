Whatever must be given up to obtain an item: the value of the best forgone alternative, not the sum of all alternatives and not only the money price.

## When to use

It is the relevant cost for any decision. On a [[Production Possibility Frontier]] it is read off the slope, and comparing it across producers gives [[Comparative Advantage]].
