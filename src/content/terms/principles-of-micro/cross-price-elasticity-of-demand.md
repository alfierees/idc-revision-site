The percentage change in the quantity demanded of one good divided by the percentage change in the price of another good.

## When to use

Read the sign: positive means [[Substitutes]], negative means [[Complements]], zero means unrelated goods (Lec 08, PS 08, PP 02).

See [[Lec_08-Elasticity|Lec 08]].
