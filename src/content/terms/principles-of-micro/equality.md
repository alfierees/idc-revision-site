The benefits of society's resources are distributed uniformly among its members. It is one side of society's big tradeoff with [[Efficiency]]: redistributing income from rich to poor moves towards equality but weakens the incentive to work and produce, so the economic pie shrinks.

## When to use

Shows up in Principle 1 (tradeoffs) and Principle 7 (governments can promote equality as well as efficiency, through tax and welfare policy). Lec 09 warns that an efficient market outcome is not necessarily a fair one: efficiency says nothing about equality.

See [[Lec_01-Ten Principles of Economics|Lec 01]].
