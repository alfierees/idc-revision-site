A method that compares two equilibria, before and after a change in an outside factor, to see what happens to price and quantity. It does not describe the journey between them.

## When to use

Solve it in four steps: draw the starting equilibrium, identify the event and which curve it affects, shift that curve (one at a time), then read off the new equilibrium. The price change that follows is a [[Movement Along a Curve]] of the curve that did not shift (Lec 07, Lec 08, PS 07, PP 02).

See [[Lec_07-Comparative Statics|Lec 07]].
