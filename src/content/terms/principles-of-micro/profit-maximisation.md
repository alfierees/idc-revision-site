A firm produces where $MR = MC$; for a price taker, $MR = P$, so it produces where $P = MC$.

## When to use

Pick the quantity where $P = MC$ on the rising part of the [[Marginal Cost]] curve, then check the [[Shutdown Point]] rule: if it produces, it produces where $P = MC$. This is why the marginal-cost curve is the competitive firm's supply curve.

See [[Lec_06-Firms in Competitive Markets|Lec 06]].
