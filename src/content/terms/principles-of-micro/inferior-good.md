A good whose demand falls when income rises, and rises when income falls: $I \uparrow \rightarrow D \downarrow$.

## When to use

Income is a non-price determinant, so a change in income shifts the whole demand curve rather than moving along it ([[Change in Demand]]). A negative [[Income Elasticity of Demand]] signals an inferior good. A [[Giffen Good]] is a non-luxury inferior good.
