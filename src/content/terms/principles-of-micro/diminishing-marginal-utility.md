Each extra unit of a good adds less satisfaction than the one before, so buyers are willing to pay less for additional units.

## When to use

It is one of the three reasons the demand curve slopes down, alongside the substitution effect and the income effect (Lec 03).

See [[Lec_03-Perfect Competition and Demand|Lec 03]].
