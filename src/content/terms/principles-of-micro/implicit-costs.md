Input costs that do not require an outlay of money: the income given up by using resources in this activity, which is the [[Opportunity Cost]]. Examples are the owner's forgone wages or the forgone interest on invested savings. Only economists count them.

## When to use

In Lec 05, Judy baking cookies gives up guitar-lesson income, and Caroline funding her factory from USD 100,000 of savings gives up 5% interest (USD 5,000), so her economic total cost is USD 105,000 however she finances it. Zero [[Economic Profit]] can still leave positive [[Accounting Profit]] (Lec 06). The course always uses economic costs, and exam questions provide the implicit-cost information.
