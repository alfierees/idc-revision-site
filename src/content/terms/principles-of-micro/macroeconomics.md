The study of economy-wide phenomena such as inflation, unemployment and growth.

## When to use

Lec 01 sets it against [[Microeconomics]]: this course is microeconomics, and Principles 8 to 10 are the macro principles (standard of living depends on productive ability, prices rise when the government prints too much money, and the short-run inflation-unemployment tradeoff). The slides only name them.
