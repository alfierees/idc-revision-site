Average total cost is [[Total Cost]] divided by quantity, which equals [[Average Fixed Cost]] plus [[Average Variable Cost]]. In the bagel example it is U-shaped, with a minimum of 1.3 at 5 and 6 bagels.

## When to use

This answers the worksheet question "what is the average total cost of producing a bagel?": it depends on quantity, for example 5.4/4 = 1.35 at 4 bagels. [[Marginal Cost]] crosses ATC at ATC's minimum, so compare the two to see whether the average is falling or rising. The worksheet's ATC at Q = 7 (1.32) inherits a rounding slip in its AFC cell; the corrected value is 1.33.
