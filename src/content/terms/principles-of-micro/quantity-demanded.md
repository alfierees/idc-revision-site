The amount of a good buyers are willing and able to purchase at a given price.

## When to use

Read it off the [[Demand Schedule]] or curve at a given price. Only a change in the good's own price changes it ([[Change in Quantity Demanded]]); other determinants shift the whole curve.

See [[Lec_03-Perfect Competition and Demand|Lec 03]].
