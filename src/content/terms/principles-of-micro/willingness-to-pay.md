Willingness to pay is the maximum price a buyer will pay for a good. A buyer's marginal benefit is their willingness to pay.

## When to use

It is the starting point for [[Consumer Surplus]]: benefit minus what the buyer pays. In the class exercise each student wrote down a maximum price for a phone and the class built a [[Demand Curve]] from the answers.

See [[Lec_09-Welfare Consumer and Producer Surplus|Lec 09]].
