A tradeoff is the sacrifice we make when we decide: every decision involves giving something up. Society's big tradeoff is [[Efficiency]] versus [[Equality]].

## When to use

Use it to link to [[Opportunity Cost]] when a question asks what is forgone by a choice. Redistributing income towards equality weakens the incentive to work, so the pie shrinks.

See [[Lec_01-Ten Principles of Economics|Lec 01]].
