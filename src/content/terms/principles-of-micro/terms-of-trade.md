The terms of trade is the rate at which one good is exchanged for another. For both sides to gain it must lie between the two countries' opportunity costs.

## When to use

In the Home and Foreign example, oranges priced in pears must lie between 1/2 and 2. At 1 both sides gain, but at exactly 1/2 or 2 one country would gain nothing. Link it to [[Opportunity Cost]] and [[Gains from Trade]].
