The benefit of one more unit to society: the [[Marginal Private Benefit]] plus the external benefit to bystanders. On the diagram the social demand curve lies above the private one by the external benefit per unit.

## When to use

In a [[Positive Externality]] question, find the optimum where MSB meets supply, which gives a quantity above the market quantity (underproduction).

See [[Lec_12-Externalities|Lec 12]].
