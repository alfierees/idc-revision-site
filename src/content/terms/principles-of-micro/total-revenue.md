Total revenue is price times quantity sold: what a firm receives from selling its output.

## When to use

It is the first column of the revenue table (with [[Average Revenue]] and [[Marginal Revenue]]). [[Economic Profit]] is total revenue minus total costs. The shutdown rule compares it with variable cost: shut down if $TR < VC$. For a price taker, $TR = P \times Q$.

See [[Lec_06-Firms in Competitive Markets|Lec 06]].
