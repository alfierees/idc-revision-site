A curve plotting tax revenue against the tax rate. At first a higher rate raises revenue; beyond some point it discourages work and other taxed activity, the taxable base shrinks, and revenue falls.

## When to use

Lec 11 uses it as a warning that very high tax rates can lower [[Tax Revenue]]. The slide is a hump-shaped picture with no numbers, so expect a conceptual question rather than a calculation.

See [[Lec_11-Taxes|Lec 11]].
