Scarcity is the limited nature of society's resources, set against unlimited needs. Economics is the study of how society manages its scarce resources.

## When to use

It is the starting point of the course: individuals, firms and society all face it, which is why every choice involves a [[Tradeoff]]. Use it to frame why a good must be rationed, by time or by price.
