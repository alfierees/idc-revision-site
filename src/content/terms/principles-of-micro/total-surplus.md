Total surplus is consumer surplus plus producer surplus, the total gains from trade in a market. It equals $MB - MC$ because the price cancels: price only splits the gain between buyers and sellers.

## When to use

It is the area between the demand and supply curves out to the quantity traded. In the shoe market (price 30, quantity 15) it is 225 plus 112.50, or 337.50. The free market maximises it: see [[Market Efficiency]]. Components: [[Consumer Surplus]], [[Producer Surplus]].
