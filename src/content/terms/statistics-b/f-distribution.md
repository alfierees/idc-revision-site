The F distribution is the ratio of two independent chi-squared variables, $F(n,m)$, with numerator and denominator degrees of freedom. It is right-skewed.

## When to use

It is the null distribution of the [[F Test for Equality of Variances]]; read the upper-tail critical value from the F table using the numerator and denominator df.

See [[Lec_07-Tests for Proportions and Variances|Lec 07]].
