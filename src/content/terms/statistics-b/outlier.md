A point with a large residual; it can flatten the fitted slope.

## When to use

In PP 03 Q7, a point with below-average $X$ but a $Y$ above the fitted line pulls against a positive relationship, so removing it makes the slope steeper. The question gives no numbers, so the reasoning rests on the point lying above the line.
