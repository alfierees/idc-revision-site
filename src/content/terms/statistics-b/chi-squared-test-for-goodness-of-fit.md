The chi-squared test for goodness of fit tests whether one categorical variable follows a stated distribution. It uses $\chi^2=\sum (O_i-E_i)^2/E_i$ with $df=$ categories $-1$.

## When to use

Use it for questions about the shape of a distribution, such as whether a die is fair. Define $E_i$ under $H_0$, check all $E_i \ge 5$, then compare $\chi^2$ with the upper-tail critical value.
