Rejecting H0 when it is actually true (a false positive). Its probability is exactly the [[Significance Level]] $\alpha$: the area of the H0 distribution inside the critical region.

## When to use

It is one cell of the four-outcome table of any test. $\alpha$ is the probability of rejecting given that H0 is true, not the probability that H0 is true; shrinking $\alpha$ necessarily grows the [[Type II Error]].

See [[Lec_06-Two Sample Tests, Error Types and Power|Lec 06]].
