The fixed recipe for answering a yes/no question about the world: name the [[Parameter]], set complementary [[Null Hypothesis]] and [[Alternative Hypothesis]] values, find the distribution of a sample statistic under H0, collect data, and reject H0 if the observed value (or a more extreme one) has probability below the [[Significance Level]]. If it is not unlikely, there is no evidence either way.

## When to use

Every test question in the course follows these steps (Lec 09 restates them as the procedure of every hypothesis test). Choose $\alpha$ before looking at the data, and remember that failing to reject is not proof that H0 is true.

See [[Lec_04-Hypothesis Testing and the Z Test|Lec 04]]. Also in [[Lec_05-Z Tests, t Tests and Two-Sample Tests|Lec 05]].
