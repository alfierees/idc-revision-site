The alternative hypothesis $H_1$ is the complement of the [[Null Hypothesis]]: if $H_0$ is false, $H_1$ must be true. It is what the data would support if $H_0$ is rejected.

## When to use

Write it as step 2 of the [[Hypothesis Testing Procedure]], and note that failing to reject $H_0$ is "no evidence either way", not proof of $H_0$.

See [[Lec_04-Hypothesis Testing and the Z Test|Lec 04]].
