Total successes over total observations, $\hat p=(\text{successes}_1+\text{successes}_2)/(n_1+n_2)$, the estimate of the common $p$ under H0.

## When to use

It goes in the standard error of the [[Two Samples Z Test for Proportions]], since H0 says both populations share one $p$. In Tom's ad example, $(18+21)/1700=0.023$. The single-sample test uses the hypothesised $p$ instead.

See [[Lec_07-Tests for Proportions and Variances|Lec 07]]. Also in [[PS_07-Proportion Tests and Two Sample t Test|PS 07]].
