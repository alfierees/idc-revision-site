A test whose statistic follows $N(0,1)$ under H0, used when the variance is known and the distribution of the test statistic under H0 is normal.

## When to use

Pick it over the [[t Test]] when the question states the population standard deviation (for example "known from previous data"). Use the [[Single Sample Z Test]] for one mean and the [[Two Samples Z Test]] for a difference of two means.

See [[Lec_05-Z Tests, t Tests and Two-Sample Tests|Lec 05]].
