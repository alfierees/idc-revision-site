$O$ is the actual count in a category; $E$ is the count that H0 predicts, $E=n$ times $p$ under H0.

## When to use

Goodness of fit: for a fair die rolled 60 times, $E=\tfrac16\cdot60=10$ per face. Independence: $E_{i,j}=p(X_i)\,p(Y_j)\,N$. Both feed the [[Chi-Squared Statistic]] $\sum(O-E)^2/E$.

See [[Lec_09-Chi-Squared Tests for Goodness of Fit and Independence|Lec 09]]. Also in [[PS_09-Chi-Squared Tests for Goodness of Fit and Independence|PS 09]].
