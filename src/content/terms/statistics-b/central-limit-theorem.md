For $n$ i.i.d. draws, the sum is approximately $N(n\mu, n\sigma^2)$ and the average is approximately $N(\mu,\sigma^2/n)$. The rule of thumb is $n \ge 30$.

## When to use

Cite it to justify a normal sampling distribution of $\bar X$ when the population is not stated to be normal, for example when verifying the conditions of a Z test or showing that $Var(\bar X)=\sigma^2/n\to 0$.
