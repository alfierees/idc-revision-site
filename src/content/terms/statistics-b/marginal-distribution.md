The distribution of one variable regardless of the other, found by summing the rows (for $X$) or the columns (for $Y$) of the [[Joint Distribution]], $p(X=k)=\sum_l p(X=k,Y=l)$.

## When to use

Use it for the margins of a joint table, for the prediction of $Y$ when $X$ is unknown (the unknown-die example gives $E(Y)=3.5$), and when checking [[Independence of Random Variables]] cell by cell.

See [[Lec_11-Joint Distributions, Correlation and Linear Regression|Lec 11]]. Also in [[PS_11-Correlation, Regression and Simulation|PS 11]]. Seen in: Statistics A.
