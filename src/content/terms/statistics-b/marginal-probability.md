The probability of one variable's category alone, estimated from the row and column totals (the margins) of a [[Contingency Table]].

## When to use

In the [[Chi-Squared Test for Independence]] the expected count of a cell is the row marginal times the column marginal times the total, $E_{i,j}=p(X_i)\,p(Y_j)\,N$, with the marginals estimated from the table.

See [[Lec_09-Chi-Squared Tests for Goodness of Fit and Independence|Lec 09]]. Also in [[PS_09-Chi-Squared Tests for Goodness of Fit and Independence|PS 09]].
