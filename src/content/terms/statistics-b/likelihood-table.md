A table of the [[Likelihood]] of each candidate $\theta$ for each observed value of $X$; the [[Maximum Likelihood Estimator]] is the largest entry in each column.

## When to use

Past-paper questions give a small likelihood table and ask for the MLE for each $X$. Read down a column (one fixed $X$) and compare within it, never across a row: for example, with likelihoods 0.5, 0.9, 0.8 for $X=0$ the maximum is 0.9, at $\theta=1$.

See [[PP_03-Estimators, Z and t Tests, Power, F Test and Chi-Squared Tests (2024 Moed A)|PP 03]]. Also in [[PP_04-Estimators, Z and t Tests, F Test, Confidence Intervals & Chi-Squared (2024 Moed B)|PP 04]].
