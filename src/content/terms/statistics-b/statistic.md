A function $T(\mathbf{X})$ of the sample vector: a number describing the random sample, such as the average, the variance, the sum, the maximum, or just $X_1$. Because the sample is random, every statistic is itself a random variable.

## When to use

Use the word to separate it from a [[Parameter]], which describes the population. An [[Estimator]] is a statistic used to estimate a parameter, and each statistic has its own [[Sampling Distribution]].

See [[Lec_01-Point Estimation and Sampling Distributions|Lec 01]].
