For $X \sim U(0,M)$ with unknown maximum $M$, the sample maximum is biased low, while $2\bar X$ is an [[Unbiased Estimator]] of $M$.

## When to use

Lec 02 uses the serial-number-of-tanks story to estimate $M$. The factor 2 relies on the mean being exactly $M/2$, so $2\bar X$ is unbiased only for a uniform starting at 0, not for any distribution's maximum.

See [[Lec_02-Point Estimation and the Variance Estimator|Lec 02]].
