The prediction that minimises expected squared error: $A=E(X)$ is the value that makes $E(X-A)^2$ smallest, and the regression line is the best linear prediction of $Y$ from $X$.

## When to use

It is the reason the expected value is used as a [[Prediction]] and the reason the [[Linear Regression]] line is chosen: the sample line minimises $\sum(y_i-\hat y_i)^2$.

See [[Lec_11-Joint Distributions, Correlation and Linear Regression|Lec 11]].
