The conditional distribution of $Y$ given $X=k$ lists the values of $Y$ and their probabilities for that fixed $X$. You get it by taking the row $X=k$ of the joint table and rescaling it by the row total so it sums to 1.

## When to use

Use it to answer questions like the distribution of one die roll given which die was used, and as the input to a [[Conditional Expected Value]].

See [[Lec_11-Joint Distributions, Correlation and Linear Regression|Lec 11]].
