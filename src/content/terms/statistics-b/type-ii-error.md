Failing to reject H0 when H1 is true (a false negative). Its probability is $\beta$: the area of the H1 distribution on the do-not-reject side of the critical value.

## When to use

$\beta$ can only be computed once you commit to a specific $\mu_{H_1}$, because H1 covers a whole range of values. Power is $1-\beta$, and moving the critical value to cut [[Type I Error]] raises $\beta$.
