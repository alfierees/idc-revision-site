A test that makes no assumption about the shape of the distribution.

## When to use

Use it when the shape is unknown or unusual, when the shape is the question (is this die fair?), or when the variable is categorical so there is no mean to test. The chi-squared tests are the course's examples, and H0 still makes a prediction in the form of expected counts.

See [[Lec_09-Chi-Squared Tests for Goodness of Fit and Independence|Lec 09]]. Also in [[PS_09-Chi-Squared Tests for Goodness of Fit and Independence|PS 09]].
