The default claim, fixing a specific parameter value; probabilities are computed assuming it is true.

## When to use

H0 is the complement of H1 and carries the equals sign, which is why the calculation can be done under it (H1 gives no specific value). For a one-sided H1, use the boundary value of H0. If the data is not unlikely under H0 there is no evidence either way; that never shows H0 is true.
