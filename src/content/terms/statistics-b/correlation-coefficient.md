The population correlation is $\rho=Cov/(SD(X)SD(Y))$, between $-1$ and $1$ and unit-free; the sample version is Pearson's $r=Cov/(S(X)S(Y))$. It measures only linear association, and $r$ is biased but consistent.

## When to use

Use it instead of [[Covariance]] to judge how strong a linear relationship is, and to get the slope $b=r\,s_Y/s_X$ in [[Linear Regression]]. Never read zero correlation as independence.

See [[Lec_11-Joint Distributions, Correlation and Linear Regression|Lec 11]].
