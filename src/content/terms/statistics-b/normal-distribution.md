The bell-shaped distribution $N(\mu, \sigma^2)$, standardised with $Z=(x-\mu)/\sigma$ so a Z table can be used; the second argument in $N(\cdot,\cdot)$ is the variance.

## When to use

Sample averages are approximately normal by the [[Central Limit Theorem]], which is what lets the [[Z Test]] work, and probabilities come from the [[Normal Distribution CDF]]. It is also the model in the MLE for a normal sample (Lec 03).

See [[Lec_01-Point Estimation and Sampling Distributions|Lec 01]]. Also in [[Lec_03-Maximum Likelihood Estimation|Lec 03]]. Seen in: Statistics A.
