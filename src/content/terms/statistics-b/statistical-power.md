$1-\beta$, the probability of correctly rejecting H0 when it is false: the area of the H1 distribution beyond the critical value.

## When to use

To calculate it, turn the critical value into a value of $\bar X$ using the H0 distribution, then find the probability beyond it using the H1 distribution at a specific $\mu_{H_1}$ (one lecture example gives 0.54). Power rises with a larger $\alpha$, a correct directional H1, a larger $n$ and a larger [[Cohen's d]].
