The entire set of individuals, items or data points we want to describe, such as all US voters or this class. A population is described by its distribution; for example adult human height is distributed $N(165, 7.5^2)$, and a [[Parameter]] is a number that describes it.

## When to use

It is the first thing to identify in an estimation or testing question: name the population, then the parameter in question. In a poll, the population is the voters and the parameter is the proportion voting for a candidate.
