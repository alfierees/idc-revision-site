A randomly selected subset of the [[Population]]: $n$ independent, identically distributed observations $X_1, X_2, \dots, X_n$. "Random" refers to which individuals end up in the sample, and mathematically the sample is a random vector.

## When to use

Every estimation and testing question assumes one. Its independence is what makes the [[Central Limit Theorem]] apply (rule of thumb $n \ge 30$) and what makes any [[Statistic]] computed from it a random variable.
