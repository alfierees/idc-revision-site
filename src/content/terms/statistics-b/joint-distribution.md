The probabilities of every pair of values of $X$ and $Y$, laid out as a table whose row and column totals are the [[Marginal Distribution]]s.

## When to use

It is the starting point of Lec 11: from the joint table you get marginals, [[Conditional Distribution]]s, [[Covariance]] and correlation, as in the four-sided die example.

See [[Lec_11-Joint Distributions, Correlation and Linear Regression|Lec 11]]. Also in [[PS_11-Correlation, Regression and Simulation|PS 11]]. Seen in: Statistics A.
