Covariance is $E((X-E(X))(Y-E(Y)))$. It captures the linear relationship between $X$ and $Y$ but depends on the units of both.

## When to use

Its sign shows the direction of a linear relationship but its size alone cannot show strength, which motivates the [[Correlation Coefficient]]. Zero covariance does not imply independence, though independence implies zero covariance.
