Estimating an unknown population [[Parameter]] by a single number computed from a sample: sample, then [[Estimator]], then parameter estimate.

## When to use

Lec 01 to Lec 03 choose an estimator (by guessing or by maximum likelihood), check that it is unbiased and consistent, and correct any linear bias. It differs from hypothesis testing (which asks a yes/no question) and from prediction (which estimates a single observation).

See [[Lec_02-Point Estimation and the Variance Estimator|Lec 02]]. Also in [[Lec_01-Point Estimation and Sampling Distributions|Lec 01]], [[PS_02-Unbiased and Consistent Estimators and Variance Simulation|PS 02]].
