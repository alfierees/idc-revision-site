The critical value times the [[Standard Error]], which is half the width of the [[Confidence Interval]].

## When to use

The interval is the estimate plus or minus the margin of error, so its total width is twice the margin. In sample-size questions (PP 01) a whole interval of width at most 5 means a margin of 2.5, not 5.

See [[PS_08-Confidence Intervals|PS 08]].
