An [[Alternative Hypothesis]] with $\ne$, giving a two-tailed test with $\alpha$ split, $\alpha/2$ in each tail.

## When to use

Wording such as "influences" or "is different from" signals a two-tailed test: at $\alpha=0.05$ the critical values are $\pm1.96$ instead of $\pm1.65$, and a [[P-Value]] from one tail is doubled. In the dairy and height example the same data is significant one-tailed in the right direction but not two-tailed, so choose the direction before seeing the data.

See [[Lec_04-Hypothesis Testing and the Z Test|Lec 04]]. Also in [[PS_04-Single Sample Z Tests and One vs Two Tailed Tests|PS 04]], [[Lec_07-Tests for Proportions and Variances|Lec 07]].
