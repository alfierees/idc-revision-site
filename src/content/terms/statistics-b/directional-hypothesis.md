A directional hypothesis is a one-sided $H_1$ ($\mu <$ or $\mu >$), which gives a one-tailed test with all of $\alpha$ in one tail.

## When to use

The tail checked follows $H_1$: right for $>$, left for $<$. Spot it from wording like "better than" or "less than", as with the dairy researchers where only the left-tailed claim rejected.
