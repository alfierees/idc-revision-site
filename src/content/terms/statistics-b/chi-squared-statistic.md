The chi-squared statistic is $\sum (O-E)^2/E$ over the categories, comparing [[Observed and Expected Frequencies]]. Squaring the gaps and dividing by $E$ makes it scale-free.

## When to use

Calculate it in both chi-squared tests after checking that every $E_i \ge 5$; under $H_0$ it follows a [[Chi-Squared Distribution]], and only a large value is evidence against $H_0$.

See [[Lec_09-Chi-Squared Tests for Goodness of Fit and Independence|Lec 09]].
