An estimator is a formula applied to a sample to estimate a parameter, such as $\bar X$ for $\mu$. A [[Point Estimation]] reports its value as a single number.

## When to use

The question is which statistic to use for which parameter, and the course answer is one that is an [[Unbiased Estimator]] and a [[Consistent Estimator]]; you check both properties for each candidate.

See [[Lec_02-Point Estimation and the Variance Estimator|Lec 02]].
