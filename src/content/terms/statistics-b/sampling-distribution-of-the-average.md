The distribution of the sample mean $\bar X$, which is $N(\mu, \sigma^2/n)$ when $n \ge 30$ or the population is itself normal. It is the [[Sampling Distribution]] of the average, and its standard deviation $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$ is the [[Standard Error]].

## When to use

It drives both the Z test denominator and the confidence interval. For example, 95% of sample means fall within 1.96 standard errors of $\mu$, which is what lets us say $\mu$ lies within 1.96 standard errors of $\bar X$.
