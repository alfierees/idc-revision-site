A contingency table shows the joint counts of two categorical variables, with the values of one variable as rows and the other as columns. The row and column totals are its margins.

## When to use

It is the data layout for the [[Chi-Squared Test for Independence]]: the margins give the marginal probabilities used to compute expected counts.

See [[Lec_09-Chi-Squared Tests for Goodness of Fit and Independence|Lec 09]].
