The discrete uniform distribution gives equal probability to each integer from $a$ to $b$, with variance $((b-a+1)^2-1)/12$.

## When to use

Use it for a generator of whole numbers, such as one returning $0,1,\dots,10$ ($a=0$, $b=10$). The continuous formula $(b-a)^2/12$ would be wrong here.
