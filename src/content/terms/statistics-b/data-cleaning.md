Data cleaning means checking that every value is in its valid range and removing missing-value codes before computing statistics.

## When to use

In the World Values Survey extra-credit project, missing answers are coded as negative numbers, which must be removed or they drag the mean down and wreck the standard deviation. Also read the scale before choosing the direction of $H_1$.
