The critical region is the tail area of total probability $\alpha$ of the test statistic's distribution under $H_0$ where we would reject $H_0$. Its boundary is the [[Critical Value]].

## When to use

Use it to run a test without a p-value: reject if the observed statistic lands inside it. It is the right tail for $>$, the left tail for $<$, and both tails (each $\alpha/2$) for $\ne$.
