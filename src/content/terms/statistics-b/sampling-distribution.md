Because a sample is a random vector, every [[Statistic]] is itself a random variable. Its distribution, meaning all its possible values and their probabilities across repeated samples, is its sampling distribution. The CLT results for the sum and the average are examples.

## When to use

Every test starts from it: the procedure asks for the distribution of the sample statistic under H0, and the p-value or critical value is read from that distribution.
