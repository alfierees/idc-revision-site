The standard deviation of a statistic's [[Sampling Distribution]]. For the sample mean it is $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$, and it is the denominator of the test statistic.

## When to use

To build a [[Confidence Interval]], estimate $\pm$ critical value $\times$ standard error, identify these two pieces from the matching hypothesis test. For a difference of means with unknown equal variances, $S.E.=\sqrt{197(1/50+1/50)}=2.81$ in the lecture example.

See [[Lec_08-Confidence Intervals|Lec 08]].
