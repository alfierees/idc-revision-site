Bias is the expected value of an estimator minus the parameter it estimates, $E(\hat\theta)-\theta$. An [[Unbiased Estimator]] has bias zero.

## When to use

In problem sets you compute $E(\hat\theta)$ and subtract $\theta$ to state the bias, for example a constant bias of $\tfrac73$ that a linear shift removes.

See [[PS_01-Unbiased and Consistent Estimators|PS 01]].
