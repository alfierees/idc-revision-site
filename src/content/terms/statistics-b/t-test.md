A test whose statistic follows a [[t Distribution]] under H0, used when the variance is unknown and must be estimated from the sample (with a normal population).

## When to use

Choose it over the [[Z Test]] when a question gives only sample standard deviations. Use the [[Single Sample t Test]] for one mean and the [[Two Independent Samples t Test]] for a difference of two means with equal variances.

See [[Lec_05-Z Tests, t Tests and Two-Sample Tests|Lec 05]].
