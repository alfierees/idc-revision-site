Degrees of freedom count the independent pieces that are summed or free to vary. For the [[Chi-Squared Distribution]] the parameter is the number of independent squared normals summed.

## When to use

You need it to read the table: $n-1$ for a single sample t test, $n_X+n_Y-2$ for a two independent samples t test (one lost per estimated mean), categories $-1$ for goodness of fit and $(r-1)(c-1)$ for independence.

See [[Lec_05-Z Tests, t Tests and Two-Sample Tests|Lec 05]].
