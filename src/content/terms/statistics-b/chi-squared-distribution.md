The chi-squared distribution with $k$ degrees of freedom is the distribution of a sum of $k$ independent squared standard normals. It is never negative and is right-skewed for small df.

## When to use

It underlies the [[t Distribution]] (a normal over a chi-squared based term), the [[F Distribution]], and the critical values used in chi-squared tests, where only the upper tail is used.

See [[Lec_05-Z Tests, t Tests and Two-Sample Tests|Lec 05]].
