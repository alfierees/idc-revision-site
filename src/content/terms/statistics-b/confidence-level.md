The confidence level is $1-\alpha$, the central share of the sampling distribution that the [[Confidence Interval]] is built to cover.

## When to use

A higher confidence level (smaller $\alpha$) gives a larger critical value and a wider interval, while a bigger sample narrows it. Setting $\alpha=0$ gives a guaranteed but useless interval.

See [[Lec_08-Confidence Intervals|Lec 08]].
