$(x-\text{mean})/SD$: how many standard deviations a value lies from the mean. Standardised data have mean 0 and standard deviation 1.

## When to use

Use it to read the Z table, $Z(t)=(t-\mu)/\sigma$, as in the [[Normal Distribution CDF]]. In Problem Set 11, standardising both variables before regression makes the two prediction equations identical in form, $\hat Z_y=0.856\,Z_x$ and $\hat Z_x=0.856\,Z_y$.

See [[PS_11-Correlation, Regression and Simulation|PS 11]].
