The probability of a pair of outcomes, $p(X_i,Y_j)$, one cell of the joint table; under independence it equals the product of the [[Marginal Probability]] values.

## When to use

In the [[Chi-Squared Test for Independence]], H0 says the joint probability equals the product of the marginals for every cell of the [[Contingency Table]], which gives the expected count $E_{i,j}=p(X_i)\,p(Y_j)\,N$.

See [[Lec_09-Chi-Squared Tests for Goodness of Fit and Independence|Lec 09]].
