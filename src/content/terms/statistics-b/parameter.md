A number that describes the population, such as its mean, variance or proportion.

## When to use

The first step of the [[Hypothesis Testing Procedure]] is to name the parameter in question (for example $p$, the probability of heads, or $\mu$), and H0 and H1 are statements about its value. In [[Point Estimation]] it is what the estimator aims at; $\rho$ is a parameter of a joint distribution.

See [[Lec_01-Point Estimation and Sampling Distributions|Lec 01]].
