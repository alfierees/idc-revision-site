The conditional expected value $E(Y \mid X=k)$ is the mean of the [[Conditional Distribution]] of $Y$ given $X=k$: an ordinary expected value computed with the conditional probabilities.

## When to use

Use it to predict $Y$ when $X$ is known. For dependent variables it predicts better than the unconditional $E(Y)$, with a smaller expected squared error.

See [[Lec_11-Joint Distributions, Correlation and Linear Regression|Lec 11]].
