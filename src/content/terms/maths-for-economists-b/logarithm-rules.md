The product, quotient, power and inverse rules for logarithms, used to combine or split log expressions.

## When to use

Used when solving log equations such as $\log x+\log(x-15)=2$: check the domain first, combine with the product rule, then convert to an exponential equation.

See [[Lec_02-Logarithmic Functions and Inverse Functions|Lec 02]].
