The extra profit from one more unit of a product; the [[Partial Derivative]] of profit with respect to that product.

## When to use

In two-product problems write revenue minus cost, then take $P'_x$ and $P'_y$ and compare them to decide which product to add or to set up conditions such as $P'_x=P'_y$. Also used in Principles of Micro.

See [[Lec_07-Partial Derivatives in Applications and Local Extrema|Lec 07]].
