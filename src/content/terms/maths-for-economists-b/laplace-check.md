The test that $f_{xx} + f_{yy} = 0$ for a function of two variables, done by computing both second partial derivatives and adding them.

## When to use

In [[PP_27-Fabric Profit, Parametric System, Log Surface & x e to the minus x (2022 Moed B)|PP 27]] (Q3c) the log surface $f=-\tfrac12\ln(x^2+y^2)$ is shown to satisfy $f_{xx}+f_{yy}=0$ by applying the quotient rule twice and checking the sum cancels.

See [[PP_27-Fabric Profit, Parametric System, Log Surface & x e to the minus x (2022 Moed B)|PP 27]].
