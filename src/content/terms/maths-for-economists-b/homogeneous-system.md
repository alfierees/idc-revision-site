A linear system in which all constant terms are zero. It always has at least one solution, the [[Zero Solution]].

## When to use

A homogeneous system can never have no solution: it has either only the zero solution or infinitely many. With more unknowns than equations there is always a free variable, as in $x+y-z=0$, $4x-2y+7z=0$, whose solutions are $(-5t, 11t, 6t)$.

See [[Lec_11-Reduced Echelon Form and Homogeneous Systems|Lec 11]].
