The curve cut from the surface $z=f(x,y)$ by the plane $z=c$; it is another name for a [[Contour Line]].

## When to use

Appears in exam questions that give a level of a [[Two-Variable Function]] and ask for the slope of the curve there, found by [[Implicit Differentiation]] (slope $-f'_x/f'_y$), as in [[PP_32-Import Profit, Level Curves, Homogeneity Sums & x to the x (2025 Moed A)|PP 32]] and [[PP_29-Profit Maximisation, Parametric Linear System, Implicit Slope & x² ln x (2023 Moed B)|PP 29]].

See [[PP_29-Profit Maximisation, Parametric Linear System, Implicit Slope & x² ln x (2023 Moed B)|PP 29]]. Also in [[Lec_05-Two Variable Functions|Lec 05]].
