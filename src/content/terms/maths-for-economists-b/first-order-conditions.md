The conditions that all partial derivatives equal zero. For a [[Lagrangian]] this means the partials in every variable and in $\lambda$; the $\lambda$ equation just returns the constraint.

## When to use

This is step 2 of the [[Lagrange Multiplier Method]]. For an unconstrained local extremum of $f(x,y)$ the conditions are $f'_x = 0$ and $f'_y = 0$, which locate the [[Critical Point]]s.

See [[Lec_09-Constrained Optimisation and Lagrange Multipliers|Lec 09]].
