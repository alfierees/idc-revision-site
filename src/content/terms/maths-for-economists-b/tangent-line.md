The line touching a curve at a point, with slope $y'$ at that point.

## When to use

For a curve given implicitly, find the $y$ values at the given $x$, compute $y'$ by [[Implicit Differentiation]], then write $y-y_0=y'(x-x_0)$.

See [[Lec_04-Implicit Differentiation and Two-Variable Functions|Lec 04]].
