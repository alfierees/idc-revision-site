$dz=f'_x\,dx+f'_y\,dy$, a linear estimate of the change in $z$; also written as a differential estimate $dP=P_x\,dx+P_y\,dy$.

## When to use

Used to estimate a value near a known point, for example $f(4.95,2.04)$ from $f(5,2)$, or the change in profit when two quantities move by small amounts.
