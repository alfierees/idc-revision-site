Rewrites a logarithm in a different base: $\log_a c = \dfrac{\log_b c}{\log_b a}$. It is the fifth of the [[Logarithm Rules]].

## When to use

Use it to solve exponential equations such as $3\cdot 5^x = 21$, where $5^x = 7$ gives $x = \ln 7 / \ln 5$, and to turn $\log_{\sqrt 3} x$ into $2\log_3 x$ so two logs share a base.

See [[Lec_02-Logarithmic Functions and Inverse Functions|Lec 02]].
