Form the [[Lagrangian]], set every partial derivative (including the one in $\lambda$) to zero, solve the system, then compare the candidates.

## When to use

Use one multiplier per constraint. Mistakes to avoid: skipping $\partial L/\partial\lambda = 0$ (it is the constraint), dividing by a variable that could be zero, and stopping at candidates without deciding which is the min and which the max. The lectures solve the box, four-variable, sphere and sum-of-squares problems this way.

See [[Lec_09-Constrained Optimisation and Lagrange Multipliers|Lec 09]]. Also in [[Lec_08-Second Derivative Test and Lagrange Multipliers|Lec 08]].
