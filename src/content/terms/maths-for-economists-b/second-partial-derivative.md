A partial derivative of a partial derivative; there are four, $f''_{xx}$, $f''_{yy}$ and the mixed $f''_{xy}$ and $f''_{yx}$, and for well-behaved functions the two mixed ones agree.

## When to use

Needed for the [[Second Derivative Test]] and for checks such as the [[Laplace Check]].

See [[Lec_06-Partial Derivatives and the Differential|Lec 06]].
