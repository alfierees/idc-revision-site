$L = f - \lambda(g - c)$, the function whose critical points solve the constrained problem. With several constraints, subtract one $\lambda_j(g_j - c_j)$ term per constraint.

## When to use

Build it as step 1 of the [[Lagrange Multiplier Method]], then set all its partials to zero. For minimising $a^2 + ah$ subject to $a^2h = 2$, $L = a^2 + ah - \lambda(a^2 h - 2)$.

See [[Lec_09-Constrained Optimisation and Lagrange Multipliers|Lec 09]].
