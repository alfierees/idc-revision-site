For a function with a variable base and a variable exponent, $y=f^g$, take $\ln$ of both sides before differentiating.

## When to use

Used for functions such as $x^{1/x}$, where $\ln y=\frac{\ln x}{x}$ is differentiated and then multiplied back by $y$; it also underlies tangent and extrema questions on functions like $x^x$.

See [[Lec_04-Implicit Differentiation and Two-Variable Functions|Lec 04]].
