The function with the roles of $x$ and $y$ swapped; its graph is the reflection of the original in the line $y = x$.

## When to use

The key pair is $y = \log_a x$ and $y = a^x$. The log passes through $(1,0)$ because $a^0 = 1$, is increasing for $a>1$ and decreasing for $0<a<1$, and has the vertical asymptote $x=0$.
