A variable whose column has a [[Leading Entry]] in the reduced matrix. It is determined by the others.

## When to use

After reducing, read each leading-entry column as a dependent variable and write it in terms of the [[Independent Variable]]s, as in $x_1 = 6 - 2t$ when $x_2 = t$ is free.

See [[Lec_10-Linear Systems and Gauss-Jordan Elimination|Lec 10]].
