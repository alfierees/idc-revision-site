A rule $z=f(x,y)$ giving one output for each pair of inputs; its graph is a surface.

## When to use

Studied by slicing the surface with planes: $z=c$ gives a [[Level Curve]], and $x=c$ or $y=c$ gives a [[Cross Section of a Surface]].

See [[Lec_04-Implicit Differentiation and Two-Variable Functions|Lec 04]].
