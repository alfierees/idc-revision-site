A matrix where each [[Leading Entry]] is 1 and alone in its column, leading entries step down to the right, and zero rows come last.

## When to use

The target of [[Gauss-Jordan Elimination]]: once the augmented matrix is reduced, columns with a leading entry give the [[Dependent Variable]]s and the others are [[Independent Variable]]s.

See [[Lec_11-Reduced Echelon Form and Homogeneous Systems|Lec 11]].
