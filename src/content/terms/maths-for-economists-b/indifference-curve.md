A [[Contour Line]] of a utility function: the bundles that give the same utility.

## When to use

Used when reading economics from contours: straight parallel contours mean perfect substitutes, hyperbolas (where $xy$ is constant) are Cobb-Douglas style smooth substitution, and L-shaped contours are the signature of perfect complements.

See [[Lec_05-Two Variable Functions|Lec 05]]. Also used in Micro-Economics I.
