The set of points where $f(x,y) = c$, drawn in the $(x,y)$ plane. Several contours together give a 2D map of the 3D surface, like a hiking map.

## When to use

Asked to draw contours: $z = 2y - 6x + 8$ gives parallel straight lines, $z = \ln x + \ln y$ gives hyperbolas $xy = e^c$, and $z = \max\{x,y\}$ gives L-shapes. In economics, contours of utility are [[Indifference Curve]]s and contours of profit are [[Isoprofit Curve]]s.
