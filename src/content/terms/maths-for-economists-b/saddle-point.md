A critical point that is neither a local max nor a local min: the surface goes up in some directions and down in others.

## When to use

A saddle also has zero gradient, so zero partials are not enough; the [[Second Derivative Test]] gives a saddle when $D<0$.

See [[Lec_07-Partial Derivatives in Applications and Local Extrema|Lec 07]]. Also in [[Lec_08-Second Derivative Test and Lagrange Multipliers|Lec 08]].
