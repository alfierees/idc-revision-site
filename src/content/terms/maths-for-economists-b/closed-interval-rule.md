A continuous function on a closed interval always has both a global maximum and a global minimum (see [[Global Extremum]]), and they are found among the [[Critical Point]]s and the endpoints.

## When to use

Use it when a question asks for the global max and min on a closed interval such as $[-2,2]$: compare $f$ at each critical point with $f$ at the two endpoints. If the interval is open at an end or unbounded, one or both extremes may not exist.

See [[Lec_01-Global Extrema, Optimization and Summation|Lec 01]].
