Maximising or minimising $f$ subject to one or more constraints $g = c$, for example utility under a budget or cost under an output target. It can be solved by substitution or by the [[Lagrange Multiplier Method]].

## When to use

Substitute when one simple constraint is easy to solve, and use Lagrange multipliers for several or awkward constraints such as $x^2+y^2+z^2=1$. The square-base box problem (volume 2, minimum cost 3) is solved both ways in the lectures.

See [[Lec_09-Constrained Optimisation and Lagrange Multipliers|Lec 09]]. Also in [[Lec_08-Second Derivative Test and Lagrange Multipliers|Lec 08]].
