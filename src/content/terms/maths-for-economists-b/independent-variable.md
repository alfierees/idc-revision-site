A free variable: its column in the reduced matrix has no [[Leading Entry]], so it can take any value and the [[Dependent Variable]]s are expressed in terms of it.

## When to use

When reading a reduced matrix, each column without a leading entry gives a free parameter, such as $x_4 = s$ and $x_5 = t$. A free column with no contradiction means infinitely many solutions.

See [[Lec_10-Linear Systems and Gauss-Jordan Elimination|Lec 10]].
