Sums split over addition, a constant factor pulls out, and the sum of a constant $c$ over $n$ terms is $nc$.

## When to use

Used when simplifying $\sum_{i=1}^{n}$ expressions, and in partial derivatives of sums where only the $i=j$ term survives.

See [[Lec_01-Global Extrema, Optimization and Summation|Lec 01]].
