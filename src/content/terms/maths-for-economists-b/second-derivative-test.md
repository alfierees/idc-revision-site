With $D=f_{xx}f_{yy}-f_{xy}^2$ at a critical point: $D>0$ gives a max or min by the sign of $f_{xx}$, $D<0$ a saddle, and $D=0$ is inconclusive.

## When to use

Use it after finding the [[Critical Point]]s of a two-variable function to classify each as a max, min or [[Saddle Point]], typically in a table of $f_{xx}$, $f_{yy}$ and $D$.

See [[Lec_08-Second Derivative Test and Lagrange Multipliers|Lec 08]].
