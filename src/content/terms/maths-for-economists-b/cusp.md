A sharp point on a graph where the derivative does not exist, such as $x^{2/3}$ at 0.

## When to use

In global extremum problems the minimum can sit at a cusp, as in the cube-root and absolute-value examples, where solving $f' = 0$ alone would miss it. Look at the piecewise form or at points where $f'$ does not exist, which are also [[Critical Point]]s.

See [[PS_01-Global Extrema and Optimisation|PS 01]]. Also in [[PP_01-Tangent Slope, Marginal Profit, Parametric System & Cube-Root Extrema (2009 Moed A)|PP 01]].
