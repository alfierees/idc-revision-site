The curve you get by cutting a surface with a plane such as $y = c$. It is an ordinary one-variable curve with a tangent line.

## When to use

Fix one variable to turn a surface problem into a one-variable one, as in the plane $x=0$ for $x^2+y^2$. The slope of such a cross section is the seed of a [[Partial Derivative]].

See [[Lec_04-Implicit Differentiation and Two-Variable Functions|Lec 04]].
