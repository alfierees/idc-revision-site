A [[Contour Line]] of the profit function, $P$ constant, with slope $-P'_x / P'_y$.

## When to use

In the surfboard problem, $(10,10)$ lies on the curve $P = 1600$ with slope $5.5$: along constant profit, each extra regular board must come with about $5.5$ more competitive boards, because $dP = P'_x\,dx + P'_y\,dy = 0$.

See [[Lec_07-Partial Derivatives in Applications and Local Extrema|Lec 07]].
