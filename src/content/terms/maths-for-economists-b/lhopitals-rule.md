For a limit of the form 0/0 or $\infty/\infty$, differentiate the numerator and the denominator separately and take the limit again.

## When to use

Used for limits of quotients such as $e^x/\ln x$ and for limits behind asymptote questions; a $0\cdot\infty$ limit is first rewritten as a quotient. It proves the growth ordering $\ln x \ll x^p \ll e^x$.

See [[Lec_02-Logarithmic Functions and Inverse Functions|Lec 02]]. Also in [[Lec_03-Exponential and Logarithmic Limits and Asymptotes|Lec 03]].
