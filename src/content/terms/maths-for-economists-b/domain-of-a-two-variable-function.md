The set of points $(x,y)$ where the formula is defined, found by applying the one-variable rules to each expression: denominators are non-zero, even roots need a non-negative argument, and logs need a strictly positive argument.

## When to use

Questions ask you to find and sketch the domain on the $(x,y)$ plane, for example $\ln\big[x(y-x^2)\big]$ needs $x(y-x^2) > 0$, which splits into two cases. Boundaries from a log are dashed because they are excluded.

See [[Lec_05-Two Variable Functions|Lec 05]].
