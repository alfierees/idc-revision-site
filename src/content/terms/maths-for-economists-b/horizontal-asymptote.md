A line $y = L$ that the graph approaches as $x \to \pm\infty$, that is, a finite limit at infinity.

## When to use

Check the limits at $\pm\infty$ (within the domain), using [[L'Hopital's Rule]] where needed. For example $x^2 \ln x$ and $\ln^2 x - x$ have no horizontal asymptote, because both tend to infinity in size.

See [[Lec_03-Exponential and Logarithmic Limits and Asymptotes|Lec 03]].
