The first non-zero entry in a row of a matrix; it is the pivot of that row.

## When to use

Used to read off a system: a column with a leading entry is a [[Dependent Variable]], a column without one is an [[Independent Variable]] and is free. In a [[Reduced Echelon Form]] every leading entry is 1 and steps to the right going down.

See [[Lec_10-Linear Systems and Gauss-Jordan Elimination|Lec 10]].
