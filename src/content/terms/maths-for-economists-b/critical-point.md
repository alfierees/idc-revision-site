A point where $f' = 0$ or $f'$ does not exist; for two variables, where both partial derivatives are zero. These are the candidates for extrema.

## When to use

In one variable, list the critical points and compare them with the endpoints to find a [[Global Extremum]]. In two variables, solving $f'_x = f'_y = 0$ is necessary but not sufficient, because a [[Saddle Point]] also qualifies, so classify with the [[Second Derivative Test]].

See [[Lec_01-Global Extrema, Optimization and Summation|Lec 01]]. Also in [[Lec_07-Partial Derivatives in Applications and Local Extrema|Lec 07]].
