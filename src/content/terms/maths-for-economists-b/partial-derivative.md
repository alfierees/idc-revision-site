The derivative in one variable with the other variables held constant; $f'_x$ is the slope of the tangent line to the curve cut from the surface by the plane $y=y_0$.

## When to use

Used for [[Marginal Profit]], for critical points (both partials zero), for the [[Total Differential]] and for implicit slopes $-f'_x/f'_y$.

See [[Lec_05-Two Variable Functions|Lec 05]]. Also in [[Lec_06-Partial Derivatives and the Differential|Lec 06]].
