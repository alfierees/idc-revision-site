The highest (local maximum) or lowest (local minimum) value of $f$ in a neighbourhood of a point, rather than over the whole domain.

## When to use

If $f$ has a local extremum at $(a,b)$ then both partial derivatives vanish there, so look among the [[Critical Point]]s; this is necessary but not sufficient, so classify with the [[Second Derivative Test]].

See [[Lec_07-Partial Derivatives in Applications and Local Extrema|Lec 07]].
