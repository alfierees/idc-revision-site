The $\lambda$ that scales $\nabla f$ to $\nabla g$ at the optimum, so that $\nabla f = \lambda \nabla g$. It measures how much the optimal value changes if the constraint constant $c$ is relaxed by one unit, a [[Shadow Price]].

## When to use

In the box problem $\lambda = 1$, so one more unit of required volume raises the minimal cost by roughly one. The lecture marks this economic reading as the standard interpretation, not stated on the slides.
