$\log_a b = n$ exactly when $a^n = b$; a log written without a base is read as base 10 in this course.

## When to use

Appears in domain checks (the argument must be positive) and equation solving, together with the [[Logarithm Rules]] and [[Change of Base]].

See [[Lec_02-Logarithmic Functions and Inverse Functions|Lec 02]].
