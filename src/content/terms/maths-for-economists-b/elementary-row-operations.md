The three moves that change a system's form without changing its solutions: add a multiple of one row to another, multiply a row by a non-zero number, or swap two rows.

## When to use

Apply them to the [[Augmented Matrix]] during [[Gauss-Jordan Elimination]]. The multiplier must be non-zero, since multiplying by 0 destroys an equation. A swap of rows is the fix when leading entries are out of staircase order.

See [[Lec_10-Linear Systems and Gauss-Jordan Elimination|Lec 10]].
