One of the planes $z = c$, $x = c$ or $y = c$ used to slice a surface in 3D.

## When to use

Intersect the surface with a coordinate plane to get an ordinary curve. For $f(x,y) = x^2+y^2$, the plane $z=2$ gives a circle whose tangent comes from [[Implicit Differentiation]], and the plane $x=0$ gives the parabola $z = y^2$.

See [[Lec_04-Implicit Differentiation and Two-Variable Functions|Lec 04]].
