A line $x=a$ near which $f(x)\to\pm\infty$.

## When to use

Check the domain boundaries: $y=\ln^2x-x$ has one at $x=0$, while $x^2\ln x$ tends to a finite value at 0 and has none.

See [[Lec_03-Exponential and Logarithmic Limits and Asymptotes|Lec 03]].
