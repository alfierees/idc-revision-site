The assumed true mechanism, such as $y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1 x_i+u_i$, that produces the observed data from regressors and unobserved errors.

## When to use

Used when simulating samples to see an estimator's sampling distribution, and in the Lecture 4 argument for why each assumption on the error is needed.
