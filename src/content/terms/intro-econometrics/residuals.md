$\hat u_i=y_i-\hat y_i$, the gap between the observed and fitted values. Distinguish it from the unobservable error $u_i$, the gap from the true line.

## When to use

[[OLS Estimation]] makes the residuals sum to zero and be orthogonal to every regressor. Positive means the point lies above the line (the model under-predicted), as with the roughly $+38$ g residual in Assignment 1.

See [[Lec_13-Exam Review|Lecture 13]]. Worked in Assignment 1.
