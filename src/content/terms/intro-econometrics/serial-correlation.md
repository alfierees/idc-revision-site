Correlation between the errors of different observations. Assumption A3 rules it out: $\mathrm{Cov}(u_i,u_j)=0$ for all $i\neq j$.

## When to use

A3 and A4 are the extra assumptions, beyond unbiasedness, that give the variance formula and the Gauss-Markov result that OLS is BLUE.
