$t(\hat\beta_1)=(\hat\beta_1-b)/se(\hat\beta_1)$: how many standard errors the estimate is from the null value $b$.

## When to use

Step 2 of the t-test: take $\hat\beta_1$ and $se(\hat\beta_1)$ from the output and $b$ from the null, then compare with the critical value.

See [[Lec_06-Hypothesis Tests & p-values|Lec 6]].
