A function of the sample used to estimate a population parameter. Because the sample is random, the estimator is itself a random variable.

## When to use

Use it to distinguish $\hat\beta$ (a random variable with a sampling distribution) from the fixed true $\beta$ when judging unbiasedness and efficiency.

See [[Lec_02-Statistics Review & OLS Estimation|Lec 2]].
