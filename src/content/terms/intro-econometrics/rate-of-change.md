$(dy/dx)/y$: the proportional change in $y$ per one-unit change in $x$, which is the slope of a log-level model. A one-unit rise in $x$ raises $y$ by about $100\beta_1$ percent.

## When to use

Use it to interpret the slope when $\ln y$ is regressed on $x$, such as the returns to a year of education in the wage example.
