An estimator is efficient if it has the minimum variance among all unbiased estimators of the parameter.

## When to use

In the Lecture 4 sampling-distribution questions, among unbiased estimators the efficient one is the one with the narrowest curve; a biased estimator with lower variance does not count. Under A0 to A4, OLS is efficient among linear unbiased estimators (BLUE).
