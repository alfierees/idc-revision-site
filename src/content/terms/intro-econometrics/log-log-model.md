$\ln y$ regressed on $\ln x$: the slope is an [[Elasticity]], so a 1% rise in $x$ changes $y$ by $\hat\beta_1$ percent, with no multiplication by 100.

## When to use

Use it when you want an elasticity directly; it appears in the money-supply model of Assignment 2 and the housing model of Assignment 3.

See [[Lec_13-Exam Review|Lecture 13]]. Worked in Assignments 2 and 3.
