The shape chosen for the relationship (level, log or quadratic), which sets how a coefficient is interpreted: in level-level the slope is units per unit, in log-level a percent per unit, in log-log a percent per percent, in level-log units per percent.

## When to use

Questions ask you to pick a form and then read the slope correctly, as in the money-supply log-log model of Assignment 2. A wrong form is a kind of misspecification.
