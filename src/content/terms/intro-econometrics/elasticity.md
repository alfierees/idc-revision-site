The percentage change in $y$ for a 1% change in $x$, $\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}\cdot\frac{x}{y}$. In a log-log model it is simply the slope (no multiplication by 100); in a level-level model it is $\hat\beta_1 x/y$.

## When to use

Use it when interpreting log models or comparing responsiveness across groups; in the lecture, interactions with $\ln abv$ gave different elasticities for wine, spirits and beer.
