Random assignment of $x$, which makes $x$ independent of the error $u$ so that differences in outcomes can be read as causal.

## When to use

Lecture 1 uses it as the way to estimate causality, as in the health and drug example $Health=\beta_0+\beta_1 Drug+u$; without randomisation, confounders can bias the estimate.

See [[Lec_01-Introduction & Causal Inference|Lec 1]].
