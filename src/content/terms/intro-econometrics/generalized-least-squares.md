A method that transforms the model so the errors are homoskedastic; [[Weighted Least Squares]] is the special case with known variance weights.

## When to use

In this course it appears mainly as the general family that [[Weighted Least Squares]] belongs to under [[Heteroskedasticity]].

See [[Lec_12-Heteroskedasticity|Lecture 12]].
