How the intercept and slope change when a variable is rescaled or shifted. Multiplying $y$ by $c$ multiplies both coefficients by $c$; adding $c$ to $y$ adds $c$ to the intercept; multiplying $x$ by $c$ divides the slope by $c$; adding $c$ to $x$ changes the intercept by $-c\hat\beta_1$.

## When to use

To derive a new coefficient, rearrange the rescaled equation into $y=a+bx$ form and match terms. Standard errors scale with their coefficients, so t-statistics are unaffected; Assignment 3 rescales lot size to square metres.
