Assumption A5, $u_i\sim N(0,\sigma^2)$, which makes the OLS estimators normal and gives exact t and F tests.

## When to use

Lectures 5 and 6 add it on top of A1 to A4 to justify t-based confidence intervals and tests in small samples.

See [[Lec_05-Confidence Intervals & Change of Units|Lec 5]].
