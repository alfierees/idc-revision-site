A regression with one regressor, $y=\beta_0+\beta_1 x+u$, where $\beta_1$ is the effect of $x$ on $y$ and $u$ is everything else that affects $y$; it is fitted by [[OLS Estimation]].

## When to use

It is the starting model for reading R output: interpret the intercept as the prediction at $x=0$ and the slope as the change in predicted $y$ per unit of $x$, as in Assignment 1.

See [[Lec_13-Exam Review|Lecture 13]]. Worked in Assignment 1.
