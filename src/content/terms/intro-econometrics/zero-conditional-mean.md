Assumption A2, $E[u\mid x]=0$: the error is unrelated to the regressor, which implies $\mathrm{Cov}(x,u)=0$ and drives unbiasedness.

## When to use

Cite it (with A1) in the proof that OLS is unbiased; it fails when something correlated with $x$ is left in the error.

See [[Lec_04-OLS Estimator Properties|Lec 4]].
