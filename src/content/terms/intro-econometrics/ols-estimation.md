Ordinary least squares picks the coefficients that minimise the sum of squared [[Residuals]], $\sum\hat u_i^2$. In the simple model this gives $\hat\beta_1 = s_{xy}/s_{xx}$ and $\hat\beta_0 = \bar y - \hat\beta_1\bar x$.

## When to use

Questions ask you to derive it from the first-order conditions: the fitted line passes through the sample means, and the residuals sum to zero and are orthogonal to every regressor.
