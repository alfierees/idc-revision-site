The change in $y$ caused by a one-unit change in $x$ with everything else held fixed. It is a counterfactual quantity, so it is never directly observed in the data.

## When to use

Lecture 1 sets up the goal of econometrics as estimating this effect; use it to explain why a raw correlation is not enough and what a randomised experiment or regression tries to recover.
