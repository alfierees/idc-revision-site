Under assumptions A1 to A4 (mean-zero errors, exogeneity, no autocorrelation, homoskedasticity), [[OLS Estimation]] has the smallest variance of all linear unbiased estimators, so it is the best linear unbiased estimator (BLUE).

## When to use

Use it to say what is lost when an assumption fails: under [[Heteroskedasticity]] A4 fails, so OLS stays unbiased but is no longer efficient, which is why [[Weighted Least Squares]] can do better.
