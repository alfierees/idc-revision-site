The strength and sign of the linear association between two variables, between $-1$ and $1$.

## When to use

Do not confuse it with the slope: in Assignment 1 hours worked on schooling has slope $-0.859$ but correlation only $-0.046$, a weak and non-causal association. In R, `use = "complete.obs"` stops missing values breaking `cor()`.
