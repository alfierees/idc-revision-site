The expected squared distance from the mean, $\mathrm{Var}(X)=E[(X-E[X])^2]$. The variance of a sum needs covariance terms: $\mathrm{var}(x+y)$ equals the sum of variances only when the covariance is zero.

## When to use

Use it in the statistics review behind the variance of $\bar X$ and of the OLS estimator.

See [[Lec_02-Statistics Review & OLS Estimation|Lec 2]].
