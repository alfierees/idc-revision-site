The term $u$ that collects all the factors other than $x$ that affect $y$. It is unobserved, and OLS needs it to be uncorrelated with $x$ to work.

## When to use

Every OLS assumption (A1 to A5) is a statement about $u$. Do not confuse it with the residual $\hat u$, which is computed from the sample.
