A model of $y$ on $\ln x$: the slope divided by 100 is the change in $y$ (in its own units) for a 1% rise in $x$.

## When to use

Compare it with the log-level and log-log models in Lecture 3, where the variable carrying the log is read in percentages.

See [[Lec_03-Nonlinear Models with OLS|Lec 3]].
