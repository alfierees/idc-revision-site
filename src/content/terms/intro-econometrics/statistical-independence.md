Joint density equals the product of the marginals. It implies zero covariance, but zero covariance does not imply independence.

## When to use

Lecture 2 stresses that the variance of a sum equals the sum of variances only when the covariance is zero, which is why independent observations matter for the variance of $\bar X$ and $\hat\beta$.
