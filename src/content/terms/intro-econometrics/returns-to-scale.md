How output or cost scales with inputs. In the log-log cost model of Lecture 7, a coefficient on $\ln Y$ of about 0.8 (below 1) means increasing returns to scale: doubling output less than doubles cost, the slide's signature of a natural monopoly.

## When to use

Read the output or cost elasticity from the log-log regression, then test it against 1 to decide between increasing, constant and decreasing returns.

See [[Lec_07-Multiple Hypothesis Tests|Lec 7]].
