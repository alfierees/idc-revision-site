$\ln y$ regressed on $x$: a one-unit rise in $x$ changes $y$ by about $100\hat\beta_1$ percent.

## When to use

Use it when the outcome moves in percentages per unit of $x$; its marginal effect is $\hat\beta_1 y$ and its rate of change is $\hat\beta_1$. Compare with the [[Log-Log Model]], where the slope is an elasticity.
