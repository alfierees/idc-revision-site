A test that several coefficient restrictions hold at the same time. The alternative $H_1$ is true if any one of the restrictions fails.

## When to use

Use an F-test when a single t-test cannot answer the question, such as whether two coefficients are both zero or whether $\beta_3=\beta_4$, by building the restricted model and comparing the residual sums of squares.

See [[Lec_07-Multiple Hypothesis Tests|Lec 7]].
