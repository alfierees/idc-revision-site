The [[Chow Test]] generalised to three or more groups (beer, wine and spirits in the lecture), run as a joint [[F-test]] on a group dummy and all its interactions or as separate regressions.

## When to use

Use it to test whether several groups share one common regression function, and be careful counting $q$. In the lecture the interactions with $\ln abv$ gave different elasticities by type and the test rejected a common demand regression.

See [[Lec_11-Interaction Terms|Lecture 11]].
