Decomposing the price of a heterogeneous good into the implicit prices of its characteristics, by regressing price on those characteristics. The time coefficient then measures the price change of a constant-quality unit.

## When to use

The lecture's computer-price example is a worked [[Omitted Variable Bias]] case: regressing $\ln price$ on `YEAR` alone gives a positive insignificant trend because newer machines are better, and adding characteristic controls flips it to strongly negative.
