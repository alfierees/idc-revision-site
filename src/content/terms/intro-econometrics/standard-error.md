The square root of the estimated variance of an estimator, $se(\hat\beta)$; the yardstick for tests and confidence intervals.

## When to use

Read it from the regression output's Std. Error column and use it in $t=(\hat\beta-b)/se(\hat\beta)$ and in confidence intervals.

See [[Lec_05-Confidence Intervals & Change of Units|Lec 5]].
