Standard errors that stay valid under [[Heteroskedasticity]] (the White form); the coefficients are unchanged.

## When to use

Report them when you suspect non-constant error variance, so that t- and F-tests are valid. See [[White Correction]] for how they are built.

See [[Lec_12-Heteroskedasticity|Lecture 12]].
