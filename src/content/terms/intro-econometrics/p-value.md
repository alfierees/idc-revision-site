The probability, if the null were true, of a t-statistic at least as extreme as the one observed. It is the lowest significance level at which you would still reject, so reject when it is below the significance level.

## When to use

Read it from the R output for a significance test ($H_0:\beta_k=0$ against $\neq 0$); small $p$ means reject. In Assignment 4 a squared term with $p=0.104$ is not significant at 5%.
