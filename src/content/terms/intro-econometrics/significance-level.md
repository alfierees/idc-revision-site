$\alpha$, the probability of rejecting a true null. It is usually 5%, with 10% and 1% also checked.

## When to use

Pick it in step 3 of a test to find the critical value $t_{\alpha/2}[df]$, or compare it with the p-value (reject when $p<\alpha$).

See [[Lec_06-Hypothesis Tests & p-values|Lec 6]].
