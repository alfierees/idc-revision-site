The distribution of an estimator's values across repeated samples of size $N$. Unbiasedness, minimum variance and efficiency are all statements about this distribution.

## When to use

Use it to explain why an estimate varies from sample to sample, and as the starting point for judging an estimator's properties.

See [[Lec_04-OLS Estimator Properties|Lec 4]].
