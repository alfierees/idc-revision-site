The number of observations that are still free to vary once the parameters have been estimated; in OLS with $K$ regressors and an intercept it is $N-K-1$.

## When to use

It sets which t-distribution to use for critical values and confidence intervals, and the variance estimate divides by it. In F-tests, $q$ is the difference in degrees of freedom of the restricted and unrestricted models.
