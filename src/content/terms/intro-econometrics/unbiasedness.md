The mean of the estimator's sampling distribution equals the true parameter, $E[\hat\theta]=\theta$. For OLS, assumptions A1 and A2 deliver it.

## When to use

Use it when asked which assumptions make OLS unbiased, or to distinguish bias (centring) from variance (spread).

See [[Lec_04-OLS Estimator Properties|Lec 4]].
