$TSS=\sum (y_i-\bar y)^2$, the total variation in the outcome. OLS splits it as $TSS = MSS + RSS$ (explained plus residual variation).

## When to use

It is the denominator of [[R-squared]]: $R^2 = MSS/TSS = 1 - RSS/TSS$.

See [[Lec_08-Goodness of Fit & Multicollinearity|Lecture 8]].
