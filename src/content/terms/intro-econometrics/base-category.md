The category left out when you build dummies, where all the dummies equal 0 and whose mean the intercept captures. Every dummy coefficient is measured relative to it.

## When to use

When reading a [[Dummy Variable]] coefficient, say it is the difference from the base category (for example MIDDLE when LEFT and RIGHT are the dummies). Omitting one category is also how you avoid the [[Dummy Variable Trap]].
