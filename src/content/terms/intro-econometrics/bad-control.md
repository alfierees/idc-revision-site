A control that is itself affected by the variable of interest (a mediator), so holding it fixed removes part of the effect you want to measure and leaves only the direct effect.

## When to use

In Assignment 5, scoring (`points`) is a mediator between experience and wages, so including it makes the experience coefficient measure only the effect net of scoring. Ask whether a control is an outcome of your variable of interest before adding it.

Worked in Assignment 5.
