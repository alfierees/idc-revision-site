The distribution of an estimator standardised with the estimated variance; it has fatter tails than the normal and converges to it as $N$ grows.

## When to use

Look up critical values $t_{\alpha/2}[N-K-1]$ in the t-table when building confidence intervals or running t-tests.

See [[Lec_05-Confidence Intervals & Change of Units|Lec 5]].
