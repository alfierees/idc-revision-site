A measure of how two variables vary together, $\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)=E[XY]-E[X]E[Y]$; its sign shows whether they tend to move in the same or opposite directions.

## When to use

It appears in the OLS slope formula (the covariance of $x$ and $y$ over the variance of $x$) and in the iid assumption that distinct observations have zero covariance. Zero covariance does not by itself imply independence.
