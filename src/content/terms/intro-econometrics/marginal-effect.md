The change in $y$ from a one-unit change in $x$, $dy/dx$. It is constant in a level-level model ($\hat\beta_1$) and varies in other forms: $\hat\beta_1 y/x$ in log-log, $\hat\beta_1 y$ in log-level, $\beta_1+2\beta_2 x$ in a quadratic.

## When to use

Questions ask you to evaluate it at a given $x$ or $y$ in a [[Quadratic Model]], or to find the group-specific effect when an [[Interaction Term]] is present, since the slope then differs by group.
