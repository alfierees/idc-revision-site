$RSS=\sum\hat u_i^2$, the sum of squared OLS residuals. The unrestricted model always has $RSS_{UR}\le RSS_R$, and the F-test asks whether the rise in $RSS$ from imposing the restrictions is too large.

## When to use

Compute it for both the restricted and unrestricted regressions when carrying out a joint hypothesis test.
