The probability-weighted mean of a random variable, $E[X]$. It is linear, so $E[aX+b]=aE[X]+b$.

## When to use

Linearity is used constantly, for example to show an estimator is unbiased ($E[\hat\beta]=\beta$) and in the definitions of variance and covariance.
