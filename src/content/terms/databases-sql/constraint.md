A rule that dictates which values are allowed in a column or columns. The database enforces it automatically, rejecting an INSERT or UPDATE that would break it.

## When to use

PP 01 Q1: what a constraint is, two examples (UNIQUE and NOT NULL), and that it applies to column(s) only. PP 02 Q3.1 tabulates six constraint types and PP 04 Q2 adds three of them with ALTER TABLE.
