A date-and-time type converted to UTC on storage and back on read, with a range ending in January 2038.

## When to use

Use it for record times such as last_update, often with DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP. Taught in [[Lec_08-Constraints & Data Types|Lecture 8]].
