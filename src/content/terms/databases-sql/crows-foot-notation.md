An ERD notation that shows cardinality with line-end symbols: a circle for zero, a bar for one and a three-pronged foot for many.

## When to use

Use it as the symbol-based alternative to min-max labels when reading an ERD. Taught in [[Lec_02-Conceptual Entity-Relationship Modeling|Lecture 2]].
