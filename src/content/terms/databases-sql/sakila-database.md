MySQL's sample DVD-rental database (tables such as `film`, `inventory`, `rental`, `customer` and `payment`), used for the course's query exercises.

## When to use

Every query in PS 03 and PS 04 runs on it, and the study guide's examples use it too. Learn how its tables join (film to inventory to rental to customer) before writing the multi-table queries.

See [[PS_03-Sakila Aggregate Queries, Multi-Table JOINs & GROUP BY|PS 03]].
