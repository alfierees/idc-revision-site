A distinct type of thing the database stores data about (patient, car, order); it usually becomes a table. The reading warns the entity is the modelled thing and becomes a table only once translated into a logical schema.

## When to use

PP 01 Q2.3: entities are the distinct types of data in the system and relationships are how specific pairs of them relate. PS 01 and PS 02 ask for several entities, each with attributes.
