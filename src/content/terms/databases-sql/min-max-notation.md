Cardinality written as minimum..maximum next to each entity, such as 1..1 or 0..M.

## When to use

Use it when reading or drawing an ERD. The label next to an entity says how many of that entity relate to one row on the other side. Taught in [[Lec_02-Conceptual Entity-Relationship Modeling|Lecture 2]].
