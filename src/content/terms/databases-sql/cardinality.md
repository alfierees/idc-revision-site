How many rows on one side of a [[Relationship]] relate to rows on the other: 1:1, 1:N or M:N, for example one customer has many rentals.

## When to use

PP 03 Q3 asks which cardinality a join result shows, and PP 04 Q6 asks about composite versus surrogate keys on a many-to-many table. An M:N link needs a [[Junction Table]] holding two foreign keys.

See [[PP_04-Join Counts, Tour ERD, Constraints & Many-to-Many Keys (Moed B, undated)|PP 04]].
