A property of an [[Entity]] that describes it, such as `first_name` or `rental_rate`. It becomes a column when the model is turned into a table.

## When to use

PS 01 and PS 02 require every entity to have several attributes, each with a [[Data Type]] and, in PS 02, constraints. The reading warns that 'attribute' is loose wording: use the instructor's terms in the exam.

See [[Read_01-SQL Keywords, Concepts & Exam Tips|Read 01]].
