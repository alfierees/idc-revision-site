A primary key based on real data, such as a username or SSN; it is tightly coupled to the business domain.

## When to use

The course says to avoid these, because a change in business rules forces painful changes to the PK and every FK pointing to it. Taught in [[Lec_03-From Conceptual to Logical Models|Lecture 3]].
