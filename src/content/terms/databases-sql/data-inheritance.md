An "is a" 1-to-1 relationship where a child entity inherits the parent's attributes; at the logical level the child's PK is also an FK to the parent.

## When to use

Use it for cases like a Salesperson being an Employee being a Person. Taught in [[Lec_03-From Conceptual to Logical Models|Lecture 3]].
