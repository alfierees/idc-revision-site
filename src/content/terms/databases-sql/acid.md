ACID stands for Atomicity (all or nothing), Consistency (data stays valid), Isolation (transactions do not interfere) and Durability (changes are permanent after a [[COMMIT]]). It lists the guarantees of a [[Transaction]].

## When to use

Appears in the reading's transactions section, where a transfer between two accounts either fully completes or is rolled back. No past-paper question tests it, so expect it as a definition to recall.
