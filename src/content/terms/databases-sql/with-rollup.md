A GROUP BY modifier that adds sub-total rows for each group plus a grand total.

## When to use

Use it for summary reports. Rollup rows show NULL in the totalled columns. Taught in [[Lec_12-WITH ROLLUP, CTEs & Indexes|Lecture 12]].
