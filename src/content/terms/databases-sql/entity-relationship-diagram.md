A diagram of the [[Entity|entities]] in a system and the [[Relationship|relationships]] between them; the step before turning the design into tables. The Entity-Relationship Model is the deliverable and the ERD is its usual visual form.

## When to use

PP 01 Q2: the second step of the three-step design process is Entity-Relationship Modelling, with entities and relationships as its two main elements. PP 03 and PP 04 turn an ERD into tables; PS 01 and PS 02 draw conceptual and logical ERDs.
