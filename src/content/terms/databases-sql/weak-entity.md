An entity that cannot be identified without its owner entity; its key includes the owner's key.

## When to use

PS 01 (hospital patient system) tests recognising a weak entity when drawing the ERD, alongside turning a many-to-many relationship into a table.

See [[PS_01-Hospital Patient System ERD|PS 01]].
