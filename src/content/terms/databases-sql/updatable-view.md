A view whose rows map 1-to-1 to rows of the underlying table, so INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE through it change the real table.

## When to use

The course lists aggregates, DISTINCT, GROUP BY, JOIN and UNION as making a view non-updatable. Taught in [[Lec_13-Indexes, Views & NoSQL|Lecture 13]].
