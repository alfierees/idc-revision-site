Man-made "rules of the game" (property rights, contract enforcement, rule of law, equal opportunity) that shape incentives to invest. Inclusive institutions give broad access to resources; extractive ones concentrate resources and power in an elite. Cross-country correlations with GDP are suggestive but do not by themselves prove causality.
