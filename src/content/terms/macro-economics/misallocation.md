Resources not sitting where they are most productive. Output is maximized when the marginal products of mobile factors are equal across firms ($MPN_1 = MPN_2$); with constant returns only the highest-TFP firms operate, with diminishing returns all firms operate and more productive ones are larger. Dispersion of marginal products signals misallocation and lowers aggregate [[Total Factor Productivity]].
