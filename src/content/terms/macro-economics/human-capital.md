The skill or quality of an hour of work, $h$, entering as $Y = AK^{\alpha}(hN)^{1-\alpha}$. If it is ignored, measured [[Total Factor Productivity]] absorbs the human-capital differences, so measured TFP growth overstates true TFP growth when $h$ rises.

See [[Lec_11-Productivity|Lec 11]].
