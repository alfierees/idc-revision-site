Output per unit of a single factor, $Y/N$ for labor and $Y/K$ for capital. Easy to measure, but each depends on the quantity of the other factor, so it can rank two plants with identical technology differently (unlike [[Total Factor Productivity]]).

See [[Lec_11-Productivity|Lec 11]].
