Changes in aggregate demand affect output and unemployment only in the short run; in the long run the economy returns to $\bar{Y}$ and $u_n$.

## When to use

Use it as the baseline assumption of the AS-AD and Phillips analysis, which hysteresis challenges.

See [[Lec_14-Aggregate Supply & the Phillips Curve|Lec 14]].
