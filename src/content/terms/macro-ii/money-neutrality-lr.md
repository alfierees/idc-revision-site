In the long run a change in the money supply moves only the price level: after the short-run boom, prices rise in proportion to $M$ and $Y$, $r$, $I$ and $C$ return to their original values.

## When to use

Use it as the long-run end of a monetary-expansion question in AD-AS: the short-run effect on $Y$ is temporary and the long-run effect is purely on $P$.
