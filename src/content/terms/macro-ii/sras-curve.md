The equation $Y = \bar{Y} + \alpha(P - EP)$ with $\alpha = s/((1-s)a)$: output exceeds its natural level only when the price level is above expectations. The curve shifts up when $EP$ rises.

## When to use

Use it to read price surprises: causation runs from unexpected prices to output, not from higher prices to higher output. The Phillips curve is this curve rewritten in unemployment and inflation.
