The capital per worker $k^*$ at which saving equals break-even investment, $sf(k) = (\delta+n+g)k$. Then $k$ and $y$ are constant (per effective worker), total output grows at $n+g$ and output per worker grows at $g$.

## When to use

Use it to compute steady-state income per worker (for example $y = k^{1/3}$ with $s = 10\%$, $\delta = 20\%$) and growth rates of $Y$, $Y/L$ and $Y/(LE)$.

Not covered by a lecture deck; appears on past papers.
