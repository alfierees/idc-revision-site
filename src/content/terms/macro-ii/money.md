Money is the stock of assets that can be readily used to make transactions. It serves as a medium of exchange, a store of value and a unit of account, and it solves the double coincidence of wants problem of barter.

## When to use

Use it for definitional questions on what counts as money and its three functions, before moving on to how the supply is measured and controlled.

See [[Lec_04-The Monetary System|Lec 04]].
