IS-LM for a small open economy with perfect capital mobility and fixed prices. The world interest rate pins $r = r^*$, so the model is drawn with IS* and LM* in $(Y, e)$ space.

## When to use

Use it for policy-effectiveness questions: with a floating rate monetary policy works and fiscal policy does not; with a fixed rate it is the reverse.
