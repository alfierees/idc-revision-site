The central bank's control over the money supply (or the interest rate) to influence prices, output and employment. Expansion shifts LM right.

## When to use

Use it as the LM-curve shifter in IS-LM, and in the open economy as the lever that works under floating rates but not under fixed rates.

See [[Lec_08-Aggregate Demand II & Applying the IS-LM Model|Lec 08]].
