A growth model in which capital per worker accumulates through saving $sf(k)$ and is eroded by break-even investment. Saving and population growth change the level of income per worker; only technological progress changes its long-run growth.

## When to use

Use it for the Solow block of the exam (Q1 to Q7 on the sample paper): find $k^*$ where $sf(k) = (\delta+n+g)k$, then ask what changes the level of income versus its growth rate.

Not covered by a lecture deck; appears on past papers.
