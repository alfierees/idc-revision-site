The short-run inflation-unemployment trade-off, $\pi = E\pi - \beta(u - u_n) + \nu$: inflation depends on expected inflation, cyclical unemployment and supply shocks. It is the SRAS curve rewritten via Okun's law.

## When to use

Use it to find inflation from $E\pi$ and the unemployment gap, and to see what shifts it (higher $E\pi$ or an adverse supply shock shifts it up). The long-run curve is vertical at $u_n$.
