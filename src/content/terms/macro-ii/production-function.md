The recipe $Y = F(K, L)$ that turns capital and labour into output. In the classical model, with constant returns to scale and fixed inputs, it pins output at $Y^* = F(\bar{K}, \bar{L})$, so GDP is supply-determined.

## When to use

Use it to show that demand-side policy cannot change long-run output, and as the starting point for factor prices via the [[Marginal Product]] of each input.

See [[Lec_03-Classical Theory & National Income|Lec 03]].
