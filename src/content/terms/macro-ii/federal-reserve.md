The US central bank. It controls the monetary base through open market operations (its preferred tool), the discount rate, reserve requirements and interest on reserves.

## When to use

Use it as the actor in policy questions: the Fed accommodates or resists fiscal expansion by moving $M$, and cases such as the Volcker disinflation and 2008 QE describe its choices.
