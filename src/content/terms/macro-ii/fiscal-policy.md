Government choices on spending $G$ and taxes $T$. Higher $G$ or lower $T$ shifts IS right, with a tax change shifting it by less than an equal change in $G$.

## When to use

Use it as the IS-curve shifter: pair it with the multiplier for the size of the shift and crowding out for the net effect on $Y$.
