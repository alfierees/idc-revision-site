Labour in efficiency units, $L \times E$ (workers times labour-augmenting technology). Variables per effective worker, $k = K/(LE)$ and $y = Y/(LE)$, are constant in the steady state.

## When to use

Use it to distinguish growth rates: $Y/(LE)$ grows at 0, $Y/L$ at $g$ and $Y$ at $n+g$ in the steady state.

Not covered by a lecture deck; appears on past papers.
