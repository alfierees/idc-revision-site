$S = Y - C - G$, the sum of private saving $Y - T - C$ and public saving $T - G$. In the closed classical economy it is the supply of loanable funds and equals investment, $S = I(r)$.

## When to use

Use it to trace fiscal policy: higher $G$ or lower $T$ cuts national saving, shifts the saving curve left, raises $r$ and crowds out investment (the Reagan deficits case study).
