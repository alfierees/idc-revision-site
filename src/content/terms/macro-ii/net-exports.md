Exports minus imports, $NX$. It falls when the exchange rate rises (a stronger domestic currency makes domestic goods dearer abroad and foreign goods cheaper at home).

## When to use

Use it as the channel in Mundell-Fleming: appreciation crowds out $NX$ and leaves $Y$ unchanged under floating rates. Also $NX = Y - (C + I + G)$, so $NX$ cannot change if $Y$ and the other components do not.
