The central bank holds banks' reserves on deposit and sits behind the banking system that creates money, where $M = C + D$ (currency plus demand deposits). Reserves are the part of deposits banks have not lent out, and the central bank's control over them is how it influences the money supply.

## When to use

Use it when explaining who controls reserves and the monetary base in the money creation process.

See [[Lec_04-The Monetary System|Lec 04]].
