An event that shifts the AD curve, such as a change in $M$, $V$, $G$ or consumer confidence. Output and prices move in the same direction.

## When to use

Use it to read off short- versus long-run effects: with sticky prices $Y$ moves first, then prices adjust until $Y$ returns to $\bar{Y}$ and only $P$ has changed.
