A regime in which the central bank commits to a rate $\bar{e}$ and buys or sells domestic currency to defend it, so it loses control of $M$. Fiscal policy then works (the central bank accommodates) and monetary policy is ineffective.

## When to use

Use it to answer what the central bank must do to defend the peg: if $e$ tends to fall it buys domestic currency and $M$ shrinks, if $e$ tends to rise it sells and $M$ grows.
