The gap between actual and planned expenditure when $Y$ is off its equilibrium level: goods produced but unsold pile up (or stocks run down), which signals firms to change output.

## When to use

Use it to read a Keynesian-cross diagram: a positive gap means $Y > PE$ so firms cut production; a negative gap means they raise it. Equilibrium has no unplanned inventory change.
