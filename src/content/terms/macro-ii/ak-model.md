An endogenous growth model with $y = Ak$: there are no diminishing returns to capital, so capital and income grow at the constant rate $sA - \delta$. The saving rate therefore changes the growth rate itself, not just the level.

## When to use

Use it as the contrast with Solow: in Solow a higher $s$ raises only the level, in the AK model it raises long-run growth.

Not covered by a lecture deck; appears on past papers.
