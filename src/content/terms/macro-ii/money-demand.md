Demand for real money balances, $(M/P)^d = L(i, Y)$: it falls with the nominal interest rate (the opportunity cost of holding money) and rises with income. The simple quantity-theory version is $(M/P)^d = kY$.

## When to use

Use it as the demand side of the money market: it determines $P$ in the classical model and, with the fixed real money supply, gives the LM curve in the short-run IS-LM model.
