The monetary tightening begun in late 1979 by Fed Chair Paul Volcker to end inflation above 10%. Interest rates rose sharply (10.4% to 15.8%) and a deep recession followed, then inflation fell.

## When to use

Use it as the worked case for liquidity preference (short run, $M \downarrow \Rightarrow r \uparrow$) and the Fisher effect (long run, lower $\pi \Rightarrow$ lower $i$), and for the sacrifice-ratio calculation.
