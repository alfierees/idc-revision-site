The extra output from one more unit of an input. A competitive firm hires each factor until its marginal product equals its real price, so $\text{MPL} = W/P$ (the real wage) and $\text{MPK} = R/P$ (the real rental price of capital).

## When to use

Use it to find factor prices and income shares; under constant returns to scale $Y = \text{MPL} \cdot L + \text{MPK} \cdot K$, so output is exactly exhausted by paying factors their marginal products.
