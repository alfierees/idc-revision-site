The possibility that a temporary downturn raises the natural rate of unemployment permanently, through skill atrophy or insider-outsider dynamics.

## When to use

Use it as the challenge to the natural-rate hypothesis; if it holds, deep recessions have lasting costs, which strengthens the case for stabilisation policy.
