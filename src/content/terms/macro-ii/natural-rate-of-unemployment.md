The rate $u_n$ toward which unemployment gravitates in the long run, at which inflation equals expected inflation. Full employment means $u = u_n$, not zero unemployment.

## When to use

Use it as the reference in the Phillips curve gap $u - u_n$; a higher $u_n$ shifts the short-run Phillips curve right.
