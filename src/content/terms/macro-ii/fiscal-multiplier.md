The change in income per unit change in government spending: $\Delta Y/\Delta G = 1/(1-\text{MPC})$ in the Keynesian cross (5 when $\text{MPC} = 0.8$). The tax multiplier is $-\text{MPC}/(1-\text{MPC})$, smaller in size because part of a tax cut is saved.

## When to use

Use the Keynesian-cross value when $r$ is fixed. In IS-LM the actual multiplier is smaller, because higher income raises $r$ and crowds out investment.
