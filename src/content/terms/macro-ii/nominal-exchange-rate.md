The exchange rate $e$. In this course (Lec 09 and the past-paper solutions) it is units of foreign currency per unit of domestic currency, so a higher $e$ means a stronger domestic currency.

## When to use

Use it as the vertical axis of the IS* and LM* diagram: a rise is an appreciation and a fall is a depreciation.

See [[Lec_09-The Open Economy Revisited & Mundell-Fleming Model|Lec 09]].
