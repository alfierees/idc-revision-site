Disinflation is a fall in the inflation rate. If a credible announcement lowers expected inflation immediately, the Phillips curve says unemployment need not rise above $u_n$, so there is no sacrifice. Without full credibility, adaptive expectations dominate and the sacrifice ratio stays large.

## When to use

Use it to compare costly and painless ways of reducing inflation, with Volcker as the partial-credibility case.
