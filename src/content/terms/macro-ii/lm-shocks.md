Exogenous changes in money demand (or money supply) that move the LM curve, such as credit card fraud raising cash use or more ATMs lowering it. They move $r$ and $Y$ in opposite directions.

## When to use

Use it to contrast with an IS shock: a rise in money demand shifts LM left, so $Y$ falls and $r$ rises.
