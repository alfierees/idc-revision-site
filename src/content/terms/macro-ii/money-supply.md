The quantity of money available in the economy, measured as M1 (currency plus demand and other checkable deposits) or M2 (M1 plus less liquid deposits and funds). It is set jointly by the central bank, the banking system and households through $M = C + D$.

## When to use

Use $M = m \cdot B$ to see how base money, the reserve-deposit ratio and the currency-deposit ratio together determine $M$; the money supply is then the exogenous policy variable in the LM curve and the quantity equation.
