An event that shifts SRAS, such as an oil price spike, new regulation, wage rises, drought or a technology gain. An adverse shock shifts SRAS up, raising prices and cutting output.

## When to use

Use it for policy-dilemma questions: do nothing, accommodate with higher $M$ (output restored, prices permanently higher), or fight inflation (deeper recession). The 1970s oil shocks are the lecture's example.
