The interest rate as quoted, not adjusted for inflation: $i = r + E\pi$. It is the opportunity cost of holding money, so money demand falls as it rises.

## When to use

Use it in the money-demand function $L(i, Y)$ and the Fisher equation; higher expected inflation raises $i$, which lowers money demand and raises $P$ immediately.

See [[Lec_05-Inflation|Lec 05]].
