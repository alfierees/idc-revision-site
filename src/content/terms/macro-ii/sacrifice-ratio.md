The percentage of one year's real GDP lost per percentage point reduction in inflation. A typical estimate is about 5.

## When to use

Use it to cost a disinflation: in the Volcker 1981 to 1985 case 9.5 points of cyclical unemployment give a 19% GDP loss over a 6.7 point fall in inflation, a ratio of about 2.8.
