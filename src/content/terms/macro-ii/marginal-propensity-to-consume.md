The fraction of each extra unit of disposable income that is spent on consumption, with $0 < \text{MPC} < 1$ in $C = A + \text{MPC}\,(Y-T)$. It sets the size of the Keynesian-cross multiplier $1/(1-\text{MPC})$.

## When to use

Use it in every multiplier question: the spending multiplier is $1/(1-\text{MPC})$, the tax multiplier is $-\text{MPC}/(1-\text{MPC})$, and the part of a tax cut that is not spent ($1-\text{MPC}$) is saved.
