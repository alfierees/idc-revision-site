$NCO = S - I$: domestic saving not invested at home and lent abroad, which also equals $NX$. A fall in $r$ raises it, increasing the supply of domestic currency and lowering the exchange rate. Lec 09 writes it as $CF(r)$.

## When to use

Use it in large-open-economy questions: a monetary expansion lowers $r$, raises NCO and depreciates the currency, while $Y$, NCO and $NX$ rise.
