A system in which banks keep only a fraction of deposits as reserves and lend out the rest, which creates money. It creates new money but not new wealth, because each new deposit comes with an equal new debt.

## When to use

Use the three-scenario logic (no banks, 100%-reserve banking, fractional reserves): only fractional reserves change the money supply, to $\frac{1}{rr}$ times the original deposit.
