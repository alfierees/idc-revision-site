Under floating rates an import restriction such as a tariff or quota shifts IS* right. The exchange rate appreciates, output is unchanged and net exports are unchanged, because the appreciation exactly offsets the restriction.

## When to use

Use it to show that import restrictions do not fix a trade deficit: trade shrinks on both sides but $NX$ and $Y$ stay put.
