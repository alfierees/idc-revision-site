Higher prices together with lower output (and higher unemployment), typical of an adverse supply shock that shifts SRAS up. The 1970s oil shocks are the lecture's example.

## When to use

Use it to recognise the signature of a supply shock as opposed to a demand shock, where prices and output move the same way.
