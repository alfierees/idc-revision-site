Under adaptive expectations, past inflation shapes expectations, which shape wages and prices, which determine current inflation, so inflation perpetuates itself absent supply shocks or cyclical unemployment.

## When to use

Use it to explain why announcing a lower target is not enough: you need slack ($u > u_n$) or a credible change in expectations.
