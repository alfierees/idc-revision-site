An economy big enough to move the world interest rate, so $r$ is endogenous and not pinned at $r^*$. It behaves like an average of the closed and small open economies.

## When to use

Use the rule of thumb: after a fiscal expansion $r$ moves as in a closed economy, $e$ moves as in a small open economy (appreciation) and $Y$ is in between.
