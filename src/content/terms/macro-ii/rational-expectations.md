People use all available information, including announced policy, when forming expectations, so credible policy can change $E\pi$ immediately rather than waiting for past inflation to fall.

## When to use

Use it to explain a lower-than-expected sacrifice ratio (Volcker) and to set up the painless-disinflation argument. It also underlies the exam-style question of whether announced future money growth affects prices today.
