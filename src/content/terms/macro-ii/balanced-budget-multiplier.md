Raising $G$ and $T$ by the same amount does not leave output unchanged: in the Keynesian cross income rises by exactly that amount, a multiplier of 1, because the spending multiplier outweighs the tax multiplier.

## When to use

Use it as the answer to balanced-budget questions in the Keynesian cross. In IS-LM the rise is smaller because $r$ rises.
