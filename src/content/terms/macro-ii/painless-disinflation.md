The claim that under rational expectations a fully credible disinflation plan lowers $E\pi$ at once, so inflation falls with no output or employment loss. It needs perfect credibility; the evidence is mixed.

## When to use

Use it as the optimistic counterpart to adaptive expectations: the Volcker sacrifice ratio was below the historical average but not zero.
