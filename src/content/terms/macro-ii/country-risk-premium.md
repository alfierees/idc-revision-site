The extra return $\theta$ that lenders demand on a country's assets to compensate for default risk, so $r = r^* + \theta$. A rise in $\theta$ shifts IS* left and LM* right and lowers $e$.

## When to use

Use it for capital-flight questions: a higher $\theta$ depreciates the currency; the model says $Y$ rises, though in practice policy and money-demand responses can offset that.
