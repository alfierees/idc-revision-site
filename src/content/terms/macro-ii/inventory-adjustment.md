The mechanism that moves the Keynesian-cross economy to equilibrium: if $PE > Y$ inventories fall below plan and firms raise production, and if $PE < Y$ inventories pile up and firms cut production.

## When to use

Use it to explain why output converges on $Y = PE$ when prices are fixed, and to say which way output moves when it is off equilibrium.
