The effect of fiscal expansion depends on how the Fed responds. Holding $M$ constant gives the standard IS-LM result; holding $r$ constant (raising $M$) gives the full Keynesian multiplier; holding $Y$ constant (cutting $M$) gives complete crowding out.

## When to use

Use it for questions that fix one variable while the other instrument moves: pick the response, shift LM accordingly and compare $Y$ and $r$.
