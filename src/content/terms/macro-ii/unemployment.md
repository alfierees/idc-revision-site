Via Okun's Law, output below potential corresponds to unemployment above its natural rate $u_n$. Combining this with the SRAS curve gives the Phillips curve $\pi = E\pi - \beta(u - u_n) + \nu$, an inflation-unemployment tradeoff.

## When to use

Use it for questions linking the unemployment gap to inflation, or to the output gap through Okun's Law.

See [[Lec_14-Aggregate Supply & the Phillips Curve|Lec 14]].
