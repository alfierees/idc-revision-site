The separation of real and nominal variables in the classical model: real variables ($Y$, $r$) are determined by the production function and the loanable funds market, then the money market pins down nominal variables ($P$, $i$).

## When to use

Use it to decide which equation determines which variable: $Y = F(K,L)$ gives $Y$, $S = I$ gives $r$, $M/P = L(r + E\pi, Y)$ gives $P$ and the Fisher equation gives $i$.
