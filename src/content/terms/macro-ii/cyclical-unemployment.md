The deviation of unemployment from its natural rate over the cycle, $u - u_n$. It is the term that links inflation to the output gap in the Phillips curve.

## When to use

Use it to compute output losses via Okun's law and inflation changes via the Phillips curve.

See [[Lec_14-Aggregate Supply & the Phillips Curve|Lec 14]].
