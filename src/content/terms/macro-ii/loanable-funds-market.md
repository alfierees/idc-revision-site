The market in which national saving (supply) meets investment $I(r)$ (demand), with the real interest rate $r$ as the equilibrating price. Because $S = I$ is equivalent to $Y = C + I + G$, it clears the goods market at the same time.

## When to use

Use it to find the effect of a policy or shock on $r$ and $I$ in the long-run classical model: shift the saving curve (fiscal policy, saving preferences) or the investment curve (technology, investment tax credit) and read off the new $r^*$.
