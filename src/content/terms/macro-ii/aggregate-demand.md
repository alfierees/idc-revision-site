The relationship between the price level and the quantity of output demanded. In Lec 06 it comes from $Y = MV/P$ and slopes down because a higher $P$ lowers real balances $M/P$.

## When to use

Use it in AS-AD questions: a rise in $M$ or $V$, or higher $G$, shifts AD right; a fall in $V$ or in confidence shifts it left. Lec 08 later derives the same curve from IS-LM.
