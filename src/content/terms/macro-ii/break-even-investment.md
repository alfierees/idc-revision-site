The investment needed to keep capital per worker constant, $(\delta+n+g)k$: depreciation of existing capital plus equipping new workers and rising efficiency. A higher $n$ rotates the line up and lowers $k^*$.

## When to use

Use it as the line crossed by $sf(k)$ in the Solow diagram; at the steady state saving per unit of capital equals $\delta$ when $n = g = 0$.

Not covered by a lecture deck; appears on past papers.
