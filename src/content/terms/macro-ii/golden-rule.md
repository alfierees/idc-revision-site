The saving rate and capital stock that maximise steady-state consumption per worker: $\text{MPK} = \delta$, or $\text{MPK} - \delta = n+g$ with growth. With Cobb-Douglas $f(k) = k^\alpha$ the golden-rule saving rate equals the capital share $\alpha$.

## When to use

Use it to say whether to save more or less: if $\text{MPK} - \delta$ exceeds $n+g$ there is too little capital, so raise $s$; if below, lower $s$.
