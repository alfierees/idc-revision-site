The pair $(Y^*, r^*)$ at which the IS and LM curves intersect, so the goods market and the money market clear together while prices are fixed.

## When to use

Use it as the starting point for any IS-LM question: shift IS or LM, find the new intersection and compare. Varying $P$ then traces out the AD curve.
