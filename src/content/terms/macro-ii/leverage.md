The ratio of a bank's assets to its capital, $\text{Assets}/\text{Capital}$ (20 in the lecture example). A high ratio magnifies both gains and losses.

## When to use

Use it to see how small shocks threaten banks: at a leverage ratio of 20, a 5% fall in asset values leaves the bank insolvent. This is why the 2008 to 2009 crisis cut bank lending.
