Derived from IS-LM by varying the price level: a higher $P$ lowers $M/P$, shifts LM left, raises $r$, cuts investment and lowers $Y$. The wealth effect is a secondary channel.

## When to use

Use it to separate a movement along AD (caused by $P$) from a shift of AD (caused by policy or shocks at a given $P$). In the open economy the channel runs through the real exchange rate and net exports instead.
