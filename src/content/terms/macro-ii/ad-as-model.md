Combines the IS-LM-derived AD curve with SRAS and LRAS to trace the short-run to long-run adjustment. When $Y \neq \bar{Y}$, prices drift until output returns to $\bar{Y}$.

## When to use

Use it for questions that ask for both the short-run and the long-run effect of a shock or policy: output changes first, then prices adjust and only $P$ is permanently different.
