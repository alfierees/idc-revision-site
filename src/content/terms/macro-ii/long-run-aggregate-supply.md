A vertical line at the natural level of output $\bar{Y} = F(\bar{K}, \bar{L})$, set by capital, labour and technology and not by the price level.

## When to use

Use it for long-run results: AD shifts change only $P$, not $Y$. Full employment means unemployment at its natural rate, not zero.
