The empirical negative link between output growth and the change in unemployment, $\%\Delta Y \approx 3 - 2\,\Delta u$ in the lecture's rule of thumb. A 1 point rise in unemployment goes with output growth about 2 points below trend.

## When to use

Use it to convert between the output gap and unemployment: in Lec 14 it turns SRAS into the Phillips curve, and in the Volcker case it converts 9.5 points of cyclical unemployment into a 19% GDP loss.
