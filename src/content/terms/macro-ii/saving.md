Saving comes in three forms: private saving $Y - T - C$, public saving $T - G$, and national saving $S = Y - C - G$, their sum. In a closed economy national saving equals investment, with the real interest rate adjusting to make them equal.

## When to use

Use it to split national saving into private and public parts, for example when a deficit lowers public saving and shifts the supply of loanable funds.
