The specific production function $Y = A K^\alpha L^{1-\alpha}$ with $0 < \alpha < 1$ and constant returns to scale. Capital earns a constant share $\alpha$ of income and labour earns $1-\alpha$ (about 70% for labour in the US, so $\alpha \approx 0.3$).

## When to use

Use it to compute factor incomes directly: $\text{MPL} = (1-\alpha)Y/L$ and $\text{MPK} = \alpha Y/K$. Past papers also use it to read off the golden-rule saving rate, which equals $\alpha$.
