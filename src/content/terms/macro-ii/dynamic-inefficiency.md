Capital per worker above the golden-rule level ($\text{MPK}$ below $\delta+n+g$), meaning the economy over-saves. Steady-state consumption per worker would be higher with a lower saving rate.

## When to use

Use it for the question where $\text{MPK} < \delta$ with $n = g = 0$: the answer is to lower the saving rate, not to change depreciation.

Not covered by a lecture deck; appears on past papers.
