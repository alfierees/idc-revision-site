In the classical model a competitive firm hires labour and rents capital until each factor's marginal product equals its real price, so $\text{MPL} = W/P$ and $\text{MPK} = R/P$. Total output is therefore distributed to factors according to their marginal products.

## When to use

Use it when asked how output is divided between labour and capital, or why factors are hired up to the point where marginal product equals real price.
