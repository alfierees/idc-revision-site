Exogenous shifts in the demand for goods that move the IS curve, such as stock market booms or crashes, business optimism or consumer confidence. They move $r$ and $Y$ in the same direction.

## When to use

Use it to analyse events like a housing crash or the 2001 recession (dot-com crash, 9/11, accounting scandals), where IS shifts left and both $Y$ and $r$ fall.
