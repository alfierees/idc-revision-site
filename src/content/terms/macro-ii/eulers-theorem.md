For a production function with constant returns to scale, $Y = MPL \cdot L + MPK \cdot K$. If competitive firms pay each factor its marginal product, total factor payments exactly **exhaust output**, so economic profit is zero.

## When to use

Use it for questions on how output is split between labour and capital: labour's share is $MPL \cdot L / Y$ and capital's share is $MPK \cdot K / Y$, and the two sum to 1. In the Solow steady state both shares are constant.
