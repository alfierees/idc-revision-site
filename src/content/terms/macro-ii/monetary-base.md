Currency in circulation plus bank reserves, $B = C + R$: the part of the money supply the central bank controls directly. It is the base that the money multiplier scales up into the money supply.

## When to use

Use it as the Fed's direct lever in $M = m \cdot B$; open market operations and the discount rate move $B$, while reserve requirements and interest on reserves move the multiplier.
