The simplest model of output with fixed prices: planned expenditure $PE = C + I + G$ is plotted against income, and equilibrium is where $Y = PE$ (the 45 degree line). Firms adjust output until planned spending equals production.

## When to use

Use it to derive the multipliers ($1/(1-\text{MPC})$ for $G$, $-\text{MPC}/(1-\text{MPC})$ for $T$). Letting investment depend on $r$ turns it into the IS curve.
