Prices adjust slowly to demand in the short run, so output moves instead of prices. Lec 06 gives menu costs, customer relations, wage contracts and social norms as reasons; the stickiness fades over a longer horizon.

## When to use

Use it to explain why demand shocks and policy move real output in the short run but not the long run, which is the difference between the sticky-price and classical models.

See [[Lec_06-AS-AD Model & Economic Fluctuations|Lec 06]].
