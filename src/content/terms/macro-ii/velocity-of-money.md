$V = PY/M$, the rate at which the average unit of currency changes hands, using nominal GDP as a proxy for transactions. If money demand is $(M/P)^d = kY$ then $V = 1/k$, so velocity is constant when $k$ is exogenous.

## When to use

Use it to move between the quantity equation and money demand. A fall in $V$ (for example cash hoarding in a crisis) shifts AD left in the short-run model.
