Monetary or fiscal action meant to reduce the severity of short-run fluctuations around trend. Lags and the difficulty of telling supply from demand shocks make it hard in practice.

## When to use

Use it in questions on how to respond to a shock: the choice between waiting for prices to adjust and shifting AD trades speed of recovery against the price level.
