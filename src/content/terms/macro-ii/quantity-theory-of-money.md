The quantity equation $MV = PY$ is an identity; it becomes a theory when velocity $V$ is held constant. Then $\pi = \Delta M/M - \Delta Y/Y$: money growth above real output growth translates one for one into inflation.

## When to use

Use the growth-rate form for inflation questions: if $M$ grows 5% and $Y$ grows 2% with constant $V$, inflation is 3%. It is a long-run theory; in the short run velocity can shift.
