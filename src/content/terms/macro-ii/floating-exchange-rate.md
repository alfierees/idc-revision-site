A regime in which the market sets $e$ and the central bank controls $M$. Fiscal expansion and import restrictions raise $e$ and leave $Y$ unchanged (LM* is vertical), while monetary expansion lowers $e$ and raises $Y$.

## When to use

Use it for Mundell-Fleming policy questions: fiscal and trade policy move only $e$ (and net exports), monetary policy moves $Y$ through $M \uparrow \to e \downarrow \to NX \uparrow$.
