Investment is a decreasing function of the real interest rate, $I = I(r)$. The real rate is the real cost of borrowing, so firms undertake a project only if its expected return exceeds $r$, and a higher $r$ kills marginal projects.

## When to use

Use it as the demand side of the loanable funds market, where $I(r)$ meets national saving to determine $r^*$.
