In the open-economy AD curve, a higher price level lowers real balances $M/P$, shifts LM* left and raises the real exchange rate $\varepsilon$, which cuts net exports and so output. The downward slope operates through net exports rather than investment, as in the closed economy.

## When to use

Use it for the small open economy with perfect capital mobility, where the world interest rate $r^*$ fixes the domestic rate.
