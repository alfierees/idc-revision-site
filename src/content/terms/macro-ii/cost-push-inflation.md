Inflation that comes from adverse supply shocks raising costs (oil, regulation, union wages). It enters the Phillips curve as $\nu > 0$ and shifts it up, with no change in unemployment.

## When to use

Use it to tell apart the source of inflation: cost-push comes from the $\nu$ term, demand-pull from unemployment below $u_n$.
