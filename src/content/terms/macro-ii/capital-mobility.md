The ability of funds to flow across borders. With perfect mobility in a small economy, the domestic interest rate is tied to the world rate, $r = r^*$.

## When to use

Use it as the key Mundell-Fleming assumption: it removes $r$ as a free variable, so only $Y$ can clear the money market.
