With sticky prices, output deviates from its natural level in the short run. Lec 06 takes the extreme case of fully fixed prices, a horizontal line at $\bar{P}$, so output is demand-determined; Lec 14 relaxes this to an upward slope.

## When to use

Use it to trace a demand shock: output moves along SRAS (above or below $\bar{Y}$) until prices adjust. Adverse supply shocks shift SRAS up.
