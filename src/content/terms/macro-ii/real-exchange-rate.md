The relative price of goods across countries, $\varepsilon = eP/P^*$. When prices can change, net exports depend on it, $NX(\varepsilon)$.

## When to use

Use it when $P$ varies, as in the open-economy AD curve: a higher $P$ lowers real balances, shifts LM* left, raises $\varepsilon$ and cuts net exports and output.
