Aggregate supply relates output to the price level. In the long run it is vertical at the natural level of output, $\bar{Y} = F(\bar{K}, \bar{L})$, because real output depends on technology and factor supplies, not on $P$. In the short run the SRAS curve slopes upward.

## When to use

Use it as the umbrella term when a question asks how output responds to the price level, then specify LRAS or SRAS.
