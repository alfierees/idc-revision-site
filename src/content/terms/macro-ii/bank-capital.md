The owners' equity in a bank, assets minus liabilities. It is the cushion against losses; in the lecture's example $1{,}000 - 950 = 50$.

## When to use

Use it with [[Leverage]] to test solvency: with assets of 1,000 and capital of 50, a fall of more than 5% in asset values wipes out the capital. Capital requirements set a minimum ratio, stricter for riskier assets.
