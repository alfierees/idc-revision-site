Inflation from high aggregate demand pushing unemployment below its natural rate, so a tight labour market raises wages, costs and prices.

## When to use

Use it for movements along the Phillips curve: a demand expansion lowers $u$ and raises $\pi$.

See [[Lec_14-Aggregate Supply & the Phillips Curve|Lec 14]].
