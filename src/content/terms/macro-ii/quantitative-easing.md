Central-bank purchases of long-term government bonds and mortgage-backed securities (2008 to 2011 in the lecture) to push down long rates once short rates are already near zero. The Fed tripled the monetary base, yet M1 grew only about 40% because banks held more excess reserves and the multiplier collapsed.

## When to use

Use it as the case study for why a rise in $B$ need not raise $M$: look at what happened to $rr$ and hence $m$.
