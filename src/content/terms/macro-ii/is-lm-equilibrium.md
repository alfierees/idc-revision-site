The intersection of the IS and LM curves, giving the unique $(Y^*, r^*)$ that clears both markets. Fiscal policy moves IS, monetary policy moves LM, and any shift changes the equilibrium.

## When to use

Use it as the base for policy and shock analysis in Lec 08: fiscal expansion raises $Y$ and $r$, monetary expansion raises $Y$ and lowers $r$.

See [[Lec_08-Aggregate Demand II & Applying the IS-LM Model|Lec 08]].
