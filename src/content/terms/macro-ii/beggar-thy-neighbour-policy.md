Boosting domestic output by depreciating the currency, which shifts demand from foreign to domestic goods without raising world demand. Home gains come at foreign losses.

## When to use

Use it to describe monetary expansion under floating rates, and import restrictions under fixed rates, in the small open economy.

See [[Lec_09-The Open Economy Revisited & Mundell-Fleming Model|Lec 09]].
