Short-run fluctuations of output and employment around the long-run trend. Consumption fluctuates less than GDP and investment fluctuates more.

## When to use

Use it for context on recessions and booms in AS-AD and IS-LM questions, together with [[Okun's Law]] for the link to unemployment.

See [[Lec_06-AS-AD Model & Economic Fluctuations|Lec 06]].
