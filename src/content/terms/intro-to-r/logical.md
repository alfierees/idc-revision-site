The Boolean [[Data Type]], with values `TRUE` and `FALSE`. A comparison such as `5>3` returns a logical value.

## When to use

In Lec 01, `r_is_awesome <- T` stores a logical value, and the script's comment says `T` and `F` can stand for `TRUE` and `FALSE`. Converting with `as.numeric(TRUE)` gives 1 and `as.logical(0)` gives `FALSE`, an example of [[Type Coercion]].

See [[Lec_01-Data Types in R|Lec 01]].
