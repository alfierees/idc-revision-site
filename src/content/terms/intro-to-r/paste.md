Joins strings into one, separated by a space by default.

## When to use

In Lec 01, `paste(name,surname)` joins the two [[Character]] variables into `"Ada Lovelace"`. The script's comment calls this concatenating the two variables.

See [[Lec_01-Data Types in R|Lec 01]].
