`a:b` builds the sequence of whole numbers from a to b, such as `1:10`.

## When to use

In Lec 03, `x <- 1:10` creates a [[Vector]] in one step. The same ranges appear in `sample(1:6,...)`, `prod(1:5)` and `sum(1:10)` in Lec 01.

See [[Lec_03-Vectors|Lec 03]].
