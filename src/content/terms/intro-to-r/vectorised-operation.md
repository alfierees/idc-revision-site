An operation applied to every element of a [[Vector]] at once, without a loop, e.g. `x+2`.

## When to use

In Lec 03, with `x <- 1:10`, the expression `x+2` adds 2 to each element and gives 3 4 5 up to 12. Other arithmetic operators work the same way on a whole vector.

See [[Lec_03-Vectors|Lec 03]].
