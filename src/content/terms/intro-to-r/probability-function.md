Gives $P(X = x)$ for a discrete variable or the density for a continuous one. R's prefix is `d`.

## When to use

Use `dbinom()` or `dpois()` for the probability of an exact value.

See [[Lec_11-Probability and Random Numbers|Lec 11]].
