Any two bundles can be compared: the consumer weakly prefers one to the other, or is indifferent, so they are never unable to decide.

## When to use

Stated as a basic assumption on [[Preferences]] alongside [[Transitivity]], and one of the conditions under which preferences can be represented by a utility function.

See [[Lec_02-Preferences|Lec 02]].
