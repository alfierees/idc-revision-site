Bundle $x$ is indirectly revealed preferred to $z$ if a chain of direct revelations links them: $x \succ_D y$ and $y \succ_D z$ give $x \succ_I z$, using transitivity.

## When to use

Use it when two bundles were never both affordable under the same budget: look for an intermediate bundle that was chosen under one budget and affordable under another. Each extra observed choice can add a link to the chain.
