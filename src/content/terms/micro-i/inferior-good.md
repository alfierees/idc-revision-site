A good whose demand falls when income rises. The word describes the sign of the income response, not the quality of the good.

## When to use

Decides the sign of the [[Income Effect]]. After a price fall the substitution effect raises demand and the income effect lowers it; for an ordinary inferior good the substitution effect still wins, and if the income effect wins the good is a [[Giffen Good]]. Seen in: Principles of Micro.

See [[Lec_07-Substitution and Income Effects|Lec 07]].
