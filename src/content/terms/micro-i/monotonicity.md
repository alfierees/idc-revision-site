More of any commodity is always preferred, so every commodity is a good and there is no satiation. This is what makes [[Indifference Curve]]s slope downward.

## When to use

It is one of the two conditions (with [[Convexity]]) for [[Well-Behaved Preferences]]; in revealed preference it guarantees the chosen bundle lies on the budget line rather than strictly inside it.

See [[Lec_02-Preferences|Lec 02]].
