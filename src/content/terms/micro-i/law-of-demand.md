Holding everything else fixed, a higher price lowers the quantity demanded and a lower price raises it, so the ordinary demand curve slopes down.

## When to use

It is an empirical regularity, not guaranteed by the consumer-choice model: a negative slope needs the substitution effect to win. It holds whenever the substitution effect dominates or the income effect reinforces it, and fails for a [[Giffen Good]]. Seen in: Principles of Micro.
