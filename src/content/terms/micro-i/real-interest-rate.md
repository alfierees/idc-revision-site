The interest rate corrected for inflation, $1+\rho=\dfrac{1+r}{1+\pi}$ with $r$ the nominal rate and $\pi$ inflation; this factor sets the slope of the two-period budget line, and higher inflation at a fixed nominal rate lowers it. Seen in: Financial Management, Macro-Economics II. Also in [[PP_01-Utility Transformations, Slutsky Effects, Labor Supply & Intertemporal Choice (2025 Moed A)|PP 01]], [[PP_05-Labour Supply, Intertemporal Choice & Consumer Theory MCQ (2020 Moed A)|PP 05]], [[PP_06-Labor Supply, Intertemporal Choice & Consumer Theory MCQ (2020 Moed B)|PP 06]], [[PP_08-Labour Supply, Intertemporal Choice & Consumer Theory MCQ (2018 Moed A)|PP 08]], [[PP_10-Labour Supply & Intertemporal Choice, Open Questions (2022 Moed A)|PP 10]].

## When to use

Use it for the slope of the [[Intertemporal Budget Constraint]] whenever a question mentions inflation, rather than the nominal rate; the line still passes through the endowment point.

See [[Lec_09-Intertemporal Choice|Lec 09]].
