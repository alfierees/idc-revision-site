Gross demand minus the [[Endowment]], $x_i - \omega_i$: positive for a net buyer, negative for a net seller. The value of the net demands sums to zero, because what is spent buying is funded by what is earned selling.

## When to use

In endowment questions, use it to say whether the consumer buys or sells each good, and check a chosen bundle with $p_1(x_1-\omega_1)+p_2(x_2-\omega_2)=0$.
