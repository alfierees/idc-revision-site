An optimum at which the consumer buys none of one good, so the indifference curve is not tangent to the budget line.

## When to use

Expect it with [[Perfect Substitutes]], where all income goes on the good with the larger ratio of weight to price, and with non-convex preferences, where the tangency point is the least preferred affordable bundle. Do not assume that tangent means optimal: check [[Convexity]] first. Seen in: Micro-Economics II.

See [[Lec_04-Optimal Bundle|Lec 04]].
