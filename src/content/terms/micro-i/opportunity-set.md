All the bundles a consumer can choose from under a constraint; for a [[Budget Constraint]] it is the [[Budget Line]] plus everything under it.

## When to use

It is the first thing to draw in a choice problem; when there are several limits (a budget, a minimum requirement, a time limit) the choice set is the intersection of all of them.

See [[Lec_01-Budget Constraint|Lec 01]].
