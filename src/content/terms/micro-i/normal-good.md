A good whose demand rises when income rises, so the demand curve shifts right. "Normal" describes the sign of the response to income, not the quality of the good. Seen in: Principles of Micro. Also in [[Lec_07-Substitution and Income Effects|Lec 07]].

## When to use

Use it to sign the [[Income Effect]]: for a normal good it reinforces the [[Substitution Effect]], so the demand curve always slopes down.

See [[Lec_05-Demand|Lec 05]].
