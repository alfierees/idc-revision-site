If A is [[Directly Revealed Preferred]] to B, then B is never directly revealed preferred to A; a choice pattern that breaks it is inconsistent (WARP).

## When to use

Test a pair of observed choices by costing each bundle at the other's prices: WARP fails if each was affordable when the other was chosen. A failure shows the choices cannot come from fixed, rational preferences; passing is necessary but does not prove rationality.
