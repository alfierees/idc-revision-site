The sum of all consumers' individual demands at each price; graphically it is the horizontal sum of the individual curves.

## When to use

Add quantities at a given price, not prices at a given quantity. Adding prices at a given quantity is a different object and is not market demand. Seen in: Principles of Micro.

See [[Lec_05-Demand|Lec 05]].
