A strictly increasing function applied to a utility function, such as squaring a positive utility, doubling it or adding a constant. It relabels the [[Indifference Curve]]s but leaves the preferences and the [[Marginal Rate of Substitution]] unchanged, because the extra factor cancels in the ratio of marginal utilities.

## When to use

Use it to check whether two utility functions represent the same preferences, and to simplify a utility function before finding the optimum; the MRS from either version is the same.

See [[Lec_03-Utility and MRS|Lec 03]].
