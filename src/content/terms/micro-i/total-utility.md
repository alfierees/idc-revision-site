The overall satisfaction from consuming a given quantity of a good. It keeps rising while [[Marginal Utility]] is positive and stops rising when marginal utility reaches zero.

## When to use

In the lecture's tables of trips to the club, total utility is the running sum of the marginal utilities, in the cardinal "units of satisfaction" approach.

See [[Lec_04-Optimal Bundle|Lec 04]].
