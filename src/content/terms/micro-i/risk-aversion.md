Preferring a sure amount to a [[Fair Lottery]] with the same expected value; reflected in a concave utility of wealth. Seen in: Microeconomics. Also in [[PS_08-Uncertainty and Risk|PS 08]].

## When to use

Appears in multiple-choice questions on fair lotteries, where the person's risk attitude decides whether the gamble is joined.

See [[PP_01-Utility Transformations, Slutsky Effects, Labor Supply & Intertemporal Choice (2025 Moed A)|PP 01]].
