With a fixed income, spend until the marginal utility per dollar is equal across goods, $MU_X/P_X = MU_Y/P_Y$, with the whole budget spent.

## When to use

In the cardinal approach, buy units in order of highest $MU/P$ until the money runs out; it is the same condition as the tangency rule that the MRS equals the price ratio.
