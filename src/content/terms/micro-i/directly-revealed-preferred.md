Bundle $x^*$ is directly revealed preferred to a different bundle $y$ if $y$ was affordable when $x^*$ was chosen, written $x^* \succ_D y$. The consumer could have had $y$ but took $x^*$.

## When to use

To test it in a problem, work out what each other observed bundle would have cost at the prices of the choice; if it costs no more than the income spent, the chosen bundle is revealed preferred to it. Only affordable alternatives are ranked below the chosen bundle.
