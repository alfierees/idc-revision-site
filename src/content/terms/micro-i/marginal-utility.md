The extra utility from one more unit of a good, holding the other good fixed: $MU_i = \frac{\partial U}{\partial x_i}$, or $\frac{\Delta TU}{\Delta Q}$ in a table.

## When to use

The ratio of marginal utilities gives the slope of an indifference curve, $MRS = -\frac{MU_1}{MU_2}$, and the optimum equalises $MU/P$ across goods. Individual marginal utilities depend on the utility function chosen, but their ratio does not.
