A gamble whose expected payoff equals its price, so it is not worth taking for a risk-neutral person.

## When to use

Comes up in uncertainty and risk questions as the benchmark for [[Risk Aversion]]: a risk-averse person prefers a sure amount to a fair lottery with the same expected value. No lecture covers it; the glossary cites a past paper.

See [[PP_01-Utility Transformations, Slutsky Effects, Labor Supply & Intertemporal Choice (2025 Moed A)|PP 01]].
