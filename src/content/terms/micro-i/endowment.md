The bundle a consumer owns before trading; income is then the value of what is owned at current prices, $p_1\omega_1 + p_2\omega_2$.

## When to use

Replace income $m$ in the budget constraint by the endowment's value. The endowment point is always affordable, so a price change pivots the budget line around it. In [[Labor Supply]] the endowment is total time plus [[Non-Labor Income]].

See [[Lec_08-Labor Supply|Lec 08]].
