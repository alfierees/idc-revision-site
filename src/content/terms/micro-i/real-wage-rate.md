The wage divided by the price of consumption, $w/p_c$: how many units of consumption one more hour of work buys.

## When to use

It is the slope of the labor-supply budget line (not the money wage $w$), and at the optimum the MRS equals it.

See [[Lec_08-Labor Supply|Lec 08]].
