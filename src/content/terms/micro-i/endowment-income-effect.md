The change in demand that comes from the change in the value of the endowment when a price changes.

## When to use

Needed when the consumer has an [[Endowment]]: a price change then splits into a pure [[Substitution Effect]], an ordinary [[Income Effect]], and this third piece, in which income moves from the old endowment value to the new one.

See [[Lec_08-Labor Supply|Lec 08]].
