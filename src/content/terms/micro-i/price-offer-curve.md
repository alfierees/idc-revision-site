The path of the optimal bundle as one price varies, with endowment or income held fixed.

## When to use

In endowment questions it shows where the consumer sells and where they buy: the part to the left of $\omega_1$ has the consumer selling good 1 and buying good 2, and the part to the right has them buying good 1 and selling good 2.
