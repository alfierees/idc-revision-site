What must be given up to get one more unit of a good; on the budget line it is the price ratio $p_1/p_2$, the units of good 2 forgone per extra unit of good 1. Seen in: Principles of Micro.

## When to use

Read it off the slope of the [[Budget Line]], $-p_1/p_2$, which depends only on prices and not on income; a price cut lowers it, which drives the [[Substitution Effect]].

See [[Lec_01-Budget Constraint|Lec 01]].
