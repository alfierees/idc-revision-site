Utility of the form $U = x_1^a x_2^b$ with $a, b > 0$: smooth, hyperbola-like indifference curves that never touch the axes. At the optimum the consumer spends a fixed share of income on each good, $\frac{a}{a+b}$ on good 1 and $\frac{b}{a+b}$ on good 2.

## When to use

In optimal-choice problems use the demands $x_1^* = \frac{a}{a+b}\cdot\frac{y}{p_1}$ and $x_2^* = \frac{b}{a+b}\cdot\frac{y}{p_2}$, where $y$ is income. Demand for each good does not depend on the other good's price.
