The quantity a consumer demands as a function of prices and income; it is the optimal bundle written in terms of those variables.

## When to use

Obtained from an [[Optimal Choice]] problem: lowering a price pivots the budget line, gives a new tangency, and plotting price against the chosen quantity traces out the demand for that good. [[Cobb-Douglas Utility]] gives a closed form. Seen in: Principles of Micro.

See [[Lec_05-Demand|Lec 05]].
