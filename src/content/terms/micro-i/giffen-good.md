An inferior good whose income effect is larger in size than its substitution effect, so a price rise increases the quantity demanded and the demand curve slopes up.

## When to use

This is where the [[Law of Demand]] breaks. The substitution effect always points toward buying more of the cheaper good; only the income effect can change sign, and for a Giffen good it wins. Seen in: Principles of Micro.

See [[Lec_07-Substitution and Income Effects|Lec 07]].
