The relationship between a good's own price and the quantity demanded, other things held fixed; it plots the demand schedule.

## When to use

Ask what moved before drawing: a change in the good's own price is a movement along the curve, while a change in related prices, income, wealth, tastes or expectations shifts the whole curve. Seen in: Principles of Micro.
