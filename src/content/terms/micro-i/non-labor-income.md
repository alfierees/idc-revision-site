Income that does not depend on hours worked, such as a transfer or a return on savings; it shifts the budget line without changing its slope. Also in [[PP_05-Labour Supply, Intertemporal Choice & Consumer Theory MCQ (2020 Moed A)|PP 05]], [[PP_06-Labor Supply, Intertemporal Choice & Consumer Theory MCQ (2020 Moed B)|PP 06]].

## When to use

In labor supply questions it is the $m$ in the endowment $(\bar R, m)$; a change in it moves the budget line in parallel, so it works through the [[Income Effect]] only.

See [[Lec_08-Labor Supply|Lec 08]].
