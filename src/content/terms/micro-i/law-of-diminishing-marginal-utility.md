In a given period, each extra unit of the same good adds less satisfaction than the one before. Total utility keeps rising while marginal utility is positive and stops rising when it reaches zero.

## When to use

It explains why the [[Demand Curve]] slopes down (a consumer buys more only if the price falls) and why the [[Utility-Maximising Rule]] settles: buying more of a good lowers its MU per dollar.
