The set of goods and services a consumer can buy given money income and prices, so it falls or rises with prices even when money income is unchanged. It is judged by whether the original bundle is cheaper or dearer to buy at the new prices. Also in [[Lec_01-Budget Constraint|Lec 01]].

## When to use

Use it in the Slutsky decomposition of a price change: a price fall raises real income, and [[Slutsky Compensation]] takes income away until the consumer can just afford the old bundle.

See [[Lec_07-Substitution and Income Effects|Lec 07]].
