Goods are substitutes if a price rise in one raises demand for the other, and complements if it lowers it; not the same as perfect substitutes or complements in preferences.

## When to use

Use it to sort the effect of a related good's price on the [[Demand Curve]]: a dearer hamburger shifts demand for chicken (a substitute) right and demand for ketchup (a complement) left.

See [[Lec_05-Demand|Lec 05]].
