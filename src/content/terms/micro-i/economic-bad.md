A commodity the consumer would rather have less of, such as pollution or noise, so utility falls as the quantity rises and the indifference curve slopes upward.

## When to use

Appears when an indifference-curve question pairs one good with one bad: the curve slopes up and "better" points up and to the left. The same item can switch roles, like water that is a good up to a point and a bad beyond it.
