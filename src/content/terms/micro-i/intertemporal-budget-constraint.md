The two-period budget line, with consumption when young and consumption when old as the two goods, linked by the interest rate and the income endowment; its slope is $-(1+r)$.

## When to use

Set it up like an ordinary budget line, then find the optimum where the indifference curve is tangent to it. Saving when young is the endowment minus consumption when young.

See [[Lec_09-Intertemporal Choice|Lec 09]].
