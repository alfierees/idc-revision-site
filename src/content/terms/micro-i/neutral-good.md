A good that does not change the consumer's utility, so an extra unit leaves the consumer equally well off and the [[Indifference Curve]]s are vertical or horizontal. In demand questions it is a good whose quantity does not respond to income. Also in [[PP_05-Labour Supply, Intertemporal Choice & Consumer Theory MCQ (2020 Moed A)|PP 05]], [[PP_06-Labor Supply, Intertemporal Choice & Consumer Theory MCQ (2020 Moed B)|PP 06]].

## When to use

Appears when goods are classified as good, bad or neutral (the lecture's water example moves from good to neutral to bad as the amount rises), and in multiple-choice statements that label goods normal, inferior or neutral.

See [[Lec_03-Utility and MRS|Lec 03]].
