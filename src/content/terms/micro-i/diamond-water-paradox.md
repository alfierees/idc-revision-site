Water is vital but cheap, while diamonds are useless for survival but expensive. The resolution is that price reflects marginal utility, not total utility.

## When to use

Use it to explain why a good with large total utility can have a low price: water is so plentiful that the last unit adds little, while diamonds are scarce so the marginal unit is valuable.
