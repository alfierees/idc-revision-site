How a consumer splits consumption between two periods, saving or borrowing along an intertemporal budget line. It is ordinary two-good consumer choice with consumption now and consumption later as the goods.

## When to use

Solve it as [[Optimal Choice]] with the [[Interest Rate]] as the relative price. For a change in the rate, split the effect on consumption when young into a [[Substitution Effect]] and an [[Income Effect]].

See [[Lec_09-Intertemporal Choice|Lec 09]].
