Preferences that are monotonic and convex: more is better and averages are preferred to extremes, giving downward-sloping, bowed-in indifference curves.

## When to use

They are the case where the tangency rule finds the [[Optimal Choice]]; with non-convex or perfect-complement preferences tangency fails and the answer is a corner or a kink.
