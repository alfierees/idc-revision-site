A consumer who prefers consumption early, so the indifference curves are steeper toward the present. Also in [[PP_04-Three Consumers, Welfare Plan & Choice Mix (2018 Sample Exam)|PP 04]].

## When to use

Appears in two-period questions as a taste fact about the consumer; the exam notes read it as a bundle with more consumption now than later, though a high enough interest rate or income tilted to the present can still make a present lover save.

See [[PP_10-Labour Supply & Intertemporal Choice, Open Questions (2022 Moed A)|PP 10]].
