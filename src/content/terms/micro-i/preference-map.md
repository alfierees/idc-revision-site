The family of [[Indifference Curve]]s describing one consumer; higher curves mean higher utility.

## When to use

Lay it over the [[Budget Line]] to find the [[Optimal Choice]]: the highest curve that still touches the line, usually at a tangency.

See [[Lec_04-Optimal Bundle|Lec 04]].
