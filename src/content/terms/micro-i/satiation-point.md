A bundle the consumer likes best of all, so that more of either good makes them worse off. The [[Indifference Curve]]s become closed rings around it, and "better" means closer to the bliss point.

## When to use

It marks where [[Monotonicity]] fails: beyond a certain amount a commodity becomes a bad.
