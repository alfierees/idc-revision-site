The consumer weakly prefers a mixture of two equally good bundles to either one, so indifference curves bow toward the origin. It is the taste for variety: averages are preferred to extremes.

## When to use

Check it before assuming that tangency gives the optimum. With strictly convex preferences the [[Marginal Rate of Substitution]] rises toward zero as good 1 increases (diminishing MRS); with non-convex preferences the best affordable bundle is at a corner.
