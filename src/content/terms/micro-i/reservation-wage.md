The lowest wage at which a person chooses to work at all; below it the optimum is zero hours.

## When to use

Find it by computing the [[Marginal Rate of Substitution]] at the corner where all time is taken as [[Leisure]]; the person works only if the wage is above it.

See [[PP_10-Labour Supply & Intertemporal Choice, Open Questions (2022 Moed A)|PP 10]].
