Prompt engineering means crafting clear and specific instructions to get the response you want from a [[Large Language Model]]. The slide calls it the most crucial aspect of using LLMs effectively.

## When to use

Guidelines include careful wording, delimiters, steps, roles and goals, and positive and negative instructions. The strategies covered are input-output, [[Zero-Shot Prompting]], [[Few-Shot Prompting]], self-criticism, iterative and [[Chain-of-Thought Prompting]].
