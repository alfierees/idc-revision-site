A prompt strategy of including a few worked examples in the prompt so the model sees the pattern. The lecture's example shows "2 + 2 = 4" and "3 + 5 = 8" before asking "4 + 6 =".

## When to use

It sits between [[Zero-Shot Prompting]] (no examples) and one-shot (one example) among the strategies of [[Prompt Engineering]].
