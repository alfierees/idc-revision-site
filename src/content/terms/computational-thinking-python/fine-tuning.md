The second phase of training a [[Large Language Model]]: the pre-trained model is refined on a **narrower dataset** to specialise in a task or knowledge area, aligning its outputs with desired outcomes.

## When to use

Contrast it with [[Pre-training]], which learns general patterns of language by predicting the next word on massive amounts of text.

See [[Lec_12-Generative AI and Prompt Engineering|Lec 12]].
