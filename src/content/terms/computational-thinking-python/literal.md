Text that the parser recognises as a direct way of writing a value, such as `2000`, `3.14`, `True` or `"hi"`.

## When to use

Know how literals of each type are written: [[Integer]] (`2_000`), [[Float]] (`1e5`) and [[String]] (single or double quotes behave identically).

See [[Lec_02-Numeric Values and Strings|Lec 02]].
