An error in a program. The term comes from 1947, when engineers working on the Harvard Mark II found a moth trapped between relays and Grace Hopper taped it into the log book.

## When to use

Lecture 1 lists correctness as doing the designed task with no bugs, beside being [[Robustness]]-minded. Changing a `float` cast to `int` is a bug for decimal input, and writing `=` where `==` was meant is a common beginner one. Compare [[Logic Error]] and [[Syntax Error]].
