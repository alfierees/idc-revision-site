Pre-training is the first phase of training a [[Large Language Model]]: the model reads massive amounts of text (books, articles, web pages) and learns to predict the next word in a sentence. More data generally means better generation.

## When to use

Know it as the general-language phase that comes before [[Fine-tuning]] on a narrower dataset. The goal is to learn general patterns of language. The "P" in GPT stands for pre-trained.
