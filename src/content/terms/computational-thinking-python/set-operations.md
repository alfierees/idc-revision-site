The four operations on two sets: union (`A | B`), intersection (`A & B`), difference (`A - B`) and symmetric difference (`A ^ B`, in exactly one of the two sets). Each also has a method form such as `A.union(B)`.

## When to use

Lec 07 motivates them with student ids registered for courses (both courses, one but not the other, either). Union, intersection and symmetric difference are symmetric, but difference is not: `A - B` differs from `B - A`.

See [[Lec_07-Dictionaries and Sets|Lec 07]].
