The 13 weekly sessions, each paired with a [[Think Python]] chapter and, for the first eight, a practical assignment.

## When to use

It is a map of the whole course: for instance session 8 is Searches and Sorting and session 9 is Classes and Objects. Session numbers run 1 to 13 while the slide files are numbered 00 to 12. Grading is 30% from 6 of the 8 assignments and 70% from the final exam.
