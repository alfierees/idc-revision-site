Asking the model to do a task with no examples in the prompt. Showing one example is one-shot, a few is few-shot.

## When to use

Lec 12 lists it among the prompt-engineering techniques beside input-output, self-criticism, iterative and chain-of-thought prompts. Giving two worked sums before asking a third is few-shot; giving none is zero-shot.
