The three cost scenarios for an algorithm: the worst case (the most work), the best case (the least) and the average case (the hardest to analyse). [[Time Complexity]] analysis splits into these three.

## When to use

For [[Linear Search]] the number of comparisons equals the target's position counting from 1. In `4 17 21 12 6 9 1 15 3`, searching for 4 takes 1 comparison (best case) and searching for 3 takes 9 (worst case, the last element).
