A number with a decimal point (`float`), such as `3.0` or `-5.677`, also written in scientific notation like `1e5`. Floats have a **limited range** (`1e309` becomes `inf`) and a **limited precision** of about 16 significant digits.

## When to use

Exam questions probe the limits (`inf`, underflow to `0.0`, truncated literals) and the rounding problem in [[Floating-Point Error]]; contrast with [[Integer]], which has no size limit.

See [[Lec_02-Numeric Values and Strings|Lec 02]].
