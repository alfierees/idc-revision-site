An **ordered, mutable** sequence of elements written in square brackets; elements can have mixed types. Elements are reached by [[Indexing]] starting at 0.

## When to use

Because lists are mutable (see [[Mutability]]) methods such as `append`, `insert` and `pop` change them in place; `append` adds its argument as one element, so appending a list makes a [[Nested List]]. Walk one with a [[For Loop]].

See [[Lec_04-Lists, For Loops and Ranges|Lec 04]].
