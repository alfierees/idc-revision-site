A large neural network controlled by billions of parameters. A language model **predicts the next word** given a piece of text, and generates text one word at a time from probabilities learned from vast amounts of text.

## When to use

Know the two training phases, [[Pre-training]] then [[Fine-tuning]], and that [[ChatGPT]] is a kind of LLM. Because it produces the most probable words, generated code must still be tested.

See [[Lec_12-Generative AI and Prompt Engineering|Lec 12]].
