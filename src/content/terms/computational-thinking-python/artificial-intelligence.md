Simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, especially computer systems. It is the outermost of the nested fields on the Lecture 12 diagram: [[Machine Learning]] sits inside AI, [[Deep Learning]] inside machine learning, and [[Generative AI]] is drawn within that nesting.

## When to use

Lecture 12 has no new Python syntax, so AI appears as a conceptual question about how these fields relate. The ChatGPT slide gives the chain: ChatGPT is a kind of LLM, an LLM is part of Generative AI, which relies on machine learning, a field in AI.
