Robustness is a program's ability to handle illegal inputs and unexpected situations sensibly. It is one of five qualities of a good program, with correctness (no [[Bug|bugs]]), readability, efficiency and elegance.

## When to use

The slides say these qualities will be required in submitted exercises. The Celsius-to-Fahrenheit converter is not robust: entering a non-numeric value such as `0C` crashes it. Later lectures add [[Try-Except]] to handle this.
