Data that does not sit in rows and columns, such as emails, text, images and audio; roughly 80 percent of business data. Structured data is the opposite: spreadsheets, databases and CSV files.

## When to use

Classify a metric as structured or unstructured in the data-types exercise (complaint email text and an Instagram photo are unstructured). Models need a numeric matrix, so unstructured data needs a translation step, which is Lecture 7: text via the preprocessing pipeline and [[Bag of Words]], images via filters. Also in [[PS_06-NLP and Image Analysis|PS 06]].
