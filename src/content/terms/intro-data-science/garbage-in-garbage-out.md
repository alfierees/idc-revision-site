Bad input data gives bad results whatever the model. Up to 80 percent of a data scientist's time goes on collecting, cleaning and organising data.

## When to use

Use it to justify time spent cleaning before modelling. Typical dirty-data problems are [[Missing Values]], duplicates, typos, inconsistent date formats and impossible values.
