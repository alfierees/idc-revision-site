Preference inferred from behaviour such as watch time or clicks, rather than stated directly. It is abundant but noisy (watched but fell asleep?).

## When to use

Contrast it with [[Explicit Feedback]] such as star ratings (Seret+ columns `watch_pct` versus `rating`). Rating and watching can disagree, and then implicit is often more honest, though the lecture uses explicit ratings because they are cleaner to evaluate.

See [[Lecture 6 - Recommendation Systems|Lec 6]].
