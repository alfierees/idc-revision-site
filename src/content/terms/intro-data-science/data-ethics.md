The responsibilities that come with using personal data: consent, purpose limitation (use data only for stated purposes), data minimisation (collect only what you need), the right to deletion, and explainability of automated decisions.

## When to use

Lecture 1 frames these as questions a CEO owns: what data may we collect, is the model fair across demographics, can we explain automated decisions. Cases to cite: the Amazon hiring algorithm (bias in, bias out) and Cambridge Analytica; key laws are GDPR and Israel's Protection of Privacy Law 1981.
