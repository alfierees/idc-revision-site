Looking only at the successes and ignoring the failures, which distorts conclusions. The lecture's example: in WW2 the military wanted to armour the parts of planes with bullet holes, but statistician Abraham Wald said to armour the parts without holes, because planes hit there never returned.

## When to use

Use it as a trap to spot when a dataset contains only what survived (for example only the startups that succeeded). Ask what data you do not have, since it matters as much as what you do.
