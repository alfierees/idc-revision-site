A distribution in which a few items account for a large share of the total while a very large number of niche items each contribute a little; the famous version is the 80/20 rule. Recommenders help users reach the niche items.

## When to use

It motivates personalised recommendation over a single Top 10 for everyone. Other long-tail examples in the lecture: word frequency (Zipf's law), city populations and website traffic.
