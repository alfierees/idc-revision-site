Descriptive (what happened), diagnostic (why it happened), predictive (what will happen) and prescriptive (what should we do). Value and difficulty rise from descriptive to prescriptive, like a staircase.

## When to use

Use it to label a business question or case study: revenue was 2.3M, up 12 percent is descriptive; revenue dropped because the top rep quit is diagnostic; a forecast for Q3 is predictive; hire 2 reps and raise ads is prescriptive.
