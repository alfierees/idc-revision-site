A chart that groups the values of one numeric variable into bins and shows the count in each, revealing the shape of its distribution.

## When to use

Use it to check shape and skew before choosing mean or median. Too few bins hide patterns and too many show noise, so start with 10 to 20 bins. PS 01 uses one on passenger age.
