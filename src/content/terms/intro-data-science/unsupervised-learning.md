Finding structure in unlabelled data, with no target to predict and no single right answer to optimise against.

## When to use

Recognise it by the lack of labels. Typical tasks are clustering ([[K-Means]]) and dimensionality reduction ([[PCA]]), evaluated more subjectively with the [[Elbow Method]] and [[Silhouette Score]] rather than accuracy. The lecture's case is StyleNation customer segmentation. Appears in [[PS_03-Mall Customers Clustering and PCA|PS 03]] and [[PS_05-Clustering and PCA with scikit-learn|PS 05]].
