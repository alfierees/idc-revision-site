Predicting a **discrete label** (a category) from features, where regression predicts a number. It is binary with two classes (churn or not, spam or not) and multiclass with three or more (product category, disease type).

## When to use

Whenever the target is a category, as in the Yalla Fitness churn case or the Titanic survival homework. The course's four classifiers are [[Decision Tree]], [[Logistic Regression]], [[KNN]] and [[Random Forest]], judged with a [[Confusion Matrix]]. Also in [[PS_01-Titanic Classification Basics|PS 01]].
