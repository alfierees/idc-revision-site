The field of AI concerned with letting computers understand, interpret and generate human language. It powers chatbots, spam filters, translation and sentiment analysis.

## When to use

Text must be turned into numbers first: lowercase, remove punctuation, tokenize, remove stopwords, stem or lemmatize, then [[Bag of Words]] and a document-term matrix. PS 06 walks through this pipeline.
