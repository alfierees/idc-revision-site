Chopping suffixes off a word by rule to reach a rough stem, which may not be a real word (amazing becomes amaz, ingredients becomes ingredi).

## When to use

Compare it with [[Lemmatization]], which uses a vocabulary and grammar to return real dictionary forms: stemming is faster and fine for large datasets, lemmatization is better when accuracy or readable output matters. Pick one and be consistent. Also in [[PS_06-NLP and Image Analysis|PS 06]].
