Walking downhill on the loss surface: start from random weights, check which direction is downhill, take a small step, and repeat until the loss reaches a minimum. The [[Learning Rate]] sets the step size.

## When to use

Use it to explain how a [[Neural Network]]'s weights are adjusted in the training loop: forward pass, compute loss, adjust weights by gradient descent, next [[Epoch]].

See [[Lecture 8 - Intro to Deep Learning|Lec 8]].
