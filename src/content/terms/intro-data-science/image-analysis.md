The field of extracting information from visual data such as photos, video, scans and satellite imagery, also called computer vision. Images are already numbers (a grid of [[Pixel]] values), so the challenge is extracting useful features, not converting the data.

## When to use

For image tasks like facial recognition, medical diagnosis, quality control or product tagging. The course's route is to slide a [[Filter]] over the image ([[Convolution]]) to build a [[Feature Map]], and later to let a [[Convolutional Neural Network]] learn its own filters. Also in [[PS_06-NLP and Image Analysis|PS 06]].
