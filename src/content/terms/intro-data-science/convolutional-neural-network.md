A deep-learning model whose filters are learned from thousands of labelled images instead of being hand-designed (edge, blur, sharpen). Given, say, 100 blank filters and 10,000 labelled photos, it works out which patterns matter.

## When to use

It is the image branch of [[Deep Learning]], teased at the end of Lecture 7 and continued in Lecture 8. The deep learning checklist suggests CNNs need roughly 10,000+ images.
