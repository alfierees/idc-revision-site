The share of all relevant items that appear in the top K recommendations: relevant items in the top K divided by total relevant items.

## When to use

Asks how many good items the recommender missed, so it suits search and discovery, whereas [[Precision at K]] suits a homepage. The lecture notes that improving one often hurts the other, echoing [[Precision and Recall]] from classification.
