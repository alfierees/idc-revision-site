A chart of the median, quartiles and whiskers that shows spread and flags outliers, and is best used to compare spread across groups (for example revenue by product category).

## When to use

Choose a boxplot when the question is spread and outliers across groups, versus a histogram for the distribution of one variable. Outliers are the points beyond $Q_1 - 1.5\,\text{IQR}$ or $Q_3 + 1.5\,\text{IQR}$, and the lecture says to investigate before deleting them.
