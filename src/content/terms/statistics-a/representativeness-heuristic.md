Judging how likely something is by how well the story fits, rather than by the set logic. In the Linda question, many people rate "bank teller and feminist" above "bank teller", even though the first is a subset of the second and cannot be more probable.

## When to use

Use it as a warning when an option sounds more plausible because it adds detail: an intersection can never be more probable than either of its events.
