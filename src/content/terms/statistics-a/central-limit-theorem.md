For i.i.d. variables with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$, the sum $S$ is approximately $N(n\mu, n\sigma^2)$ and the average $\bar{X}$ is approximately $N(\mu, \sigma^2/n)$.

## When to use

Use it to turn a question about a sum or an average of many observations into a normal-probability question: find the mean and variance of $S$ or $\bar{X}$, standardize, and read the [[Z Table]]. The Moed B 2023 practice paper ends with questions of this type.
