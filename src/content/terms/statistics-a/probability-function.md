A rule $P$ that assigns every event $A$ a number $P(A)$ between 0 and 1, governed by the three probability axioms.

## When to use

It is one of the three ingredients of a random experiment, with the sample space and the events. Its input is an event, not a single result.

See [[Lec_02-Probability Function and Conditional Probability|Lec 02]].
