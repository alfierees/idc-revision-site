A table with the possible values $x_i$ in the first row and their probabilities $P(X=x_i)$ in the second; the probabilities must sum to 1.

## When to use

Build it from the sample space of an experiment (three coins, a die) and use it to compute $E(X)$ and $Var(X)$; a running total of its second row gives the [[Cumulative Distribution Function]].

See [[Lec_05-Random Variables, Expected Value and Variance|Lec 05]].
