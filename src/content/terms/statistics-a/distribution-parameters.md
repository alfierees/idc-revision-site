The numbers that fix a family's PMF, for example $(a,b)$, $(n,p)$, $p$, $(N,D,n)$ or $\lambda$. In $X \sim U(a,b)$, the values $a$ and $b$ are the parameters.

## When to use

In a "which distribution?" question, identify the shape and then read the parameters off the story (for example 30 rolls with success probability $1/20$ gives $(n,p)=(30,1/20)$).
