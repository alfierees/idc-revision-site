A contestant picks one of three doors (one hides a car, two hide goats), the host opens another door showing a goat, and the contestant may switch. Switching wins the car with probability $2/3$.

## When to use

Solve it with a tree diagram and conditional probability: the host must avoid the car, so his choice carries information. Your first pick stays at $1/3$, so the other unopened door gets $2/3$.
