$E(X)=\sum x_i P(X=x_i)$: each possible value weighted by its probability, the balance point of the distribution. It need not be a value the variable can take (an expected age of 20.86).

## When to use

Compute it from a distribution table, or use the rules for linear transformations, $E(a+bX)=a+bE(X)$. Linearity does not extend to non-linear functions of $X$.
