$U(a,b)$ on the integers $a,\dots,b$, each with probability $1/(b-a+1)$; $E=(a+b)/2$ and $Var=\big((b-a+1)^2-1\big)/12$.

## When to use

A fair die is $U(1,6)$. A variable such as $Y=5X-20$ built from a die roll reuses the known mean and variance through the linear transformation rules.
