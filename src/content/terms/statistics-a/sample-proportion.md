$\hat{P}=X/n$: the fraction of $n$ observations that are successes, approximately $N\big(p, p(1-p)/n\big)$.

## When to use

Use the same check as for the binomial, $np \ge 5$ and $n(1-p) \ge 5$. It gives the same answer as the count version, since "at most 74 of 100" and "at most 74 percent" are the same event.
