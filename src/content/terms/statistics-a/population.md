The distribution from which the observations are drawn, for example all students when the sample is 5 students asked about preferred temperature.

## When to use

Its mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$ are the inputs to the central limit theorem, for example $\bar{X} \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2/n)$.

See [[Lec_12-Central Limit Theorem and Normal Approximations|Lec 12]].
