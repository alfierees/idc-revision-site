Observations that share one distribution (they come from the same population) and do not affect each other (random selection makes them independent).

## When to use

It is the condition the [[Central Limit Theorem]] and the [[Law of Large Numbers]] need: $X_1,\dots,X_n$ i.i.d. with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$.

See [[Lec_12-Central Limit Theorem and Normal Approximations|Lec 12]].
