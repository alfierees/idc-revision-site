A table of $\Phi(z)=P(Z \le z)$, the area to the left of $z$; the row gives the first decimal of $z$, the column the second.

## When to use

For a right tail use $1-\Phi(t)$, for an interval use $\Phi(t)-\Phi(s)$, and to find a cutoff look inside the table for the probability and read off $z$.

See [[Lec_11-Uniform and Normal Distributions|Lec 11]].
