$P(\lambda)$: the number of independent events in a fixed interval, with $P(X=k)=e^{-\lambda}\lambda^k/k!$. Its mean and variance are both $\lambda$.

## When to use

Use it for a count of events per interval at a known average rate, such as colds per year, after scaling $\lambda$ to the length of the interval asked about.

See [[Lec_07-Known Distributions|Lec 07]].
