For every normal distribution, about 68%, 95% and 99.7% of the area lies within 1, 2 and 3 standard deviations of $\mu$ (exactly $0.6827$, $0.9545$ and $0.9973$ on the slides).

## When to use

Use it as a shortcut when a cutoff is a whole number of standard deviations from the mean, for example $P(X>82)$ for $N(76,3^2)$ is half of $1-0.9545$. For any other cutoff you need standardization and the Z table.
