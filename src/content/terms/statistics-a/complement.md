$\bar{A}$ is everything in the sample space $\Omega$ that is not in $A$.

## When to use

It appears when translating events into words and in the [[Complement Rule]]; the four cells of a [[2x2 Probability Table]] are built from $A$, $\bar{A}$, $B$ and $\bar{B}$.

See [[Lec_01-Sets and the Probability Function|Lec 01]].
