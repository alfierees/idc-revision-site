The three rules defining a probability function: $P(A) \ge 0$, $P(\Omega)=1$, and $P(A \cup B)=P(A)+P(B)$ for disjoint events.

## When to use

Every other rule (complement, monotonicity, inclusion-exclusion) is derived from them. Additivity only applies when the events are disjoint; otherwise the overlap is double counted.
