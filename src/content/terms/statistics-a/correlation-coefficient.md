$\rho = Cov(X,Y)/(SD(X)\,SD(Y))$ is a unit-free number between -1 and 1 that measures linear association.

## When to use

Use it to compare the strength of relationships (the lecture's rough benchmarks are about 0.2 weak, 0.5 medium and 0.8 strong, but these depend on the field), or to read a sign and strength from a [[Scatter Plot]]. A high $\rho$ does not show that one variable causes the other.
