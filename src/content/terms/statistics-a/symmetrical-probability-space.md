An experiment where every result is equally likely (a fair die, a fair coin, a well-shuffled deck), so $P(A)=|A|/|\Omega|$ and probability reduces to counting.

## When to use

Count $|A|$ and $|\Omega|$ with the counting rules, and for conditioning use $P(A|B)=|A \cap B|/|B|$. Combinations with replacement are not symmetrical, so dividing by the number of outcomes fails there.

See [[Lec_04-Symmetrical Probability Spaces and Combinatorics|Lec 04]].
