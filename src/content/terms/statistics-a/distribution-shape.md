The named family a variable follows, for example $U$, $B$, $G$, $HG$ or $P$. In $X \sim U(a,b)$, $U$ is the shape.

## When to use

Once a variable is recognised as a known shape (or a linear transformation of one), its possible values, PMF, expected value and variance come for free. The lecture's "which distribution?" game matches each scenario to a shape and its parameters.
