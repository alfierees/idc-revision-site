$Var(X+Y)=Var(X)+Var(Y)+2\,Cov(X,Y)$.

## When to use

For independent variables the covariance is 0, leaving $Var(X)+Var(Y)$; this is what gives $Var(S)=n\sigma^2$ for a sum of $n$ i.i.d. variables in the central limit theorem.

See [[Lec_08-Joint Distributions, Conditional Distributions and Covariance|Lec 08]].
