$P(\bar{A}) = 1 - P(A)$. It is derived from the axioms, because $A$ and $\bar{A}$ are disjoint and together make up $\Omega$.

## When to use

Use it when the complement is easier to compute than the event itself, and to fill margins such as $P(\bar{B})=1-P(B)$ in a [[2x2 Probability Table]].

See [[Lec_02-Probability Function and Conditional Probability|Lec 02]].
