A plot where each dot is one $(X,Y)$ combination, showing the joint distribution of two variables.

## When to use

Use it to read the direction of a linear relationship, which is the sign of the covariance. The lecture's example shows a curved relationship that still has a correlation of 0.

See [[Lec_08-Joint Distributions, Conditional Distributions and Covariance|Lec 08]].
