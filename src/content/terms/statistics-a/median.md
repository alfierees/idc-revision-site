The 50th percentile, $Md=C_{0.5}$: the value where $F=0.5$.

## When to use

Solve $F(Md)=0.5$ for a continuous variable. In general $Md \ne E(X)$; they coincide only for symmetric distributions such as the uniform and the normal.

See [[Lec_10-Continuous Random Variables and the Uniform Distribution|Lec 10]].
