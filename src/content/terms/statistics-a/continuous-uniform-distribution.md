A continuous variable with constant density $1/(b-a)$ on $[a,b]$, for example a randomly chosen number between 0 and 10 with $X \sim U(0,10)$.

## When to use

Use it when every value in an interval is equally likely; probabilities are rectangle areas. Its mean and median coincide because it is symmetric.
