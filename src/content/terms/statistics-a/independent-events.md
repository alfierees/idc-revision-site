$P(A|B)=P(A)$, equivalently $P(A \cap B)=P(A)P(B)$: learning $B$ does not change the chance of $A$.

## When to use

Test it by checking whether $P(A \cap B)$ equals the product $P(A)P(B)$. It is not the same as disjoint: independent events with positive probabilities cannot be disjoint, and vice versa.
