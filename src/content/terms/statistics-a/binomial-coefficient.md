$\binom{n}{k}$, read "n choose k", is the number of ways to pick $k$ of $n$ items when order does not matter.

## When to use

Use it to count unordered selections without replacement (a hand of cards, a committee), and as the counting factor in the [[Binomial Distribution]] and [[Hypergeometric Distribution]] PMFs.

See [[Lec_04-Symmetrical Probability Spaces and Combinatorics|Lec 04]].
