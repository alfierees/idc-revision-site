$Cov(X,Y)=E(XY)-E(X)E(Y)$; its sign gives the direction of a linear relationship.

## When to use

It is needed for the [[Variance of a Sum]] and as the numerator of the [[Correlation Coefficient]]. Independent variables have zero covariance, but zero covariance does not imply independence, because covariance captures only linear relationships.

See [[Lec_08-Joint Distributions, Conditional Distributions and Covariance|Lec 08]].
