An occurrence whose outcome is uncertain before it happens, such as rolling a die or flipping a coin.

## When to use

Model it by naming its three ingredients: the sample space, the events you care about, and the probability function.

See [[Lec_01-Sets and the Probability Function|Lec 01]].
