The bell-shaped continuous distribution $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$ with $E(X)=\mu$ and $Var(X)=\sigma^2$, symmetric around $\mu$. Its second parameter is the variance, not the standard deviation.

## When to use

Its CDF has no closed form, so standardize and read $\Phi$ from the Z table. It is also the approximate shape of sums and averages of many independent variables.
