$Var(X)=E\big((X-E(X))^2\big)=E(X^2)-(E(X))^2$: the expected squared distance from the mean.

## When to use

The shortcut $E(X^2)-(E(X))^2$ is usually quicker; do not confuse $E(X^2)$ with $(E(X))^2$. Use $Var(bX+a)=b^2 Var(X)$ for linear transformations.
