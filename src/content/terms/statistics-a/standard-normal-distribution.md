$N(0,1)$, written $Z$, with CDF $\Phi(t)=P(Z \le t)$, which has been tabulated once and for all.

## When to use

Any normal probability is turned into a $Z$ probability and read from the Z table, using $P(Z>t)=1-\Phi(t)$ and $\Phi(-t)=1-\Phi(t)$.

See [[Lec_11-Uniform and Normal Distributions|Lec 11]].
