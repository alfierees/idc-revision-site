The distribution of $X$ given that $Y=y_j$ is known: $P(X=x_i|Y=y_j)$ is the joint probability divided by $P(Y=y_j)$. It is a new distribution and sums to 1.

## When to use

In a joint table, take the column of $Y=y_j$ and divide every cell by the column total. If $X$ and $Y$ are independent, every conditional distribution of $X$ equals its marginal distribution.
