If $A \subseteq B$ then $P(A) \le P(B)$: a larger event cannot have a smaller probability.

## When to use

It gives only a lower bound for the larger event (if under-14s have probability 0.1, under-18s have at least 0.1). It also explains why adding detail to a story, as in the Linda example, never makes it more probable.
