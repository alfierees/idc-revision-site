$n$ observations $X_1,\dots,X_n$ drawn from the same population distribution; random selection makes them independent.

## When to use

It is the starting point for the central limit theorem: name the population, check the observations are i.i.d., then work with the sample average or sum.

See [[Lec_12-Central Limit Theorem and Normal Approximations|Lec 12]].
