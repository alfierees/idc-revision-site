The average of a sample gets closer to the population expected value as the sample grows: $\bar{X} \to \mu$ as $n \to \infty$.

## When to use

The slides prove it from the [[Central Limit Theorem]]: $\bar{X}\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2/n)$, and as $\sigma^2/n \to 0$ the distribution collapses onto $\mu$. It sits in the normal-distribution part of the exam.

See [[Lec_12-Central Limit Theorem and Normal Approximations|Lec 12]].
