A grid of the intersections of $A$, $\bar{A}$, $B$ and $\bar{B}$, with row and column totals in the margins. The four inner cells partition $\Omega$ and the margins are the row and column totals.

## When to use

Put whatever the question gives into the table, then fill in the rest by subtraction; every probability asked for is then a cell or a margin. It also gives conditional probabilities straight from the table (a cell divided by a margin).
