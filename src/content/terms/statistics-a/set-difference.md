$A \setminus B$: the elements of $A$ that are not in $B$, equal to $A \cap \bar{B}$. The lecture also writes it $A-B$.

## When to use

For sizes, $|A \setminus B|=|A|-|A \cap B|$: subtract the overlap. It is redundant, since it can be written with intersection and complement.
