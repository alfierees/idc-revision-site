$\bar{X}=\sum X_i/n$: the single number reported from a random sample of $n$ observations.

## When to use

By the central limit theorem $\bar{X} \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2/n)$, so $E(\bar{X})=\mu$ and its standard deviation is $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$. Standardize with that standard deviation.

See [[Lec_12-Central Limit Theorem and Normal Approximations|Lec 12]].
