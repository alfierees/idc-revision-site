$A \cap B$ is the set of elements that are in both $A$ and $B$.

## When to use

It appears in the [[Inclusion-Exclusion Rule]], in the definition of [[Conditional Probability]] ($P(A \cap B)/P(B)$) and in every inner cell of a [[2x2 Probability Table]].

See [[Lec_01-Sets and the Probability Function|Lec 01]].
