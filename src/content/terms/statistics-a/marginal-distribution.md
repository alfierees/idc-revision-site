The distribution of one variable regardless of the other, found as the row totals or column totals of the joint table. It is called marginal because it is written in the margin of the table.

## When to use

Sum the rows to get $P(X=x_i)$ or the columns to get $P(Y=y_j)$. If every conditional distribution equals the marginal, the two variables are independent.

See [[Lec_08-Joint Distributions, Conditional Distributions and Covariance|Lec 08]].
