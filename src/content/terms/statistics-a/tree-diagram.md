Nodes and branches holding conditional probabilities; multiply along a path to get the intersection, and add across paths that end in the same event.

## When to use

Check your work: branches leaving a node sum to 1 and all end nodes sum to 1. It supports total probability, Bayes' theorem and the Monty Hall problem.
