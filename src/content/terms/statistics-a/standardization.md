$Z=(X-\mu)/\sigma$: a linear map to mean 0 and variance 1; subtract the expected value and divide by the standard deviation.

## When to use

It is step one of the normal recipe: standardize $t$, then find $\Phi$ in the Z table. Work backwards from a probability with $C=\mu+\sigma Z$.

See [[Lec_11-Uniform and Normal Distributions|Lec 11]].
