$X$ and $Y$ are independent if $P(X=x_i,Y=y_j)=P(X=x_i)P(Y=y_j)$ for every pair of values, so knowing one tells you nothing about the other.

## When to use

Check every cell of a joint table: one failing cell is enough to show dependence. Independence implies $Cov(X,Y)=0$, but zero covariance does not imply independence.
