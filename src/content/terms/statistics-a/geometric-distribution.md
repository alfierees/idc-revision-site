$G(p)$ is the number of tries up to and including the first success in independent Bernoulli trials; it takes the values $1,2,\dots$ with no upper limit.

## When to use

Use it for "keep going until the first success" stories (buying scratch tickets until one wins, rolling a die until a 20). Distinguish it from the [[Binomial Distribution]], which counts successes in a fixed number of tries.
