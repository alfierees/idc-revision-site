A process with $m$ stages, having $n_1, n_2, \dots, n_m$ possible outcomes at each stage, has $n_1 \cdot n_2 \cdots n_m$ outcomes in total.

## When to use

It underlies every counting formula, so use it when unsure how to count $|A|$ or $|\Omega|$ in a symmetrical probability space. Adding a condition (such as "at least one digit is 1") can make a naive multiplication overcount.

See [[Lec_04-Symmetrical Probability Spaces and Combinatorics|Lec 04]].
