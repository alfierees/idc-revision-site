$\sqrt{Var(X)}$: the spread of $X$ in the same units as $X$, which fixes the units problem of variance.

## When to use

Remember $SD(bX+a)=|b|\,SD(X)$, and convert a variance to a standard deviation before standardizing a normal variable.

See [[Lec_05-Random Variables, Expected Value and Variance|Lec 05]].
