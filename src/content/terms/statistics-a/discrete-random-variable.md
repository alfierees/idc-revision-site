A random variable whose possible values can be listed in order with gaps between them.

## When to use

Describe it with a [[Distribution Table]] or [[Probability Mass Function]] and compute its [[Expected Value]] and [[Variance]] directly from the table.

See [[Lec_05-Random Variables, Expected Value and Variance|Lec 05]].
