$P(A|B)=P(A \cap B)/P(B)$ is the probability of $A$ once $B$ is known to have happened. Conditioning on $B$ shrinks the sample space to $B$.

## When to use

Use it whenever a question says "given" or "if we know". Direction matters: $P(A|B)$ and $P(B|A)$ share the numerator but have different denominators, and it can be read off a [[2x2 Probability Table]] as a cell divided by a margin.
