$P(A \cap B)=P(A|B)\,P(B)=P(B|A)\,P(A)$: the definition of conditional probability rearranged to give the probability of an intersection.

## When to use

Multiply along the branches of a tree diagram, and write the intersection two ways to derive Bayes' theorem. For independent events $P(A|B)=P(A)$, so it reduces to $P(A)P(B)$.

See [[Lec_03-Conditional Probability, Bayes and Independence|Lec 03]].
