A grid of $P(X=x_i, Y=y_j)$ with $X$ down the side and $Y$ across the top.

## When to use

Row and column sums give the marginal distributions, and a column divided by its total gives a conditional distribution. Use these sums as checks: all probabilities sum to 1.

See [[Lec_08-Joint Distributions, Conditional Distributions and Covariance|Lec 08]].
