$f(x)=F'(x)$, the rate of change of the CDF. The area under $f$ between two points is the probability of landing between them.

## When to use

The total area is 1 and a density can exceed 1, because it is a height, not a probability. Use $P(a \le X \le b)=F(b)-F(a)$ for intervals, and note $P(X=x)=0$ for any single value.
