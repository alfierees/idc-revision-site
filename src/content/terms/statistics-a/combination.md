An unordered selection: $n!/(k!(n-k)!)$ without replacement, and $\binom{n+k-1}{k}$ with replacement.

## When to use

Choose it when the question says a group, a hand or a committee (order does not matter); a race placing or a password is a permutation instead. Drawing without putting back is without replacement.
