Confusing the chance of the evidence given innocence with the chance of innocence given the evidence, as in the Sally Clark case.

## When to use

A reminder that $P(A|B) \ne P(B|A)$ in general; to reverse a conditional probability, use Bayes' theorem and include the prior.

See [[Lec_01-Sets and the Probability Function|Lec 01]].
