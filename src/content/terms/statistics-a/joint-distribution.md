The probability of every combination of values of two random variables, listed together with those combinations.

## When to use

It gives more accurate probabilities when you know the value of the other variable, and it reveals the relationship between the variables (through covariance and correlation).

See [[Lec_08-Joint Distributions, Conditional Distributions and Covariance|Lec 08]].
