A variable that takes the value 1 (success) with probability $p$ and 0 (failure) with probability $1-p$. Its expected value is $p$ and its variance is $p(1-p)$.

## When to use

Use it for a single yes/no trial, such as one shot with success probability 0.8 or a 20-sided die with $X=1$ if the roll is 5 or lower. Adding $n$ independent Bernoulli variables gives the [[Binomial Distribution]].
