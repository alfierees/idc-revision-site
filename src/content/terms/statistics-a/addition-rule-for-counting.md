If a condition can be met in different, mutually exclusive ways, the total number of outcomes is the sum of the counts for each way (3 routes or 4 roads gives $3+4=7$ ways).

## When to use

Use it when the options are alternatives; contrast with the multiplication principle, where choices happen in stages and the counts multiply. The lecture's three-digit numbers example (0 cannot lead) splits the count into cases and adds them.
