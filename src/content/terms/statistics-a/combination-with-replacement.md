An unordered selection where repeats are allowed, with $\binom{n+k-1}{k}$ outcomes. These outcomes are not equally likely: for three coin flips the 4 outcomes (3 heads, 2 heads, 1 head, 0 heads) have probabilities $1/8$, $3/8$, $3/8$, $1/8$.

## When to use

Counting alone cannot give the probability here, since the space is not symmetrical. The lecturer said this case is not in the exam material; only the other three counting cases are examined.

See [[Lec_04-Symmetrical Probability Spaces and Combinatorics|Lec 04]].
