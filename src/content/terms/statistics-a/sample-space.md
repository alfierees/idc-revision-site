The set $\Omega$ of all possible results of a random experiment; how finely to list results is a modelling choice (a coin may have only heads and tails, or also land on its edge).

## When to use

Fix it first when modelling an experiment. $P(\Omega)=1$, and in a symmetrical probability space $|\Omega|$ is the denominator of every probability.
