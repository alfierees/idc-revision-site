$n! = n(n-1)\cdots 1$, with the convention $0! = 1$.

## When to use

It is the building block of the permutation formula $n!/(n-k)!$ and the combination formula $n!/(k!(n-k)!)$; $n!$ also counts all orders of $n$ items, for example the finishing orders of 5 runners ($5!=120$).
