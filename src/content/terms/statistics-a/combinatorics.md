The counting methods used to find how many outcomes an experiment or event has, based on whether order matters and whether items are replaced.

## When to use

Use it in a [[Symmetrical Probability Space]], where a probability is the number of outcomes in the event divided by the number of outcomes in the sample space.

See [[Lec_04-Symmetrical Probability Spaces and Combinatorics|Lec 04]].
