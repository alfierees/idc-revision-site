An early model or sample of a product created to test and evaluate concepts, design, functionality and usability; a tool for thinking, not a final product.

## When to use

Use this in the second diamond, when building, testing and refining a solution with users. Covered in [[Lec_11-Practical Innovation 3|Lec 11]].
