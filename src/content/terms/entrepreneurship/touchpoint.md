Any moment of interaction between a customer and a brand, product, or service — online, in person, or via communication channels — before, during, or after a purchase.

## When to use

Use this in the Discover stage, when gathering and mapping evidence about users' experiences. Covered in [[Lec_10-Practical Innovation 2|Lec 10]].
