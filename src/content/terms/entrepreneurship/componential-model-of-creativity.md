Amabile & Pratt (2016) dynamic model: creativity = domain skills × creativity-relevant processes × task motivation, mirrored at the organisational level and linked by dynamic feedback loops.

## When to use

Use this when explaining what enables or suppresses creativity in people and teams. Covered in [[Lec_08-Problem Solving|Lec 08]].
