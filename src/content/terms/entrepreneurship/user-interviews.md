Listening-first discovery: define your learning goal, pick representative users, ask open-ended questions, record everything; primary goal is deep understanding of user experiences and needs.

## When to use

Use this in the Discover stage, when gathering and mapping evidence about users' experiences. Covered in [[Lec_10-Practical Innovation 2|Lec 10]].
