Something newly created that may never be used or benefit anyone; not the same as innovation (Wilson 2016).

## When to use

Use this when you need to separate the course's core vocabulary, especially in questions that contrast creativity, invention and innovation. Covered in [[Lec_01-Introduction to Innovation|Lec 01]].
