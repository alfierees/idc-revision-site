A climate where people can take risks and share unconventional ideas without fear of ridicule; precondition for divergent thinking and learning from failure.

## When to use

Use this when explaining what enables or suppresses creativity in people and teams. Covered in [[Lec_08-Problem Solving|Lec 08]].
