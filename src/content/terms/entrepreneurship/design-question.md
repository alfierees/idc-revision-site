The "How might we…" sentence that closes the first diamond: user + need + problem, broad enough to invite ideas, narrow enough to act on; must be framed *before* brainstorming.

## When to use

Use this at the end of the first diamond, before brainstorming solutions. Covered in [[Lec_10-Practical Innovation 2|Lec 10]].
