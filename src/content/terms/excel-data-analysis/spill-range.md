The block of cells that one formula fills automatically when it returns several values. You enter the formula once in the top-left cell and Excel fills the rest. Also in [[Lec_01-Sorting, Filters & Database Functions|Lec 01]].

## When to use

An [[XLOOKUP]] with a two-column return range spills first and last name into two cells, and one with a three-column range spills across three. [[SEQUENCE]] and [[FILTER]] spill the same way.

See [[Lec_06-Lookups with XLOOKUP and INDEX MATCH|Lec 06]].
