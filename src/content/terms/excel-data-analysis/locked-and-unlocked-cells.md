A per-cell flag set in Format Cells > Protection. Every cell is locked by default, but the flag only takes effect once [[Sheet Protection]] is switched on.

## When to use

Untick Locked on the cells people may edit first, then protect the sheet. On the Time Sheet only the month, year and start and stop cells are unlocked, so users cannot overwrite the date column, the hour formulas or the salary.

See [[Lec_05-Time Functions & Protecting Cells|Lec 05]].
