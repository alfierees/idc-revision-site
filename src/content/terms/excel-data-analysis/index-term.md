Returns the cell at a given row and column of a range, written `=INDEX(array, row_number, [column_number])`.

## When to use

It is the second half of [[INDEX and MATCH]], where [[MATCH]] supplies the row and column positions.

See [[Lec_06-Lookups with XLOOKUP and INDEX MATCH|Lec 06]].
