Returns the week of the year for a date, so 15 December 2024 is week 51.

## When to use

It sits beside [[WEEKDAY]] in the Lec 04 date-function sheet; use it when a question asks which week of the year a date falls in.

See [[Lec_04-Date Functions, Named Ranges & Data Validation|Lec 04]].
