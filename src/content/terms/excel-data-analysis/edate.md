Shifts a date forward or back by a whole number of months and keeps the same day number, so `=EDATE(E5,2)` moves 15 December 2024 to 15 February 2025. A negative number goes backwards.

## When to use

Use it to add months to a date; it is better than adding 60 days because months have different lengths.

See [[Lec_04-Date Functions, Named Ranges & Data Validation|Lec 04]].
