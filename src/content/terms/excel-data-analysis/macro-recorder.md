Records your clicks and writes them as VBA code. The output is verbose because it keeps every `Select` and every stray click.

## When to use

Used for the first macro exercises (the series macro and the erase macro). A recorded macro is just editable text, so you can open the editor and change a range such as `d10000`.

See [[Lec_08-VBA Macros|Lec 08]].
