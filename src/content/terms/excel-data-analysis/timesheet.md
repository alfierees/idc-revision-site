A monthly attendance sheet that turns start and stop times into regular hours, overtime and pay. The user types only the month and year, and the dates, hours and salary come from formulas.

## When to use

It combines the Lec 05 ideas: dates built with [[DATE]] and `+1`, hours as `(Stop-Start)*24`, an [[IF Function]] splitting regular hours (up to 8) from overtime, and [[Sheet Protection]] leaving only the inputs unlocked. In the class example, total salary was NIS 419.33.

See [[Lec_05-Time Functions & Protecting Cells|Lec 05]].
