Returns the hour component of a time as a whole number, so `=HOUR(E5)` on 9:45:22 gives 9. Its cell uses the General format, not a time format.

## When to use

Part of the group of functions (with `MINUTE` and `SECOND`) that take a time value apart, shown in the Time Functions sheet.

See [[Lec_05-Time Functions & Protecting Cells|Lec 05]].
