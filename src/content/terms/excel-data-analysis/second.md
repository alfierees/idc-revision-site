Returns the second component of a time as a whole number, so `SECOND` of 09:45:22 gives 22. It is the last of the three functions, with [[HOUR]] and [[MINUTE]], that take a time apart.

## When to use

Give the result cell the General format because it is a plain whole number. It is the counterpart of [[TIME]], which builds a time from its parts.

See [[Lec_05-Time Functions & Protecting Cells|Lec 05]].
