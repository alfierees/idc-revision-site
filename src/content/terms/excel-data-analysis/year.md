Extracts the year from a date, so `YEAR` of 15 December 2024 is 2024. It is the companion of [[DAY]] and [[MONTH]].

## When to use

Subtracting `YEAR` values is crude: 31 December 2023 to 1 January 2024 is one day but the year difference is 1. Use [[DATEDIF]] for complete years.

See [[Lec_04-Date Functions, Named Ranges & Data Validation|Lec 04]].
