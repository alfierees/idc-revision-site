Returns the minute component of a time as a whole number, so `MINUTE` of 09:45:22 gives 45. It pulls one part out of a time value, alongside [[HOUR]] and [[SECOND]].

## When to use

Give the result cell the General format, not a time format, because it is a plain whole number. Use it when you need to take a time apart rather than build one with [[TIME]].

See [[Lec_05-Time Functions & Protecting Cells|Lec 05]].
