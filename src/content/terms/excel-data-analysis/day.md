Extracts the day of the month from a date, as in `=DAY(E5)` giving 15; it is the companion of `MONTH` and `YEAR`.

## When to use

In the calendar the last day of the month is found with `DAY` of an [[EOMONTH]] result, as in `DAY($O$2)`, which a conditional-formatting rule then uses to hide overflow days.

See [[Lec_04-Date Functions, Named Ranges & Data Validation|Lec 04]].
