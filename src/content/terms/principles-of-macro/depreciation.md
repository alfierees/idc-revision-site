The loss of capital value through wear and tear. Subtracting it from GDP gives [[Net Domestic Product]], so $\text{Depreciation} = GDP - NDP$. Seen in: [Macro-Economics](/subjects/macro-economics).

## When to use

Keep gross and net consistent: net investment is gross investment minus depreciation, and output net of depreciation pairs with net investment. Mixing the two is the most common exam slip.

See [[Rec_02-The Three Approaches to Measuring GDP|Rec 02]]. Also in [[Rec_03-Capital Accumulation, the Savings Identity & GNP vs GDP|Rec 03]].
