A self-reinforcing cycle of low income, low saving and low investment, where $s f(k) < (\delta + n)k$ and capital per worker shrinks. Taught in [Lec 07](/subjects/principles-of-macro/lectures/lec-07-golden-rule-and-poverty-trap).

## When to use

Test it by comparing savings per worker with the break-even line at the given $k$.
