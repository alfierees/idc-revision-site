The real wage is the nominal wage deflated by prices, $W/P$: how many goods a worker's pay buys. Seen in: [Macro-Economics](/subjects/macro-economics).

## When to use

In competitive equilibrium $W/P = MPL$, so compute it by differentiating the production function; the price level cancels out of the firm's hiring decision.
