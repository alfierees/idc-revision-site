The nominal exchange rate adjusted for relative prices, $e P_f / P_d$. Taught in [Lec 10](/subjects/principles-of-macro/lectures/lec-10-key-drivers-of-gdp-uses).

## When to use

A real depreciation raises exports and lowers imports.
