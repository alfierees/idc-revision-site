Friedman's theory that consumption follows long-run expected income, so temporary income changes matter little. Taught in [Lec 10](/subjects/principles-of-macro/lectures/lec-10-key-drivers-of-gdp-uses).

## When to use

Use it to judge whether a temporary tax cut will raise consumption.
