Net exports are exports minus imports, $NX = EX - IM$. A positive value is an [[Export Surplus]] and a negative value an [[Import Surplus]]. Seen in: [Macro-Economics II](/subjects/macro-ii).

## When to use

It is the $NX$ in $Y = C + I + G + NX$ and in the [[Savings Identity]], where it is the part of savings lent abroad. Watch the sign the question asks for, since export and import surplus are negatives of each other.

See [[Rec_02-The Three Approaches to Measuring GDP|Rec 02]].
