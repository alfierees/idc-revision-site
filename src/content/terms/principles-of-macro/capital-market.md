The competitive market where firms rent capital until the real rental rate equals its marginal product, $R/P = MPK$. Capital demand is the $MPK$ curve, and in the short run capital supply is vertical at the existing stock.

## When to use

Use it for shock questions: more capital lowers $R/P$ but raises wages, while losing capital (an earthquake) raises the rental rate per unit but lowers total capital income $\alpha Y$. A higher unit rent does not mean higher total payments.
