The budget balance, $S_g = T - G$, where $T$ is net taxes (taxes less transfer payments); positive in surplus, negative in deficit.

## When to use

In Rec 03 Q6, taxes of 180 less transfers of 10 give $T = 170$ and $G = 50 + 90 = 140$, so $S_g = 30$. Transfers are not government spending on output. With a balanced budget $G = T$, so $S_g = 0$.
