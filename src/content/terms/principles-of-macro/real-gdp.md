Real GDP is output valued at constant base-year prices, $\sum_i p_i^{base} q_i^{t}$, or equivalently nominal GDP divided by the price-level change. It strips out price changes so only quantities move.

## When to use

Ask which year's quantities and which year's prices before multiplying. In the base year real and nominal GDP are equal, so use nominal GDP for the base year in growth-rate questions.
