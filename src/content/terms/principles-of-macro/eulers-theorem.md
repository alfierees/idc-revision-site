With constant returns to scale and competition, paying each factor its marginal product uses up exactly all output: $(1-\alpha)Y + \alpha Y = Y$.

## When to use

It is why the factor shares sum to one: labor income is $L \cdot MPL = (1-\alpha)Y$ and capital income is $K \cdot MPK = \alpha Y$, with nothing left over.
