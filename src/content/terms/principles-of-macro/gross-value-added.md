A firm's production (sales plus the change in inventory) minus the raw materials it buys from other firms: the extra value created at that stage. Subtracting depreciation gives net value added.

## When to use

It is step 1 of the exam recipe: value added = sales + change in inventory - payments to other firms - depreciation. Add government value added (its wages and rent) to get output $Y$.
