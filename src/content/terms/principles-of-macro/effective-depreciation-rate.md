The rate $n + \delta$, where $\delta$ is the capital depreciation rate and $n$ the labor (population) growth rate. It is the capital per worker that must be replaced or spread over new workers each period.

## When to use

It enters the Solow law of motion $\Delta k = sAk^{\alpha} - (n+\delta)k$ and the steady state $k^{*} = \left(\frac{sA}{\delta + n}\right)^{\frac{1}{1-\alpha}}$. These appear only on the formula sheet; there is no recitation on them.
