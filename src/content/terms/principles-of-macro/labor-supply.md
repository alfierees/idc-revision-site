Labor supply slopes upward: a higher [[Real Wage]] draws more people into work. Seen in: [Macro-Economics](/subjects/macro-economics).

## When to use

It is the second curve in the [[Labor Market]] diagram, crossing [[Labor Demand]] to set employment and the real wage. In the earthquake exercise it is taken to be unit-elastic.
