The inputs used to produce output, mainly labor and capital, whose owners earn the income side of GDP. Rec 02 lists four: workers (wages), physical capital owners (rent), financial capital owners (interest) and owners or shareholders (profit).

## When to use

These are the four income types the [[Income Approach]] adds up, and in Rec 05 and Rec 06 the factors are modelled as just $K$ and $L$.

See [[Rec_02-The Three Approaches to Measuring GDP|Rec 02]]. Also in [[Rec_05-Production at Full Employment — Cobb-Douglas, Marginal Products & Returns to Scale|Rec 05]].
