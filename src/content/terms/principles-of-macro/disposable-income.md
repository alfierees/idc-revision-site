The income households have left after taxes, $Y^d = Y - T$, which they can spend or save. The formula sheet writes $Y^d = Y - T - S_b$, with $T$ net taxes and $S_b$ zero unless stated.

## When to use

It gives private savings, $S_p = Y^d - C$. When a question supplies household and firm tables, follow Rec 03's route instead: household income minus household taxes minus consumption.

See [[Rec_03-Capital Accumulation, the Savings Identity & GNP vs GDP|Rec 03]]. Also in [[Read_01-Formula Sheet — Solow, Growth Accounting & the Open Economy|Read 01]].
