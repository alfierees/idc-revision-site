The relative change of any variable, $g = \frac{F_{t+s}}{F_t} - 1$; the average per-period rate uses the $s$-th root, $\bar{g} = \left(\frac{F_{t+s}}{F_t}\right)^{\frac{1}{s}} - 1$.

## When to use

Growth compounds, so never divide multi-year growth by the number of years: in Rec 01 a 19% two-year rise is 9.07% a year, not 9.5%. Inflation is the same formula applied to the price level.

See [[Rec_01-Nominal & Real GDP, Price Indices & GNP|Rec 01]]. Also in [Ex 04](/subjects/principles-of-macro/problem-sets/exercise-4).
