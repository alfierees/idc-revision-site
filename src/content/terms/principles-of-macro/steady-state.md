The steady state is where net investment per worker is zero, $\Delta k = 0$, so capital per worker stops changing. Under Cobb-Douglas, $k^{*} = (sA/(\delta + n))^{1/(1-\alpha)}$. Seen in: [Macro-Economics II](/subjects/macro-ii).

## When to use

Set the [[Solow Model]]'s law of motion to zero and solve for $k^{*}$, then $y^{*} = A k^{*\alpha}$. It appears only on the formula sheet in this course.

See [[Read_01-Formula Sheet — Solow, Growth Accounting & the Open Economy|Read 01]].
