The marginal product of capital is the extra output from one more unit of capital, holding labor fixed: $MPK = \partial Y/\partial K$. With Cobb-Douglas it is $MPK = \alpha A (L/K)^{1-\alpha}$, which falls as capital rises and rises as labor rises. Seen in: [Macro-Economics](/subjects/macro-economics).

## When to use

Differentiate the production function to get it, or read its sign off the ratio $L/K$. In equilibrium it equals the [[Real Rental Rate]], $R/P = MPK$.
