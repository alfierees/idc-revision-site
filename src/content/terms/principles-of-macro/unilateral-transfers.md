One-way payments such as gifts, grants and aid, recorded in the current account. Taught in [Lec 09](/subjects/principles-of-macro/lectures/lec-09-savings-investment-and-international-trade).

## When to use

Remember that a grant is a transfer, but a loan is a capital movement.
