The fractions of GDP paid to each factor. Under Cobb-Douglas with constant returns, capital gets $\alpha$ and labor gets $1-\alpha$, and the shares stay constant. Seen in: [Macro-Economics](/subjects/macro-economics).

## When to use

Total factor payments move one-for-one with output, so to sign a change in labor or capital income you only need to sign the change in $Y$. The shares change only if $\alpha$ itself changes.
