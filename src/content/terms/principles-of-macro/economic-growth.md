A sustained rise in output per person over time, measured by growth in real [[GDP per Capita]]. Growth rates combine multiplicatively: $(1+g_{\text{percap}})(1+g_{\text{pop}}) = 1+g_{\text{GDP}}$.

## When to use

Per-capita questions in Rec 01 use that product rule, and Rec 06 splits output growth into productivity, capital and labor contributions with [[Growth Accounting]]. Use real, not nominal, figures.

See [[Rec_06-The Labor & Capital Markets, Factor Payments & Growth Accounting|Rec 06]]. Also in [[Rec_05-Production at Full Employment — Cobb-Douglas, Marginal Products & Returns to Scale|Rec 05]].
