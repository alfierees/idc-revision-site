Investment is firms' spending on buildings, infrastructure, machines and equipment, plus the change in inventories (unsold new output adds to inventory). In this course only firms invest.

## When to use

It is the $I$ in $Y = C + I + G + NX$ and in the [[Savings Identity]]. Keep it gross or net of [[Depreciation]] consistently with the output measure you use: net investment is gross investment minus depreciation.

See [[Rec_03-Capital Accumulation, the Savings Identity & GNP vs GDP|Rec 03]].
