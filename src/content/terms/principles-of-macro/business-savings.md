The profit a firm keeps after taxes and dividends, $S_b = \text{Profit} - \text{Taxes} - \text{Dividends}$; only undistributed profit counts.

## When to use

In savings-identity questions, build $S_b$ for each firm from its P&L (revenue minus wages, payments to other firms, interest, taxes and dividends), then use it in $S_p + S_b + S_g = I + NX$. Dividends and interest become household income, so counting them here double counts. The formula sheet assumes $S_b = 0$ unless the question says otherwise.
