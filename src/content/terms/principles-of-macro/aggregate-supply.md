The output the economy can produce. In the [[Classical Model]] it is set on the supply side by the [[Production Function]]: capital, labor and productivity at full employment, $Y = A K^{\alpha} L^{1-\alpha}$. Seen in: [Macro-Economics II](/subjects/macro-ii).

## When to use

Rec 05 and Rec 06 name Aggregate Supply only as the topic their production-function and factor-market material builds toward; the notes do not teach it as a separate curve or model.

See [[Rec_05-Production at Full Employment — Cobb-Douglas, Marginal Products & Returns to Scale|Rec 05]]. Also in [[Rec_06-The Labor & Capital Markets, Factor Payments & Growth Accounting|Rec 06]].
