The Solow residual is the part of output growth that growth in capital and labor cannot explain, attributed to productivity growth, $\Delta A/A$. Seen in: [Macro-Economics](/subjects/macro-economics).

## When to use

In [[Growth Accounting]] questions, subtract the share-weighted input growth from output growth to solve for it. A negative residual means productivity fell, for example when output grew 2% but inputs should have delivered 4%.

See [[Rec_06-The Labor & Capital Markets, Factor Payments & Growth Accounting|Rec 06]].
