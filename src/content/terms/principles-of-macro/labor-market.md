The labor market is where the downward-sloping [[Labor Demand]] curve (the MPL curve) meets the upward-sloping [[Labor Supply]] curve, which pins down employment and the [[Real Wage]].

## When to use

Use it for comparative-statics questions: shock one factor, then trace the effect through the MPL to the real wage and employment. The wage bill is $(W/P) \times L$.
