NIFF is the income of foreign factors producing in the country (which leaves) minus the income of the country's own factors producing abroad (which comes in). It is the bridge between the two measures: $GNP = GDP - NIFF$.

## When to use

If $GDP > GNP$ then $NIFF > 0$. That wedge is about factor income, not exports and imports. With no information on payments to foreign factors, the course assumes $NIFF = 0$.
