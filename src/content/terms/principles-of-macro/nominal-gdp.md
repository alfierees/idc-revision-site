Nominal GDP is output valued at current-year prices, so it is affected by both changes in quantities and changes in prices.

## When to use

Compute it as price times quantity summed over goods for each year. It cannot tell you whether the economy produced more, so convert it to [[Real GDP]] first; in the base year the two are equal.

See [[Rec_01-Nominal & Real GDP, Price Indices & GNP|Rec 01]].
