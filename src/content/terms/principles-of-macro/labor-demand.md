Firms hire workers until the [[Real Wage]] equals the [[Marginal Product of Labor]], $W/P = MPL$. Because the MPL falls as labor rises, the labor-demand curve slopes downward. Seen in: [Macro-Economics](/subjects/macro-economics).

## When to use

Draw it as the MPL curve and cross it with [[Labor Supply]] to find employment and the real wage. The area under the curve up to employment is total output.
