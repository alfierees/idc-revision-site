The long-run model in which the economy is at full employment and output is set on the supply side by the [[Production Function]]. Labor and capital are traded in competitive markets and each is paid its marginal product.

## When to use

It frames Rec 05 and Rec 06: start from $Y = A K^{\alpha} L^{1-\alpha}$, then price the factors with $W/P = MPL$ and $R/P = MPK$.

See [[Rec_05-Production at Full Employment — Cobb-Douglas, Marginal Products & Returns to Scale|Rec 05]]. Also in [[Rec_06-The Labor & Capital Markets, Factor Payments & Growth Accounting|Rec 06]].
