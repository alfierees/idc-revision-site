The Solow model describes capital per worker $k = K/L$ by its law of motion, $\Delta k = sAk^{\alpha} - (n+\delta)k$: savings per worker minus the [[Effective Depreciation Rate]] times $k$. Seen in: [Macro-Economics II](/subjects/macro-ii).

## When to use

It appears only on the formula sheet; the course has no recitation on it. Set $\Delta k = 0$ to find the [[Steady State]], and note that here $s$ is the savings rate, not years.

See [[Read_01-Formula Sheet — Solow, Growth Accounting & the Open Economy|Read 01]].
