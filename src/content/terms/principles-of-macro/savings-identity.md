Total savings equal investment plus net exports, $S_p + S_b + S_g = I + NX$ with $NX = EX - IM$. It follows from setting the expenditure and income decompositions of output equal.

## When to use

Build each sector's savings from the data, then solve for the missing item, usually [[Investment]]. A country that saves more than it invests runs an export surplus and lends the excess abroad.
