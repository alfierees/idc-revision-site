The savings rate that gives maximum consumption per worker; under Cobb-Douglas it equals $\alpha$, so $s^{GR} = \alpha$, with consumption per worker $c = (1-s)y$. Seen in: [Macro-Economics II](/subjects/macro-ii).

## When to use

It appears only on the formula sheet, which has no recitation behind it: read it as setting the savings rate equal to capital's share of output.

See [[Read_01-Formula Sheet — Solow, Growth Accounting & the Open Economy|Read 01]].
