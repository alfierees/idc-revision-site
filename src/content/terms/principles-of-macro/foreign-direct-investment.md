Investment in the economy that is owned and operated by foreign entities. Also called FDI. Taught in [Lec 05](/subjects/principles-of-macro/lectures/lec-05-production-and-growth).

## When to use

Use it to tell apart foreign ownership from foreign portfolio investment.
