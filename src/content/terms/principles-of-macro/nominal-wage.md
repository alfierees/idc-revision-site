The nominal wage $W$ is the wage quoted in money, for example 4000 NIS a month.

## When to use

Divide it by the price level to get the [[Real Wage]] $W/P$, which is what the classical labor-market analysis works with.

See [[Rec_06-The Labor & Capital Markets, Factor Payments & Growth Accounting|Rec 06]].
