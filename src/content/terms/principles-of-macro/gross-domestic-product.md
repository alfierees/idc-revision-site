The total market value of all final goods and services produced within a country's borders in a period, written $Y$ and computed as $\sum_i p_i q_i$. It is a geographic measure. Seen in: [Macro-Economics](/subjects/macro-economics).

## When to use

It can be built three ways (value added, income, expenditure) that must agree, which gives a self-check on exam questions. Only final goods count, to avoid double counting intermediate goods. Definition questions turn on borders, not who owns or buys the output.

See [[Rec_01-Nominal & Real GDP, Price Indices & GNP|Rec 01]]. Also in [Ex 04](/subjects/principles-of-macro/problem-sets/exercise-4).
