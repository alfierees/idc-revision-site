The production function says how output depends on the inputs, $Y = F(K, L)$, and with productivity $A$ included $Y = F(K, L, A)$. The course uses the [[Cobb-Douglas Production Function]]. Seen in: [Macro-Economics II](/subjects/macro-ii), [Macro-Economics](/subjects/macro-economics).

## When to use

Differentiate it to get the marginal products, or scale all inputs by $t$ to read off [[Returns to Scale]]. It is the starting point of the classical full-employment model.

See [[Rec_05-Production at Full Employment — Cobb-Douglas, Marginal Products & Returns to Scale|Rec 05]].
