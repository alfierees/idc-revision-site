Total factor productivity is the general productivity of the economy, the multiplier $A$ in $Y = AK^{\alpha}L^{1-\alpha}$. With the same capital and labor, better technology, organisation or institutions raise output in proportion to $A$. Seen in: [Macro-Economics](/subjects/macro-economics).

## When to use

Because $A$ enters multiplicatively, a 50% rise in $A$ raises output by 50%. In [[Growth Accounting]] its growth, $\Delta A/A$, is the residual left after input growth.
