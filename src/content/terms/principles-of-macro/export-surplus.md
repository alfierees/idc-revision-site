Exports exceed imports ($NX > 0$): the country saves more than it invests and lends the difference abroad.

## When to use

It follows from $S = I + NX$. Watch the sign the question asks for: import and export surplus are negatives of each other, so $NX = -20$ is an export surplus of $-20$, an import surplus of 20.
