Measures GDP as the total income earned from production: wages, rent, interest and profit paid to the four factors of production. Net value added equals originated income, so adding depreciation (and taxes, where the question includes them) gives the gross figure.

## When to use

Everything a firm adds must be paid out to someone, which is why the income total matches value added and expenditure. In MacroLand, wages 450, rent 180 and profit 790 give $Y = 1{,}420$, the same as the other two approaches.
