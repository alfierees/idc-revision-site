Private consumption is households' spending on firms' products (food, clothing, media), the $C$ in the expenditure identity $Y = C + I + G + NX$.

## When to use

Plug it into the expenditure identity, or back it out once output, investment, government spending and net exports are known. It also enters private savings, $S_p = Y^d - C$.

See [[Rec_02-The Three Approaches to Measuring GDP|Rec 02]].
