The share of each extra unit of disposable income that goes to consumption. Also called MPC. Taught in [Lec 10](/subjects/principles-of-macro/lectures/lec-10-key-drivers-of-gdp-uses).

## When to use

Use it with $MPC + MPS = 1$.
