Inflation is the percentage change of a price index between two years, $\pi = p_{t+s}/p_t - 1$; with the CPI in one-year steps it is $\pi_t = (CPI_t - CPI_{t-1})/CPI_{t-1} \times 100$. The average yearly rate over $s$ years is $(p_{t+s}/p_t)^{1/s} - 1$. Seen in: [Macro-Economics](/subjects/macro-economics).

## When to use

Use it to turn a price index into a growth rate, or to deflate [[Nominal GDP]] into [[Real GDP]]. Remember that $s$ here is the number of years, not the savings rate.

See [[Rec_01-Nominal & Real GDP, Price Indices & GNP|Rec 01]].
