The price index implied by GDP: nominal GDP divided by real GDP. Its percentage change is the GDP inflator, $\pi = \frac{p_{t+s}}{p_t} - 1$. Seen in: [Macro-Economics](/subjects/macro-economics).

## When to use

Real GDP is nominal GDP divided by the deflator, and in the base year the deflator is 1, so real equals nominal. Divide by $(1+\pi)$ to deflate; do not subtract $\pi$.

See [[Rec_01-Nominal & Real GDP, Price Indices & GNP|Rec 01]]. Also in [Ex 04](/subjects/principles-of-macro/problem-sets/exercise-4).
