The output elasticity of an input is the percentage change in output caused by a 1% change in that input, holding the other fixed. In Cobb-Douglas, $\alpha$ is the elasticity of capital and $1-\alpha$ that of labor.

## When to use

Raising one input by $g\%$ raises output by only (its exponent) $\times\ g\%$. The two elasticities sum to 1 under constant returns, so raising both inputs by 1% raises output by 1%.
