The market where saving supplies funds and borrowers demand them, with the interest rate as the price. Taught in [Lec 08](/subjects/principles-of-macro/lectures/lec-08-savings-and-investment).

## When to use

Use it to predict how shocks to saving or investment move the interest rate.
