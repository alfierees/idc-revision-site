Modigliani's theory that people smooth consumption over their lifetime: borrow when young, save in middle age, dissave in retirement. Taught in [Lec 10](/subjects/principles-of-macro/lectures/lec-10-key-drivers-of-gdp-uses).

## When to use

Use it to explain saving differences by age.
