The PPI is the cost of a basket of domestically produced goods using current-year quantities (a Paasche-type index).

## When to use

Price the current year's quantities at each year's prices, divide, and subtract 1 to get the change. Imported goods are excluded, whereas investment goods such as machines are included; the [[Consumer Price Index]] does the reverse, with base-year quantities.
