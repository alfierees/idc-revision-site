An index of the cost of a fixed basket of consumer goods at base-year quantities, $\text{CPI}_t = \frac{\text{basket value}_t}{\text{basket value}_{\text{base}}} \times 100$. Holding quantities fixed means changes reflect prices only. Seen in: [Macro-Economics](/subjects/macro-economics).

## When to use

Price the base-year basket at old and new prices and take the ratio minus 1. The inflation rate is the year-on-year change in the index, not the change from the base year. Exclude investment goods from the CPI; it differs from the [[Producer Price Index]] only because the weights differ.
