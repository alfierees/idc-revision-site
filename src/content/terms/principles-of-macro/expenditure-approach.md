Measures GDP as total spending on final goods, $Y = C + I + G + NX$ (or $Y = C + I + G$ in a closed economy). It must equal the value-added and income approaches.

## When to use

In the exam recipe, take $Y$ from value added and plug it into $Y = C + I + G + NX$ with net investment to back out the missing piece, usually $C$. $G$ includes all government spending (purchases plus wages and rent), and unsold output counts as investment through inventories.

See [[Rec_02-The Three Approaches to Measuring GDP|Rec 02]]. Also in [[Rec_03-Capital Accumulation, the Savings Identity & GNP vs GDP|Rec 03]].
