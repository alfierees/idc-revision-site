The real rental rate $R/P$ is the price of renting one unit of capital in terms of goods, where $R$ is the nominal rent and $P$ the price of the final good. In competitive equilibrium $R/P = MPK$.

## When to use

It is set where the downward-sloping [[Marginal Product of Capital]] curve meets the vertical capital supply. A higher rate per unit does not mean higher total capital income, which is $\alpha Y$ under Cobb-Douglas.
