The value of final output produced by a country's residents wherever they produce it: GDP plus net factor income from abroad, $GNP = GDP + NIFF$ when NIFF is income earned abroad less income foreigners earn at home. Seen in: [Macro-Economics](/subjects/macro-economics).

## When to use

An Israeli resident working in Japan counts in Israel's GNP and in Japan's GDP. Rec 03 and the formula sheet define NIFF the other way round, as net payments to foreign factors, and write $GNP = GDP - NIFF$; check which convention a question uses. The GDP and GNP gap is factor income, not the trade balance.

See [[Rec_01-Nominal & Real GDP, Price Indices & GNP|Rec 01]]. Also in [[Rec_03-Capital Accumulation, the Savings Identity & GNP vs GDP|Rec 03]].
