The value of final output produced by a country's residents wherever they produce it: $GNP = GDP - NIFF$, where NIFF is net payments to foreign factors (what foreign factors earn here minus what residents earn abroad). Seen in: [Macro-Economics](/subjects/macro-economics).

## When to use

An Israeli resident working in Japan counts in Israel's GNP and in Japan's GDP. Rec 01 describes the same link as GDP plus net factor income *from abroad*, which is simply $-NIFF$. The GDP and GNP gap is factor income, not the trade balance.

See [[Rec_01-Nominal & Real GDP, Price Indices & GNP|Rec 01]]. Also in [[Rec_03-Capital Accumulation, the Savings Identity & GNP vs GDP|Rec 03]].
