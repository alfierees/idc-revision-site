The total income paid to a factor, its price times its quantity: the real wage bill is $\frac{W}{P} L = MPL \cdot L$ and capital's payment is $\frac{R}{P} K = MPK \cdot K$.

## When to use

Under Cobb-Douglas these equal $(1-\alpha)Y$ and $\alpha Y$. The trap is treating a higher rate per unit as higher total payments: a capital loss raises the rental rate but lowers total capital income.
