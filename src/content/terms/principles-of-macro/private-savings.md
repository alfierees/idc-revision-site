Private (household) savings is disposable income not consumed, $S_p = Y^d - C$. In a recitation table it is household net income minus household taxes minus consumption.

## When to use

It is one of the three savings components that add up to $S$ in the [[Savings Identity]]. Dividends and interest paid out by firms are household income, so they belong in $S_p$, not in [[Business Savings]].
