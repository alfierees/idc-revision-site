The decomposition of output growth into productivity, capital and labor contributions weighted by factor shares: $\frac{\Delta Y}{Y} = \frac{\Delta A}{A} + \alpha\frac{\Delta K}{K} + (1-\alpha)\frac{\Delta L}{L}$.

## When to use

Given output, capital and labor growth and the shares, solve for the unknown term, usually productivity. In Rec 06's Aspamia example inputs should deliver 4% but output grew 2%, so productivity fell 2%. The formula sheet writes the shares as $S_K$ and $S_L$.

See [[Rec_06-The Labor & Capital Markets, Factor Payments & Growth Accounting|Rec 06]]. Also in [[Read_01-Formula Sheet — Solow, Growth Accounting & the Open Economy|Read 01]].
