GDP divided by population, a first proxy for average material living standards that ignores distribution.

## When to use

Growth rates multiply: $(1+g_{\text{percap}})(1+g_{\text{pop}}) = 1+g_{\text{GDP}}$, so 5% real growth with 2% population growth gives about 3% per capita. Use real GDP per capita for living standards, since nominal can rise on inflation alone.
