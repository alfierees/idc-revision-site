Net domestic product is GDP minus depreciation, $NDP = GDP - \text{depreciation}$: the output left after replacing the capital that wore out during the year.

## When to use

Given two of GDP, NDP and [[Depreciation]] you can back out the third. If you work with NDP, [[Investment]] must be net as well.

See [[Rec_03-Capital Accumulation, the Savings Identity & GNP vs GDP|Rec 03]].
