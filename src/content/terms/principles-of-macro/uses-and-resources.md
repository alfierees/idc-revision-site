The uses-and-resources report balances what the economy has available against what it is spent on: $Y + IM = C + I + G + EX$, which rearranges to $Y = C + I + G + NX$.

## When to use

It is the final-expenditure route to GDP. Imports join the resources side and exports the uses side; keep output and investment both gross or both net of depreciation.
