A ceiling on the valuation used to convert a [[SAFE]] into shares at the next priced round. If the round values the company above the cap, the SAFE converts at the lower cap price, so the early investor gets more shares per dollar than the new investors.

See [[Session 3 - Raising Debt and Capital|Session 3]].
