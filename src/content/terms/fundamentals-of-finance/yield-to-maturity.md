The market discount rate that equates a [[Bond]]'s price to the [[Present Value]] of its cash flows. Price and yield move in opposite directions, and the relationship is convex.

## When to use

Compare coupon rate to yield: coupon above yield means a premium bond, equal means par, below means a discount bond. Excel: `YIELD`, `RATE`; the price is `-PV(r, n, C, FV)`.
