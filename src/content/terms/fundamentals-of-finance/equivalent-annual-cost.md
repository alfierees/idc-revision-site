EAC spreads a project's cost over its life as an annuity, $NPV\div$ the annuity factor, so projects with different lifespans compare on a like-for-like annual basis. The equivalent annual value (EAV) is the same idea for benefits.

## When to use

Use it when alternatives repeat or have different horizons, such as machine replacement or lease versus buy. Excel: $EAC=-\texttt{PMT}(r,n,NPV)$; choose the lower cost, or the higher annual value.
