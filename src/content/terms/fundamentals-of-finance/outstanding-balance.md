The [[Present Value]] of a [[Loan]]'s remaining payments at the contract rate; what must still be repaid to close the loan. For an equal-payment loan with $k$ payments left, $B_t=-\texttt{PV}(r,k,E)$.

## When to use

A standard loan question (Lesson 05 calls it one of three things you will always be asked): find the balance at time $t$. For loans with uneven payments read it off the amortization table or discount the remaining cash flows. It is also the reference for any early-termination fee, see [[Refinancing]].

See [[Lesson_05-The Four Loan Types|Lesson 05]].
