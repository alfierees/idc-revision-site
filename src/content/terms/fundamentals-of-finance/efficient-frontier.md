The minimum variance frontier is the left edge of every achievable ($\sigma$, $E(r)$) pair: for each expected return, the portfolio with the lowest variance. Its upper half, above the [[Minimum Variance Portfolio]], is the efficient frontier.

## When to use

Use it to tell efficient from dominated portfolios: below the minimum variance portfolio you can raise return at no extra risk. With many assets each frontier portfolio holds all of them. Taught in [[Lesson_08-Risk, Return & Diversification]] and [[Lesson_09-Capital Market Line]].
