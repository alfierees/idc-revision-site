$PI=NPV/I$: the [[Net Present Value]] created per dollar of initial investment. Some texts use $PV/I$; the course uses $NPV/I$.

## When to use

Use it to rank projects under a budget constraint (capital rationing): rank by highest PI and spend down the budget, funding the last project fractionally if it is divisible. For mutually exclusive projects just pick the highest NPV.
