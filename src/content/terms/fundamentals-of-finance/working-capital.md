Cash tied up in inventory and receivables net of payables. An increase ties up cash (a negative cash flow) and is recovered (positive) when the project winds down.

## When to use

Subtract $\Delta WC$ in the [[Free Cash Flow]] build each year, and recover the cumulative working capital at the end of the project before computing [[Net Present Value]].

See [[Lesson_06-Capital Budgeting & Free Cash Flow|Lesson 06]].
