$\pi=CPI_{end}/CPI_{start}-1$: the rate at which the price level rises, which erodes what each dollar can buy. For example, a CPI moving from 1024 to 1047 is $\pi=2.25\%$.

## When to use

Use it with the [[Fisher Equation]] to get the real rate. Use expected inflation to value things today and actual inflation to re-price later, and scale payments contractually linked to inflation before discounting.
