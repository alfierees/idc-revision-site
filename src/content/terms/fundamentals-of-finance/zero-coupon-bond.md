A [[Bond]] with no [[Coupon]]s, just one payment of [[Face Value]] at maturity. Pure discounting: $P=FV/(1+r)^n$.

## When to use

A zero always trades at a discount, since there are no coupons to lift the price. Example from the lecture: a 4-year zero paying 100 at $r=6\%$ is worth $100/1.06^4=79.21$.
