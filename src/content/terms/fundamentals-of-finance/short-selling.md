Selling a borrowed share now ($+P_0$) and buying it back later ($-P_1-Div_1$), which gives the asset a negative portfolio weight. Per 1 received you repay $1+r$, so a short sale is borrowing at the stock's own return.

## When to use

Use it when a portfolio weight is negative or above 100% (e.g. $x_A=1.4$, $x_B=-0.4$). It extends the frontier beyond the two assets, but losses are unbounded. Taught in [[Lesson_08-Risk, Return & Diversification]].
