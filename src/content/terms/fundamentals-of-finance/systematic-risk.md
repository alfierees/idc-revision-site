The market-driven, undiversifiable part of an asset's total variance; it is what [[Beta]] measures and what the market pays for. It is $\sigma^2_{systematic}=\beta^2\sigma_M^2$.

## When to use

Total variance splits into systematic and [[Idiosyncratic Risk]]: $\sigma^2_{specific}=\sigma^2_{total}-\sigma^2_{systematic}$, and the share of systematic risk is systematic variance divided by total variance. Taught in [[Lesson_10-Security Market Line & CAPM]]; [[Lesson_08-Risk, Return & Diversification]] shows why it survives diversification.
