Consumer Price Index: the price level of a fixed basket of consumption goods. Inflation is the percentage change in the CPI between two dates.

## When to use

Use it to compute [[Inflation]] as $CPI_{end}/CPI_{start}-1$, or to scale a payment that is contractually linked to inflation before discounting it.

See [[Lesson_04-Inflation, Real Rates & Expected Inflation|Lesson 04]].
