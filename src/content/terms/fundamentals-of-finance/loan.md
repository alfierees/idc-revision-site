Debt repaid through interest plus principal; every payment splits into interest (always the rate times the current balance) and principal. The four types differ only in how the principal is scheduled: interest-only, equal-principal, equal-payment and bullet.

## When to use

Loan questions ask for the payment, the [[Outstanding Balance]] at a given date, or the split of one payment into interest and principal, using an [[Amortization]] table. For an equal-payment loan see [[Mortgage]]; for a lump sum at the end see [[Bullet Loan]].
