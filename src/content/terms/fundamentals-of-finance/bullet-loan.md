A loan where a fixed payment is made each period and the remaining principal is cleared in one final balloon at maturity. If the payment is below the interest due the balance grows, and if it exceeds the interest the balance falls gradually.

## When to use

Use it for questions with a fixed payment that does not fully amortize the loan, where you must find the final balloon or compare payment to interest ($E$ against $rL$). It is one of the four loan types in [[Amortization]].

See [[Lesson_05-The Four Loan Types|Lesson 05]].
