Return measured in purchasing power: the [[Nominal Rate]] with [[Inflation]] stripped out. It is a ratio, $r_{real}=\frac{1+r_{nom}}{1+\pi}-1$, not a subtraction (see the [[Fisher Equation]]).

## When to use

Discount real cash flows (such as an inflation-protected payment) at the real rate and nominal cash flows at the nominal rate; both routes give the same PV if used consistently. The approximation $r_{nom}-\pi$ is only good for small rates.

See [[Lesson_04-Inflation, Real Rates & Expected Inflation|Lesson 04]].
