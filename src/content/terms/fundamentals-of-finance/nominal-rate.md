A return measured in money units, not adjusted for [[Inflation]]. It is what the bank pays in dollars, as opposed to the [[Real Interest Rate]], which measures purchasing power.

## When to use

Discount nominal cash flows at the nominal rate and real cash flows at the real rate; convert between them with the [[Fisher Equation]].

See [[Lesson_04-Inflation, Real Rates & Expected Inflation|Lesson 04]].
