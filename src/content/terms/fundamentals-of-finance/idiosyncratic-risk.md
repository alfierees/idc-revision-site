The asset-specific, diversifiable part of total variance, also called specific risk. Total variance is systematic plus specific, so $\sigma^2_{specific}=\sigma^2_{total}-\beta^2\sigma_M^2$.

## When to use

Use it to split an asset's risk: the gap between an asset and the Capital Market Line is its specific risk, and [[Diversification]] removes it. Taught in [[Lesson_10-Security Market Line & CAPM]] (variance decomposition $\sigma_i^2=\beta_i^2\sigma_m^2+\sigma_\varepsilon^2$) and [[Lesson_08-Risk, Return & Diversification]].
