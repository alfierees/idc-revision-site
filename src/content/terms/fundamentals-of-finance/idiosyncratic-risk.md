The asset-specific, diversifiable part of total variance, also called specific risk. Total variance is systematic plus specific, so $\sigma^2_{specific}=\sigma^2_{total}-\beta^2\sigma_M^2$.

## When to use

Use it to split an asset's risk: the gap between an asset and the Capital Market Line is its specific risk, and [[Diversification]] removes it. The cheat sheet is the only course source for this split.
