The extra expected return investors demand for bearing risk: cost of capital $=$ risk-free rate $+$ risk premium. The market risk premium is $E(r_m)-r_f$, and in the CAPM an asset's premium is $\beta_i[E(r_m)-r_f]$.

## When to use

Estimate the market premium from history (about 7% over 1926 to 2008) or forward-looking from the Gordon model (about 5% in the deck's example). Taught in [[Lesson_08-Risk, Return & Diversification]] and [[Lesson_10-Security Market Line & CAPM]].
