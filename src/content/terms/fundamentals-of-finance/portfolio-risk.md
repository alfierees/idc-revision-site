The variance of a two-asset portfolio: $\sigma_p^2=w_1^2\sigma_1^2+w_2^2\sigma_2^2+2w_1w_2\sigma_1\sigma_2\rho$, where $\rho$ is the [[Correlation]] between the assets. For three assets, add every asset's own variance term plus twice every pair's covariance term.

## When to use

Used with [[Expected Return]] in the risk and diversification questions of HW 09. A lower $\rho$ lowers portfolio risk, which is the whole point of [[Diversification]] and of finding the [[Minimum Variance Portfolio]].

See [[HW_09-Risk, Return & Diversification|HW 09]].
