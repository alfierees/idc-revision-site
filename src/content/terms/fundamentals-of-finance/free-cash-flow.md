$EBIT(1-\tau)+Dep-CAPEX-\Delta WC$: the marginal, after-tax cash a project generates, ignoring financing. Salvage proceeds, net of capital-gains tax, are added at the end.

## When to use

Use it to build each year's cash flow before discounting at the project's $r$. Exclude interest and loan repayments, ignore sunk costs, include opportunity costs, synergies and cannibalization, and recover [[Working Capital]] at the end.
