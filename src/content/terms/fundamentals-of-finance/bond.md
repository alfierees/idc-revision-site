A loan to the issuer that pays periodic [[Coupon]]s plus the [[Face Value]] at maturity. Its price is the present value of those cash flows discounted at the market yield, and price and yield move in opposite directions.

## When to use

Use it for pricing questions: price a straight bond with Excel `-PV(r, n, C, FV)`, classify it as premium, par or discount, and re-price it after the market yield changes. Also covers prices before versus after a coupon, partial-redemption and CPI-linked bonds.
