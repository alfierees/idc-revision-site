$1+r_{real}=\frac{1+r_{nom}}{1+\pi}$: the exact link between real and nominal rates, a ratio rather than a subtraction. The shortcut $r_{real}\approx r_{nom}-\pi$ only works for small rates.

## When to use

Use it to convert between nominal and real rates, then match cash flows to rates: discount nominal flows at the nominal rate and real flows at the real rate. Both routes give the same present value.
