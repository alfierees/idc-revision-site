Deciding which projects to fund by comparing their incremental cash flows. The goal is to get the right cash flow for each year into the [[Net Present Value]], following five principles: cash is king, only marginal effects, opportunity cost, separate financing from investment, and depreciation is not cash.

## When to use

Use it for any project-evaluation question that gives sales, costs, CAPEX and tax: build the [[Free Cash Flow]] each year, ignore sunk costs, include synergies and cannibalization, and then apply NPV.
