$\rho=Cov(A,B)/(\sigma_A\sigma_B)$: how closely two assets' returns move together, from -1 to 1. A positive covariance means they move together, zero means unrelated, and negative means they move in opposite directions.

## When to use

It is the cross term in the [[Portfolio Risk]] formula, so a lower $\rho$ gives a lower portfolio risk. Watch the special cases: $\rho=-1$ allows a riskless hedge, and $\rho=1$ gives no [[Diversification]] benefit.
