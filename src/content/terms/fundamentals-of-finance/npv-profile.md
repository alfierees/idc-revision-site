A plot of [[Net Present Value]] against the [[Discount Rate]]. It crosses zero at the [[Internal Rate of Return]], so the project is accepted at any rate below the IRR, and it shows where two projects' rankings cross over.

## When to use

Use it to see why IRR and NPV can rank mutually exclusive projects differently, and to spot multiple IRRs when the cash flows change sign more than once (HW 02).

See [[Lesson_02-Perpetuities, NPV & IRR|Lesson 02]].
