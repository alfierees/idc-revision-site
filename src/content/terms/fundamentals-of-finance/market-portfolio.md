The value-weighted portfolio of all risky assets, $x_i=V_i/V_M$. In equilibrium it equals everyone's tangency (MVE) portfolio. In practice it is proxied by the S&P 500.

## When to use

It anchors both lines: the [[Capital Market Line]] runs from $r_f$ through $M$, and on the [[Security Market Line]] $M$ has $\beta=1$. Taught in [[Lesson_09-Capital Market Line]].
