The principal a [[Bond]] repays at maturity, also called par value (for example 100 or 1000). The [[Coupon]] is quoted as a rate on it.

## When to use

Use it as the final cash flow when pricing a bond, and to compare price with par: a price above face value is a premium bond and below is a discount bond. Prices are pulled to par as maturity approaches.
