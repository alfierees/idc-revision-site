The table that splits each loan payment into interest and principal and tracks the balance period by period. Interest is always $r$ times the current balance, and what separates the four [[Loan]] types is how the principal is scheduled.

## When to use

Use it whenever a question asks for the interest or principal in a given payment, or the [[Outstanding Balance]] after some years. Excel `PMT`, `IPMT` and `PPMT` build the schedule for an equal-payment [[Mortgage]].
