The mix of two assets $A$ and $B$ with the lowest possible [[Portfolio Risk]]. With correlation $\rho=-1$ risk can be driven to zero; with $\rho=1$ there is no diversification, so the MVP is simply the less risky asset.

## When to use

Used in two-asset risk questions (HW 09): find the weight $X_A$ that minimises portfolio variance. The cheat sheet gives the general formula $X_A=\frac{\sigma_B^2-\rho\sigma_A\sigma_B}{\sigma_A^2+\sigma_B^2-2\rho\sigma_A\sigma_B}$ and shortcuts for $\rho=-1$, $0$ and $1$.

See [[Read_01-Finance Cheat Sheet|Cheat sheet]].
