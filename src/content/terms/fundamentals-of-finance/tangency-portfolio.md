The mix of risky assets where a line from $r_f$ just touches the efficient frontier: the mean-variance efficient (MVE) portfolio, with the highest [[Sharpe Ratio]] of any risky portfolio.

## When to use

It is the only risky portfolio a mean-variance investor should hold, whatever their risk tolerance; risk is then set by the split with $r_f$. In equilibrium it equals the [[Market Portfolio]]. The course does not ask you to compute its weights for three or more assets. Taught in [[Lesson_09-Capital Market Line]].
