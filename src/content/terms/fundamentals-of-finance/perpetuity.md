A level payment $C$ every period forever. Letting an [[Annuity]] run to infinity gives $PV=C/r$, so most of the value sits in the early payments.

## When to use

Use it for any never-ending level stream; if the payments grow at $g$, switch to the [[Growing Perpetuity]], $PV=C/(r-g)$, valid only when $r>g$.

See [[Lesson_02-Perpetuities, NPV & IRR|Lesson 02]].
