The excess return per unit of total risk, $\frac{E(r_i)-r_f}{\sigma_i}$: the slope of the line from $r_f$ through the portfolio.

## When to use

Use it to find efficient portfolios: they all have the highest Sharpe ratio, equal to the slope of the [[Capital Market Line]] (e.g. portfolios with Sharpe 0.8, 0.55, 0.8, 0.76: the two with 0.8 are efficient). Taught in [[Lesson_09-Capital Market Line]] and [[Lesson_11-Final Tutorial]].
