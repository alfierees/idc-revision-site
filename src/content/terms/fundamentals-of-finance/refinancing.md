Replacing a [[Loan]] with a new one when market rates change. The old loan's fair value (remaining payments discounted at the new market rate) is compared with its book [[Outstanding Balance]].

## When to use

In the early-termination fee question, if rates have fallen the loan is worth more to the bank than its balance, so the fee is $\max(\text{fair value}-\text{book balance},0)$ (HW 06).

See [[Lesson_05-The Four Loan Types|Lesson 05]].
