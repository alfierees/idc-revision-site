The periodic interest a [[Bond]] pays, quoted as a rate on the [[Face Value]] (for example 5% on a face value of 100 pays 5 a year). The coupons form an annuity inside the bond price.

## When to use

Use it when pricing a bond: compare the coupon rate with the market yield to tell a premium, par or discount bond. For semi-annual coupons use half the stated coupon on each date.
