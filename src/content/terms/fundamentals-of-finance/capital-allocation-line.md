The straight line of all mixes of one risky portfolio A and the riskless asset: $E(r_p)=r_f+\frac{E(r_A)-r_f}{\sigma_A}\sigma_p$, with $\sigma_p=x_A\sigma_A$. Its slope is A's [[Sharpe Ratio]].

## When to use

Use it whenever an investor splits wealth between one risky portfolio and $r_f$; $x_A>1$ means borrowing. The CAL through the [[Tangency Portfolio]] is the optimal one, and in equilibrium it is the [[Capital Market Line]]. Taught in [[Lesson_09-Capital Market Line]].
