The Gordon formula $PV=C/(r-g)$ for payments that grow at $g$ forever, where $C$ is the first payment. It is valid only if $r>g$; otherwise the sum diverges.

## When to use

Use it to value an endless growing stream. For $C=100$, $r=10\%$, $g=3\%$ it gives $100/0.07=1{,}428.57$.
