A non-cash expense that lowers taxable profit and so creates a tax shield of $\tau\times Dep$. It is subtracted to compute taxable EBIT and then added back after tax, because no cash actually leaves.

## When to use

Use it when building [[Free Cash Flow]]: it is deducted before tax and added back afterwards. For example, depreciation of 12,000 at a 25% tax rate saves 3,000 of tax.
