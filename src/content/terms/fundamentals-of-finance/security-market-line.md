The line giving any asset's required return from its [[Beta]]: $E(R)=r_f+[E(R_m)-r_f]\beta$, where $E(R_m)-r_f$ is the risk premium. This is the CAPM, and it prices single assets and inefficient portfolios.

## When to use

If you are given $\beta$, always use the SML, even for an efficient portfolio. The resulting $E(R)$ can be used as the discount rate $r$ (PP 01). For efficient portfolios mixing the risk-free asset and the market, use the [[Capital Market Line]] instead.
