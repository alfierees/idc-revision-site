$FV=C(1+r)^t$: compounding money forward to a later date, so that each period's interest itself earns interest. Simple interest instead pays $r$ only on the original principal, $C(1+rt)$.

## When to use

Use it to push a cash flow forward in time at rate $r$, or when the question asks what an investment grows to. Excel `FV`. Over long horizons the value is very sensitive to $r$.
