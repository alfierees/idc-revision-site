Combining imperfectly correlated assets so that they partly offset each other, which removes [[Idiosyncratic Risk]]. For $n$ equal-risk assets the risk falls towards a floor of $\sigma\sqrt{\rho}$, not zero.

## When to use

Use it in portfolio questions: the portfolio risk is lower than the average of the individual risks whenever $\rho<1$. For $n$ identical stocks, $\sigma_p=\sigma\sqrt{1/n+\rho(1-1/n)}$, and adding stocks beyond about 30 barely helps.
