A finite stream whose payments grow at $g$ each period, with $PV=\frac{C}{r-g}\left(1-\left(\frac{1+g}{1+r}\right)^n\right)$. Only the net rate $r-g$ erodes value, so faster growth means a higher value.

## When to use

Use it when payments rise each year, such as growing cash flows in a project or salary-linked savings. The finite formula still works if $g\ge r$, unlike the perpetual case.
