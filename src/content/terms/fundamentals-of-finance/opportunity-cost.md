The value of a resource's best alternative use. Using something you already own is not free: the project must be charged the rent or sale price forgone, even though no cash changes hands.

## When to use

One of the five principles for building [[Free Cash Flow]]: include the opportunity cost as a project cash flow (for example building on land you already own) before computing [[Net Present Value]].

See [[Lesson_06-Capital Budgeting & Free Cash Flow|Lesson 06]].
