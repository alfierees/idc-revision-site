A dollar today is worth more than a dollar tomorrow, because today's dollar can be invested and earn the rate $r$. The [[Discount Rate]] is the exchange rate between money at different dates.

## When to use

The idea behind the whole course: compounding moves cash forward ([[Future Value]]), discounting moves it back ([[Present Value]]). NPV, IRR, bond prices and loan schedules are all applications of it.
