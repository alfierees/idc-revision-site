Tobin's result that investing splits into two independent steps: build the efficient risky portfolio (the same for everyone, independent of preferences), then choose how much of it to hold against the riskless asset (which depends on risk aversion).

## When to use

Use it to explain why everyone holds the same risky mix and controls risk only through $x_{rf}$: more risk-averse investors hold more of the riskless asset. Taught in [[Lesson_09-Capital Market Line]].
