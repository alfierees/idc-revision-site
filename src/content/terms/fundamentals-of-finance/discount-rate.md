$r$, the exchange rate between money at different dates; the required return used to discount cash flows. Everything in the course (NPV, IRR, bond prices, loan schedules) is an application of moving cash flows along the time axis with it.

## When to use

Use it to discount every cash flow in a problem. It also carries the cost of debt, so financing flows are left out of the cash flows. Nominal cash flows take a nominal rate and real flows take a real rate.
