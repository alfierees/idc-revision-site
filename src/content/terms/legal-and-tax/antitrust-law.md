Competition law, which regulates corporate activity to prevent monopolistic concentration and promote fair competition. In Israel it restricts dominant companies from merging with competitors in the same market.

## When to use

Treat it as one of the hard-law regulations a venture must obey, alongside [[Regulatory Compliance]] and employment law.

See [[Lec_02-Legal Entrepreneurship — Fundamental Concepts|Lec 02]].
