An obligatory transaction is a contract that faces the future and carries risk: performance and compensation happen later, not at the moment of agreement (for example selling an apartment "on paper" before it is built).

## When to use

Use it to explain why contract law matters to ventures, since investment, employment, SaaS subscriptions and founders' vesting are all obligatory transactions. Contrast it with a real transaction.
