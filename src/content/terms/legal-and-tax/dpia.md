A mandatory GDPR risk assessment (Art. 35) carried out before starting high-risk processing, such as continuous AI processing, data brokering, or health or financial data. It is Privacy by Design turned into a procedure.

## When to use

Use it when a startup launches an AI feature, large-scale tracking or sensitive-data processing: it must assess and document the risk to people's rights first.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
