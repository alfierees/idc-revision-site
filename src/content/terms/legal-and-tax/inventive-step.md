A patent requirement that the invention goes beyond the obvious. With novelty and technological applicability it forms the test examined under the Patents Law 1967.

## When to use

Use it when asked whether an invention qualifies for a patent: check novelty, non-triviality/inventive step and technological applicability in turn.
