The entity that decides the purposes and means of processing personal data (GDPR Arts. 24 and 26), and carries primary responsibility for compliance, including the duty to demonstrate it. In Google Spain the CJEU treated search engines as controllers of the data they index.

## When to use

Use it to allocate duties in a GDPR scenario: first decide who is the controller and who is only a processor acting on its behalf, then assign the compliance and data subject obligations to the controller.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
