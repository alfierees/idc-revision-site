CA 3158/20, Achi Tekuma v. Meshulam. A 2004 sale of lease rights was recorded at 305,000 dollars while the parties secretly agreed a different, undocumented deal for tax reasons. Justice Grosskopf held that an illegal transaction cannot be legitimised by concealing it, so even the hidden contract is void. The doctrine: illegality of purpose defeats a contract, and disguising it does not rescue it.

## When to use

Cite it when facts show a deal structured around an unlawful aim (for example hiding payments for tax reasons): the legality-of-purpose condition fails and the contract is not enforced.
