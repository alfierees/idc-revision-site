A CJEU ruling of 4 July 2023 on whether burying data processing in Terms of Use is a valid legal basis for ad-targeting. A contract or terms basis cannot be stretched to justify pervasive behavioural advertising; genuine consent or another valid basis is needed.

## When to use

Use it with Art. 6 questions on lawful bases: terms of use are not valid consent for behavioural ad-targeting.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
