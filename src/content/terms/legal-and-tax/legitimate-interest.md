One of the six lawful bases for processing under GDPR Art. 6: the controller's legitimate interests, balanced against the data subject's rights. The others are consent, contract, legal obligation, vital interests and public interest.

## When to use

Use it when asked which basis fits a processing activity. The lecture warns that consent is often not the best basis; pick the one that genuinely fits.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
