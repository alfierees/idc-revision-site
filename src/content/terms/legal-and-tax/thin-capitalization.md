A company financed mainly by loans, often from its owners, rather than by equity, leaving no equity safety cushion in a crisis.

## When to use

Use it in the [[Northern Chicks Case]] scenario: thin capitalization combined with improper management can lead to the shareholders' loans being subordinated behind external creditors.

See [[Lec_03-Company Law & Corporate Structures|Lec 03]].
