In the course, the reason modern data-protection law exists: cloud-scale data collection shifted the balance of power to the companies holding our data, so privacy can no longer be left to each person's control over their own information. The burden moves to companies, which must build privacy into products from the start.

## When to use

Use it to explain why the GDPR is so strict and why privacy compliance is treated as a product-design duty, not only a legal one.
