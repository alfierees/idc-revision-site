Law applying beyond the territory of the legislating state. Under Article 3 the GDPR binds any organisation established in the EU and any organisation anywhere that processes the personal data of EU residents, with no office, server or employee in Europe needed.

## When to use

Use it to show a non-EU startup is in scope the moment an EU resident can sign up. The AI Act borrows the same reach.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
