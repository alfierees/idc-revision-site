Property rights narrower than ownership, such as a lease, lien or tenancy: a carved-out slice of control. Ownership is the absolute right, the fullest bundle of control.

## When to use

Use it in the property-rights foundation of the IP lecture: contrast absolute and limited rights, and both with contractual rights that bind only the parties.

See [[Lec_05-Intellectual Property|Lec 05]].
