The Tax Ordinance regime for options granted to controlling shareholders, service providers and consultants, the groups excluded from [[Section 102]]. The gain is taxed as ordinary income at marginal rates, and the tax event is the exercise of the options, earlier than under Section 102 and without waiting for a cash sale.

## When to use

Use it when a consultant or advisor is offered equity: it explains why their after-tax value is lower than an employee's and why bringing them on as an employee or officer can help.

See [[Lec_06-Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP)|Lec 06]].
