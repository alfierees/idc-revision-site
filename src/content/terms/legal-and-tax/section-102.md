A statutory safe harbour in the Tax Ordinance that lets employee equity gains be taxed as capital gains (25%) rather than ordinary employment income (up to about 59%), provided the company follows its rules (a [[Trustee]], a holding period, Israel Tax Authority filing). It applies only to employees and officers, not to controlling shareholders, service providers or consultants, who fall under [[Section 3(i)]].

## When to use

Use it when asked how an option grant is taxed, which of the three tracks ([[Capital Gain Track]], [[Ordinary Income Track]], [[Non-Trustee Track]]) fits, or whether a given recipient qualifies at all.
