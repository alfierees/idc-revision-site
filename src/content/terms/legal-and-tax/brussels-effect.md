The EU's export of its standards through market power. It is cheaper for global firms to apply EU rules everywhere than to build weaker products per region, and other governments copy the text, so the GDPR became the template for privacy laws worldwide.

## When to use

Use it to explain why GDPR-style laws appear in Japan, Brazil, China, India and Israel, and the founder's takeaway: build to GDPR and you are largely compliant worldwide.
