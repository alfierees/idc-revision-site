Ownership is the legal right to possess and use an asset, including the ability to transfer that right, covering physical assets such as land and equipment and digital assets such as websites and software. The lectures stress that a startup's IP must sit inside the company, not in a founder's personal name.

## When to use

Use it when asking who holds title to a startup's assets, and when explaining why IP is assigned to the company and what licences leave with the owner.
