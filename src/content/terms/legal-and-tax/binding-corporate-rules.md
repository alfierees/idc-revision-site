Internal rules, under Art. 47 GDPR, that allow transfers of personal data within a multinational group.

## When to use

List it as one of the routes out of the EU, with [[Adequacy Decision]] (Art. 45) and [[Standard Contractual Clauses]] (Art. 46). It suits intra-group transfers specifically.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
