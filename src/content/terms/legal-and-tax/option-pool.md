An option pool is a block of shares reserved for employee stock options. It is often created pre-money as a condition of an investment, and ungranted pool options count in the fully diluted share capital. A pool created pre-money is counted in the pre-round fully diluted shares, so it dilutes the existing holders (the founders) rather than the new investor.

## When to use

Use it in cap-table questions: remember it sits inside the fully diluted number that investors and lawyers negotiate on, and that the tax treatment of options granted from it is covered in the ESOP lecture.
