The date, set in advance, on which an unexercised option dies. The market standard is 10 years from the grant date.

## When to use

Use it with the termination rules: unvested options expire immediately on leaving, vested ones survive about 3 months (12 for death or disability), and for Cause they die at once.

See [[Lec_06-Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP)|Lec 06]].
