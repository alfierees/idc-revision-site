The open questions on who authors AI output and whether training models on protected works infringes [[Copyright]]. Across jurisdictions, the more substantive the human creative contribution, the more likely protection attaches; Israel has no court ruling yet on copyright in AI-created content.

## When to use

Use it for AI-era IP questions: China protected an image where human prompts and parameters were substantial, the US refuses machine owners and inventors and assesses training as [[Fair Use]], and the EU stresses transparency about training data.
