The factual element of contract formation (the actus reus): whether the agreement is specific enough to enforce. A contract need not be perfectly complete if serious intention is proven, because courts have completion mechanisms to fill gaps.

## When to use

Use it when a deal has a missing term (which plot, no price, a missing appendix) or one side calls the document a mere MOU. Pair it with serious intention and the Dor Energy and Hof HaTchelet cases.
