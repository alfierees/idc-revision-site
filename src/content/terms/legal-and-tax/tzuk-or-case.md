CA 407/89: Justice Barak rejected the idea that a corporation is artificial. It is a real bearer of rights and duties, but it exists only through human beings and acts only through people, which is what [[Organ Theory]] captures.

## When to use

Use it for the philosophical justification of [[Separate Legal Personality]]; [[Modiin Case]] then extends organ theory to criminal liability.

See [[Lec_03-Company Law & Corporate Structures|Lec 03]].
