A woman bought a painting for 250 shekels at a flea market that turned out to be a stolen Reuven Rubin. The museum won and she had to return it, because the sale was not in the ordinary course of the seller's business, so condition 4 of the market overt rule failed. Property rights are stronger than contractual rights, so the bar is high.

## When to use

Use it when a good-faith buyer claims title from a non-owner: run the four conditions of the market overt rule, and note that selling something outside the seller's normal business defeats the claim.
