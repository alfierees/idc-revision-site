The EU's third-generation digital law, adopted in 2024. It is risk-based, with heavier obligations as potential harm rises (prohibited, high-risk, minimal-risk), and role-based, with Providers (Art. 16) and Deployers (Art. 26).

## When to use

Use it as the GDPR playbook applied to AI: extraterritorial reach, roles mirroring controller and processor, and compliance built in from the design stage.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
