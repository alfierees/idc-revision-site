The five failure modes a founder manages against: no founders' agreement, an improper corporate structure, no NDAs, neglecting privacy (for example the GDPR), and improper open-source use. Each maps to one of the three legal pillars; the lecture singles out open-source licence terms as the risk founders underestimate.

## When to use

The ready-made checklist for a legal risk assessment, such as section 5 of the midterm assignment: name the risk, its pillar, and the mitigation.
