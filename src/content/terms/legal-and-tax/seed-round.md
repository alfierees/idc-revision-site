The financing stage after [[Bootstrapping]] and Pre-Seed whose purpose is to finance the MVP and obtain market or concept validation, typically from angels and relatively small seed funds.

## When to use

Use it when placing a funding scenario on the financing ladder (Bootstrapping, Pre-Seed, Seed, Round A, B, C, IPO) and picking the matching investor type and legal documents.
