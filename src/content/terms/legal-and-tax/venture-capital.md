Funds investing institutional money (pension funds, banks) in startups that already have a product and proven demand, in return for shares and a seat at the table, with high involvement in management and strategic decisions.

## When to use

Use it to contrast with [[Angel Investors]]: more capital than an angel's early cheque, but more control given up.
