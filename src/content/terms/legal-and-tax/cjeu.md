The Court of Justice of the European Union, which turns the GDPR's words into binding reality. The Supervisory Authority enforces and fines, while the CJEU interprets what the law means.

## When to use

Four landmark rulings to know: Google Spain, SCHUFA, Meta and the Schrems cases. To predict exposure, watch both the authorities and the court.
