The Ordinary Income Track is one of the three Section 102 tax tracks. It runs with a trustee, the employee is taxed at marginal rates (up to about 59%), and the company gets a tax deduction.

## When to use

Use it when comparing the three tracks: the Capital Gain Track gives the employee the low 25% rate but the company loses its deduction, while here the employee pays more and the company deducts.
