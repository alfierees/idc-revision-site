A company owned or controlled by another company, the parent, which typically holds more than 50% of its shares and oversees its operations. It may run day to day independently but stays bound by the parent's policies and strategic direction.

## When to use

Use it when asked how to ring-fence risk, enter a new jurisdiction or separate product lines within a [[Corporate Structure]].
