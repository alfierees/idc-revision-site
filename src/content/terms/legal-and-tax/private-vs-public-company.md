A private company's shares are not traded on an exchange and it reports only to the Registrar of Companies, while a public company offers shares to the public and faces full-disclosure duties, reporting also to the Securities Authority and the exchange. Public companies also need at least 4 directors, at least 2 external directors and a CEO, and the chair generally cannot also be the CEO.

## When to use

Use it when asked how governance differs: the heavier regulation exists because dispersed public investors cannot monitor management, and a company becomes public through an IPO.
