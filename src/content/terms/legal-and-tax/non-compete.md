A clause preventing an employee from working for competitors or starting a competing business for a set period after leaving, to protect trade secrets. Under the Supreme Court ruling in AES v Saar it is generally unenforceable in Israel, except in narrow cases such as protecting genuine trade secrets or in return for special compensation.

## When to use

Use it when a founder relies on a non-compete to lock in talent: say it is generally unenforceable and point to NDAs, IP assignment and incentives instead.
