Justice Rivlin's partial pushback against [[Apropim Case]]: the goal is the parties' intent, but the starting point is the language, which cannot be given a meaning it cannot bear. The court moves back and forth between language and circumstances, with a rebuttable presumption that the contract's purpose is what its ordinary wording suggests.

## When to use

Use it in interpretation questions as the move that re-anchors the text after Apropim, softening it without abandoning the pendulum.
