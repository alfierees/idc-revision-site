Post-money valuation is the value of the company immediately after the investment, simply pre-money valuation plus the new capital: Pre-money + Investment = Post-money. The investor's stake is investment divided by post-money.

## When to use

Use it in funding-round arithmetic: from a stake and an amount you get post-money as investment divided by ownership percentage (for example 2.5 million dollars for 25% gives 10 million dollars post-money and 7.5 million dollars pre-money).
