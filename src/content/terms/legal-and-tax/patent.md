A patent protects inventors of technological innovations with a 20-year monopoly from the filing date, in exchange for publicly disclosing how the invention works. It needs novelty, non-triviality, inventive step and technological applicability, is examined and registered at the Israel Patent Authority, and needs maintenance fees to stay alive.

## When to use

Use it in choose-the-right-IP-regime questions: patent a technical invention you can disclose, but keep a technical advantage you can hide as a trade secret, because patenting publishes it.
