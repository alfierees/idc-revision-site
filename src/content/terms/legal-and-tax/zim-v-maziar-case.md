Justice Zilber held that freedom of contract stands below the sanctity of life: public policy voids exemption clauses over life and safety. The court does not abolish freedom of contract, but life-and-death matters nullify such waivers.

## When to use

Use it as an example of [[Cogent Provisions]], alongside [[Lagil Trampoline Case]] and [[Levin v Levin Case]], when a clause tries to waive a mandatory protection.
