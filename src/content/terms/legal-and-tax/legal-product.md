The idea that legal obligations are implemented in the product itself, not only in a contract in a drawer. A sign-up flow, consent dialog, privacy policy link or deletion button operationalises the law, so each interface and data flow is a legal decision and compliance is a design requirement.

## When to use

Use it as the framing in digital-regulation questions: for each UI element, ask which legal rule (for example GDPR consent or erasure) it makes visible.
