One of the three trademark registration tests: the sign must be sufficiently distinguishable from other signs in the same field (e.g. iPhone, Starbucks). A sign that directly describes the product lacks distinctive character and is refused.

## When to use

Use it to judge a brand name: arbitrary or invented names are strong, descriptive or generic ones ("Orange Juice", "Computer") are refused.
