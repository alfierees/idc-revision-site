The 2013 leaks showing that US firms (Google, Facebook, Microsoft) gave the NSA access to user data through programs such as PRISM, which undermined trust in US data protection.

## When to use

Use it as the starting point of the Schrems saga: the distrust it caused led to [[Schrems I Case]], then [[Schrems II Case]] and the later [[Data Privacy Framework]].

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
