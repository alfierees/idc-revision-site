A claim a shareholder brings on the company's behalf against those who harmed it. The lecture lists the right to file one if officers harm the company among the minority shareholder's defensive rights.

## When to use

Use it in minority-protection questions where officers have damaged the company and the small shareholder cannot outvote the controllers.
