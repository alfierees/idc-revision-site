A privacy policy is a public notice that discloses how personal data is collected, stored and used. It is legally required (for example under GDPR) the moment a business touches personal data.

## When to use

Use it in website and product questions on which documents the law forces on you: a privacy policy is mandatory, whereas terms of service are optional in law but wise to adopt.
