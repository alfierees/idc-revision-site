A partnership is a legal association of two or more persons or companies who run a business together to make a profit and are not incorporated in another form. Partners have personal, joint and several liability, and each acts as an agent who can bind the others.

## When to use

Use it in entity-choice and company-versus-partnership questions: it is easy and cheap to form (no registration, up to 20 partners except professional ones) but one partner's mistake can bankrupt the rest personally.
