Power is divided between the branches of government (legislature, executive, judiciary) with mutual oversight so that no branch can dominate. In Israel the checks include the State Comptroller, the courts' judicial review and the media, and Montesquieu's idea is that power checks power.

## When to use

Use it in questions on how the Israeli system restrains state power, alongside the [[Rule of Law]].
