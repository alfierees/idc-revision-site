The EU's Directive 2002/58/EC, the source of the cookie banner: websites must obtain user consent before placing certain cookies. Being a Directive, it needs national transposition, unlike the directly binding GDPR.

## When to use

Use it for cookie questions. A tracking cookie is regulated twice: ePrivacy covers consent to place it, and the GDPR covers processing its ID as personal data.
