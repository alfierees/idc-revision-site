Real estate is immovable property such as land and houses, the classic example of the ancient property-law categories (alongside movables) before property law reached intangible assets like IP.

## When to use

Use it as the reference example for property rights (ownership of real estate is the fullest bundle of control) when introducing IP.

See [[Lec_05-Intellectual Property|Lec 05]].
