One of the screens for patentability under the Patents Law 1967: the invention must relate to a technological field with a practical application. It sits beside [[Novelty]] and [[Non-Triviality]] (the inventive step).

## When to use

Use it in patent-test questions, together with the other conditions, before filing with the [[Israel Patent Authority]].

See [[Lec_05-Intellectual Property|Lec 05]].
