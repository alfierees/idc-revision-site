Collecting the law into systematic statutes or codes. Israel takes the drive toward codification, along with good faith, from the Continental tradition.

## When to use

Use it in the common law versus continental comparison: the civil-law approach trusts the code, and Israel borrows adversarial procedure from common law but codification from continental law.
