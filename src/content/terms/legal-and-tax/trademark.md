A sign used to identify the goods or services of a particular trader and distinguish them from another's: words, names, letters, numbers, logos, graphic symbols, 3D shapes or combinations. It must be registrable (unique, not descriptive, generic or misleading), have [[Distinctiveness]], and be in use or genuinely intended for use.

## When to use

Use it in questions on whether a brand can be registered: arbitrary or invented signs (Apple for computers) succeed, while descriptive or generic ones (Computer for a computer shop) are refused, as are misleading marks or copies like Nikee.

See [[Lec_05-Intellectual Property|Lec 05]].
