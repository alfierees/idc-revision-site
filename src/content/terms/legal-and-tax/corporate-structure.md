The definition of a company's internal structure: the type of entity (Ltd., private company, partnership) and the roles of its managers. It determines legal liability, taxation and fundraising opportunities, and whether the company can grant employee incentives such as stock options.

## When to use

Appears when a question asks which vehicle a founder should choose. "Improper corporate structure" is one of the five core risks of a venture, and the Ltd. is the usual startup default over a partnership (personal liability) or a public corporation (heavy reporting).
