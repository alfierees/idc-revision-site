A mixed system blending Common Law (adversarial procedure, evidence, agency) and Continental Law (good faith, codification). Norms form a hierarchy: Constitution, Laws, Regulations, Orders, private law. The Supreme Court sets binding precedent, but only the ratio decidendi binds, not obiter dicta.

## When to use

Use it for source-of-law questions: a contract term that conflicts with a statute or regulation (hard law) is void to that extent, and higher norms authorise and constrain lower ones.
