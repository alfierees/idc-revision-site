The five tools a court uses to fill a missing detail once serious intention and definiteness are shown: an agreed mechanism, dispositive legal provisions (such as s.46's reasonable price), the principle of optimal performance, custom, and reasonableness and good faith (s.39).

## When to use

Use them when a party tries to escape over a missing detail: courts complete the contract to save it, as in Dor Energy and Hof HaTchelet, unless the gap reflects a real lack of consent.
