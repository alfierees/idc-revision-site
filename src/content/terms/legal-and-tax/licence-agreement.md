An agreement granting permission to use an asset (software, brand, IP) for payment or on conditions while the licensor keeps ownership. An EULA is the end-user version for purchased software and digital products.

## When to use

Use it when listing the contracts a startup needs. The lecture says a licence agreement is required only depending on circumstances, unlike a privacy policy which is mandatory once personal data is touched.
