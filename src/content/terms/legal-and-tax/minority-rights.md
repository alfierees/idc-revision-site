Protections the law gives minority shareholders against majority abuse: the right to information, voting at the general meeting, a derivative suit if officers harm the company, petitioning the court over minority oppression, and in some cases proposing topics for the general meeting.

## When to use

Use it for the majority versus minority agency problem. External directors in public companies (at least two) also exist to protect the company's interest, including the minority's, against a controlling shareholder.
