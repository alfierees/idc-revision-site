The exercise of the right of access: a request by an individual for a copy of the personal data a controller holds about them. Companies need a DSAR process as part of their GDPR governance.

## When to use

Use it for the right of access among the eight data subject rights. In design questions it implies a "download my data" flow.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
