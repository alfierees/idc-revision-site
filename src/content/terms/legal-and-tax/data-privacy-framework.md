The EU-US Data Privacy Framework of July 2023, a new adequacy decision that restored a lawful route for transferring personal data from the EU to the US. It followed the Schrems saga, in which Safe Harbor (Schrems I) and then Privacy Shield (Schrems II) were invalidated.

## When to use

Use it when a question asks whether EU user data can lawfully be hosted on US cloud infrastructure, and to complete the Safe Harbor, Privacy Shield, Data Privacy Framework chain.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
