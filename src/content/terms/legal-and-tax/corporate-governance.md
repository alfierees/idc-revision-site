The system by which a company is directed and controlled. The Companies Law splits power between the shareholders' general meeting (compared to the legislature), the officers, meaning the CEO and board (the executive), and the employees, and limits how much the board may delegate to the CEO.

## When to use

Use it for "who holds which power" questions: appointing the board, approving financial statements, appointing the CEO and deciding dividends stay with the shareholders or the board, while ongoing administration can be delegated. Also where external directors and the limits of boards come up.
