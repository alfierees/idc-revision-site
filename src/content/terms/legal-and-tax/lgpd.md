Brazil's general data protection law (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados), one of the GDPR-inspired laws that followed the Brussels Effect.

## When to use

Use it as an example when asked how the GDPR influenced laws elsewhere. The lecture lists it alongside China (PIPL), Japan (APPI), India (DPDPA 2023) and others.
