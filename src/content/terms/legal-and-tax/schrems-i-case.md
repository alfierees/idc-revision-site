Schrems I (6 October 2015) was Max Schrems' challenge that led the CJEU to invalidate the EU-US Safe Harbor framework. Companies scrambled to standard contractual clauses instead.

## When to use

Use it as the first of the two Schrems rulings (Schrems II then struck down Privacy Shield); both decide whether EU data can lawfully go to US infrastructure.
