The court held that a binding real-estate contract had formed despite a missing appendix, treating it as minor. It eroded the traditional essentials (nature of the property, delivery date, nature of the transaction, payment dates) to just three: date of delivery, price and type of transaction. Justice Or completed the rest through completion mechanisms and custom, to save the contract.

## When to use

Cite it for the doctrine that courts strain to save a deal where serious intention is clear, rather than let a party escape on a technicality, and for the principle of optimal performance.
