Soft law is what a founder chooses to regulate through legal tools (contracts, founders' agreements, NDAs), with force from mutual agreement. Hard law is what the founder is obliged to follow under a binding framework (statutes, regulations, court rulings), with force from the state. Hard law always prevails: a contract term that conflicts with a statute is void to that extent.

## When to use

Use it when a clause clashes with a mandatory rule: parties can contract around default rules but never against mandatory ones, and risk must be managed against both foundations.
