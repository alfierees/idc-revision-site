The rate on ordinary employment income, up to about 59% in Israel. In option plans it is the rate applied when the plan does not qualify for the 25% capital gains rate, for example when shares are sold before the 24-month holding period.

## When to use

Use it in Section 102 comparisons: Ordinary Income and Non-Trustee tracks tax the employee at the marginal rate, and a premature sale under the Capital Gain Track falls to it too.
