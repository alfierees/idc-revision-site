A distribution of company profits, in cash or stock, to shareholders (Section 190 of the Companies Law). It may only be paid if the company has sufficient profits and the distribution does not endanger solvency, which protects creditors.

## When to use

Use it when asked what rights a share carries, or when shareholders try to drain a fragile company. Dividend decisions stay with the board and cannot be delegated to the CEO.
