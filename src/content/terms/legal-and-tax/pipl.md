PIPL is China's Personal Information Protection Law, listed in the lecture among the national data-protection regimes alongside the GDPR.

## When to use

Cite it as the Chinese counterpart when a question asks about data-protection law outside the EU.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
