A security granting a unit of ownership in the company, giving participation rights, influence over management and entitlement to profits. It bundles voting rights (Companies Law s.188), dividends (s.190), a proportional claim on residual assets after all debts are paid on liquidation, and class rights fixed by the Articles.

## When to use

Use it to contrast a share (ownership) with a [[Bond]] (debt) and an [[Option (Security)]] (a right to buy), and when asked what rights a shareholder holds.

See [[Lec_03-Company Law & Corporate Structures|Lec 03]].
