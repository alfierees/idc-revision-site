The Roman-Germanic tradition (France, Germany), built on a written, codified body of law such as a Civil Code. The judge is active and investigates, documents are submitted in advance, and pre-defined norms decide the case.

## When to use

Contrast it with [[Common Law]]. Israel takes good faith and [[Codification]] from this tradition.
