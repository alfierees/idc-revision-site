An IP regime protecting industrial designs with unique visual features: the appearance of a product, not how it works. It is registered, gives exclusivity for a limited period, and aims to encourage innovative product design.

## When to use

Use it to pick the right regime: look of a product means design, function means patent, an artistic work means copyright, brand signs mean trademark. A smartphone can carry all four at once.
