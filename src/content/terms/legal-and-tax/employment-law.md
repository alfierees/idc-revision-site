Law that protects workers' rights and regulates the employer-employee relationship: minimum wage, working hours, vacation days and protection against workplace discrimination. It aims at fair and equal working conditions for all employees.

## When to use

Use it as one of the mandatory external rules (hard law) a venture must obey as it grows and hires, alongside compliance and antitrust law.
