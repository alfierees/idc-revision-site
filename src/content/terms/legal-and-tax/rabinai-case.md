CA 77/158 (Hava Rabinai): a short memorandum signed 20.1.72 named the parties, the lot, the price, an advance and a payment schedule. Despite calling itself a memorandum it contained all the essential terms, so it satisfied serious intention and definiteness and was binding: the MOU label does not control, the substance does.

## When to use

Use it when asked whether an MOU binds: the parties' intent decides, and a memorandum with all essential terms can already be a contract. Rabinai also says to examine intention exactly at the moment of signing.
