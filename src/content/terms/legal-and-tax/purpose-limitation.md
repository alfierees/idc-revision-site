Purpose limitation is one of the Article 5 principles of the GDPR: personal data may only be processed for purposes that are specified, explicit, legitimate and limited, so you cannot repurpose data freely.

## When to use

Use it when a product wants to reuse collected data for a new purpose, alongside the other Article 5 principles.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
