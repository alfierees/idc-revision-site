A venture's web of agreements (founders, investors, customers, suppliers, employees), the practical startup-level view of contract law. A contract is a legal document binding the parties to specific obligations once offer and acceptance are fulfilled.

## When to use

Use it for the startup framing, where contracts are one of three pillars with business structure and regulation. Cross-border contracts need a jurisdiction clause stating which country's courts and law govern.
