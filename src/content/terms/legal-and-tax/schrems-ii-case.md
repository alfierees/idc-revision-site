Max Schrems' 16 July 2020 challenge in which the CJEU invalidated the [[Privacy Shield]] (the 2016 replacement for Safe Harbor). It upheld [[Standard Contractual Clauses]] as valid but required a case-by-case check of whether the destination country's surveillance laws undermine them, placing the burden on both the EU exporter and the importer.

## When to use

Use it when a question asks whether EU user data can lawfully be hosted on US cloud infrastructure, or how transfer mechanics become an architecture decision for a founder.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
