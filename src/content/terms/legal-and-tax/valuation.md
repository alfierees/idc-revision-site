The monetary value of a company as assessed by the market or a professional evaluation: how much it is worth for investment or sale. It determines what ownership percentage investors get for their money, as investment divided by [[Post-Money Valuation]].

## When to use

Use it in fundraising questions: a higher valuation means less dilution for the same cheque, and the five methods (profit or revenue multiple, comparables, revenue-based, entry-barrier cost, user-based) can give very different numbers.
