The right to be forgotten is the right to have one's data erased (the right to erasure), created in the Google Spain case (2014), where the CJEU held people can require search engines to de-list outdated or irrelevant personal information. It was later codified in the GDPR.

## When to use

Use it among the data-subject rights: it implies a product must be able to truly delete an account's data, and Google Spain made search engines controllers.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
