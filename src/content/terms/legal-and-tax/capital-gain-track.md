The Section 102 track, with a trustee, that taxes the employee's gain at 25% instead of the ordinary income rate of up to about 59%. The company forgoes its corporate-tax deduction in return. The tax event is the sale or release of the shares, not exercise.

## When to use

It is the track to know thoroughly, and the one most Israeli startups use. The 25% rate requires a [[Holding Period]] of 24 months from grant; selling earlier is allowed but taxed at the high marginal rate.
