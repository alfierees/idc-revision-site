Patent claims are the part of a patent application that defines the scope of the protection requested, drawing the legal boundary of the invention. They are the most important part of the application.

## When to use

Cite them when a question asks what exactly a patent protects: the full description and drawings explain the invention, but the claims set the legal boundary.
