Shareholders' power to vote on critical corporate decisions such as electing the board, approving mergers and acquisitions, amending bylaws, issuing new shares and approving dividends. Rights vary by share class, so some holders (for example founders with super-voting shares) can have disproportionate influence.

## When to use

Use it when an investor negotiates influence in an [[Investment Agreement]], where voting rights are one of the clauses and the founder's concern is loss of control.
