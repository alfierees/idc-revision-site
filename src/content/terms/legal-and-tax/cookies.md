Small files a website places on a device to remember information about a visit, including tracking data. The ePrivacy Directive requires consent before placing certain cookies, and the GDPR treats cookie IDs as personal data.

## When to use

Use them for the legal basis behind the cookie banner. The four privacy risks are tracking, targeted advertising, data sharing and security.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
