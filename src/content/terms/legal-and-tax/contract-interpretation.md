Working out what the parties meant, balancing language against circumstances under s.25. After the 2011 amendment both are read together, but if the intent is explicit in the language, the language governs. Where several readings are possible, prefer the one that upholds validity; for a standard form, read against the drafter (contra proferentem).

## When to use

Use the pendulum of [[Apropim Doctrine]] and its pushback in Vegetable Growers, then the s.25 rules. Gaps are filled by custom (s.26) and good faith (s.39).
