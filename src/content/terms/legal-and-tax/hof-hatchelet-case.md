CA 133/89 (Blue Coast). A contract sold one of the seller's plots without saying which, and the seller later called it a mere MOU lacking definiteness. The court completed the missing term and transferred ownership. On a missing price, the court will complete it with a reasonable price if the parties simply did not know the market value, but cannot if the gap reflects a disagreement, which signals no consent.

## When to use

Use it on definiteness questions: a contract need not be perfectly complete if serious intention is proven, and courts have completion mechanisms. Check whether the gap shows a deliberate lack of agreement (no completion) or just an unfilled detail (completion).
