The quantitative layer inside or alongside a [[Service Agreement]]: it commits the provider to measurable service levels such as response times, system availability and maximum downtime. For example, an internet provider promises 99.9% uptime and restoration within four hours of an outage.

## When to use

Use it when a question needs a measurable standard, for instance how a client can measure compliance and trigger compensation or corrective action if the commitment is breached.
