The price per share the employee must pay the company to convert an option into a share, also called the strike price. It is fixed at the grant date, and on exercise the company issues new shares.

## When to use

Use it for the option payoff: the holder captures $\max(S-K,0)$ per share, where $S$ is the share value at sale and $K$ the exercise price. If $S \le K$ the option is underwater and is not exercised.
