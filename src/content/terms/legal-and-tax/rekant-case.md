The Rekant case is the Barak-Zamir debate on whether a clause can override the principle of equality (here, age-based). Justice Barak held that constitutional values like equality prevail over freedom of contract, while Justice Zamir warned this endangers the status of contract law by undermining predictability and stability.

## When to use

Use it when discussing whether freedom of contract is absolute: the lecture's conclusion is that no right is absolute and freedom of contract today is weak and eroded.
