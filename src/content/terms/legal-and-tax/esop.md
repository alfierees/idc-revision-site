The umbrella plan adopted by the company that sets the general terms for all option grants (pool size, vesting defaults, tax track, administration). It lets a cash-poor startup pay staff in future upside instead of present cash.

## When to use

Use it for grant mechanics (vesting, strike, expiry, termination) and the Section 102 tax track. Options beat direct share grants because there is no cost at grant and tax is deferred.
