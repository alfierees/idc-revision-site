Lagil Trampoline v Nahmias: a liability-waiver sign had no validity because it was not part of the contract (its text appeared inside the facility, not at ticket purchase, the moment of formation) and because sanctity of life cannot be waived on a sign.

## When to use

Use it, with Zim v Maziar and Levin v Levin, on cogent provisions and public policy: contract terms cannot override mandatory rules, and exemption clauses about life are void.
