The first test before a contract can form: whether the person is legally competent to contract. Minors and legally incapacitated persons cannot express rational will (Sections 3, 4 and 8, Legal Capacity and Guardianship Law).

## When to use

Use it as the first check in a formation question. The objective test for serious intention also bends for signers with impaired capacity (Bochbot).
