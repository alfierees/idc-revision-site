US independent valuation setting the fair market value of common stock for option pricing. Lec 06 mentions 409A valuations only in passing, as one of the services an option-plan administrator provides alongside running the plan.

## When to use

Expect it only as background to option pricing: the exercise price is fixed at grant and is typically set at fair market value, which is why a valuation matters. Per the exam cheat sheet, not Lec 06 itself.
