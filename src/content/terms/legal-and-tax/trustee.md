The linchpin of the Capital Gain Track: the third party that holds the options and shares on the employee's behalf throughout the holding period, which is how the tax authority enforces the 24-month rule. Without a trustee the plan falls into the [[Non-Trustee Track]].

## When to use

Use it in [[Section 102]] implementation questions: the board approves the trustee, the trustee submits the plan to the Israel Tax Authority, board approvals are deposited within 45 days and signed option agreements within 90 days.

See [[Lec_06-Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP)|Lec 06]].
