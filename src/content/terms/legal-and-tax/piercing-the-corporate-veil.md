Piercing the corporate veil is the exceptional power of a court to look through the company to its shareholders or officers and impose personal liability. Under Section 6 it applies where the separate personality is abused: fraud or wronging creditors, taking unreasonable risk about repaying debts, or a shareholder's awareness or willful blindness to improper conduct.

## When to use

Use it for shareholder-liability questions, stressing it is exceptional (the Salomon principle is protected) and that subordination (Northern Chicks) is the gentler alternative that keeps the veil.
