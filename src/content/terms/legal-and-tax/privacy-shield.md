The Privacy Shield was Safe Harbor's 2016 replacement as the EU-US data-transfer framework. It was invalidated in Schrems II (16 July 2020).

## When to use

Use it in the Schrems timeline: Safe Harbor (struck down 2015), Privacy Shield (struck down 2020), then the Data Privacy Framework (2023).
