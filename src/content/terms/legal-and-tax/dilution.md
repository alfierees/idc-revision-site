The fall in existing holders' ownership percentage whenever new shares are issued for a new stakeholder (investor, employee or convertible). The aim is that a smaller slice of a larger pie is worth more.

## When to use

Appears in cap-table calculations. To grant a target percentage $p$ you must issue $N = pS/(1-p)$ new shares, where $S$ is current fully diluted shares, because the new shares also enlarge the total. Multiplying $S$ by $p$ under-delivers.
