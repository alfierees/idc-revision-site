Under Art. 45 GDPR, the EU Commission declares a country's data-protection laws adequate, so personal data can flow there freely. Israel currently holds adequacy status, and [[Amendment 13]] is the effort to keep it.

## When to use

Use it for cross-border transfer questions: adequacy is the first route, with [[Standard Contractual Clauses]] (Art. 46) and [[Binding Corporate Rules]] (Art. 47) as fallbacks. Losing adequacy would push Israeli companies handling EU data onto SCCs.
