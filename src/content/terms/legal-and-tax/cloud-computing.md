Accessing computing power and digital resources over the internet instead of buying and maintaining servers. The three models are IaaS (renting the infrastructure, you manage the OS, apps and data), PaaS (renting a platform, you manage your application and data) and SaaS (using finished software, you manage nothing).

## When to use

Moving from IaaS to PaaS to SaaS trades control for convenience. It matters for cost, control and the contracts and [[SLA]]s signed with providers.
