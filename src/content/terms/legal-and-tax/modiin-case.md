A Supreme Court case (Justice Barak) holding that organ theory can impose criminal liability on a company, but only where justified. The mental element of the organ (a senior officer) is treated as the corporation's own. Offences a company typically commits, such as tax offences, fit; bigamy does not.

## When to use

Use it on separate legal personality: a company acts only through people, and organ theory attributes their conduct and intent to it.
