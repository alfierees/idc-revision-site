Under Section 30 of the Contracts Law a contract is void if it is illegal, immoral or contrary to public policy. It is the counterweight to freedom of contract (Section 24).

## When to use

Use it for questions on limits to contract freedom: cite Section 24 versus Section 30 and cases such as Egged v Mashiach (indirect restraint on freedom of occupation), Zim v Maziar and Levin v Levin.
