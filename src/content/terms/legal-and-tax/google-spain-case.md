A 2014 CJEU ruling (Google Spain SL v AEPD and Mario Costeja Gonzalez). The court held that individuals can require search engines to de-list links to outdated or irrelevant personal information, which created the right to be forgotten, later codified in the GDPR. Search engines were treated as controllers of the personal data they index.

## When to use

Cite it for the right to be forgotten (erasure) and for the point that a search engine is a controller. The lecture lists it first among the cases that turned the GDPR text into hard law.
