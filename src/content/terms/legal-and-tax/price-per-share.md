Price per share is what a new investor pays for each share: pre-money valuation divided by pre-round fully diluted shares. It uses pre-money because that is the value of the existing shares only.

## When to use

Use it in round calculations once pre-money is known (for example 7.5 million dollars over an assumed 1,000,000 shares, a count not given on the slides, is 7.50 dollars a share), then divide the investment by the price to get new shares.
