The contract between founders and investors setting the terms of an investment: how and on what conditions the investor joins, how many shares they receive, their rights and how they can influence management. It protects both founders and investors.

## When to use

Use it in fundraising questions. Typical clauses are valuation, share allocation, voting, veto, pre-emptive, exit and information rights. It follows the non-binding term sheet.
