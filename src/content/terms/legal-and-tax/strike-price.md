The pre-agreed price per share at which an option holder may buy shares within a fixed period. In the ESOP lecture it is the exercise price, fixed at the grant date, so any growth above it is the employee's profit.

## When to use

Use it in option payoff questions: per share the payoff is the share price less the strike, never below zero, so loss is capped and upside is open.
