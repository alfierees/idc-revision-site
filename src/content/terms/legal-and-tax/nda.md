A non-disclosure agreement obligates the parties to keep shared information confidential, to prevent leakage of sensitive data. Example: an NDA between a company and a new employee barring disclosure of internal developments.

## When to use

Use it in founder risk lists (no NDAs risks leaking ideas or technology) and as the real protection when non-competes are unenforceable.
