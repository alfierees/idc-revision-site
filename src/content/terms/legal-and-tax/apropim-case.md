The Ministry of Housing apartment-purchase case in which Justice Barak set out the pendulum model of interpretation. The state promised to buy contractors' unsold apartments, and the contract spelled out the deduction for delay clearly for the centre but only for late submission of the purchase request in the periphery. That gap forced the court to choose how to read the contract. See [[Apropim Doctrine]] for the doctrine.

## When to use

Cite the facts when asked how a court should read a contract with a gap: Barak held that moving from language to circumstances needs no preliminary condition, and that saying the language is clear marks the end of interpretation.
