A case on a combination transaction with incomplete documents. Justice Barak (majority) held that in special and exceptional circumstances the writing requirement can be relaxed through good faith, and good faith prevails over the writing principle, provided the promisee changed position by relying on the contract and the withdrawing party is at fault (induced the reliance).

## When to use

Use it when s.8 Land Law makes writing constitutive for a real estate deal but one party relied on it. It is an exception to the rule, not the rule; apply the two conditions of reliance and fault.
