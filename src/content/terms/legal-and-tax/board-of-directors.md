The organ appointed by shareholders that sets strategy and supervision, appoints and dismisses the CEO, and approves the financial statements (Companies Law s.92). Operational, reversible powers can be delegated to the CEO, but appointing the CEO, approving financial statements, general strategy, dividend decisions, significant capital changes and an IPO cannot.

## When to use

Use the delegation line in questions on who may decide what: the board monitors the CEO, so it cannot delegate the very decisions that constitute that monitoring. The lecture also flags weak boards, for example when appointed by the controlling shareholder.
