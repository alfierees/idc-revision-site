Organ theory attributes the mental and behavioural elements of a senior officer (an organ) to the corporation itself, so that the corporation can have intent and conduct. Justice Barak held it can impose criminal liability on a company where justified, for example for tax offences.

## When to use

Use it for questions on how an artificial entity can be liable: cite organ theory to give the company a "mind", alongside the Tzuk Or and Modiin cases.
