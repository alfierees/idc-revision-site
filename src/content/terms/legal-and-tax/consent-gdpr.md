One of the lawful bases for processing: the subject gave explicit, informed consent for a specific purpose. It must be freely given, specific, informed and as easy to withdraw as to give.

## When to use

Remember it is not the only basis and often not the best: if processing is necessary to deliver the service, the contract basis covers it. The [[Meta Ruling]] held that terms of use cannot be stretched to justify pervasive behavioural advertising without genuine consent.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
