An interim agreement whose legal effect depends on the parties' intent and definiteness. It may be a stage in negotiations a party can still withdraw from, or already a binding contract. In Rabinai a short memorandum with all essential terms was held binding.

## When to use

Use it when a document is called an MOU: the label does not control, so test serious intention and whether the essential terms (parties, asset, transaction, price, payment dates) are present.
