The Patents Law, 1967 is the Israeli statute whose requirements an invention is checked against before a patent application is filed with the Israel Patent Authority.

## When to use

Cite it as the source of the patent conditions (novelty, non-triviality, inventive step, technological applicability) when answering Israeli patent questions.

See [[Lec_05-Intellectual Property|Lec 05]].
