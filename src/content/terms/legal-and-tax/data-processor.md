A party that processes personal data on the controller's behalf (Art. 28), for example a cloud vendor. It is bound by a data-processing agreement with the controller.

## When to use

Use it to tell a vendor acting on instructions apart from the controller who decides purposes and means, which determines who carries the primary GDPR responsibility.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
