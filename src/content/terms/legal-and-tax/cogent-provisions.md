Mandatory legal rules that a contract may not override, whatever the parties agree. The lecture's cases are Zim v. Maziar, Lagil Trampoline and Levin v. Levin.

## When to use

Use them to attack exemption or waiver clauses: sanctity of life cannot be waived, and a clause barring access to court is void.
