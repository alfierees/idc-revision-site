One of the two duties Section 192(a) of the Companies Law places on an officer, alongside loyalty: to act with reasonable skill and diligence for the company's benefit. The officer's actions count as the corporation's own actions.

## When to use

Use it in officer-liability and agency-problem questions together with the duty of loyalty.

See [[Lec_03-Company Law & Corporate Structures|Lec 03]].
