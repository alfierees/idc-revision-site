Safe Harbor was the 2000 EU-US framework for transferring personal data. It was invalidated by the Schrems I ruling on 6 October 2015, after which companies turned to standard contractual clauses (SCCs).

## When to use

Use it as the first step in the Schrems timeline, which matters for whether EU user data can lawfully be hosted on US cloud infrastructure.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
