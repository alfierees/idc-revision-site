Privacy by Design means privacy protections are engineered into the product from the start rather than bolted on after launch (paired with Privacy by Default, where the most protective settings are the out-of-the-box state). It is codified in Article 25 of the GDPR.

## When to use

Use it to show that GDPR compliance is a product-design discipline for engineers and product managers, not only lawyers; the DPIA is this idea turned into a procedure.
