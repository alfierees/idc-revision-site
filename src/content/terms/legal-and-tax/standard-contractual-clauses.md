Pre-approved contract terms under Art. 46 GDPR that bind the data importer to EU-level protection. They are one of four transfer mechanisms, with the [[Adequacy Decision]] (Art. 45), [[Binding Corporate Rules]] (Art. 47) and codes of conduct.

## When to use

Use it for questions on moving EU data outside the EU, noting that [[Schrems II Case]] upheld SCCs but requires a case-by-case check, and that losing adequacy would push Israeli companies onto SCCs.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
