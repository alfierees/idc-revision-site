A document negotiated at the Investment Rounds stage alongside the [[Investment Agreement]], with the Articles then amended for investor rights such as [[Liquidation Preference]] and [[Anti-Dilution]]. The course registry describes it as a non-binding summary of the main investment terms, drafted before the definitive agreement. <!-- TODO: not found in lecture notes; needs human review -->

## When to use

Use it when listing what a company does at the Investment Rounds stage: negotiate the term sheet and investment agreement, amend the Articles, and keep clean records for [[Due Diligence]].
