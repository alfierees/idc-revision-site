The Israeli body, at the Ministry of Justice, where patent applications are filed under the Patents Law 1967. Examiners check novelty, non-triviality and inventive step and may demand clarifications and amendments. If approved, the patent is entered in the patent register.

## When to use

Use it in the patent registration steps: preliminary search, filing a description with claims, abstract and fees, then approval, 20 years from filing and maintenance fees. The lecture ties only the patent process to this body.
