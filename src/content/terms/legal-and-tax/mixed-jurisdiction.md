A legal system that combines Common Law and Civil (Continental) Law. Israel takes the adversarial procedure, evidence rules and agency from Common Law (via the King's Order in Council) and good faith and codification from Continental Law. Scotland and Louisiana are the lecture's other examples.

## When to use

Use it when asked to classify Israeli law or explain why precedent and codified statutes coexist.
