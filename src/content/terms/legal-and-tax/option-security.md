An option is a derivative: a contract giving you the right, but not the obligation, to buy (or sell) an asset, usually a share, at a pre-agreed strike price within a fixed period. The payoff is capped on the downside (the premium) and open on the upside: $\max(S_T-K,0)-\text{premium}$.

## When to use

Use it when a question asks why options suit employee equity: the holder's loss is limited and the upside is open, so an employee can only win.
