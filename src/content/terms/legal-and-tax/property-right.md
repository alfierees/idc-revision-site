A property right is a legal right that gives a person direct control over an asset, tangible or intangible, letting the holder use it, benefit from it and protect it against harm by others. It is one of the strongest rights: attached to a specific asset, enforceable against any infringer, and enduring over time.

## When to use

Use it as the foundation for IP questions: IP is a species of property right and so inherits its strength compared with a merely contractual right.
