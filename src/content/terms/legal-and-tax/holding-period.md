Under the Section 102 Capital Gain Track the employee must not sell or release the shares for 24 months from the grant date to get the 25% capital gains rate. A sale earlier is still allowed, but the gain is then taxed at the marginal rate (up to about 59%). The trustee holds the options and shares to enforce this.

## When to use

Use it in Section 102 tax calculations: the clock runs from the grant date (not exercise or vesting), so check whether the sale is before or after 24 months before choosing the rate.
