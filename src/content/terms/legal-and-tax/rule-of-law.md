The Rule of Law means all entities, including the state, must act in accordance with the law, with no one above it. Formally it requires enforcement and equality before the law, and substantively that law is published, clear and non-retroactive.

## When to use

Use it, with separation of powers, as one of the ideas restraining state power in the Israeli legal system.
