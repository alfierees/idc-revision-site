Section 24: a contract's content may be whatever the parties agree, covering freedom of design and freedom of content. It is limited by public policy (Section 30), and the lecture calls it quite weak and quite eroded today.

## When to use

Use it as one pole of the Section 24 versus Section 30 tug-of-war, including the Barak and Zamir debate in Rekant.
