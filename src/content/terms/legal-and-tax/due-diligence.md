The process in which a potential investor or partner examines all aspects of a company (finances, client base, legal agreements, IP assets) before investing or closing a deal. It verifies that representations are accurate and surfaces hidden problems.

## When to use

Use it to show how early legal shortcuts (no founders' agreement, IP left in a founder's name, missing compliance) come due at a funding round or exit and can kill or reprice the deal.
