Under Section 1 of the Contracts Law a contract is formed by offer and acceptance. An offer (s.2) shows serious intention to contract and is definite enough to be accepted; acceptance (s.5) is a notice from the offeree delivered to the offeror, showing serious intention to contract in accordance with the offer.

## When to use

Use it for formation questions: the contract forms when the acceptance notice is delivered (constructive delivery under s.60(b) is enough), not when the minds meet. Then test serious intention and definiteness.
