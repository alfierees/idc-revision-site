Personally identifiable information is any information relating to an identifiable person. It covers basic identifiers, online identifiers (IP addresses, cookies, browsing history), financial and professional data, and sensitive special-category data such as biometric, genetic and health data.

## When to use

Use it to decide whether the GDPR applies: IP addresses and cookie IDs count even without a name, which pulls ad-tech and tracking into scope.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
