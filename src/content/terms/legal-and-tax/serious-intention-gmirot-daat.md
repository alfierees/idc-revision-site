The mental element of a contract (loosely its mens rea). The test is objective ([[Barashi Case]]) and looks at intention exactly at the moment of signing ([[Rabinai Case]]), but the objective test is insufficient for a signer with impaired capacity, whose act of signing does not reflect true will.

## When to use

Use it in formation questions: an offer needs serious intention plus [[Definiteness (Mesuyamut)]], and facts about a vulnerable or confused signer point to the Barashi exception.
