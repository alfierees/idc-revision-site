The four stages of a venture (Pre-Incorporation, Incorporation, Investment Rounds, Exit), each with its own legal priorities. Getting the legal work right early is cheap, while fixing it during diligence or an exit is expensive or impossible.

## When to use

Use it for the recurring question of what legal aspect matters most right now, matching documents to a stage, for example [[Founders' Agreement]] and [[Vesting]] pre-incorporation, or [[Term Sheet]] and [[Investment Agreement]] at investment rounds.
