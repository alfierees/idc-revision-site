Under Companies Law s.4 a company is capable of any right or obligation consistent with its nature: it can own, contract, sue, be sued and even bear criminal liability. A corporate veil separates the company's debts from its shareholders' personal debts, which is what delivers [[Limited Liability]].

## When to use

Use it as the starting point for veil questions: the rule comes from [[Salomon Case]], and it is limited by [[Piercing the Corporate Veil]] and the [[Subordination Doctrine]].
