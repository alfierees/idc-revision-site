Initial public offering: the first sale of a company's shares to the public on a stock exchange. It is the last rung of the funding ladder (Bootstrapping, Pre-Seed, Seed, A, B, C, IPO) and is both a fundraising event and a liquidity event that lets early investors cash out.

## When to use

Use it in startup-lifecycle questions on the exit stage (M&A or IPO) and on public companies, where shares are traded and disclosure is strict.
