Protection over secret information with economic value that is kept confidential. It needs no registration or state examination and can last forever, but only while the information stays secret; once it is leaked, reverse-engineered or independently discovered the protection is gone.

## When to use

Use it in the patent versus trade-secret choice: patenting publishes the invention in exchange for a 20-year monopoly, while a trade secret stays hidden but gives no protection against independent discovery (the Coca-Cola formula is the example).
