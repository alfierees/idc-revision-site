The branch of law governing the validity of contracts and the requirements for a binding agreement. Israel's main statutes are the Contracts (General Part) Law 1973 and the Contracts (Remedies for Breach) Law 1970. It is a constant tug of war between freedom of contract (s.24) and public policy (s.30).

## When to use

Frame a contract answer by the four stages (pre-contractual, formation, performance, termination or breach) and the tests for each, remembering that substance beats form.
