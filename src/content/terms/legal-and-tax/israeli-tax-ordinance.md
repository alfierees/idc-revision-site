The Income Tax Ordinance, the Israeli tax statute. Section 102 gives employee equity a capital gain regime (25%) instead of ordinary employment income (up to about 59%); Section 3(i) covers those outside Section 102 and is less favourable.

## When to use

Use it in ESOP questions to decide which section applies: Section 102 for employees and officers, Section 3(i) for controlling shareholders, consultants and service providers.
