Wealthy individuals who invest their own money in very early-stage startups, when the idea is still in development. They have low day-to-day involvement but offer mentorship, connections and guidance.

## When to use

Contrast with [[Venture Capital]] in fundraising questions: an angel writes an early cheque on belief in the idea, while a VC fund invests larger sums after product and market are proven and takes a seat at the table.
