Under Art. 51 GDPR, each member state's own national data-protection regulator, with investigation and enforcement powers (for example Ireland's DPC, which oversees many US tech firms' EU headquarters).

## When to use

Use it for GDPR enforcement questions: authorities investigate and fine, the [[EDPB]] coordinates them for consistent application, and the [[CJEU]] interprets the law. The maximum fine is the higher of EUR 20 million or 4% of global turnover.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
