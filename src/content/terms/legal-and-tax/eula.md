An End User Licence Agreement defines the terms of use for purchased software or digital products and limits the company's liability for misuse. It is typically required.

## When to use

Use it when sorting venture documents into those the law forces on you (privacy policy) and those adopted out of self-interest (terms of service), and to distinguish it from a licence agreement.
