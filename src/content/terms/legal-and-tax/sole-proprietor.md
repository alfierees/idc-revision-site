A business that is not a separate legal entity: the business is the person, with personal liability and no separation. Its appeal is simple single-layer taxation and deductible business expenses.

## When to use

Use it when choosing an entity: the course rule of thumb is to consider switching to a company once expected annual revenue passes about 500,000 NIS, or when taking partners, investors or real operational risk.
