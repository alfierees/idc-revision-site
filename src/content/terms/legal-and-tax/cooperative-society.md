A uniquely Israeli, member-owned entity that blends economic activity with egalitarian principles. Members have equal rights regardless of investment, there are no controlling shareholders, and the rule is one member, one vote. Profits are shared by participation, not capital, and the Registrar of Cooperative Societies supervises it, not the Companies Law.

## When to use

Use it in the entity menu (kibbutzim, moshavim, Egged) and with the [[Pri HaEmek Case]], where the articles-as-contract idea blocks a majority rewriting members' deal.
