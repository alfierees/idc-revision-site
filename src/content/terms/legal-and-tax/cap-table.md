A table or spreadsheet managing the company's share capital, showing who holds what. It is a living document that changes with new hires, rounds and instruments. A holder's ownership percentage is the shares they hold divided by total outstanding shares.

## When to use

Start any dilution or round calculation here, and decide whether the total is issued shares or [[Fully Diluted Share Capital]], since every later calculation depends on that choice.
