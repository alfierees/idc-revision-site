The EU-level body (Arts. 68 to 76) that coordinates the national supervisory authorities, issues guidance and ensures the GDPR is applied consistently across all 27 member states.

## When to use

Use it for GDPR enforcement questions: national authorities investigate and fine, the EDPB keeps them consistent, and the CJEU interprets the law.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
