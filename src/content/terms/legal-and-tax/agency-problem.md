A conflict between those who own (principals) and those who manage or control (agents). Section 192(a) of the Companies Law makes officers act only for the company, and the conflict runs management against shareholders, majority against minority, and shareholders against creditors. The last is fuelled by limited liability encouraging excessive risk.

## When to use

Use it to spot a breach of the s.192(a) duty of loyalty and care when an officer's personal interest pulls against the company's. The lecture's example is a director who invests in a relative's company although better proposals exist.
