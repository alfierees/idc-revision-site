A contract defining the terms on which a supplier provides services to a business client (responsibilities, timelines, financial conditions), protecting the company if the other side fails to comply.

## When to use

Use it when asked which instrument governs a supplier and business-client relationship, and when a measurable service-quality layer needs to be added through an [[SLA]].
