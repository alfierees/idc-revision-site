Section 34, Sale Law: a good-faith buyer can get good title even from a non-owner if four conditions are met: good faith (subjective); for value; good faith at the time the asset is transferred; and the asset was bought from someone who appeared to be the seller, in the ordinary course of that seller's business.

## When to use

Use it for a stolen or disputed good bought by an innocent buyer: tick off all four conditions. Knaan shows the ordinary-course condition failing.
