The Contracts (General Part) Law, 1973 (60 sections on formation, interpretation and validity). Together with the Contracts (Remedies for Breach) Law, 1970 (20 sections) it is one of the two statutes governing contracts. It includes s.2 and s.5 on offer and acceptance, s.12 good faith in negotiation, s.25 interpretation and s.39 good faith in performance.

## When to use

Use it to anchor contract answers in the right section: formation, interpretation (s.25), gap-filling and good faith. Remedies for breach sit in the separate 1970 law.
