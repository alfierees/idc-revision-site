Protection for creators of works in the arts, culture and software, giving exclusive rights and an economic incentive to create. It arises automatically on creation, with no registration, fee or examination, so a startup's source code is protected from the first commit.

## When to use

Choose it when the asset is code, content or a creative work. Contrast with patents and trademarks, which need registration. For AI output, see [[AI and Copyright]].
