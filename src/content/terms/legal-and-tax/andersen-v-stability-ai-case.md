A US case asking whether using training data based on protected works infringes copyright. The court emphasised the line between data analysis and direct creative use of copyrighted works. The lecture gives no final ruling, only the question and that line.

## When to use

Cite it, together with the [[New York Times AI Case]], as the US training-data battleground when discussing [[AI and Copyright]] and [[Fair Use]].
