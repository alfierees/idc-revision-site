Section 8 of the Land Law makes a written document a condition of validity for a real-estate transaction, so without writing it is invalid. [[Kalmer v Gai Case]] softens this: in special and exceptional circumstances good faith can prevail over the writing principle.

## When to use

Use it in real-estate contract questions, checking Kalmer's two conditions: change of position (reliance) and the degree of fault of the party withdrawing.

See [[Lec_04-Contract Law|Lec 04]].
