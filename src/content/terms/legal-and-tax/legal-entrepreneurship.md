The course's frame for the law a founder needs: a venture's legal universe sits on three recurring pillars, business structure (what kind of entity it is and who owns and controls it), the contractual aspect (relationships and obligations with people and firms) and regulation (mandatory external rules such as privacy law). The lecture's point is that law is an anchor, not a brake: a proper framework protects against future risk and builds credibility with investors.

## When to use

Use it to organise any case or exam answer: sort each issue into structure, contracts or regulation before analysing it.
