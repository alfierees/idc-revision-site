The EU programme launched in 2015 to create mandatory, uniform digital rules across all member states. Its law arrives in three generations: the GDPR, then the DSA and DMA (2022), then the AI Act (2024).

## When to use

Use it to place each EU instrument in its generation and to explain why one uniform standard lowers cross-border costs and spreads through the Brussels Effect.
