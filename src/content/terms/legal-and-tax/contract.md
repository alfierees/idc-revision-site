A legally binding agreement that the law will enforce. In Israel it needs offer and acceptance, intention to create legal relations, legal capacity, legality of purpose and certainty, but not consideration.

## When to use

Run the conditions in order, and remember that consent is wider than contract: political deals, illegal, informal, coerced or unfair agreements are not enforced.
