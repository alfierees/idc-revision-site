Periodic fees the patent holder must keep paying to keep a patent alive after it is registered. Protection lasts 20 years from the filing date.

## When to use

Use it in the patent lifecycle: file, examine, register, then pay maintenance fees until expiry, after which competitors may copy the invention.

See [[Lec_05-Intellectual Property|Lec 05]].
