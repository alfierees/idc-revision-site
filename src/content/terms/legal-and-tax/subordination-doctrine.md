Suspension of a shareholder's right to repayment of a loan until the company has fully paid its obligations to other creditors (Section 6(c), per Justice Rubinstein in the [[Northern Chicks Case]]). The shareholder loans are treated as if they were equity, so external creditors are paid in full first.

## When to use

Use it when [[Thin Capitalization]] and improper management meet shareholder loans: subordination preserves the veil and only re-orders the queue, so it is a gentler tool than [[Piercing the Corporate Veil]].
