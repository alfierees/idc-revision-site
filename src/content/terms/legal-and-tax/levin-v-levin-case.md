Two holdings from Justice Zamir. A divorcing couple wrote that their separate alimony agreement had no legal validity; the court held it binding because substance (true purpose and circumstances) outweighs form, and the context showed genuine intent. Separately, a clause barring the parties from going to court contradicts public policy and is void, since it blocks access to justice.

## When to use

Use it for intention to create legal relations (an express no-legal-effect clause does not stop a contract binding) and, as a cogent-provision example, for clauses that exclude court access.
