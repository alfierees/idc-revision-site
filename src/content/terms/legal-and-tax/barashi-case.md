CA 3601/96. Relatives had a mentally and physically unstable elderly man sign a gift contract transferring his assets to them, although he was never declared incompetent. The Supreme Court (Justice Beinisch) held that the objective test for serious intention is insufficient where the signer has impaired capacity, because signing does not then reflect true will, and dismissed the appeal.

## When to use

Cite it when a signer is vulnerable or lacked understanding: the objective test of [[Serious Intention (Gmirot Da'at)]] bends for impaired capacity, even without a formal declaration of incompetence.
