One of the Article 5 GDPR principles: collect only data that is relevant and adequate for the stated purpose, with no hoarding "just in case".

## When to use

Cite it when a product collects more personal data than its purpose needs. It sits beside purpose limitation and accountability, so the controller must also be able to prove it complies.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
