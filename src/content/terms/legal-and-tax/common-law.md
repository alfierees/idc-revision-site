The English tradition (also the USA), where written law plus judicial precedent is the primary source and the judge is a relatively passive referee. The trial is driven by cross-examination, and persuasive arguments decide the case. Israel inherited rules of evidence, agency and adversarial procedure from it.

## When to use

Contrast it with [[Continental Law]]: common law trusts the contest, civil law trusts the code. Together they explain why Israel is a [[Mixed Jurisdiction]].
