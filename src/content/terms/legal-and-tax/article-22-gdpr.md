The right not to be subject to purely automated decisions with significant effects. It is the right rising fastest, because it directly constrains AI-driven scoring and ranking.

## When to use

Use it for automated profiling and credit-scoring questions, with the [[SCHUFA Case]] as the authority: automated credit scoring counts as automated decision-making when the score effectively determines an outcome.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
