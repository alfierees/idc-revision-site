CJEU ruling of 7 December 2023 on Germany's largest credit-rating agency. It held that automated credit scoring counts as automated decision-making under [[Article 22 GDPR]] when the score effectively determines an outcome, so transparency and human accountability for the algorithm are required.

## When to use

Use it for questions on AI-driven scoring or ranking and the limits [[Article 22 GDPR]] places on it.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
