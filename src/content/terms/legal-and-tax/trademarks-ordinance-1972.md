The Trademarks Ordinance [New Version], 1972, which governs trademarks in Israel: it protects symbols, words, logos and other identifying signs and stops competitors using them in ways that mislead the public.

## When to use

Use it as the Israeli source of law when a question asks whether a sign can be registered as a [[Trademark]].

See [[Lec_05-Intellectual Property|Lec 05]].
