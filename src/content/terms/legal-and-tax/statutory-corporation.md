An entity defined by its own statute and created by law, with no owner. The lecture's examples are public bodies such as the Academy of the Hebrew Language and the Council for Higher Education.

## When to use

Use it in the menu of legal entities when a question asks which vehicle is created by statute rather than registered by founders.

See [[Lec_03-Company Law & Corporate Structures|Lec 03]].
