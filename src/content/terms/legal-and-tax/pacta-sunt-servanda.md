Pacta sunt servanda ("agreements must be kept") is the Roman-law principle that is the bedrock of contract law.

## When to use

Use it as the starting point when explaining why contracts are enforceable, before the limits set by public policy and cogent provisions.

See [[Lec_04-Contract Law|Lec 04]].
