The minimum period before any [[Vesting]] begins, typically one year. In the lecture's example, after the one-year cliff 25% of the options vest and the rest vest monthly; leave before the cliff and you get nothing.

## When to use

Use it in grant-timeline questions, for example a 10,000-option grant over four years where an employee leaves before month 12.

See [[Lec_06-Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP)|Lec 06]].
