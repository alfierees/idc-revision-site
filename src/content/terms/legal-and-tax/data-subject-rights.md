The eight enforceable GDPR rights (Arts. 12 to 23): to be informed, of access (the DSAR), to rectification, to object to processing, regarding automated decisions and profiling (Art. 22), to be forgotten, to data portability, and to restrict processing.

## When to use

Use it to list the rights and, for each, the product feature it forces a founder to build (privacy notice, data export, edit profile, opt-out, true deletion).

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
