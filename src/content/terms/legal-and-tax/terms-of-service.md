The document between operator and users that defines users' rights and the conditions of use, protecting the company against misuse. It is not legally required, but strongly advisable.

## When to use

Use it in the website and product trio question: the [[Privacy Policy]] is legally required (for example under GDPR), the Terms of Service is not, and the [[Licence Agreement]] and [[EULA]] depend on the asset.
