Justice Barak's pendulum model of interpretation, which moves from the circumstances to the language and back to the circumstances, treating the circumstances as the more significant tool. It replaced the original language-first reading of s.25(a). Vegetable Growers and the 2011 amendment of s.25 later softened it back toward the text.

## When to use

It is the single most important interpretive doctrine for contract questions. Argue it against the language-first view, then note that clear explicit language now governs under the amended [[Section 25 Contracts Law]].
