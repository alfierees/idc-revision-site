A legal termination for serious wrongdoing such as fraud or wilful misconduct. It is defined narrowly because a dismissal for cause wipes out even vested options, which expire immediately.

## When to use

Use it with the termination table: without cause, vested options survive 3 months; for cause, they die at once. Since a broad definition gives the company power to claw back equity, it is negotiated carefully.
