Tax on profit from selling an asset. In this course it is the 25% rate that applies to an employee's gain on the Section 102 [[Capital Gain Track]].

## When to use

Compare 25% against the marginal rate of up to about 59% when valuing an option, and remember that exercising is not a tax event on this track, only the sale.

See [[Lec_06-Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP)|Lec 06]].
