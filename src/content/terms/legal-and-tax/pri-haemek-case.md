The Pri HaEmek cooperative became insolvent, and its general meeting voted to waive limited liability and attribute the cooperative's debts to its members personally. The Supreme Court (Justice Barak, majority) cancelled the decision: stripping limited liability is a substantive change to the foundational bargain and needs the unanimous consent of all members, not a majority.

## When to use

Use it for questions on whether a majority can rewrite the articles: the articles are a contract, and a majority cannot unilaterally change who bears the debts.
