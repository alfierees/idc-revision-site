Salomon v. Salomon & Co. (England, 1897) held that a company is a distinct legal entity from its shareholders even when the main shareholder runs it in practice. Incorporation creates a genuine legal wall, not a sham.

## When to use

Cite it as the foundation of separate legal personality, and as the principle courts protect when they only exceptionally pierce the veil.
