A separate legal entity created to distinguish the personal assets of shareholders from those of the company. It gives shareholders limited liability and lets the venture raise capital by issuing shares.

## When to use

Use it when comparing a company with a sole proprietor or partnership, or when a question turns on who bears the venture's debts and whether founders' personal assets are protected.
