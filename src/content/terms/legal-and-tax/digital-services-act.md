The EU law on platform transparency: duties of online platforms to be open about content, ranking and ads. It is a second-generation Digital Single Market instrument, adopted in 2022 with the DMA.

## When to use

Use it for the platform transparency domain, and to place it in the three generations of EU digital law.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
