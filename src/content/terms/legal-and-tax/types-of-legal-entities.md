The menu of vehicles Israeli law offers a business: [[Sole Proprietor]], [[Partnership]], [[Association (Amuta)]], [[Cooperative Society]], [[Statutory Corporation]] and the company (Ltd.). The founder picks the one whose liability, taxation and control profile fits the venture.

## When to use

Use it in entity-choice questions: sole proprietors and partnerships expose the owners personally, while the company buys limited liability at the cost of double taxation and formality.
