The document the Registrar issues once the company has been formed. It follows the filing of an application, the articles, a declaration of first directors and the registration fee.

## When to use

Use it in the formation steps for a company: choose the type, file the documents, receive the certificate.

See [[Lec_03-Company Law & Corporate Structures|Lec 03]].
