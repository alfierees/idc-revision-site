Funding the company from the founders' personal resources, and friends and family, rather than outside investors. It is the first rung of the financing ladder, before pre-seed.

## When to use

Place it at the start of the ladder (bootstrapping, pre-seed, seed, A, B, C, IPO), where cheques are smallest and dilution is lowest.
