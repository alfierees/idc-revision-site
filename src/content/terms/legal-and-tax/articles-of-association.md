The company's foundational governing document, its internal constitution, which is mandatory under s.15 of the Companies Law. Mandatory content (s.18) covers the name, objectives, registered share capital and limitation of liability, while s.19 lists optional content. The articles are also a contract between the company and its shareholders and among the shareholders.

## When to use

Use the articles-as-contract idea when members try to change their deal unilaterally, as in the Pri HaEmek case. Also remember that investor rights such as liquidation preference and anti-dilution are written into the articles in funding rounds.
