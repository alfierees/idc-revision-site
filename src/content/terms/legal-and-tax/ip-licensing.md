An IP licence grants the right to use a patent or trademark while the owner retains ownership, enabling broad distribution. Types in the lecture are software licences, reseller licences and open-source licences.

## When to use

Use it when a question asks how a company can let others use its IP without selling it, or flags open-source licence terms (e.g. copyleft such as GPL) that could force release of proprietary code.
