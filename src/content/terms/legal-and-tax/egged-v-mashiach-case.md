The court held that an indirect contractual restriction on freedom of occupation is equivalent to a direct restriction (even one implied by a Basic Law), so the contract can be void as contrary to public policy under Section 30.

## When to use

Cite it for the point that you cannot evade public policy by being subtle, as an example on the Section 30 side of the freedom of contract versus public policy tug-of-war.
