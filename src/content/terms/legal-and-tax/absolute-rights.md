Property rights such as ownership of real estate, which give the fullest bundle of control over the asset. They are the counterpart of [[Limited Rights]], which carve out only a slice of control.

## When to use

Use it when an IP or property question asks you to classify a right: ownership is absolute, while a lease, lien or tenancy is limited.
