A shareholder's exposure is capped at the amount they invested in their shares; creditors generally cannot pursue their personal assets. It flows from the company's separate legal personality and exists to encourage reasonable risk-taking.

## When to use

Use it to explain why founders incorporate rather than partner (partners are personally liable and can bind each other). Courts may deviate from it, and shareholders cannot be stripped of it by a mere majority vote (Pri HaEmek).
