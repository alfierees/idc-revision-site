A non-profit entity with separate legal personality, like a corporation, registered with the Registrar of Associations. Its purposes must be social and its profits cannot be distributed to members; it gets tax benefits and can receive donations under s.46.

## When to use

Use it in the entity-choice comparison: it fits non-profits, not a venture that wants to distribute profit, where a company is the vehicle.
