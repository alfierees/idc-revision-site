Issued shares plus every potential share from options (granted and ungranted), warrants and convertible notes. Investors and lawyers always negotiate on this number, not on issued shares alone.

## When to use

Use it as the base for cap-table maths: the price per share is the pre-money valuation divided by the pre-round fully diluted shares.
