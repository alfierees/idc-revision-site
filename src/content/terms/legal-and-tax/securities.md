The instruments a company issues to fund itself. The three core ones differ in one way: shares are ownership (votes under s.188, dividends under s.190, a residual claim on liquidation), bonds are debt, and options are a right to buy shares later.

## When to use

Shows up when asking how a company raises money or what rights an investor or employee actually holds (a share versus an option versus a loan).
