The timetable of vesting dates on which the employee becomes entitled to exercise the options or a portion of them. The example in the lecture is a grant vesting over 4 years, with a one-year cliff and monthly vesting after it.

## When to use

Use it when asked how many options are exercisable at a given date: until an option vests it cannot be exercised, and unvested options expire immediately on termination.
