The Section 102 route without a trustee: the employee is taxed at the marginal rate (up to about 59%) and the company gets no deduction, the worst of both worlds. Exercise triggers tax on the paper spread even with no cash.

## When to use

Use it in the three-track comparison: only the Capital Gain Track gives the employee 25%, and without a trustee you fall into this track.
