The common-law requirement that each side exchange something of value. Israeli contract law dropped it, so a gratuitous promise can still bind.

## When to use

Do not import the common-law rule into an Israeli exam answer: gift cases such as Barashi turn on serious intention, not consideration.

See [[Lec_04-Contract Law|Lec 04]].
