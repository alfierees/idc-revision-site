Rights that protect inventions, creations and trademarks from unauthorised use. They include patents, copyrights, design rights, trade secrets and trademarks. As a species of property right, IP is valid against the whole world, not only against parties to a contract.

## When to use

Use it to choose the right regime for an asset (copyright, patent, trade secret, trademark, design) and to check that founders have assigned all IP to the company, since IP is often a startup's whole value.
