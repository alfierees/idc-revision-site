An appointed privacy officer required of certain organisations (Arts. 37 to 39), responsible for oversight of GDPR compliance and liaison with regulators.

## When to use

Appears in the list of governance obligations, alongside the DPIA, RoPA, a DSAR process and a privacy policy. Use it when asked what roles and artefacts a GDPR-compliant company must have.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
