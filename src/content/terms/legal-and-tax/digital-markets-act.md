The EU law covering digital competition, which works as antitrust for digital gatekeepers. It is a second-generation instrument of the Digital Single Market, adopted in 2022 together with the DSA.

## When to use

Use it for the digital competition domain among the five domains of digital regulation, and to place it in the three generations (GDPR, then DSA and DMA, then the AI Act).

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
