The individual whose personal data is processed, and the holder of the GDPR's enforceable rights. The GDPR reaches any organisation anywhere that processes the personal data of EU residents.

## When to use

Use it to identify who is protected in a GDPR scenario and which rights that person can exercise against the controller.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
