The Israeli tax authority (ITA). In a Section 102 plan the trustee submits the plan to the ITA for approval; grants may start only 30 days after submission, and the trustee files quarterly and annual reports (Forms 146 and 156).

## When to use

Use it in the Section 102 implementation timeline and when a question asks what disqualifies a plan: late deposits or grants made too early can lose the capital gain treatment.
