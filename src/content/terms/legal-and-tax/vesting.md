Earning the right to equity or options gradually over time, so a departing founder or employee keeps only the vested part. It stops a departing co-founder walking away with dead equity, and for employees it creates a long-term retention effect (golden handcuffs).

## When to use

Use it in founders' agreement and option-grant questions, including termination: unvested options expire immediately, and vested options survive for a limited window depending on the reason for leaving.
