Raising capital to support operations from personal equity, private investors, venture capital funds and crowdfunding. The investor ladder runs from angels (very early) to VC (proven product) to an IPO (mature), trading capital for control.

## When to use

Use it for stage-by-stage questions: which investor type fits, what legal documents each round needs (term sheet, investment agreement), and how valuation drives the ownership percentage and dilution.
