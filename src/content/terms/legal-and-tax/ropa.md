RoPA is the Records of Processing Activities required by Article 30 of the GDPR: a living register of what data you process, why, which categories of subjects, how long you keep it and how it is protected.

## When to use

Use it in compliance-tool questions alongside the DPIA (Art. 35), DPO appointment and breach notification.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
