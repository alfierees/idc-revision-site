The Companies Law of 1999, the statute behind the lecture on company structures. It sets the organs of the company (general meeting, board, CEO), their powers, and the officers' duties of loyalty and care (s.192).

## When to use

Use it when a company question asks who has authority or which duty applies; statutory references in company-law questions are to this law unless stated otherwise.
