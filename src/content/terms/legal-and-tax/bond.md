A loan from an investor to a company (or government): a contractual obligation to repay principal plus interest. The bondholder is a creditor, not an owner.

## When to use

Use it to contrast the three securities: shares are ownership, bonds are debt, and options are a right to buy. Example from the lecture: 1,000 NIS at 5% a year pays 50 NIS interest annually plus the 1,000 NIS back at maturity.
