Portable property such as furniture and vehicles, as opposed to real estate (land and houses). Property law first regulated these tangible assets before reaching intangible ones like IP.

## When to use

Use it in the IP lecture's property-rights foundation to contrast tangible assets with intangible IP, where the protected boundary is harder to define.
