Amendment 13 to Israel's Privacy Protection Law, the legislative effort to keep Israel's regime aligned with the GDPR standard so that its EU adequacy recognition is not lost.

## When to use

Use it to explain why Israel changed its privacy law: losing [[Adequacy Decision|adequacy]] would force every Israeli company handling EU data onto cumbersome standard contractual clauses. It is the [[Brussels Effect]] acting directly on Israeli law.

See [[Lec_07-Digital Regulation|Lec 07]].
