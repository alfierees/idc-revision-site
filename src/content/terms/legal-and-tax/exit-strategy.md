The founders' or owners' plan for leaving the company or realising a profit, defining how and when it will be sold, exit the market or go public via an IPO.

## When to use

Use it at the exit stage of the startup lifecycle, where warranties and indemnities, taxes and a final review of IP, contracts and compliance matter.
