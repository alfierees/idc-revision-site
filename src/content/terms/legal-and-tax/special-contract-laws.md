Statutes for particular contract types (Consumer Protection, Standard Contracts, Insurance Contracts, Gifts, Sale and Banking Laws) that apply under the lex specialis principle, because one side is structurally weaker and needs extra protection. The lecture calls this the cry of fairness.

## When to use

Use it when one party is a consumer or the weaker side of a standard form: stricter protection applies against the stronger drafting party, and examples include a notary for a prenuptial agreement and writing for real estate.
