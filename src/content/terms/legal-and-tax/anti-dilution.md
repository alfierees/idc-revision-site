Investor protection for a down round, when new shares are issued at a lower price than the investor paid. It reduces the conversion price of the investor's shares to preserve their relative ownership and value. Lec 08 names two types: full ratchet and broad-based weighted average.

## When to use

Use it for down-round questions: invest at 10 dollars a share, next round at 5, and anti-dilution re-prices the earlier shares closer to the lower price. Lec 08 only names the two types and does not work through the mechanics.
