The EU's General Data Protection Regulation, in force since 25 May 2018, protects personal data and applies to all entities, not one sector. Under Art. 3 it binds anyone established in the EU and anyone anywhere who processes the personal data of EU residents, so no EU office is needed; fines reach EUR 20 million or 4% of global annual turnover, whichever is higher.

## When to use

Use it when a founder's product (even one based in Tel Aviv or San Francisco) can be signed up for by EU residents; the lecture treats extraterritorial reach as the trap founders fall into. Also the legal hook behind privacy policies, cookie banners and the six lawful bases for processing.
