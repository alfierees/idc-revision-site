A contract among co-founders that aligns vision, roles and authorities, sets the profit and share split, protects the company if a founder leaves (vesting, IP ownership), and fixes decision-making and dispute-resolution mechanisms.

## When to use

Use it as the most important early document: having none is the first core risk, and its absence is what sank the course's Scope 360 example.
