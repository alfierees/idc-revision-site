Intervals on which $f''$ has constant sign, found with a sign table of the zeros and undefined points of $f''$.

## When to use

Asked directly in questions such as the concavity of $\frac{-2}{15x^5}+\frac{1}{12x^3}$: concave up on $(-2,0)$ and $(2,\infty)$, concave down on $(-\infty,-2)$ and $(0,2)$.

See [[Lec_11-Concavity, Inflection Points and Asymptotes|Lec 11]].
