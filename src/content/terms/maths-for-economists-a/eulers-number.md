The constant $e\approx 2.718$, defined as the limit of $\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)^x$ as $x\to\infty$.

## When to use

It is the base of the natural logarithm and the target value of the $1^{\infty}$ Euler limits. It also gives the standard result $\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{e^x-1}{x}=1$, quoted without proof in the notes.
