The expression $\frac{f(a+h)-f(a)}{h}$, the slope of the secant line through $(a,f(a))$ and $(a+h,f(a+h))$. Its limit as $h\to 0$ is the derivative.

## When to use

Simplify it first (expand and cancel the $h$) before letting $h\to 0$. For $f(x)=x^2+1$ it reduces to $2x+h$.
