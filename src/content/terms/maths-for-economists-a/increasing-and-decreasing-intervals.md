Intervals where $f'$ is positive (the function rises) or negative (the function falls).

## When to use

Find the domain, differentiate, locate where $f'$ is $0$ or undefined, and test a point in each interval. A change from increasing to decreasing marks a relative maximum, and the reverse a minimum.
