A constant factor can be pulled out of a derivative: $(cf)'=c f'$.

## When to use

Use it with the power rule, for example $\left(\frac{x^3}{7}\right)'=\frac{3}{7}x^2$. The integral version pulls constants out of an integral and a sum in the same way.
