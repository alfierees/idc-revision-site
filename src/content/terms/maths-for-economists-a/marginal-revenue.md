The derivative $R'(x)$, the approximate extra revenue from selling one more unit.

## When to use

For $R(x)=1296x-0.12x^3$, set $R'(x)=0$ to get $x=60$. Since $R''(x)<0$ for every $x>0$, marginal revenue falls as output rises.
