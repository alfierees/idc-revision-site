The graph of $f(-x)$ flips $f$ across the y axis, and the graph of $-f(x)$ flips it across the x axis. A minus sign inside the bracket acts on $x$; one outside acts on $y$.

## When to use

Use it with the shift rules to sketch transformed graphs. The same flip across the x axis is the quick method for drawing an absolute value.
