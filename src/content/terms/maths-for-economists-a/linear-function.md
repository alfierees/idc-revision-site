A function $y=ax+b$ whose graph is a straight line. The slope $a$ is the change in $y$ per one-unit rise in $x$, and the intercept $b$ is the value of $y$ at $x=0$.

## When to use

Use it when a quantity changes at a constant rate, such as the straight-line depreciation of software from $12000$ to $2000$ over $8$ years, which gives $V(t)=12000-1250t$.
