Feeding the output of one function into another. In $f(g(x))$ the inner function $g$ acts first and its result becomes the input of $f$; the order matters, since in general $f(g(x))\ne g(f(x))$.

## When to use

Replace every $x$ in the outer function by the inner one and re-check the domain. For $g=\sqrt{x}$ and $f=2-x$ the two orders give $2-\sqrt{x}$ and $\sqrt{2-x}$. Later it is the setting for the chain rule.
