A rule that assigns to each allowed input number exactly one output number. Several inputs may share an output (squaring sends both $2$ and $-2$ to $4$), but one input may never have two outputs.

## When to use

Use it to decide whether an equation counts as a function: isolate $y$ and check that every allowed $x$ gives one unique $y$. A $\pm$ with a non-zero quantity under the root means it is not a function.
