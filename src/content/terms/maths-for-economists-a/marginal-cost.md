The derivative $C'(x)$, the approximate extra cost of producing one more unit.

## When to use

For $C(x)=300+100x-x^2$, $C'(30)=40$ against an exact change of $39$ from $30$ to $31$ units. $C'>0$ says cost rises; whether marginal cost itself rises needs the second derivative.

See [[Lec_09-Marginal Functions and Tangent Lines of Composites|Lec 09]].
