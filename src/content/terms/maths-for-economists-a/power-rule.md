The derivative of a power is $(x^a)'=a x^{a-1}$. It holds for any real exponent $a$, including fractions and negatives.

## When to use

Rewrite roots and reciprocals as powers first: $\sqrt[n]{x^m}=x^{m/n}$ and $\frac{1}{x^k}=x^{-k}$, so $\left(\sqrt{x^3}\right)'=\frac{3}{2}\sqrt{x}$ and $\left(\frac{3}{x^7}\right)'=-\frac{21}{x^8}$.
