The line $x=a$ where $f$ is undefined and tends to $\pm\infty$ as $x$ approaches $a$. It can be one-sided: $e^{1/x}$ blows up only from the right at $0$.

## When to use

Factor and cancel first. A zero denominator with a non-zero numerator, like $x=2$ for $\frac{3x-1}{x-2}$, gives an asymptote, while a cancelled factor gives a hole. Check each side separately.
