The logarithm with base $e$, written $\ln$. It is the inverse of $e^x$, so $\ln(e^{2x})=2x$.

## When to use

Take $\ln$ of both sides to solve exponential equations, and expect it in derivatives such as $(\ln f)'=\frac{f'}{f}$ and $(a^f)'=f'a^f\ln a$.
