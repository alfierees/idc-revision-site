A function is continuous at $x=a$ when $f(a)$ is defined, the limit as $x\to a$ exists, and that limit equals $f(a)$. Visually, you can draw the graph through $a$ without lifting the pen.

## When to use

Single-formula functions are continuous across their domain, so for a piecewise function test the three conditions only at the seam points. Solving for a parameter such as $a=1$ means equating left limit, right limit and value.
