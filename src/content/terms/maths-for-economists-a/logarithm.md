The inverse of an exponential function: $\log_a b$ is the power to which $a$ must be raised to get $b$. A logarithm is an exponent, and it is only defined for positive arguments.

## When to use

Use it to solve for an exponent, for example $e^{2x}=10$ gives $x=\frac{\ln 10}{2}$. Always check the answer against the domain, since arguments must be positive.
