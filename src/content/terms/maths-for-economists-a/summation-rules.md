Sums split over addition, constants pull out of a sum, and the sum of a constant $c$ over $n$ terms is $n\cdot c$. The third rule's formula is blank on the worksheet and is reconstructed in the lecture note.

## When to use

Use them to break a big sum into known pieces, for example $\sum_{i=1}^{10}(2^i+3i-5)=2046+3(55)-10(5)=2161$. A sum of products does not split into a product of sums.
