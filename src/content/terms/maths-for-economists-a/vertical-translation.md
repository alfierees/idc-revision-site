The graph of $y=f(x)+c$ is the graph of $f$ shifted up by $c$ units, and down if $c$ is negative.

## When to use

Complete the square to see a shift, for example $x^2-4x+1=(x-2)^2-3$ is $(x-2)^2$ moved down $3$. For $8\cdot 2^x-5$ the horizontal asymptote moves to $y=-5$.
