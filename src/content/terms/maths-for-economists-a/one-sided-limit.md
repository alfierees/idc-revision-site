The value $f(x)$ approaches as $x$ nears a point from the left only ($x\to a^-$) or from the right only ($x\to a^+$).

## When to use

Use it at seam points of piecewise functions, near absolute values and for infinite limits. If the two sides differ, the two-sided limit does not exist.
