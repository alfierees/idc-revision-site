$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx=\left[F(x)\right]_a^b=F(b)-F(a)$ for an antiderivative $F$ of $f$. It is the signed area between the graph and the x axis, counting area below the axis as negative.

## When to use

The constant $C$ cancels, so it is not needed. For a piecewise function split the interval at the breakpoints and add the pieces; finite jumps do not change the area.
