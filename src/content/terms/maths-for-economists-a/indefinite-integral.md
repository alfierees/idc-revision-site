The family of antiderivatives $F(x)+C$ of a function $f$, where $F'=f$.

## When to use

Integration undoes differentiation, so check by differentiating. Remember the constant $C$, which is dropped in a definite integral.

See [[Lec_08-Chain Rule, Exponential Derivatives and Integrals|Lec 08]].
