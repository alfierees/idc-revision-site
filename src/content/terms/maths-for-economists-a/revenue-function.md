Revenue is price times quantity sold, written as a function of price or of quantity. With the demand equation $x=6000-30p$ it becomes $R(p)=p(6000-30p)$.

## When to use

Build it from the price-demand equation before finding profit or maximal revenue. For $p=1296-0.12x^2$ it is $R(x)=1296x-0.12x^3$, which peaks at $x=60$.
