The steepness of a line, $m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$, the change in $y$ per one unit change in $x$. Once you have $m$, plug in either point to get the line $y-y_1=m(x-x_1)$.

## When to use

Use it to find the equation of a linear function from two points, as in the depreciation question. The same point-slope form is reused later for tangent lines.
