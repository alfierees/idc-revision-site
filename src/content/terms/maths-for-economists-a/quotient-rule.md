The derivative of a quotient is $\left(\frac{f}{g}\right)'=\frac{f'g-fg'}{g^2}$.

## When to use

Use it for a ratio of functions, as in $\left(\frac{1+x}{x}\right)'=-\frac{1}{x^2}$. Splitting the fraction into separate powers first often gives the same result.
