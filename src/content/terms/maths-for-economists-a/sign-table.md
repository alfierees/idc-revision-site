A table of the sign of $f'$ (or $f''$, or an absolute value's inside) on each interval between the split points. It is used to read off monotonicity, concavity or which rule of a piecewise form applies.

## When to use

Split at zeros and at excluded domain values, test one point per interval, and record numerator and denominator signs separately for quotients.

See [[Lec_10-Increase and Decrease Intervals and Relative Extrema|Lec 10]].
