The graph of a quadratic function. Its vertex is at $x=-\frac{b}{2a}$, and it opens up if $a>0$ or down if $a<0$.

## When to use

Use the vertex formula to locate a maximum or minimum without differentiating, for example the profit-maximising price $p=130$ in the clock-radio question.
