A point where the one-sided slopes are opposite infinities, forming a sharp point, so there is no tangent line. The function is not differentiable there.

## When to use

Seen at $x=0$ for $\sqrt[3]{x^2}$. Compare with a vertical tangent, where the infinities have the same sign. A cusp can still be a relative extremum, as at $(1,0)$ for $\sqrt[3]{(x-1)^2}$.
