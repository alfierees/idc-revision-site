The derivative of a constant is zero: $f(x)=c\Rightarrow f'(x)=0$.

## When to use

One of the basic differentiation rules, used term by term when differentiating a sum, for example the constant $300$ in $C(x)=300+100x-x^2$ drops out.

See [[Lec_07-The Derivative, Differentiability and Rules|Lec 07]].
