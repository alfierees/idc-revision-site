If $f$ is continuous on an interval and has no zero there, it keeps a constant sign throughout that interval. So testing one point in each interval gives its sign.

## When to use

Solve inequalities by moving everything to one side, finding the zeros and the points where the function is undefined, then testing one point per interval, as for $\frac{10x-1}{x^2-6x+5}\le -1$. Do not multiply across by an expression of unknown sign.
