The line $y=L$ that $f$ approaches as $x\to\pm\infty$. The two ends can differ, and a graph may cross its horizontal asymptote.

## When to use

Take limits at both ends: $\frac{3x-1}{x-2}\to 3$ and $e^{1/x}\to 1$. A function never has both a horizontal and an oblique asymptote on the same side.
