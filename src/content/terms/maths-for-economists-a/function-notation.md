Writing $f(x)$, read "f of x", for the output of the rule $f$ when the input is $x$. To evaluate, replace every $x$ in the formula by whatever sits inside the brackets, brackets included.

## When to use

Use it for expressions such as $f(a+10)-f(a)$, where you substitute the whole bracket for $x$. Differences of square roots are then simplified with a [[Conjugate]].
