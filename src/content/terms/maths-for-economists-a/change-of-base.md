The identity $\log_a c=\frac{\log_b c}{\log_b a}$, which rewrites a logarithm in any convenient base.

## When to use

Use it to compute a logarithm on a calculator that has only $\ln$ or $\log_{10}$, and to match bases before applying the other log rules.
