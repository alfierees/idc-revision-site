The derivative of a product is $(fg)'=f'g+fg'$.

## When to use

Use it when two non-trivial functions multiply, for example $(2x-9)(x^2+6)$ gives $6x^2-18x+12$. Expanding first and differentiating gives the same answer as a check.

See [[Lec_07-The Derivative, Differentiability and Rules|Lec 07]].
