The derivative of a composition: differentiate the outer function (keeping the inside untouched), then multiply by the derivative of the inner one, $[g(f(x))]'=g'(f(x))\cdot f'(x)$.

## When to use

Use it for layered functions, such as $[g(3x)]^4$ with three layers, which gives $12[g(3x)]^3g'(3x)$. Forgetting the factor from the inside is the usual mistake. Evaluate the inner function first at the given point.
