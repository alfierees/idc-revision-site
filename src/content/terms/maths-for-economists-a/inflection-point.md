A point where $f$ is continuous and $f''$ changes sign, so the concavity switches. $f''(c)=0$ is not required, and $f''(c)=0$ alone is not enough.

## When to use

Use only those two conditions: a flat tangent, a corner or a vertical tangent are not the test. The steepest slope of $f$ occurs at an inflection point, and excluded domain values never count.
