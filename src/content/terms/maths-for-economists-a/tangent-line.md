The line touching a curve at a point, with slope $f'(a)$ and passing through $(a,f(a))$. Its equation is $y-f(a)=f'(a)(x-a)$.

## When to use

Find the point, then the slope, then use point-slope form, for example $y=-6x-8$ for $x^2+1$ at $x=-3$. A tangent line exists only if the curve settles on one direction from both sides, so corners and cusps have none. For composites use the chain rule to get the slope.
