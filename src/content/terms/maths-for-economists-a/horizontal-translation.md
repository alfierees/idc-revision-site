The graph of $y=f(x+c)$ is the graph of $f$ shifted $c$ units to the left, and $f(x-c)$ shifts it $c$ units to the right. The sign inside the bracket works against intuition.

## When to use

Test a point if unsure: $f(x-2)=(x-2)^2$ has its vertex at $x=2$. It is also how $8\cdot 2^x=2^{x+3}$ becomes a shift left by $3$.
