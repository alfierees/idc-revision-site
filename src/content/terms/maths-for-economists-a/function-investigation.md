The full routine for sketching a function: domain and continuity, asymptotes, axis intercepts, a sign table for $f'$ (extremes, increase and decrease), a sign table for $f''$ (inflection, concavity), then the graph.

## When to use

Apply it to a question like $f(x)=\left(1+\frac{2}{x}\right)^2$, which has a vertical asymptote at $x=0$, a horizontal asymptote $y=1$, a minimum at $(-2,0)$ and an inflection point at $(-3,\frac{1}{9})$.

See [[Lec_12-Asymptotes, Function Investigation and Reading the Derivative|Lec 12]].
