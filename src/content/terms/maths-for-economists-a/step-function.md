A piecewise-constant function whose value jumps at each breakpoint. The telephone charge billed per started minute is the example: 4.8 dollars for the first minute, then 3.5 dollars for each extra minute or part of one.

## When to use

Appears in "or part of it" pricing questions. Draw each step with an open circle at the left end and a closed circle at the right end.

See [[Lec_02-Piecewise Functions and Business Applications|Lec 02]].
