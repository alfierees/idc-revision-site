With the chain rule built in, $(\ln f)'=\frac{f'}{f}$ and $(\log_a f)'=\frac{f'}{f\ln a}$.

## When to use

For example $y=\ln(4x+1)$ gives $y'=\frac{4}{4x+1}$.
