Revenue minus cost, $\pi(p)=R(p)-C(p)$. In the clock-radio question it is a downward parabola, so its maximum sits at the vertex.

## When to use

Set it positive to find the profitable range of prices ($80<p<180$ there) and use the vertex for maximum profit. Note the profit-maximising price need not maximise revenue.
