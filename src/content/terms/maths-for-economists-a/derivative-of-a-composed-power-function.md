For $y=[f(x)]^n$, the derivative is $y'=n[f(x)]^{n-1}\cdot f'(x)$. It is the chain rule with an outer power $n$.

## When to use

For example $y=(3x^2+1)^4$ gives $y'=24x(3x^2+1)^3$. Do not drop the factor $f'(x)$.
