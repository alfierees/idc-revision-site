The limit of the difference quotient, $f'(x)=\lim_{h\to 0}\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}$, if it exists. It is the slope of the tangent line at $x$, and it must exist as a finite number from both sides.

## When to use

Test a point in order: domain, continuity, then left and right slopes. It is also the starting point for marginal functions, sign tables for monotonicity and the second derivative for concavity.
