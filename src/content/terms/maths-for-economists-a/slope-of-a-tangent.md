The limit of the secant slopes, $\lim_{h\to 0}\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}$, which is the idea behind the derivative.

## When to use

Introduced through $f(x)=x^2+1$: the slope at $x=1$ is $2$ and at a general $x$ it is $2x$. Use it to get the tangent line at a given $x$.
