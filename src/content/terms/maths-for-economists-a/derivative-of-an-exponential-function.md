With the chain rule built in, $(e^f)'=f'e^f$ and $(a^f)'=f'a^f\ln a$, for $a>0$, $a\ne 1$.

## When to use

For example $y=e^{4x+1}$ gives $y'=4e^{4x+1}$, which is why the matching integral carries a factor $\frac{1}{4}$.
