A form such as $\frac{0}{0}$, $\frac{\infty}{\infty}$, $\infty-\infty$ or $1^{\infty}$ whose value is not fixed until the expression is rewritten. It is a signal to rewrite, not an answer. Note that $\frac{c}{0}$ with $c\ne 0$ is not indeterminate: it is an infinite limit.

## When to use

Factor and cancel, use a conjugate, combine into one fraction, or factor out the biggest term. For $1^{\infty}$ use the Euler limit recipe, after checking the base really tends to $1$. The worksheet's exact list of "three cases" is not stated in the notes.
