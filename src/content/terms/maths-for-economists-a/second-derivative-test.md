At a point with $f'(c)=0$: $f''(c)>0$ gives a minimum, $f''(c)<0$ gives a maximum, and $f''(c)=0$ is inconclusive.

## When to use

Use it only where $f'(c)=0$, for example $R''(60)=-43.2<0$ confirms a maximum of revenue at $x=60$. If $f''(c)=0$ or $f'$ does not exist, fall back on the sign of $f'$ around $c$.

See [[Lec_11-Concavity, Inflection Points and Asymptotes|Lec 11]].
