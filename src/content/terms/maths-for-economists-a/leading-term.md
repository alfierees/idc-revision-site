The highest-degree term of a polynomial, which dominates the behaviour for large $\lvert x\rvert$.

## When to use

Use it to evaluate limits at infinity, for example $\frac{-2x^2}{5x}=-\frac{2x}{5}$ shows the limit as $x\to-\infty$ is $+\infty$. Alternatively divide top and bottom by the highest power of $x$ in the denominator.
