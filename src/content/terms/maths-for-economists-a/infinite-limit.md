A limit where $f(x)$ grows without bound as $x$ approaches a point, recorded as $+\infty$ or $-\infty$. It does not exist as a number, but the direction is recorded.

## When to use

Appears when the numerator tends to a non-zero number and the denominator to $0$. Check the sign on each side: an even power or absolute value keeps both sides the same, an odd power or a bare $(x-a)$ makes them differ.
