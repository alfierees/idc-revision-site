A local maximum or minimum, found at a critical point where $f'$ changes sign: plus to minus is a maximum, minus to plus is a minimum. It can occur even where $f'$ does not exist, as at a cusp or corner.

## When to use

Use the sign table of $f'$, or the second derivative test where $f'(c)=0$. If the sign does not change, as for $\sqrt[3]{x-1}$ at $x=1$, there is no extremum.
