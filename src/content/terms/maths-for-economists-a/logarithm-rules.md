The product, quotient and power identities for logarithms: $\log_a(bc)=\log_a b+\log_a c$, $\log_a\frac{b}{c}=\log_a b-\log_a c$ and $\log_a b^n=n\log_a b$, together with change of base. Multiplying adds exponents, dividing subtracts them, and a power multiplies the exponent.

## When to use

Use them to expand or combine logs. Common mistakes: $\log(b+c)$ does not split, and $\log_a b^n=n\log_a b$ is not $(\log_a b)^n$.
