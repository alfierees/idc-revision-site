The derivative $f'(x)$, which estimates the change in $f$ when $x$ rises by one unit, from $x$ to $x+1$. It is also called the rate of change of $f(x)$ with respect to $x$.

## When to use

State the meaning in words with the unit step: if $N'(10)=40$ for sales against advertising in thousands of dollars, another 1000 dollars raises sales by about $40$ units. The estimate is close to the exact change when the function is nearly straight over one unit.
