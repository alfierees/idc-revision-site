Compact notation $\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i$ for a sum over an index. The subscript says where counting starts, the number on top where it stops, and the expression after it is the term.

## When to use

Rewrite a long sum with a pattern in the brackets, for example $(x_3+3+9)+\dots+(x_{10}+10+9)=\sum_{i=3}^{10}(x_i+i+9)$. From $i=m$ to $n$ the number of terms is $n-m+1$.
