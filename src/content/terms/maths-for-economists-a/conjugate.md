The same expression with the sign flipped on a root term, such as $\sqrt{a+5}+\sqrt{a-5}$ for $\sqrt{a+5}-\sqrt{a-5}$. Multiplying by it turns a difference of square roots into a difference of squares and clears the root.

## When to use

Use it on a $\frac{0}{0}$ limit with a root, such as $\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\sqrt{5x+9}-3}{x}=\frac56$, and when differentiating $\sqrt{x}$ from the definition.

See [[PS_04-Limits and Continuity|PS 04]].
