A function defined by different formulas on different intervals of $x$. The join points need separate checks for limits, continuity and differentiability.

## When to use

Check the boundary points: both closed ends must agree, and for continuity or differentiability test only the seam where the formula changes. A jump settles non-differentiability in one line.
