The production cost written as a function of quantity or of price. Substituting the demand equation into $C(x)=72000+60x$ gives $C(p)=432000-1800p$.

## When to use

Rewrite cost in terms of price when revenue is also in terms of price, so the two can be compared and the profit function formed.
