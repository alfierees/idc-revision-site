A single hole where the limit exists but the function is undefined or has a different value there. A cancelled common factor gives one, such as $\frac{x^2-4}{x-2}$ at $x=2$, where the limit is $4$.

## When to use

Cancel first when finding asymptotes: a factor that cancels top and bottom gives a hole, not a vertical asymptote. Continuity fails at it because the limit differs from the value.
