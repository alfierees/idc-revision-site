A point where the function is continuous but the two one-sided slopes are different finite numbers, so there is no tangent line and the function is not differentiable.

## When to use

Seen where pieces meet, such as $4-x^2$ and $(x-2)^2$ at $x=2$ (slopes $-4$ and $0$). It is a critical point, and it can be an extremum even though $f'$ does not exist.

See [[PS_07-Derivative Definition, Differentiability and Differentiation Rules|PS 07]].
