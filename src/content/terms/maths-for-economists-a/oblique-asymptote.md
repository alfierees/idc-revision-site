A slanted line $y=mx+b$ that $f$ approaches as $x\to\pm\infty$. Divide the polynomials: the quotient is the line and the remainder fraction tends to $0$.

## When to use

Look for it when the numerator's degree is one more than the denominator's, for example $\frac{x^2-2x}{x+2}=x-4+\frac{8}{x+2}$ gives $y=x-4$.
