The value, or $\pm\infty$, that $f(x)$ approaches as $x\to\pm\infty$.

## When to use

For quotients of polynomials compare degrees: lower on top gives $0$, equal degrees give the ratio of leading coefficients, higher on top gives $\pm\infty$. Take care that $\sqrt{x^2}=\lvert x\rvert$ when $x<0$.
