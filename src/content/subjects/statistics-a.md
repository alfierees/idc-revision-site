Statistics I for Economics (Asael Sklar, Reichman University, Year 1 Semester 1). The course builds probability from sets and axioms, then covers conditional probability and Bayes, combinatorics, discrete and continuous random variables, joint distributions, covariance and correlation, the normal distribution and the central limit theorem.
