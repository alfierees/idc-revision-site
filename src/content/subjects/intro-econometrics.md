Introduction to Econometrics (Dr. Aluma Dembo, Reichman University, Year 2 Semester 1). The foundational metrics course: simple and multiple regression with OLS, the classical assumptions, hypothesis testing with t and F statistics, goodness of fit, multicollinearity, log and quadratic models, dummy variables, interaction terms and the Chow test, and heteroskedasticity, all worked through R output. Lecture notes cover lectures 8 and 10 to 13 only.
