Legal and Tax Aspects of Entrepreneurship (Dr. Yuval Reinfeld, Reichman University, Year 2 Semester 1). Which legal questions matter at each stage of a startup: the Israeli legal system, contract law, company law and corporate structures, intellectual property, digital regulation and the GDPR, employee stock options and Section 102 tax, and cap-table basics. Assessment is 10% group midterm assignment and 90% final (the example exam pairs an open case question with multiple-choice questions).
