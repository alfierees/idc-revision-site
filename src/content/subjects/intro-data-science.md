Data Science for Entrepreneurs (Daniel Karalnik and Liam Tal, Reichman University, Year 2 Semester 1). The full data-science workflow in Python: exploring data with pandas and charts, classification (decision trees, logistic regression, KNN, random forests), regression and regularisation, clustering and PCA, recommendation systems, NLP and image analysis, and an introduction to deep learning. Homeworks and recitations practise each step in Jupyter notebooks, and the course ends with a group final project.
