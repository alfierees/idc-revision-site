> Counted from the question list of every past paper on this site, [[PP_01]] (2009 Moed A) to [[PP_34]] (2025 Moed B). A topic counts once per paper, however many parts it has. The classification is mine, from each paper's "What this paper tests" table, so treat the numbers as a guide to weight, not an official count.

## How often each topic appears

| Topic | All 34 papers | 2016 to 2025 (20) | 2021 to 2025 (10) |
|---|---|---|---|

| Derivative from the definition (differentiability, or a tangent slope) | 28 | 17 | **10** |
| Piecewise function: continuity at the seam, often with a parameter | 28 | 16 | 8 |
| Full function investigation | 28 | 15 | 8 |
| Transformations: $f(x+c)$, $f(-x)$, $-f$, $\lvert f\rvert$, a shifted exponential | 18 | 14 | **10** |

| True or false claims | 6 | 3 | 0 |

**What this says:** every paper since 2016 has an economics question, and every paper since 2021 has a derivative from the definition and a transformation. The full investigation is in three papers out of four; the papers without one (2016 Moed B, 2017 Moed B, 2019 Moed A and both 2024 papers) have more short segments instead. Integrals only arrived in 2019 but have been in half the recent papers. True or false has not appeared since 2020 Moed B, and $f'$-graph reading not since 2019 Moed B.

## Where to spend the time

1. **The investigation routine** (§8 to §10 of the [[Cheat Sheet (Lec 1–12)|cheat sheet]]). It is the most points in one place, and the functions repeat: cube roots and $\tfrac23$ powers with cusps, rational functions with holes, $(1 + \tfrac2x)^2$, a piecewise parabola and root.
2. **The economics question.** Every paper. The marks are in the meaning sentences, not the derivative.
3. **Piecewise + the definition of the derivative.** A fixed script: continuity, both one-sided difference quotients, then name the point.
4. **The limits list** (§2 to §4): about 4 short limits a paper, and the Euler trap where the base does not tend to 1.
5. **Tangents of composites** and **transformations**: short, frequent, and almost free once practised.
