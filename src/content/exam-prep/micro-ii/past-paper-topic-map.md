## The market question, paper by paper

Every paper's market uses a quasi-linear consumer and the firm $f = l^{1/4}k^{1/4}$, so the cost is always $C = 2p\,x^2$ when both input prices are $p$.

| Paper | Demand | Cost | Equilibrium | Policy asked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 A | $100 - 2x$ | $x^2$ | $x = 25$, $p = 50$ | consumer subsidy 20; cap at 10 |
| 2023 B / 2025 A | $120 - 2x$ | $2x^2$ | $x = 20$, $p = 80$ | fixed price 90 and 60 (same welfare 1125) |
| 2023 example | $100 - 2x$ | $x^2$ | $x = 25$, $p = 50$ | fixed price 60 (W 1200), 90 (FS falls to 425) |
| 2024 A | $60 - 2x$ | $4x^2$, then $4x$ | $x = 6$, $p = 48$; then $x = 28$, $p = 4$ | minimum price: FS peaks at 50, worse above 52 |
| 2024 B / 2025 B | $180 - 2x$ | $2x^2$ | $x = 30$, $p = 120$ | cap at 80: CS 1600, FS 800, W 2400 |
| 2026 A | $100 - 2x$ | $x^2$ | $x = 25$, $p = 50$ | weights on CS and FS: ceiling 33.33, floor 66.67 |

## True/false claims that come back

| Claim (short) | Verdict | Papers |
|---|---|---|
| The planner prefers $A$, so $A$ Pareto dominates | False | 2023 example, 2026 A |
| Efficient $A$ dominates inefficient $B$ | False | 2025 A, PS5 |
| Quasi-linear technology $g(l) + k$: labour never rises with output | False | 2023 A, 2026 A |
| Quasi-linear technology: MC equals $p_k$ beyond a quantity | True | 2023 B |
| Same input bundle when both input prices are multiplied | True | 2023 A, 2025 B |
| A preference change moves the PPF | False | 2023 B, 2023 example, 2025 A |
| Identical Cobb-Douglas shares: the contract curve is the diagonal | True | 2023 example, 2024 A, 2025 A |
| A ceiling always raises CS | False | 2023 example, 2025 A |
| A consumer subsidy lowers welfare | True | 2023 B, 2025 B |
| Lump-sum tax fixes an externality | False | 2023 A |
| Equilibrium utility at least endowment utility | True | 2023 B |
| Endowment on the contract curve: no trade | True | 2025 B |

## What this means for revision

1. **The market question is free points.** Same firm, same consumer, every year. Drill the short-side rule and the four areas until they take five minutes (the [[Graph Gallery]] has all six markets).
2. **The production economy is a recipe.** Six steps, then three comparative statics. Practise 2023 B Q2 and 2026 A Q3 in full.
3. **True/false is half the paper.** Learn the counterexamples in [[Common Mistakes]]: they are reused almost word for word.
4. **2024-style papers** add an exchange economy and one of public goods, a planner or a perfect-substitutes firm. Do 2024 A and B once each.
