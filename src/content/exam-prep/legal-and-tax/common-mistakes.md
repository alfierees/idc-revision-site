## Contracts and agreements

| Mistake | Why it's wrong | Check |
|---|---|---|
| A memo of understandings binds because it lists terms | titled **non-binding**, it shows no gmirat da'at; substance beats label only when the label is not an express "non-binding" | DataVault Q3 |
| Israeli contracts need consideration | **not required** in Israel | Lecture 4 |
| Every contract must be in writing | only some deals, e.g. **real estate** (s.8 Land Law) | Lecture 4 |
| An NDA you did not read does not bind | gmirat da'at is tested **objectively**: a signature binds | example exam, Part 1 |
| Good faith starts once the contract is signed | **s.12** covers negotiation; s.39 performance | MCQ sheet Q34 |
| A non-compete always holds | generally **unenforceable** in Israel unless it protects a real trade secret or is paid for | Lecture 2 |

## Companies

| Mistake | Why it's wrong | Check |
|---|---|---|
| The board appoints the board | the **general meeting** does (s.57); the board appoints the CEO (s.92) | example exam Q3 |
| A Ltd. has the simplest tax | it is taxed **twice**: corporate tax, then dividend tax | DataVault Q10 |
| Limited liability always protects the shareholders | the veil can be pierced (s.6): fraud, unreasonable risk to creditors | DataVault Q11 |
| A shareholder loan ranks with the other creditors | it can be **subordinated** (*Northern Chicks*) | MCQ sheet Q46 |
| A bondholder is an owner | a bond is a **loan**: the holder is a creditor | Lecture 3 |

## Intellectual property

| Mistake | Why it's wrong | Check |
|---|---|---|
| The company owns what its founders built | the **creator** owns it until a **written assignment** | DataVault Q2 |
| Registration in my name proves I own it | between founders it settles nothing, above all for a **joint** work | example exam, Part 1 |
| Patent the algorithm to keep it safe | a patent **publishes** it and lasts 20 years; a trade secret lasts while secret | MCQ sheet Q64 |
| Copyright must be registered | it is **automatic** | example exam Q18 |
| A trade secret is protected whatever you do | it needs **reasonable efforts** (NDAs, access control) | Lecture 5 |

## GDPR

| Mistake | Why it's wrong | Check |
|---|---|---|
| An Israeli company is outside the GDPR | it applies to anyone processing data of people **in the EU** (Art. 3) | DataVault, Part 1 |
| A cookie notice or ToS is consent | consent is an **affirmative act before** collection (*Meta*, 2023) | DataVault Q5 |
| Consent is the only lawful basis | there are **six** (Art. 6) | Lecture 7 |
| "Anonymous" financial data is low risk | financial data and AI training are **high risk**: a **DPIA** first | DataVault Q6 |
| The fine is up to 4% | €20M **or** 4%, **whichever is higher** | Lecture 7 |
| Privacy Shield still covers US transfers | struck down by *Schrems II*; the 2023 **Data Privacy Framework** replaced it; SCCs survive | DataVault Q18 |

## ESOP and Section 102

| Mistake | Why it's wrong | Check |
|---|---|---|
| An option obliges you to buy | it is a **right, not an obligation** | example exam Q1 |
| The 24 months run from vesting or exercise | from the **grant** | DataVault Q8 |
| The tax is due at exercise (Capital Gain Track) | at **sale** or release from the trustee | Lecture 6 |
| Every founder can use Section 102 | **controlling shareholders** and consultants fall under **Section 3(i)** | DataVault, Part 1 |
| A promise of equity is a 102 grant | needs a written grant, a trustee and the 30/45/90-day deadlines | DataVault Q9 |
| Vested options are lost on any dismissal | only **for cause**; otherwise 3 months to exercise (12 for death or disability) | ESOP set Q5, Q11 |
| Mixing the 45 and 90 days | **45** for the board's grant approval, **90** for the signed agreements | ESOP set Q9 |

## Cap tables

| Mistake | Why it's wrong | Check |
|---|---|---|
| \$3M for 20% → pre-money = 3 / 0.2 = \$15M | that is the **post**-money; pre is \$12M | ESOP set Q27 |
| Post-money = 15 + 3 | the post-money already includes the money | ESOP set Q26 |
| A 2% grant = 2% of today's shares | the new shares enlarge the total: use $S p / (1 - p)$ | ESOP set Q25 |
| Ownership on issued shares | deals use **fully diluted** shares, pool included | ESOP set Q23 |
| A pre-money pool dilutes the investor too | it dilutes the **existing holders only** | ESOP set Q30 |
| Full ratchet is founder-friendly | it is the harshest; **broad-based weighted average** is the friendly one | ESOP set Q33 |
| Guess the next letter in the cycle | the ESOP set's A, B, C, D cycle is an artefact; the content decides | ESOP set |
