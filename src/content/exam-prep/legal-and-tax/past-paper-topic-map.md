## The exam's layout

| Part | What | Points |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | one startup case, "advise X", up to **1 page** | **25** |
| 2 | **15 multiple-choice** questions, 4 options | **75** (5 each) |

That is the instructor's example exam. The DataVault paper prints 20 MCQs "× 5 = 100" next to a "25 + 75" header, which cannot both hold: trust the structure (one case, then MCQs), not its point split.

## The case: the same story twice

| | Example exam (SmartMeal) | DataVault practice exam |
|---|---|---|
| Founders | Dana and Amir, equal shares | Liron, Maya and Oren, 33.3% each |
| The memo | "Initial Understandings – Non-Binding" | "Collaboration Memo", non-binding |
| IP dispute | jointly built algorithm, registered in Dana's name, payments to Dana | Maya licenses the ML algorithm to a competitor, says she built it alone |
| Decision dispute | Dana approved the use of information without Amir | Maya decided alone; Liron wants an equal say |
| Third issue | a signed NDA lets NutriLabs use information beyond the purpose | EU user data with only a cookie notice (GDPR), **and** Oren's promised Section 102 equity with no vesting schedule |
| Asked | founders' agreement; NDA; IP assignment | founders' agreement; IP; GDPR; Section 102 |

Expect the same frame with one or two of NDA, GDPR and Section 102 swapped in. The template is in the [[Legal & Tax Cheat Sheet|cheat sheet]] (§9) and the [[Exam Question Playbook]].

**What comes back almost word for word**

- What an option is: **a right, not an obligation** (example Q1, ESOP set Q1).
- The **Capital Gain Track** and its **24 months from grant** (example Q14, Q19; DataVault Q8; MCQ sheet Q72, Q76, Q79).
- Who **appoints the board** (the general meeting, s.57) and what the board **cannot delegate** (example Q3, Q4).
- Why **vesting** (to keep founders and employees committed; example Q11, DataVault Q17).
- Why **valuation** matters (it sets how much equity a given amount buys; example Q20, DataVault Q20).
- **Copyright** for software code; **trade secret** for a confidential algorithm (example Q18, DataVault Q16).
- The **cliff** (MCQ sheet Q24 = Q78) and **shareholder-loan subordination** (Q46 = Q80).

## Keyed answers that need a second look

Every key is kept on the paper pages. These are the ones where the lecture points elsewhere: know both.

| Paper and question | Key | The issue |
|---|---|---|
| Example exam, Part 1 (IP) | IP "may remain with the creator"; registration supports Dana | the algorithm was built **jointly**; registration is not proof of ownership between founders. Add that line |
| DataVault, Part 1 (Section 102) | Oren is eligible; ~30% vs 50%+; 24 or 60 months; file within 90 days of grant | Section 102 does **not** cover controlling shareholders (a 33.3% founder may be one: Section 3(i)). Lecture rates: **25%** and up to **~59%**; 24 months from grant, no 60-month track; deadlines 30, 45, 90 days |
| DataVault Q10 | Ltd. gives "the simplest tax treatment" | a company is taxed **twice** (corporate tax, then dividend tax) |
| DataVault Q16 | D, trade secret for an algorithm "as source code" | copyright (C) is at least as defensible for source code |
| DataVault Q3, Q8, Q15 | defensible but overstated | a "non-binding" label is evidence, not conclusive (*Rabinai*); "or vest date" is unsupported; no dollar ranges in the lecture |
| MCQ sheet Q9 | A, substantive rule of law = rights, equality, justice | the course slide labels **publication, clarity, non-retroactivity** as substantive: an exam by this lecturer may follow the slide |
| MCQ sheet Q34 | D, s.39 covers negotiation and performance | negotiation is **s.12**, s.39 is performance |
| MCQ sheet Q64 | B, trade secret: no time limit, lost on disclosure | D (no protection against independent discovery) is also true |
| ESOP set Q2 | B, the trustee "deposits" | the company deposits the agreements **with** the trustee |
| ESOP set, all 40 | keys run A, B, C, D in a cycle | every key still matches the lectures; do not guess by the pattern |
