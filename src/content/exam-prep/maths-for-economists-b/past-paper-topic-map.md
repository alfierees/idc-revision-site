> [!warning] The linear system disappeared in 2024
> It was in **29 of the 33 papers** and every paper from 2009 to 2023, then in none of the four from 2024 and 2025, which had a one-variable optimisation and a log-base question instead. Lectures 10 to 12 are still in the course, so prepare it: it is the most mechanical 25 points on the paper if it comes back.

## The four questions, paper by paper

| Paper | Profit / surface | Second question | Third question | Function study |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 B | Profit target, max by $D$, revenue in prices | Tangent of $(\ln x)^x$, $0\cdot\infty$, critical points of a product with $e^{x+y}$ | Level-curve slope, $0^0$, Lagrange | $xe^{-\sqrt x}$ on $[0, 2)$ |
| 2025 A | Log-cost profit: marginal, differential, saddle, $P_{xy}$ | Domain and contours of $e^{2x/(x^2+y^2)}$, $y'$, "is it critical?" | $\sum x_j f_{x_j}$, Lagrange with ten variables | $x^x + 1$ on $[0.4, 1)$ |
| 2024 B | Slice tangent, marginal cost, marginal-profit equation | Profit with a per-unit tax, inverse function, log limit | $g(x) = f(e^x, 0)$ extremum, limit, $y'$ | $\ln x + \ln(2 - x)$ |
| 2024 A | Marginal profit, tangent from a partial, $f_x = f_y$ | Global extremum, $\tfrac00$ and $0^0$ limits, minimum average cost | Orchard maximum, implicit slope | $\log_x(x - 1)$ |
| 2023 B | Cost slice tangent, marginal profit, max by $D$ | **Parametric system** | Level curve of $e^x + e^y + e^{1-x-y}$, critical point | $x^2\ln x$ on $(0, 1]$ |
| 2023 A | Profit, differential, saddle, Lagrange on cost | **Parametric system** | Domain, slope, $x^{\sqrt x}$ limit, tangent of $x^{\ln x}$ | $\dfrac{e^x + e^{-x}}{e^x - e^{-x}}$ on $[1, \infty)$ |
| 2022 B | Profit, a parameter from marginal profit, max by $D$ | **Parametric system** | Log surface, missing critical point, limit | $xe^{-x}$ on $(0, \infty)$ |
| 2022 A | Marginal profit, cross-section tangent | **Parametric system** | Domain of a sum of logs, $y'$, $D$ test, log differentiation | $\ln x + \tfrac1x$ on $[\tfrac12, \infty)$ |
| 2021 B | Revenue in prices and quantities, meaning of a partial | **Parametric system** | Implicit slope, limit, classify $x^2 + y^2 - x^2y^2$ | $\dfrac{3}{(e^x - 4)(e^x - 1)}$ on $[\tfrac12, 1]$ |
| 2021 A | Log-cost profit, saddle, Lagrange on a circle | **Parametric system** (two equal columns) | Implicit slope, slice extremum | $\ln\!\left(\tfrac1x - 1\right)$ |
| 2020 C | Level curve, slope, slice, limit | Constrained profit (Lagrange), local extrema | **Parametric system** | Domain, contours of $\ln(xy)$, differential |
| 2020 B | Average profit, $dx/dy$ on a level curve | **Parametric system** (four unknowns) | Level-curve slope, limit, extrema of $(x-2)(y+3)e^{x+y}$ | Mixed partials, slice, log differentiation |
| 2020 A | Partials in a business setting, profit in prices | **Parametric system** | Level-curve slope, two limits, Lagrange on a circle | $\tfrac{x}{\ln x}$ on two intervals |

Column order follows the topics, not the question numbers: the papers move the questions around.

The 20 older papers (2009 to 2019) test the same things with a heavier dose of systems and Lagrange. Worth doing for practice on the parts that are rarer now:

- **Homogeneity identities** $x f_x + y f_y$: [[PP_03|2010 B]] Q1, [[PP_18|2018 B]] Q1.
- **$x^x$ and its minimum**: [[PP_10|2014 B]] Q2, [[PP_17|2018 A]] Q4.
- **Full studies** of $x - e^x$ on three intervals ([[PP_15|2017 A]] Q2), $x(\ln x)^2$ ([[PP_16|2017 B]] Q2), $x^2e^x$ ([[PP_02|2010 A]] Q4).
- **Contours of $\max\{x, y\}$**: [[PP_18|2018 B]] Q3.
- **Reading a reduced matrix**, true or false on zero columns: [[PP_12|2015 B]] Q4, [[PP_10|2014 B]] Q3.
