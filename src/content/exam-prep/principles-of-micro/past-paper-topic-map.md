## What this means for revision

1. **Master the market block first.** Q1 to Q5 are 25% of the grade and follow the same script every year: equilibrium, surplus, a per-unit tax or subsidy, DWL, then a ceiling or a Pigouvian tax. See the [[Cheat Sheet (Lec 1–14)|cheat sheet]] §8 to §11.
2. **Next tier (every paper):** shifts, comparative advantage, externality gap, price controls.
3. **Then the near-certain calculation types:** the elasticity new-price question and the "revenue rose, what is the new quantity" question.
4. **Costs of the firm** come up as one or two short calculations a paper (AFC, economic profit, long-run equilibrium). Learn the identities and they are quick points.
5. Trade, tariffs and quotas (Lec 13 Part 2) have appeared only once in a multiple-choice question. Know the tariff table, but don't over-invest.
