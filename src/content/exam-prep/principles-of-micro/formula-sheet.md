> Linear curves throughout: demand $P = a - bQ$, supply $P = c + dQ$. Every number in the market block (Q1 to Q5) comes from these few lines.

## Market equilibrium

$$
Q^* = \frac{a - c}{b + d}, \qquad P^* = a - bQ^*
$$

From $Q$-form curves ($Q_d = m - nP$, $Q_s = k + jP$): $P^* = \dfrac{m - k}{n + j}$.

Market curve from individuals: add **quantities** at each price, $Q_{mkt}(P) = \sum_i q_i(P)$.

## Surplus

$$
CS = \tfrac12 \, Q^* \, (a - P^*), \qquad PS = \tfrac12 \, Q^* \, (P^* - c), \qquad TS = CS + PS
$$

Per unit: CS = WTP − P. PS = P − MC.

## Taxes and subsidies (per unit)

| | Tax $T$ on sellers | Subsidy $s$ to sellers |
|---|---|---|
| New supply | $P = c + T + dQ$ | $P = c - s + dQ$ |
| New quantity | $Q_T = \dfrac{a - c - T}{b + d}$ | $Q_s = \dfrac{a - c + s}{b + d}$ |
| Price gap | $P_B - P_S = T$ | $P_S - P_B = s$ |
| Government | revenue $= T \cdot Q_T$ | cost $= s \cdot Q_s$ |
| DWL | $\tfrac12 \, T \, (Q^* - Q_T)$ | $\tfrac12 \, s \, (Q_s - Q^*)$ |

Tax on **buyers**: demand shifts down, $P = a - T - bQ$. Same $Q_T$, $P_B$, $P_S$.

$$
\text{Buyers' burden} = P_B - P^* = \frac{b}{b+d}\,T, \qquad \text{Sellers' burden} = P^* - P_S = \frac{d}{b+d}\,T
$$

## Price controls

Plug the controlled price $\bar P$ into both curves: $Q_d(\bar P)$ and $Q_s(\bar P)$.

$$
\text{Shortage (ceiling)} = Q_d - Q_s, \qquad \text{Surplus (floor)} = Q_s - Q_d, \qquad Q_{traded} = \min(Q_d, Q_s)
$$

## Elasticity

$$
E_d = \frac{\%\Delta Q^d}{\%\Delta P}, \qquad \%\Delta x = \frac{x_2 - x_1}{(x_1 + x_2)/2}
$$

$$
P_2 = P_1 \cdot \frac{2 + x}{2 - x} \;\; (x = \%\Delta P), \qquad Q_2 = Q_1 \cdot \frac{2 + y}{2 - y} \;\; (y = \%\Delta Q), \qquad x = \frac{y}{E_d}
$$

$$
E_I = \frac{\%\Delta Q^d}{\%\Delta I}, \qquad E_{AB} = \frac{\%\Delta Q_A}{\%\Delta P_B}, \qquad E_s = \frac{\%\Delta Q^s}{\%\Delta P}
$$

$TR = P \times Q$. Elastic: $P$ and $TR$ move opposite ways. Inelastic: same way. Unit: $TR$ unchanged.

## Costs and profit

$$
TC = FC + VC, \qquad AFC = \frac{FC}{Q}, \qquad AVC = \frac{VC}{Q}, \qquad ATC = \frac{TC}{Q} = AFC + AVC, \qquad MC = \frac{\Delta TC}{\Delta Q}
$$

$$
MP_L = \frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta L}, \qquad MC = \frac{w}{MP_L} \;\; (\text{labour the only variable input})
$$

$$
\text{Economic profit} = TR - \text{explicit} - \text{implicit}, \qquad \text{Accounting profit} = TR - \text{explicit}
$$

## The competitive firm

$$
P = AR = MR, \qquad \text{produce where } P = MC, \qquad \text{Profit} = (P - ATC) \cdot Q
$$

| | Condition |
|---|---|
| Produce (short run) | $P \ge \min AVC$ |
| Stay (long run) | $P \ge \min ATC$ |
| Long-run equilibrium | $P = MR = MC = \min ATC$, profit $= 0$ |

## Externalities

$$
MSC = MPC + \text{external cost}, \qquad MSB = MPB + \text{external benefit}
$$

Market: $MPB = MPC$. Optimum: $MSB = MSC$. Corrective tax or subsidy to reach $Q_{target}$:

$$
t = P_D(Q_{target}) - P_S(Q_{target}) \quad (\text{positive: tax; negative: subsidy of } \lvert t \rvert)
$$

## PPF and trade

$$
\text{OC of 1 unit of } X = \frac{\text{units of } Y \text{ given up}}{\text{units of } X \text{ gained}} = \frac{\text{hours per } X}{\text{hours per } Y}
$$

Terms of trade must lie strictly between the two producers' opportunity costs. Exports $= Q^S - Q^D$ at $P_W$; imports $= Q^D - Q^S$. Tariff revenue $= \text{tariff} \times \text{imports}$.
