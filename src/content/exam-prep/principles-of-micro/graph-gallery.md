> Step through the interactive graphs with the arrows, then use the last step to play. The static diagrams are the lecture figures, with the one thing to read off each.

## 1. The market block: equilibrium, surplus, tax, DWL

This is Q1 to Q4 of every past paper. The default numbers are the 2025 Moed A olive oil market: $P = 60 - 0.5Q$, $P = 30 + 0.25Q$, a tax of 2 on sellers. Try the presets: with steep demand buyers carry most of the tax, with steep supply sellers do. Drag the tax below zero for a subsidy.

```graph
type: linear-market-tax
a: 60
b: 0.5
c: 30
d: 0.25
t: 2
```

The same graph with the 2022 Moed B jeans market ($P = 350 - 2Q$, $P = 250 + 2Q$, tax 20):

```graph
type: linear-market-tax
a: 350
b: 2
c: 250
d: 2
t: 20
```

## 2. The competitive firm: P = MC, profit, shutdown

Step through: MC, then AC, then the price line, then $P = MC$, then the profit or loss rectangle. In the last step drag the price below min AC (loss) and below min AVC (shut down).

```graph
type: perfect-competition-firm
```
