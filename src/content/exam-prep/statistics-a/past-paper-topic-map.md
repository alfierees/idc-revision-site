## What this means for revision

1. **Q1 to Q8 are a third of the marks and need no tables**: drill the probability rules, Bayes and linear transformations until they take under five minutes each.
2. **Q16 to Q20 are almost mechanical**: one integral, one uniform percentile, one standardization, one CLT. Memorise the five-step CLT checklist.
3. **Know all six discrete distributions** by their story: the paper picks two or three of them each year, and you can't predict which.
4. **Q12 has been off-syllabus** three times out of five. Don't spend time on Markov or Chebyshev.
5. Several papers have typos or "all answers accepted" questions (flagged in each paper). If your number matches no option, re-read the question once, then pick the closest and move on.
