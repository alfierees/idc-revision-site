## What the open questions are

| Paper | Open question 1 | Open question 2 |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 A | CML, SML and a mispriced share (Q11) | NPV, IRR and payback for three projects (Q12) |
| 2025 B | jPhone franchise FCF and NPV | four bonds with the same risk |
| 2024 A | business plan FCF: sunk R&D, a loan, an alternative use of funds | five projects, five decision rules |
| 2024 B | cloth face masks FCF, NPV and the price of walking away | Danny's trip: a savings plan with an annuity due |

So the FCF table turned up in three of four in-class papers, and in both take-homes.

## Question types that repeat

| Type | Seen in | The move |
|---|---|---|
| A loan with fees at both ends, 10 months, 12% monthly | 2024 A Q6, 2025 B MCQ 2 (**same numbers**, 18.46%) | cash in vs cash out, monthly IRR, annualise |

| Price one bond from another bond's yield | 2025 A Q8, 2025 B open 2 | same risk, same yield |
| A pension or trip: save monthly, withdraw at the start | 2024 B Q7, 2024 B open 2, retake Q4 | annuity due for the withdrawals, FV of deposits |

| Choose projects under a budget or exclusivity | 2024 A Q8 and open, 2024 B Q8, 2025 A Q12 | the decision table |
| Depreciation and cash flow | 2024 A Q1 | the shield is $t_c\times DEP$ |
